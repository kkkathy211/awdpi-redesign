引言



本篇内容面向在澳大利亚开展或计划开展公益活动的华人及亚裔社群组织、非营利机构从业者，以及关注公益与社区发展的个人,同时也适用于身处海外、关注或希望支持亚裔女性国际发展计划(AWDPI)工作的亚裔女性及亚裔社群。了解澳大利亚的慈善与监管体系，对于更好地参与相关活动、支持或获取相关资源具有重要意义。


澳大利亚拥有成熟且具有高度公信力的慈善与非营利监管体系。根据澳大利亚慈善与非营利委员会（Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission, ACNC）发布的《澳大利亚慈善机构报告（第 11 版）》，澳大利亚慈善部门每年创造约2,220 亿澳元的收入，雇佣约 154 万人，每年吸引约 377 万名志愿者参与。这表明，慈善部门不仅是社会支持体系的重要组成部分，也是推动公共利益、促进社区福祉和社会凝聚力的重要力量。


在这一体系下，慈善机构的设立、治理、信息披露、筹款行为和税务待遇，均受到多层级法律制度及相关监管框架的规范与调整。

This overview is intended for community organisations, nonprofit practitioners, and individuals, particularly those from Asian and culturally and linguistically diverse backgrounds, who are interested in establishing or operating charitable activities in Australia. It is also relevant for Asian women and Asian communities overseas who are interested in supporting, engaging with, or seeking support from Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI).


Australia has a mature and highly trusted regulatory framework for charities and not-for-profits. According to ACNC’s Australian Charities Report (11th edition), Australia’s charity sector generates approximately AUD 222 billion in annual revenue, employs around 1.54 million people, and engages approximately 3.77 million volunteers annually. This demonstrates that the charity sector is not only a vital part of Australia’s social support system, but also a major force in advancing public benefit, community wellbeing, and social cohesion.


Within this framework, the establishment, governance, reporting obligations, fundraising conduct, and tax treatment of charities are all regulated through multiple layers of legal and compliance structures.

1. 澳大利亚慈善机构由谁监管

Swipe to view the English version


在澳大利亚，慈善机构的主要监管机构是澳大利亚慈善与非营利委员会（ACNC）。ACNC为为全国性的独立慈善监管机构，负责慈善机构的注册、维护公开的慈善机构登记册、监督持续性合规义务，包括年度信息申报与财务报告务。同时，ACNC通过发布指引并采取相称的合规措施，以促进行业透明度并增强公众信任。


根据机构的具体活动及其运营所在的州或领地，澳大利亚的慈善机构还需遵守其他相关法律及合规要求。澳大利亚税务局(Australian Taxation Office, ATO) 负责依据联邦税法评估并决定机构是否符合税务优惠资格，包括所得税豁免及可扣税礼品接收机构资格的认证（详见第六章）。


筹款活动主要受各州和领地法律规范。慈善机构需遵守其开展筹款活动所在地的相关规定，这些规定可能包括注册、许可或报告义务，且各司法辖区之间存在差异（详见第五章）。


此外，《澳大利亚消费者法 (Australian Consumer Law(ACL))同样适用于慈善机构。尽管其并非专门针对筹款活动的监管法律，但其禁止误导性或欺骗性行为及虚假陈述，包括在筹款宣传及对外沟通中的相关行为.

1. Who Regulates Charities in Australia


In Australia, charities are primarily regulated by the Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC), the independent national regulator of charities. The ACNC is responsible for registering charities, maintaining the public Australian Charities Register, and overseeing ongoing reporting obligations, including annual information statements and financial reports. It also provides guidance and takes proportionate compliance action to support transparency and public trust in the sector.


Charities in Australia also need to comply with other laws and compliance obligations, depending on their activities and where they operate. The Australian Taxation Office (ATO) is responsible for determining eligibility for Commonwealth tax concessions, including income tax exemption and deductible gift recipient (DGR) endorsement (see section 6 below).


Fundraising is not regulated by the ACNC. Instead, it is primarily regulated by state and territory laws, and charities must comply with the requirements in each jurisdiction where they raise funds, which may include registration, permits, or reporting obligations (see section 5 below).


In addition, the Australian Consumer Law (ACL) applies to charities in the same way as other organisations. While it does not specifically regulate fundraising, it prohibits misleading or deceptive conduct and false representations, including in fundraising appeals and public communications.




2. 注册慈善机构的基本要求

Swipe to view the English version


在澳大利亚，拟申请注册为慈善机构的组织，必须符合《慈善机构法(Charities Act) 2013 (Cth)》规定及相关法规的要求，并具备向 Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission（ACNC）注册的资格。一般而言，这包括：


  • 仅以具有公共利益的慈善宗旨为目的

  • 具备非营利性质（not-for-profit）

  • 不具有任何被法律认定为不合格的宗旨（例如从事或推动违法活动或严重犯罪行为）

  • 遵守ACNC治理标准（Governance Standards）

  • 如在境外开展活动，遵守ACNC对外活动标准（External Conduct Standards）


《慈善机构法(Charities Act) 2013 (Cth)》还规定了慈善目的的主要类别，包括促进健康、教育、社会福利、人权、文化、环境保护等。因此，从事妇女权益保护、反歧视、反暴力、弱势群体支持及相关公共利益工作的组织，其宗旨在适当设计与治理安排下，可能与澳大利亚慈善法认可的慈善目的相契合。

2. Core Requirements for Charity Registration


In Australia, an organisation seeking registration as a charity must meet the requirements set out in the Charities Act 2013 (Cth) and relevant regulations, and be eligible for ACNC registration. Broadly, this includes:


  • having only charitable purposes for the public benefit;

  • being not-for-profit;

  • not having any disqualifying purposes (such as engaging in or promoting unlawful activities or serious offences);

  • complying with the ACNC Governance Standards; and

  • complying with the ACNC External Conduct Standards, if the charity operates outside Australia. 


Charities Act 2013 (Cth) also sets out the principal categories of charitable purpose in legislation, including the advancement of health, education, social welfare, human rights, culture, and environmental protection. As a result, organisations working in areas such as women’s rights protection, anti-discrimination, anti-violence initiatives, and support for vulnerable communities may, if properly structured and governed, align with charitable purposes recognised under Australian law.



3. 注册后的持续合规义务

Swipe to view the English version


在澳大利亚，慈善机构完成注册后，仍需持续履行合规义务。所有注册慈善机构通常都需要向 ACNC 提交年度信息申报表（Annual Information Statement, AIS）；中型和大型慈善机构通常还需提交年度财务报告。一般而言，相关申报应在机构报告期结束后 6 个月内完成。


ACNC 目前依据年收入将慈善机构划分为三类：

  • 小型（small）：年收入低于 50 万澳元；

  • 中型（medium）：年收入 50 万澳元至低于 300 万澳元；

  • 大型（large）：年收入 300 万澳元及以上。


其中，中型慈善机构的财务报告通常需经审阅或审计，大型慈善机构则通常需经审计。该分级安排体现了“规模越大、透明度和监管要求越高”的原则，同时也有助于减轻小型慈善机构的合规负担。


除 ACNC 的报告义务外，慈善机构在实际运营中通常还需要遵守更广泛的合规要求。这些要求可能包括其他监管规定（如募捐法律）、行业标准、国际框架（适用于跨境活动）、内部政策，以及资助方或捐赠方提出的要求。对这些要求的管理是日常运营的重要组成部分。下一部分将以筹款与公众宣传为例进行简要说明。


3. Ongoing Compliance Obligations After Registration


In Australia, registration is only the beginning of a charity’s compliance journey. All registered charities are generally required to lodge an Annual Information Statement (AIS) with the ACNC, and medium and large charities are generally also required to submit annual financial reports. In most cases, these filings are due within six months after the end of the charity’s reporting period.


The ACNC currently classifies charities by annual revenue as follows:

● Small: under AUD 500,000

● Medium: AUD 500,000 to under AUD 3 million

● Large: AUD 3 million and above


Medium charities are generally required to have their financial reports reviewed or audited, while large charities are generally required to have them audited. This tiered system reflects a proportionate regulatory approach: larger organisations are subject to higher transparency expectations, while smaller charities face lower compliance burdens.


Beyond ACNC reporting, charities often need to meet a wider range of compliance obligations depending on their scope of work and how they operate. These can include other regulatory requirements (such as fundraising laws), industry standards, international frameworks (for cross-border work), internal policies, and requirements set by donors or grant-makers. Managing these obligations is an important part of day-to-day operations. The next section uses fundraising and public communications as a common example.



4. 筹款与公众宣传的合规要求

Swipe to view the English version


慈善机构在开展募捐、项目宣传、公开陈述、销售商品或服务时，除遵守 ACNC 治理要求外，还可能受到 澳大利亚消费者法（ACL） 的约束。根据ACL的规定，慈善机构不得：


  • 从事误导性或欺骗性行为

  • 提供任何虚假或误导性陈述

  • 通过不公平或不合情理方式向公众施压

  • 对资金用途、机构身份或捐赠效果进行失实表达


这意味着，对慈善机构而言，透明、准确和负责任的公众沟通不仅是品牌和声誉要求，也是法律与合规要求的一部分。对于涉及公众捐赠的组织，建立清晰的募捐说明、用途披露和申诉处理机制，十分重要。

4.Compliance in Fundraising and Public Communications


When charities engage in fundraising, public campaigns, representations about their work, or the sale of goods and services, they may be subject not only to ACNC governance requirements but also to the Australian Consumer Law (ACL). ACL makes clear that charities must not:

  • undertake misleading or deceptive conduct

  • provide any false or misleading representation

  • use unfair or unconscionable pressure tactics

  • misrepresent the use of funds, their identity, or the impact of donations

This means that, for charities, transparent, accurate, and responsible communication is not merely a matter of reputation; it is also part of legal and compliance practice. For organisations seeking donations from the public, it is especially important to maintain clear fundraising explanations, proper disclosure of fund use, and effective complaint-handling mechanisms.



5. 税务优惠与可扣税礼品接收机构资格

Swipe to view the English version


符合条件的澳大利亚慈善机构可能享有若干税务优惠，包括所得税豁免、商品及服务税(GST)优惠以及在某些情况下适用的边缘福利税（FBT）优惠。


这些税务优惠由澳大利亚税务局(ATO)负责管理，并需通过ATO的单独认证(endorsement)程序获得。虽然在ACNC注册为慈善机构通常是前提条件之一，但这并不意味着机构会自动获得所有税务优惠资格。即ACNC负责慈善机构的注册，而ATO则依据税法负责评估资格并批准相关税务优惠的认证申请。


此外，并非所有注册慈善机构都具备可扣税礼品接收机构（Deductible Gift Recipient, DGR）资格。DGR资格是一项由ATO单独认证的身份，使捐赠人能够对符合条件的捐款申请所得税扣除，因此对依赖公众捐赠的机构尤为重要。DGR资格需符合特定类别及法律标准进行评估与批准。通常情况下，ACNC注册是DGR认证的前提条件之一，但并不保证机构一定符合或获得该资格。

5. Tax Concessions and Deductible Gift Recipient Status


Eligible Australian charities may be entitled to certain tax concessions, including income tax exemption, GST concessions, and, in some cases, fringe benefits tax (FBT) concessions. These concessions are administered by the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) and require separate endorsement by the ATO. 


While ACNC registration as a charity is generally a prerequisite, it does not automatically result in entitlement to all tax concessions. Separately, charities must apply for endorsement to access these concessions, and eligibility is assessed under ATO requirements. The ACNC is responsible for registering charities, while the ATO determines eligibility and administers tax law, including endorsement decisions.


In addition, not all registered charities are endorsed as Deductible Gift Recipients (DGRs). DGR endorsement is a separate ATO status that allows donors to claim income tax deductions for eligible gifts made to the organisation. It is subject to strict eligibility criteria based on specific DGR categories under tax law. ACNC registration is generally required for DGR endorsement, but it does not guarantee eligibility or approval.



6. 亚裔女性国际发展计划在澳大利亚的发展

对于 AWDPI 而言，理解澳大利亚慈善监管体系，有助于在澳大利亚开展更规范、更稳健和更具公信力的公益工作。围绕妇女权益、反歧视、反性别暴力、支持弱势群体和促进社会包容等领域的项目，在澳大利亚具有明确的公共利益价值；而一个清晰的法律与监管框架，也为组织的本地治理建设、跨境合作、项目管理和公众沟通提供了可参考的制度基础。


在实践层面，AWDPI 在澳大利亚发展期间将继续重点关注以下方面：

  • 明确并公开组织宗旨及非营利属性

  • 建立清晰、负责和可问责的治理结构

  • 完善财务记录、项目记录和内部控制机制

  • 在对外传播和募捐中保持真实性、透明度和审慎性

  • 强化风险控制、资源追踪和受益人保护。

6. AWDPI’s Development in Australia


For AWDPI, understanding Australia’s charity regulatory system is valuable for developing public-interest work in a more compliant, credible, and sustainable way. Programmes relating to women’s rights, anti-discrimination, prevention of gender-based violence, support for vulnerable communities, and social inclusion all carry clear public-benefit significance in Australia. A well-defined legal and regulatory framework also offers an important reference point for local governance, cross-border cooperation, project management, and public communications.


In practical terms, AWDPI will continue prioritising the below while developing and practising in Australia:

  • clearly articulating its charitable purpose and not-for-profit character

  • establishing a governance structure that is clear, responsible, and accountable

  • maintaining sound financial records, project documentation, and internal controls

  • ensuring that fundraising and public messaging remain truthful, transparent, and careful

Strengthening risk management, resource tracking, and beneficiary protection.



7.结语

Swipe to view the English version


总体而言，澳大利亚慈善制度以清晰规则、分级监管、信息公开和公众信任为核心。对于希望在澳大利亚长期开展公益事业的机构而言，合规并非单纯的行政要求，而是建立社会信任、拓展合作机会和实现可持续发展的重要基础。


作为AWDPI 亚太地区的重要分支，AWDPI 澳大利亚办公室于2025年3月在澳大利亚成立，自成立以来已举办多场线上和线下活动，以联结并支持本地社区。通过教育推广、社区外展与合作，AWDPI致力于提升关于权利与支持服务的信息可及性，尤其关注亚裔社区。其工作重点在于提高意识、建立联结，并赋能个人与社区寻求帮助、彼此支持。AWDPI澳大利亚办公室目前正在申请成为澳大利亚慈善与非营利委员会（ACNC）注册慈善机构。

7. Conclusion


Overall, Australia’s charity system is characterised by clear rules, proportionate regulation, transparency, and a strong emphasis on public trust. For organisations seeking to undertake long-term public-interest work in Australia, compliance is not merely an administrative requirement; it is a foundation for credibility, partnership-building, and sustainable development.


As a key national office of AWDPI in the Asia-Pacific region, AWDPI Australia was established in Australia in March 2025.  Since its inception, AWDPI Australia has since hosted a range of online and in-person events to engage and support local communities. Through education, outreach, and collaboration, AWDPI Australia works to improve access to information on rights and support services, with a particular focus on Asian communities in Australia. Its efforts centre on raising awareness, building connections, and empowering individuals and communities to seek help and support one another. AWDPI Australia is currently applying for registration as an ACNC charity.
