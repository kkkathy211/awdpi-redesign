Fully funded — no tuition, living, or travel costs for selected scholars.

Project type

Research / Postdoc

Host institution

European Commission

Overview

The MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships support outstanding researchers worldwide to conduct advanced research projects in Europe or globally. The program encourages interdisciplinary research, international mobility, and career development. Projects may focus on gender equality, feminist economics, migration, public policy, social justice, climate justice, global health, or inclusive governance. Researchers design their own research proposal in partnership with a European host institution.

Location

European Union Member States or Associated Countries (Global Fellowship option: Europe + Third Country phase)

Duration and format

  • 12–24 months (European Fellowship)

  • 24–36 months (Global Fellowship including outgoing phase)

  • Full-time research appointment at host institution.

Eligibility

  • PhD degree obtained before call deadline

  • Maximum 8 years post-PhD research experience

  • Must comply with mobility rule (not residing in host country more than 12 months in last 36 months)

  • Open to all nationalities (Chinese nationals eligible)

Benefits

  • Competitive salary (living allowance)

  • Mobility allowance

  • Family allowance (if applicable)

  • Research, training and networking costs

  • Institutional overhead funding

  • Career development support

  • Gender equality plan compliance required from host institutions

 Application materials

  • Research proposal (Part B scientific narrative)

  • CV (Europass format)

  • Ethics self-assessment

  • Host institution commitment letter

Cost

 No application fee. (Fully funded by EU Horizon Europe.)

Deadline

10 September 2026 (17:00 Brussels time)

Keywords

gender research, postdoctoral fellowship, EU funding, migration studies, feminist policy, global mobility, Horizon Europe

Official link

https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships

Additional notes

  • Host institution must have a Gender Equality Plan (mandatory under Horizon Europe).

  • Strongly competitive program (success rate approx. 15–20%).

  • Pre-proposal discussions with host supervisors highly recommended.
