As the Chinese community makes up an integral part of the Malaysian society, it is important that Malaysian Chinese women receive due attention to their lives and legitimate rights. This report focuses on the present situation of domestic violence against Chinese women in Malaysia, means of self-defense and social relief measures. Policies for local women’s rights and interests protection and laws on domestic violence are summarized to promote legal knowledge on the existing policies and means of self-defense, and provide ways to seek social aids.
