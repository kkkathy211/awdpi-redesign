We are the controller of this personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Belgian Act of 30 July 2018 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data.

## The personal data we collect

What we collect depends on how you deal with us.

- **If you ask us for support:** the name or nickname you give us, your contact details (for example your WeChat ID, WhatsApp number or email address), the municipality where you live, and what you choose to tell us about your situation and needs. This can include:
  - special categories of personal data, such as information about your physical or mental health, your sex life or sexual orientation, your racial or ethnic origin, or your religious beliefs;
  - information about criminal offences, such as violence committed against you;
  - your residence status and information about your children.
- **About other people:** when you tell us about other people, such as a partner, a family member or the person who harmed you, we record only what we need to support you.
- **If you donate:** your name, contact details, the amount, date and purpose of your gift, and the information we need to send you a receipt.
- **If you volunteer with us:** your name, contact details, languages, skills and availability, and records of the work you do with us.
- **If you work with us as a partner or contact us:** your name, your organisation, your contact details and the content of your messages.

We collect this information from you directly, through messages, calls and emails. Sometimes another organisation, such as a service that refers you to us, or another AWDPI organisation, gives us your details, but only with your agreement.

## Staying anonymous

You do not need to give us your real name to get support. You can use a nickname, and you decide what you tell us. You are not required by law or by any contract to give us personal data, but if you leave some out, we may not be able to help in every way, for example by referring you to another service.

For special categories of data: your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)). Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time by telling us. This does not affect what we did before you withdrew it. After that, we stop using the data for that purpose, but we may keep a record of the support we gave where another legal basis applies (see [How long we keep it](#how-long-we-keep-it)).

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling.

## Information about other people

When you tell us about someone else, such as the person who harmed you, we do not tell that person that we hold information about them, because doing so could put you at risk and would seriously impair our work (GDPR Article 14(5)(b)).

## Who we share it with

What you tell us about your situation is confidential. We share it outside AWDPI Belgium only with your agreement, except where Belgian law requires or allows it, for example to help a person who is in serious danger. If we have to share information without your agreement, we share only what is necessary and, where it is safe to do so, we tell you.

- **Our team.** The board members, staff and volunteers handling your request see only what they need, and they must keep it confidential.
- **Support organisations**, such as shelters, lawyers, psychologists or interpreters, with your agreement.
- **Other AWDPI organisations**, only when this is needed to help you, for example if you move to another country, and with your agreement.
- **Service providers** that process data on our instructions, such as our email and document storage provider. They are bound by contract to use it only to provide their service to us.
- **Professional advisers**, such as our accountant and lawyers, who must keep it confidential.
- **Police, the public prosecutor and the courts**, only where Belgian law requires or allows it.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## Messaging apps

Many people contact us through WeChat or WhatsApp. These apps are run by other companies under their own privacy policies:

- **WeChat** is run by Tencent. Messages to our WeChat accounts are processed by Tencent, including in mainland China, where the authorities may have access to them.
- **WhatsApp** is run by Meta. Messages are end-to-end encrypted, but Meta processes information about your use of WhatsApp, including in the United States.

If you prefer, you can contact us by email or phone instead. If someone else may look at your phone, consider deleting your conversations with us after reading them.

## Our website

awdpi.org is run for the whole AWDPI network by AWDPI United Kingdom. How the website handles information about visitors is explained on our [Privacy](/privacy/) page.

## Where we store it and transfers outside the EEA

We keep our records, including our email, with service providers that store them in the European Union.

We transfer personal data outside the European Economic Area (EEA) in these cases:

- **To other AWDPI organisations**, only when this is needed to help you and with your agreement. The European Commission has recognised the United Kingdom as providing an adequate level of protection. For AWDPI United States and AWDPI Australia, we use the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses; where they are not in place, we transfer your data only with your explicit consent, after telling you about the risks (GDPR Article 49(1)(a)).
- **To service providers outside the EEA**, under an adequacy decision of the European Commission, such as the decision on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **Through WeChat.** The European Commission has not recognised China as providing an adequate level of protection. When you contact us on WeChat, we will tell you about the risks described under [Messaging apps](#messaging-apps) and offer you other ways to reach us, and we discuss your situation there only with your explicit consent (Article 49(1)(a)).

You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards we use.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it, and then delete it or anonymise it:

- **Support records:** while we are supporting you, and then for up to 3 years after your case is closed, so that we can deal with any complaint or legal claim and protect people at risk. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
- **Donation and accounting records:** as long as Belgian accounting and tax law requires.
- **Enquiries and partner correspondence:** up to 2 years after our last contact.
- **Volunteer records:** while you volunteer with us and up to 2 years afterwards.

If you ask us to stop contacting you, we keep your contact details on a "do not contact" list, so that we respect your request.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
- have it **rectified** if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
- have it **erased**;
- **restrict** how we use it;
- **object** to how we use it where we rely on our legitimate interests, and object at any time to our use of it for fundraising or other direct marketing;
- **receive it in a portable format**, or have it sent to another organisation, where we use it on the basis of your consent and by automated means;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time, where we rely on consent.

Some of these rights do not apply in every case. For example, we may withhold information that would reveal personal data about someone else or put someone at risk.

To use any of these rights, write to [{{< org be email >}}](mailto:{{< org be email >}}). This is free. We reply within one month; if your request is complex, we may extend this by up to two further months and will tell you why. If we need to confirm who you are, we will do it in a way that keeps you safe.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, you can tell us at [{{< org be email >}}](mailto:{{< org be email >}}), and we will look into it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit), Rue de la Presse 35 / Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels; contact@apd-gba.be; +32 2 274 48 00. You can lodge a complaint [online](https://www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be/citoyen/agir/introduire-une-plainte) or with a paper form. If your complaint is about one of your rights, the Authority asks you to make your request to us first and wait one month for our reply. You can also go to court.

## Children

Our services are for adults. If you are under 18 and need help, please tell us and we will help you find services for young people. You can also call 103 (Écoute-Enfants, in French) or 102 (Awel, in Dutch).

## How we protect it

We limit access to personal data to the people who need it, keep case information confidential, and use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. If a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will tell you without undue delay, and we report breaches to the Data Protection Authority where the law requires.
