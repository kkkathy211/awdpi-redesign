This privacy notice explains how **{{< org au legalName >}}** ("AWDPI Australia", "we" or "us") collects, holds, uses, discloses and protects personal information about people who ask us for support, donate, volunteer, work with us or contact us. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth).

AWDPI Australia is an independent organisation within the AWDPI international network. AWDPI United Kingdom, AWDPI Belgium and AWDPI United States are separate organisations. Each is responsible for the personal information it handles and has its own privacy notice.

## The information we collect

What we collect depends on how you deal with us.

- **If you ask us for support:** the name or nickname you give us, your contact details (for example your WeChat ID, WhatsApp number or email address), the state and suburb or city where you live, and what you choose to tell us about your situation and needs. This can include sensitive information and health information, such as your physical or mental health, your racial or ethnic origin, your sexual orientation or practices, or violence you have experienced, as well as your visa status and information about your children.
- **About other people:** when you tell us about other people, such as a partner, a family member or the person who harmed you, we record only what we need to support you.
- **If you donate:** your name, contact details, the amount, date and purpose of your gift, and the information we need to send you a receipt.
- **If you volunteer with us:** your name, contact details, languages, skills and availability, and records of the work you do with us.
- **If you work with us as a partner or contact us:** your name, your organisation, your contact details and the content of your messages.

We collect most information directly from you, through messages, calls and emails. Sometimes another organisation, such as a service that refers you to us, or another AWDPI organisation, gives us your details, but only with your agreement.

We collect sensitive information only with your consent, or where the law allows it without consent, for example where it is necessary to lessen or prevent a serious threat to someone's life, health or safety.

## Staying anonymous

You do not need to give us your real name to get support. You can stay anonymous or use a nickname, and you decide what you tell us. You do not have to give us any information, but if you leave some out, we may not be able to help in every way, for example by referring you to another service.

## How we use it

We use personal information for the purposes for which we collected it:

- to give you the support you ask for, such as language support, legal information, emotional support, referrals to local support services, and follow-up;
- to lessen or prevent a serious threat to someone's life, health or safety;
- to share your story, for example in an interview published in our Stories section, only if you agree, and only in the form you agree to;
- to process donations, send receipts and keep the records that Australian law requires;
- to send you news or fundraising messages, if you have agreed to receive them;
- to organise volunteering, answer enquiries and work with partner organisations;
- to run our organisation, for example to keep accounts, deal with complaints and meet our legal obligations;
- to produce research and statistics on gender-based violence, using only information that has been de-identified.

We use personal information for another purpose only with your consent or where the APPs allow it.

You can ask us at any time to stop sending you news or fundraising messages, and every email we send has an unsubscribe link.

We do not use computer programs to make decisions that could significantly affect your rights or interests.

## Who we disclose it to

We do not sell personal information, and we do not disclose it for advertising.

What you tell us about your situation is confidential. We disclose it outside AWDPI Australia only with your consent, except where the law requires or allows us to, for example to lessen or prevent a serious threat to someone's life, health or safety, or where a court orders it.

- **Our team.** The staff and volunteers handling your request see only what they need, and they must keep it confidential.
- **Support organisations**, such as refuges, lawyers, counsellors or interpreters, only with your consent.
- **Other AWDPI organisations**, only when this is needed to help you, for example if you move to another country, and with your consent.
- **Service providers** that handle information for us, such as our email and document storage provider and payment processors. They may use it only to provide their service to us.
- **Professional advisers**, such as our accountants, auditors and lawyers, who must keep it confidential.
- **Authorities**, such as the police or courts, only where the law requires or allows it.

When you donate online, your card details are handled by the payment processor. We do not receive or store your full card number.

## Where we store it

We keep our records, including our email, with service providers that store them in Australia, with access limited to the people who need it.

## Disclosure outside Australia

We may disclose personal information to recipients outside Australia in these cases:

- **Other AWDPI organisations** in the United Kingdom, Belgium and the United States, only when this is needed to help you and with your consent.
- **Service providers** that are based in, or process information in, other countries, including the United States.

Before we disclose personal information to someone outside Australia, we take reasonable steps to make sure they handle it in line with the APPs, for example through a written agreement.

**WeChat and WhatsApp.** If you choose to contact us through WeChat or WhatsApp, your messages are handled by the companies that run these apps, under their own privacy policies:

- **WeChat** is run by Tencent. Messages to our WeChat accounts are processed by Tencent, including in mainland China, where the authorities may have access to them.
- **WhatsApp** is run by Meta. Messages are end-to-end encrypted, but Meta processes information about your use of WhatsApp, including in the United States.

By choosing one of these apps, you consent to your messages being disclosed to these companies outside Australia. If you give this consent, we cannot make sure that they handle your information in line with the APPs, and we will not be accountable under the Privacy Act for how they handle it. If you prefer, you can contact us by email instead. If someone else may look at your phone, consider deleting your conversations with us after reading them.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it, and then destroy it or de-identify it:

- **Support records:** while we are supporting you, and then for up to 3 years after your case is closed. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
- **Donation and financial records:** for 7 years, as Australian company and tax law requires.
- **Enquiries and partner correspondence:** up to 2 years after our last contact.
- **Volunteer records:** while you volunteer with us and up to 2 years afterwards.

## Access and correction

You can ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading. You can also ask us to delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Write to [{{< org au email >}}](mailto:{{< org au email >}}). We reply within 30 days, and we do not charge for this. If we need to confirm who you are, we will do it in a way that keeps you safe. If we refuse access or a correction, we will tell you why in writing and how you can complain. We may refuse access where the APPs allow it, for example where giving access would put someone's life, health or safety at risk or would unreasonably affect someone else's privacy.

## Our website

awdpi.org is run for the whole AWDPI network by AWDPI United Kingdom. How the website handles information about visitors is explained on our [Privacy](/privacy/) page.

## Children

Our services are for adults. If you are under 18 and need help, please tell us and we will help you find services for young people. You can also call Kids Helpline on 1800 55 1800 at any time.

## How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference and loss, and from unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. We limit access to the people who need it and keep case information confidential. If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm to you, we will tell you and, where the law requires, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

## Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your personal information, write to [{{< org au email >}}](mailto:{{< org au email >}}). We will acknowledge your complaint, look into it and give you a written reply within 30 days.

If we do not reply within 30 days, or you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the OAIC:

- online, using the [privacy complaint form](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints/lodge-a-privacy-complaint-with-us);
- by email to oaicintake@oaic.gov.au;
- by post to GPO Box 5288, Sydney NSW 2001.

For enquiries, call the OAIC on 1300 363 992. If you need an interpreter, call the Translating and Interpreting Service on 131 450 and ask for the OAIC.
