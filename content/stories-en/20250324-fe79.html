1972 – 2025:

From Womanhouse to She, Her, Ours


In 1972, the "Womanhouse" exhibition initiated by Judy Chicago and Miriam Schapiro became a landmark moment in women's art history. This groundbreaking experiment allowed women artists, for the first time, to use the "home" as a medium to explore daily life, social roles, and gender issues, expanding the boundaries of women's artistic expression.


53 years later, AAAAH CULTURE pays tribute to this spirit with She, Her, Ours. We aim to create a true artistic space belonging to women creators, where their works, stories, and voices can blossom this March.



Why "She, Her, Ours"?



In over ten past events, more than 90% of AAAAH CULTURE's brand curators, sponsors, partners, audiences, and even team members have been women. We have witnessed firsthand the outstanding contributions of women creators across industries, and we deeply understand how vital a supportive platform for them truly is.


She, Her, Ours is more than just a market—it's a stage. It brings together 30+ women artists and brand curators from diverse backgrounds, spanning fields such as painting, fashion, jewelry, fragrance, home goods, and publishing. Their independent and powerful works converge here, reflecting women's past, present, and future.

AWDPI

Asian Women Development Plan International





AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against overseas Asian women, and promoting their empowerment and development. It evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program) established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams registered as charities in the UK, Belgium, and the United States. AWDPI has built extensive partnerships with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. AWDPI advocates for attention to the living challenges faced by overseas Asian women and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more development opportunities for overseas Asian women in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them transform from marginalized groups into a vital force driving social progress.
