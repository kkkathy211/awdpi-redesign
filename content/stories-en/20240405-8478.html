Introduction




In order to gain an in-depth understanding of the growth trajectories, management models, and development experiences of U.S. nonprofit organizations, and to provide feasible reference recommendations for the strategic planning and long-term expansion of AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) in the United States, we hereby launch the "U.S. Nonprofit Organization Research Initiative," funding relevant research projects and ultimately producing research reports to promote AWDPI's professional development, improve the living and development environment for Asian women overseas, and jointly build a women's community rooted in love and equality.


01

Research Focus


  • Applicants may select 1–2 U.S. nonprofit organizations that provide services and empowerment support for women as research subjects, with particular attention to U.S. nonprofits focusing on vulnerable groups such as ethnic minority women, Asian American women, and marginalized women;

  • the aforementioned nonprofit organizations should have been established for no less than 5 years, and should have high visibility and influence in the United States;

  • emphasis should be placed on the growth trajectory, management model, development experience, and partnership resources of the selected organizations;

  • based on AWDPI's current situation, applicants should provide targeted, decision-making-oriented development recommendations.

  • The research process should prioritize primary data collection rather than desk research alone, including but not limited to field investigations and key informant interviews.














02

Research Outputs



  • The expected output is a "Research Report on U.S. Nonprofit Organizations" (specific title to be determined by the researcher);

  • AWDPI will compile and organize these into a "Compendium of Research on U.S. Nonprofit Organizations,";

  • which will be publicly released by AWDPI.














03

Eligibility



  • This funding program is currently open only to AWDPI internal members, including volunteers and advisors;

  • applicants should have in-depth knowledge of the U.S. nonprofit sector, gender issues, and nonprofit organizations serving U.S. ethnic minority women, Asian American women, and marginalized women; applicants should have in-depth knowledge of the U.S. nonprofit sector, gender issues, and nonprofit organizations serving U.S. ethnic minority women, Asian American women, and marginalized women;

  • must possess the necessary academic research capabilities;;

  • applicants with prior research experience will be given priority;

  • individuals or teams are both eligible.














04

Funding Quantity and Amount


  • Depending on the quality of the proposal and final report, funding for each project will range from 3,000 to 20,000 RMB;;

  • a total of 2–5 research projects will be funded.
