Happy New Year




















At the beginning of the year, on this auspicious day, all is good.
The spring sun brings blessings, and prosperity and good fortune arrive together.


On this joyful New Year's occasion, we send afar words of kindness and noble spirit:

May we always uphold the sincere devotion of the red plum blossom, and maintain a steadfast and flourishing resolve.


On this wonderful festival, AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International and the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project wish you

a Happy New Year, with happiness and well-being that endure.


The future still awaits,

May you and I, without needing adornment, still shine with natural beauty,

And even if the road ahead has bumps, may we tread them flat into a smooth path!
