11/25 - 12/10 16 Days of Activism

Call for Online Participants: Mobile Letterbox Campaign

Ending Gender-Based Violence



The annual United Nations "16 Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence" is approaching. This year's campaign, themed "Activism! UNiTE to end violence against women and girls," calls on more people to join efforts to eliminate gender-based violence and support women and girls worldwide in claiming their rights.


Every year, November 25 marks the UN's International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women. The 16 Days of Activism begins on this day and continues until December 10, Human Rights Day. The campaign aims to mobilize society, the public, governments, and UN agencies to amplify impact and encourage action to end gender-based violence.


Avoice will join forces with partners to launch a mobile letterbox advocacy campaign on 11/25, encouraging people to send handwritten postcards to women affected by gender-based violence as a gesture of encouragement, warmth, and support.

#HearHerHealHer# Campaign Introduction


From 11/25 to 12/10, beginning with the International Day for the Elimination of Gender-Based Violence on 11/25, Avoice, together with partners, will launch the #HearHerHealHer# mobile letterbox campaign@JIEMEN @FeminismConnect, launched the #Hear Her, Heal Her# mobile mailbox activity, using the format of "collecting cards, delivering warmth" to send warmth to women who have experienced gender-based violence and domestic violence.


You can handwrite a caring postcard to a female friend who has experienced violence, or share a story from your own life.
