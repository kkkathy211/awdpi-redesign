Column Introduction:


The Avoice Bi-monthly Report is a work report that documents the main activities and key progress of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project every two months. Within Avoice's daily management, it serves as one of the key mediums to promote internal information exchange and cross-departmental communication and collaboration.

Starting from 2023, we will officially and continuously release the Avoice Bi-monthly Report to the public. We hope that through this report, we can share with all partners who care about Avoice's development our major work and phased achievements, present a transparent and open Avoice, and help everyone better understand how Avoice turns individual small efforts—through the dedication of various departments, volunteers, expert advisors, and partners—into significant, long-term progress.

Bi-monthly Big Data


Monthly Summary of Rescue Work


  • From February to March (incomplete statistics), Avoice has received 10 new women seeking help and is currently providing support to 6 clients;

  • In addition, 6 female clients closed their cases, with a total of 3 case feedbacks received; among them, 2/3 of the clients expressed high satisfaction, and 2/3 of the clients considered staff responses to be very timely.


Volunteers

During the February–March bi-monthly period (incomplete statistics), 126 volunteers delivered a total of 216 services, accumulating 96,015 hours. Additionally, 49 volunteers were recognized as Outstanding Volunteers of the Bi-month, and 6 volunteers were recognized as Outstanding Team Leaders of the Bi-month. The full list is included in the appendix.



Bi-monthly Highlights


  1. Established a team leader training system and drafted a training manual;

  2. Completed and implemented the volunteer training system;

  3. Attended the 2023 UAE Global Women's Summit and established connections with multiple international organizations and experts;

  4. Prepared to launch Avoice's 2023 Spring Recruitment;


★Avoice Bi-monthly Report – Issue 2, 2023★

Summary of Bi-monthly Work


Department of Project Quality


Academic Research Division


From February to March 2023,

  • The Academic Research Division completed the report "Overview of the Impact of Domestic Violence on Asian Women in the United States Post-Pandemic and Support Services," which has achieved a cumulative readership of 32.1k on our official WeChat account since publication. The first draft of the "Report on Workplace Survival of Asian Women in Belgium" has also been completed and will be further refined in the next bi-monthly period;

  • In addition, the Academic Research Division produced two issues of the Avoice Watch Monthly Bulletin—selected global highlights on gender equality progress and overseas Asian development. Topics covered include news commentary, overseas crime news, gender equality, and Asian voices. For full details, please read: February 2023 issue, March 2023 issue.


Monitoring & Evaluation Division


From February to March 2023,

  • The Monitoring & Evaluation Division produced the Avoice Bi-monthly Report for December 2022–January 2023 and published it on platforms including WeChat Official Account, advancing Avoice's transparency and compliance;

  • Additionally, the Monitoring & Evaluation Division finalized the volunteer training system and preliminarily completed the department head training manual, promoting the professional capacity building of the organization.


Project Design Division


From February to March 2023,

  • The Project Design Division completed the planning and implementation of the 2023 International Women's Day activities. The lecture "Women's Protection and Empowerment in Humanitarian Work" attracted 70 participants and received positive feedback;

  • Established the 2023 AWDP Forum Planning Team and steadily advanced related work;


Department of Rescue Support


Case Coordination Unit


From February to March 2023,

  • The case coordination unit has received 10 new women seeking help and is currently supporting 6 clients;

  • Additionally, the Case Coordination Unit continued to optimize the case recording system, reinforced learning of the 2023 New Rescue Model training content, and improved volunteer service capabilities within the department;


Legal Support Unit


From February to March 2023,

  • The legal support unit has provided legal aid services to 5 newly added women seeking help, with legal support completed in 1 case, and is currently providing legal aid to 4 women seeking help;

  • Additionally, the Legal Support Unit completed and implemented the volunteer training manual, advancing the professionalization of support work;


Psychological Support Unit


From February to March 2023,

  • The psychological support unit is currently providing psychological support services to 1 woman seeking help and has launched psychological support services for Avoice internal staff;

  • Additionally, the Psychological Support Unit advanced the construction of a professional volunteer network, further enhancing the professionalization of psychological support services;


Language Support Unit


From February to March 2023,

  • The language support unit is currently providing language support services to 1 woman seeking help;

  • Additionally, the Language Support Unit has established a translation team and published one French-translated article, "Conversation with Annie Ernaux: 'I am always indignant.'" and one German video, "Domestic Violence Is Not Just About Beating! What Can We Do?";


Empowerment Support Unit


From February to March 2023,

  • The empowerment support unit is currently providing empowerment support services to 1 woman seeking help;

  • Additionally, the Empowerment Support Unit completed a work reorganization, optimized internal structure and personnel management, and advanced the construction of an empowerment support resource database;


Department of Partnership Development


From February to March 2023,

  • The Partnership Development Department made contact with 6 potential partners including Zhongzhi Technology, and co-created a science comic on women's breast health with Zhongzhi Technology's Health Division;

  • Was invited to attend the 2023 UAE Global Women's Summit and established connections with multiple international organizations and experts, laying the foundation for future collaboration between Avoice and government agencies, rescue, and charitable organizations in more countries and regions;

  • Additionally, the Partnership Development Department iterated its partnership network and designed a partnership evaluation system, laying the groundwork for future work;


Communications Department


From February to March 2023,

  • The total number of followers across official accounts (WeChat, Xiaohongshu, LinkedIn, etc.) reached 8,944, with 877 new followers on WeChat, 35 on Xiaohongshu, and 28 on LinkedIn. A total of 43 posts were published across platforms, accumulating over 105,000 views. The most viewed post was "Avoice Research | Overview of the Impact of Domestic Violence on Asian Women in the United States Post-Pandemic and Support Services," which reached 32,000 readers;

  • The Communications Department produced 9 original articles, including 7 feature interviews and 2 column articles. Two articles—"Avoice Healing & Her Voice | Interview with Liu Yuanfen, CEO of Lixin Foundation: The Current State of Protection for Chinese Women's Rights in the U.S." and "Avoice Healing & Her Voice | Conversation with the Founder of the National Women's History Alliance: Being an Information Hub for Women's History and for Women's History Organizations"—each surpassed 20,000 reads;


Fundraising Department


From February to March 2023,

  • The Fundraising Department completed its work guidance manual and collaborated with PCA to complete a fundraising market research in Belgium;

  • Additionally, the Fundraising Department participated in UK fundraising market research, mapped out fundraising opportunities, and developed an action plan;


Department of General Affairs


Human Resources & Administration Division


From February to March 2023,

  • The Human Resources & Administration Division organized one management meeting, covering 26 department heads;

  • Additionally, the Human Resources & Administration Division conducted one training session for newly appointed leaders, involving 17 members; organized recruitment for new members in the Psychological Support Unit, successfully recruiting 6 new members—welcome to the Avoice family!

  • The HR recruitment team conducted one training session on "Application of the Recruitment System" and coordinated with departments to upload and publish 2023 Spring Recruitment positions, involving 11 business units and 77 positions. We welcome your application to join Avoice.


IT Support Division


From February to March 2023,

  • The IT Support Division released 7 website updates, published 4 articles/academic research reports, and completed 10% of the new website development;

  • Additionally, supported one team in building a Feishu automation tool, which is now used by 20 members;
