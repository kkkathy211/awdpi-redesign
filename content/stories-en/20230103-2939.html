2022 Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Global Development Forum (hereinafter referred to as "the Forum") was initiated by the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project and has received extensive attention and participation from the International Rescue Network, overseas Chinese communities, public welfare organizations across sectors, and experts in relevant academic fields. It provides a platform for groups worldwide affected by gender-based violence to exchange views, methods, and experiences, calling on the international community to pay greater attention to gender issues and advance the protection and empowerment of overseas Chinese women. The conference will select and showcase interdisciplinary research outcomes on topics such as the protection of Chinese women, movements against gender-based violence, and women's leadership, with discussions guided by core values including "participation, inclusion, and empowerment."


01

Forum Introduction


This year's forum will be held on January 7, 2023 via an online conference platform Zoom and will focus on gender-related data across technology, public welfare, workplace, education, law, psychology, policy, and health to summarize and share Avoice's development achievements and future plans.

Building upon academic discussions, this forum aims to integrate theory with public welfare practice, offering innovative perspectives on combating gender-based violence through intergenerational, interdisciplinary, and cross-regional dialogue and collaboration. Through a hybrid model of online guidance and offline support, it will mobilize global volunteers to provide assistance and support to Avoice's target population. During the event, the forum will actively build a knowledge-sharing network for organizational members and scholar-guests from relevant fields, creating practical opportunities to engage in gender-related issues and further expanding the living space and voice of overseas Chinese women.

Participants: All Avoice members, registered audience members, invited guests, and invited media

Organizers: Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Dongfang Miao Xi Public Service Promotion Association of Hainan Province

Executive Team: 2022 Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Global Development Forum Organizing Committee

# Organizer Introduction

The Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program) was launched in early 2020 at the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic. It is dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and promoting gender equality by providing comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing domestic violence. By establishing a cooperative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, Avoice advocates public awareness and support for the challenges faced by overseas Chinese women, striving to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development. The project supports survivors of domestic violence in transforming into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.

Guest Introductions


The forum will feature "Expert Lectures", "Thematic Dialogues", "Paper Reviews" and invite outstanding women from various fields to speak and engage in discussions. These women, from diverse regions and age groups, have deep expertise in business, academia, scientific research, and civil society organizations. They include both accomplished industry leaders who have secured influential positions over years of work, as well as energetic new voices emerging on the scene, collectively demonstrating the powerful force of women. They have consistently focused on women's issues and long worked to expand the living space for overseas Chinese women, participating in or leading various women's rights initiatives and contributing to the sustainable development of women's movements. At this forum, the guests will offer professional and in-depth discussions from diverse perspectives, integrating academic insights with organizational members and public welfare practitioners to explore emerging trends in combining gender-related research with public welfare project design, and offering recommendations for building a broad and effective network against gender-based violence.
