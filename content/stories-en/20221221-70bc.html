2022 Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Global Development Forum (hereinafter referred to as "the Forum") was initiated by the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project and has received extensive attention and participation from the International Rescue Network, overseas Chinese communities, public welfare organizations across sectors, and experts in relevant academic fields. It provides a platform for groups affected by gender-based violence worldwide to exchange views, methods, and experiences, advocate for greater international attention to gender issues, and advance the protection and empowerment of overseas Chinese women. The Forum will select and showcase interdisciplinary research outcomes on topics such as the protection of Chinese women, movements against gender-based violence, and women's leadership, with discussions guided by core values including participation, inclusion, and empowerment.


01

Forum Introduction


This year's Forum will be held on January 7, 2023 on an online conference platform Zoom and broadcast via partner platforms, focusing on gender-related data in areas such as technology, philanthropy, workplace, education, law, psychology, policy, and health to summarize and share Avoice's development achievements and future plans.

Building upon academic discourse, this Forum aims to integrate with practical philanthropy, offering innovative perspectives on combating gender-based violence through intergenerational, interdisciplinary, and cross-regional dialogue and collaboration. Through a hybrid model of online guidance and offline support, it will mobilize global volunteers to provide assistance and support to Avoice's target groups. During the event, the Forum will actively build a knowledge-sharing network for organizational members and scholar-guests from relevant fields, creating practical opportunities to engage in gender-related issues and further expanding the living space and voice of overseas Chinese women.

Participants: All Avoice members, registered audience, invited guests, and invited media

Organizer: Dongfang Miaoqi Public Service Promotion Association of Hainan Province, A voice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project

Co-organizer: Dongfang Miaoqi Foundation

Executive Team: 2022 Avoice Women Global Development Forum Organizing Committee

# Organizer Introduction

The Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project was launched in early 2020 at the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic. It is dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and promoting gender equality by providing comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing domestic violence. By building a cooperative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, social organizations, and industry experts, the project advocates public awareness and support for the challenges faced by overseas Chinese women, striving to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development. It supports these women in transforming from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.

03

Program Design


The Forum will feature three main segments: "Expert Lectures", "Thematic Dialogues", "Paper Reviews" and invite outstanding women from various fields to speak and engage in discussions. These women, from diverse regions and age groups, are deeply engaged in different sectors—business, academia, scientific research, and social organizations. They include both accomplished industry leaders who have gained significant influence over the years, as well as energetic emerging voices. Together, they demonstrate the powerful potential of women. They have consistently focused on women's issues and long worked to expand the living space for overseas Chinese women, participating in or leading various women's rights initiatives and contributing to the sustainable development of women's activities. During this Forum, the speakers will approach the topics from diverse perspectives, engaging in professional and in-depth discussions with organizational members and social welfare practitioners on the integration of academic research and public welfare project design, offering insights and recommendations for building a broad and effective network against gender-based violence.
