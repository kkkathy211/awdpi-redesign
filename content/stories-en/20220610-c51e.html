Guest speaker: Liu Ying 


Research Advisor, Development Finance Program, Institute of New Structural Economics, Peking University

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project UK Partnership Development Member

Former Office Director, CITIC Construction Angola KK Phase II Urban Infrastructure Project Department

Master of Public Policy, University of Oxford

Bachelor of Portuguese Language and Literature, Peking University



Liu Ying, graduated from Peking University and the University of Oxford, and currently works at the Institute of New Structural Economics, Peking University, conducting research on development finance projects. 2016 From 2020 to at CITIC Construction Angola's K.K. KK Phase II Urban Infrastructure Project Department, she participated on-site in the management, quality inspection, and temporary handover of sub-projects for large-scale urban infrastructure projects including roads, water supply, and drainage systems covering 249 hectares of planned land, closely collaborating and communicating with local government departments, and coordinating K.K. the overall planning of the new city, as well as the updating and formulation of regulatory planning. 2021 In she served as a research advisor for the Municipal Government of Londrina, Brazil's 2040 Urban Strategic Planning Project. After receiving early admission to Peking University's Portuguese program, she has continuously struggled to reconcile and resist being labeled as merely a Portuguese language learner or user. She believes Portuguese is one of the most beautiful languages in the world, and has kept exploring and practicing its application—such as translating children's picture books and a Mozambican novelist's full-length work, and working as an engineering translator in Portuguese-speaking countries—constantly deepening her understanding of the language. Although a pure language major offers very limited career options, it has opened doors for her to other cultures, traditions, and ways of life, and also provided an opportunity to accumulate her first financial capital; she has also consistently focused on women's protection issues, 2022 joining the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project in Avoice, Avoice focusing on partnership development in the UK, and committed to mobilizing more resources to support victims of domestic violence.

About Elephant Good Tracks (summary):
Vision: Empower youth through knowledge, empower organizations through information. Mission: Help youth find their goals, help organizations make sound decisions.
Since 2021, Elephant Good Tracks has taken 500 top global enterprises and career elites from world-renowned universities as benchmarks, primarily inviting passionate, dream-driven, and story-rich young guests from various industries to promote career education, popularize industry knowledge and development trends, enabling youth across China, especially in western regions, nationwide, to better understand future careers and necessary preparations, enhancing their career awareness, broadening horizons, and addressing information asymmetry and barriers to improve their future employability and survival skills, encouraging them to become authentic and whole individuals centered on their own interests and passions. Helping them discover their interests earlier and more effectively; meanwhile, committed to connecting domestic and overseas professionals to provide information consulting services for decision-making in startups, NGOs, SMEs, and investment institutions.
