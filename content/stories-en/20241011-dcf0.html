The "2024 AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum," originally scheduled for August 2024, has received overwhelming responses and support. Deeply encouraged by this outpouring, we feel an even greater responsibility to enhance the quality and impact of this year's forum.


To further enrich the forum content, invite more distinguished speakers, and optimize the technical platform to provide a smoother and more interactive experience for participants, after extensive consultation and careful consideration by the organizing committee, we have decided to postpone the opening of the forum to November 23–24, 2024. Therefore, the call for paper submission deadlines will also be extended accordingly. Below is the updated call for papers for this year's forum:


I. AWDPI Annual Forum


The 2024 AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum is about to launch. Centered on the themes "Orange the World—Building a Future Without Violence" and "A New Era of Shaping Female Identity," the forum will host in-depth dialogues with government agencies, academic institutions, nonprofit organizations, scholars, opinion leaders, and concerned individuals worldwide who are committed to the development of Asian women, offering an intellectual feast that unites wisdom, exploration, and passion.


[Paper Review]As a core and continuing component of the AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum, the paper review segment will be expanded and innovated upon its achievements over the past three years, and we are once again publicly calling for submissions of papers and research to be showcased during the forum.


For more detailed information about the 2024 AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum, please follow our official WeChat account, where we will gradually introduce the exciting forum agenda and our highly anticipated keynote speakers!


II. Call for Papers and Research Submissions


1. Purpose of the Paper Review

• To demonstrate AWDPI's academic standards and capabilities;

• To build a bridge for AWDPI members and scholars engaged in gender studies to participate in academic exchange;

• To promote interdisciplinary dialogue and co-create an academic network with experts and scholars from diverse fields.


2. Paper Review Process

• Thematic Paper Review and Presentation: The AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum will dedicate 2–2.5 hours specifically to the review and presentation of papers on related themes.

• Selected Paper Presentations and Expert Comments: From the submissions, we will select 4–6 high-quality papers. Each selected author will present their work in an 8-minute presentation, followed by approximately 10 minutes of expert commentary and suggestions.

• Online Poster Exhibition and Exchange : If the number of submissions is sufficient, we will also introduce an online poster exhibition. High-quality submissions will be turned into digital posters (posters) for attendees to freely "navigate" and view across different virtual meeting spaces. Presenters will have the opportunity to personally explain their academic posters and engage in in-depth discussions with scholars from around the world, jointly exploring new academic insights.


3. Eligible Submitters

 This call for papers is open to:

• Current AWDPI members

• AWDPI Talent Pool members and former members

• Individuals referred by current AWDPI members

• Members of the public and students with strong interest in the forum themes and who are engaged in related research


4. Paper Submission Themes

Any topic related to fields AWDPI is involved in or planning to enter, including but not limited to:


• Movements against gender-based violence

• Protection of Asian women overseas

• Initiatives for violence-free virtual spaces

• Women's leadership

• Women and health

• Women and intimate relationships

• Women and media representation

• Women's political and workplace empowerment

• Women and technological innovation

• Women and environmental protection, cultural heritage preservation

• Women and artistic expression

• Women and education

• Women and social change

• Women and family roles

• Women and migration

• Women and digital security

• Women and religious beliefs

• Women and literature

• Women and international relations

• Women and sports

• Women and aging

• Overseas Chinese women workers


All academic disciplines are welcome. We especially encourage interdisciplinary research submissions and participation, such as:

• Technology + Philanthropy (e.g., the role and current state of women's online forums in supporting public sphere development)

• Policy + Women's Health (e.g., women's health from medical and social security perspectives)

• Workplace + Education (e.g., cultivating workplace competence among overseas Chinese women)

• Law + Psychology (e.g., psychological trauma in children exposed to domestic violence and its correlation with adult criminal behavior)


Paper topics and content must align with AWDPI's vision of "Creating a World of Love and Equality for Women" and its values of "Participation, Inclusion, and Empowerment."


5. Paper Review Panelists

Experts and scholars from fields such as gender equality, international communication, women's empowerment, and philanthropy will be invited to review the papers.


6. Paper and Research Selection Criteria

• Submissions may be in Chinese or English; for other languages, please contact the Academic Department separately:[email protected]

• Clear purpose, focused theme, solid evidence, logical structure, and concise language

• Must follow academic formatting standards, including title, author name, abstract, keywords, main text, and references

• Recommended length: no more than 15,000 Chinese characters or 8,000 English words

• Papers must be original; plagiarism is strictly prohibited, and no infringement of others' legal rights is allowed

• Previously published papers will not be accepted

• This forum especially encourages authors with strong interest or ideas about the themes—even those who have not previously participated in formal academic forums—to submit. Experimental studies, literature reviews, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and other formats are all welcome to promote academic exchange and vitality. We also welcome students without complete research results to submit and share high-quality research proposals, ongoing research, term papers, or short essays.
