PART 1

Avoice Annual Forum


Avoice (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program) launched the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project in the early stages of the global COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, aiming to establish a broad and effective domestic violence response network for overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence. After more than two years of development, the project has expanded to cover all European countries and parts of North America.


To summarize the efforts of the past two years and better plan for future development, Avoice plans to host its annual forum, which will be divided into three parts: Guest Sharing], [Roundtable Forum, and Paper Review This announcement is for the Paper Review Call for Paper and Research Submissions.


For more detailed information about the Avoice Annual Forum, please follow our official WeChat account, where we will gradually introduce our exciting forum agenda and highly anticipated keynote speakers!


PART 2

Call for Papers and Research Submissions


Image source: freepik


1. Purpose of the Paper Review


1. To showcase Avoice's academic standards and capabilities

2. To provide Avoice members with opportunities to participate in academic activities

3. To build connections with more scholars in related fields


2. Paper Review Process


1. The Avoice Annual Forum will dedicate 2–2.5 hours specifically for paper review and presentation

2. We will select high-quality 6 submissions from the entries, and invite each selected author to give an 8-minute presentation, followed by approximately 5 minutes of feedback and suggestions from academic reviewers

3. We will also select several other high-quality submissions for online poster presentations, where participants can "move between" different virtual meeting rooms to view presenters' academic posters and listen to their explanations


3. Eligibility for Paper Submission


1. This call for papers is open to

  • current active Avoice members

  • Avoice reserve pool and former members

  • friends introduced by Avoice members who express interest in participating

2. When reviewing and selecting submissions, priority for review and presentation opportunities will be given first to current active Avoice members, then to Avoice reserve pool and former members, and lastly to friends introduced by Avoice members, in cases where submission quality is comparable


4. Paper Submission Themes


1. Any topic related to areas Avoice has worked on, is currently working on, or plans to engage in, including but not limited to:

  • 16 Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence

  • Protection of Overseas Asian Women

  • Violence-Free Spaces

  • Women's Leadership

2. Submissions from all academic disciplines are welcome. We especially encourage interdisciplinary research, such as:

  • Technology + Philanthropy (e.g., women's websites and forums supporting public sphere construction and their current status)

  • Policy + Women's Health (e.g., women's health from medical and social security perspectives)

  • Workplace + Education (e.g., competence development for overseas Chinese women in the workplace)

  • Law + Psychology (e.g., psychological trauma in children exposed to domestic violence and its correlation with adult criminal behavior)

3. Paper topics and content should align with Avoice's vision of "creating a world of love and equality for women" and its values of "participation, inclusion, and empowerment"


5. Paper Reviewers


We will invite guest experts from the Avoice expert database and well-known scholars in the field of women's rights to serve as paper reviewers


6. Paper and Research Selection Criteria


1. Submissions in Chinese or English are accepted; for other languages, please contact the Academic Department separately for consultation:[email protected]

2. Clear purpose, distinct theme, sufficient evidence, logical structure, and concise language

3. Must follow standard academic format, including title, author name, abstract, keywords, main text, and references

4. Recommended maximum length: 15,000 Chinese characters or 8,000 English words

5. Submissions must be original; plagiarism is prohibited, and no infringement of others' legal rights is allowed

6. Previously published papers will not be accepted

7. This forum encourages authors who are highly interested in or passionate about the themes—even those who have not previously participated in formal academic forums—to actively submit. Experimental studies, literature reviews, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and other types of submissions are all welcome to promote academic exchange and vitality. We also welcome students without complete research results to submit and share high-quality research proposals, ongoing studies, term papers, or short essays


7. Submission Method and Deadline


1. Please submit by scanning the QR code below or clicking " Read More " at the end of this article:

2. Priority submission deadline: 23:59 Beijing Time, December 11, 2022 (all submissions received by this time will be reviewed)

3. Final submission deadline: 23:59 Beijing Time, December 18, 2022 (submissions received after the priority deadline but before this date will be reviewed based on reviewers' availability, but review cannot be guaranteed))

4. The submission portal will close at 23:59 Beijing Time, December 18, 2022
