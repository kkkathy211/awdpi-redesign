At the recent 2024 AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) Forum, they—representing diverse fields, regions, and age groups—gathered together, offering a brilliant intellectual feast through their unique perspectives and profound insights.


Within the vast landscape of the international entrepreneurial ecosystem, they focused on the strengths and challenges faced by women leaders, encouraging women to bravely explore and break through their limits; when addressing global climate change, a challenge confronting all humanity, women fully demonstrated their communication skills and cross-sector collaboration abilities, showcasing undeniable influence.



Let us listen to their voices together and explore the paths of women in international entrepreneurship and climate action.




Through their words, we witnessed their reflections and explorations within the international entrepreneurial ecosystem, and also felt their firm commitment to global climate governance. Opportunities abound, courage endures—every one of them has the chance to display their talent and ambition, and the power to contribute to building a more prosperous, just, and sustainable world.


We look forward to hearing more of their strong and powerful voices in international entrepreneurship, climate governance, and beyond in the future.
