Sound has never been the only bridge connecting us.

The understanding in a shared glance,

the warmth conveyed through gestures,

the sincerity carried in words,

can all help us cross our differences and reach one another.


"Let the world hear the unheard voices"—

what we hear is not complaint, not outcry,

but each individual person,

their passions, perseverance, thoughts, and strength;

it is when we let go of the bias of "normal,"

that we realize:

within different lives,

lie equally rich souls,

and also the glimmers of light

that can shine into each of our lives.




Scan the QR code,

or click "Read More",

at the end of this article


to register. October 25, we await your presence,

to listen with us to the echoes in silence.
