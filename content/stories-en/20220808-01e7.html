VIVA Paints Life .

Everyone is an artist of life




When it comes to women's life stories, what comes to your mind first?

The texture of daily life and the fabric of social relationships leave feelings, marks, and meanings on women's minds and bodies.

They form their sense of self-boundaries through receiving external impacts and making internal reflections, construct themselves within social discourses, and use their own lived experiences to bridge the gap between their authentic selves and external perceptions, becoming unique female figures and leaving behind diverse meanings of life.

Who We Are | Partner Organizations


Viva la Vida is an international organization with influence in over 50 countries, inspiring young people to reflect on life and know themselves, using art as a medium to better perceive life and build meaningful emotional connections with others. To date, VIVA has become a globally influential cross-cultural IP, with partners including the United Nations, G20 YEA, APEC, Facebook, Toutiao, Alibaba, and China Unicom.


Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women) is dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and promoting gender equality, providing comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence.

Through building a cooperation network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, social organizations, and industry experts worldwide, and uniting broader youth public welfare forces, the project advocates public attention and support for the living challenges faced by overseas Chinese women, creating a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, and helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners promoting gender equality and protecting vulnerable groups.
