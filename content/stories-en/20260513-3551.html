How long has it been,

since you last truly paid attention to an orange?

Its colour, its scent,

the delicate, clear sound it makes

as you peel it apart with your fingers—

We're used to living fast,

but rarely pause

to listen to those small yet real feelings

that belong uniquely to us.

This time, we'd like to invite you to slow down together.



About this workshop


This is not a writing class.

There are no requirements or standards like "you must know how to write."

We'll do some gentle and tender exercises together:

Write down whatever thoughts or feelings arise in the moment;

Reconnect with the everyday through your senses,

Use games to open up expression,

Use stories to look back at yourself.


Through writing and drama—

  • awaken your awareness of small things around you,

  • reflect on your personal growth and experiences living abroad,

  • and practice listening and expressing within a safe space.


You don't need to prepare a "good story,"
because you, as you are, are already the best storyteller.



What will we do together?


We'll begin with a simple "Check-in,"
sharing the moment that stood out most to you today.

We'll build connection through games:

"I'm No.1, I'm No.2…"
within repeating and scrambled numbers, slowly finding your own voice and rhythm.


Then, we'll move into writing—

  • freely jotting down whatever comes to mind (even if it's just "I don't know what to write")

  • Spend five minutes truly getting to know a lovely orange 🍊

  • Write about your journey here today—how you arrived, by what path

  • Rewrite your personal story from the perspective of a mythic hero

  • Write a letter to your past self


It might be quiet, it might feel awkward,

but every word you write is a gentle and honest step toward yourself.

Finally, we'll come together to share and reflect,

and leave with a renewed sense of possibility.


About safety and boundaries


This is a safe space, gently held and protected:

  • We respect all forms of expression—no judgment, no scrutiny

  • We encourage participation, but you can always choose to pause or step away

  • There may be photo or video documentation during the event; please speak up at any time if you feel uncomfortable

  • Here, everything is grounded in your sense of safety and comfort

A final note


Perhaps you've been busy and exhausted,

longing to find a safe outlet for yourself;
Perhaps you carry many unspoken words inside,

yet have never found the right moment to express them.

Then come and join us—

on a spring afternoon,

in a space that is safe and gentle,

let's truly observe, listen, write, and feel together.

Let us begin with the scent of an orange,
and rediscover ourselves 🍊

We look forward to your presence.
