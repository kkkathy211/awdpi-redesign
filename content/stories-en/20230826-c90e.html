Bimonthly Data Overview


Monthly Summary of Rescue Work


  • Avoice has newly added support for 3 women seeking help, and is currently providing assistance to 9 clients

  • In addition, 5 case feedbacks were received in 2023, with the needs of all clients basically resolved; among them, 4 out of 5 clients expressed high satisfaction, and 4 out of 5 clients considered staff responses to be very timely

  • In 2023, Avoice has cumulatively provided services to 19 clients, covering 10 countries and regions including the UK, New Zealand, Belgium, and the US 

Monthly Summary of Fundraising Work


  • Avoice conducted fundraising activities for the Asian Women Development Forum, connected with 3 potential funders, and submitted fundraising proposals to each

Monthly Summary of Communications Work


  • Total followers across official accounts and other platforms reached 9,818 (an increase of 388 compared to April–May, a 4% month-on-month growth)

  • A total of 43 posts were published across platforms (an increase of 22 compared to April–May, a 105% month-on-month growth), with cumulative views reaching 19,393 (an increase of 8,548 compared to April–May, a 79% month-on-month growth). The post with the highest readership was "Avoice Recruitment | Legal and Financial Professional Volunteers Wanted – Ongoing," read by 1,736 people


Bimonthly Outstanding Volunteers


  • A total of 60 volunteers were recognized as outstanding volunteers. The full list is provided below. Thank you to all volunteers for your participation and dedication. We look forward to your continued efforts in the next bimonthly period!



Bimonthly Key Events


  1. Initiated the writing of anti-domestic violence legal education handbooks for 6 countries

  2. Departments collaborated actively to prepare for the AWDPI Forum

  3. Released the Empowerment Support Unit Volunteer Guidance Manual, further optimizing the empowerment support workflow

  4. Departments basically completed spring recruitment and onboarding training

  5. Preliminary cooperation agreements reached with Chiang Mai United Charity Foundation (Thailand), CPSA ED, and HSTAR Scotland


★Avoice Bimonthly Report – Issue 4, 2023★

Bimonthly Work Overview


I. Quality Assurance Department


Research Division


During June to July 2023,

  • the Research Division collected and published observation bulletins for June and July, finalized the Belgium domestic violence report and the Belgium workplace survival report, and held an anti-domestic violence training session;

  • additionally, the Research Division assisted in preparing for the AWDPI Forum, finalized sub-forum proposals, and completed recruitment and screening for paper reviewers.


Monitoring and Evaluation Division


During June to July 2023,

  • the Monitoring and Evaluation Division assisted in addressing requests from the Empowerment Support Unit, conducted multiple interviews with team leaders and volunteers, promoted optimization of internal workflows within the Empowerment Support Unit, and assisted in producing the Empowerment Support Unit Volunteer Guidance Manual;

  • furthermore, the Monitoring and Evaluation Division further refined the bimonthly report publication process and standards, promoting transparency and openness in Avoice's operations.


Project Design Division


During June to July 2023,

  • the Project Design Division advanced all aspects of planning for the AWDPI Forum, and facilitated coordination with the Academic, Guest Relations, Operations, Communications and Visual Design, Language Support, and Fundraising departments.


IT and Information Division


During June to July 2023,

  • the IT and Information Division provided technical support to various departments, such as assisting the UK National Office with document migration and supporting the HR department with permission changes and email account recovery;

  • the IT and Information Division conducted spring recruitment, completed hiring and training of new members, and iterated the multidimensional recruitment spreadsheet;

  • additionally, the IT and Information Division advanced the development of AWDPI Website 2.0, completed UI/UX designs for parts of the main AWDPI website and portal 2.0 version, finalized handover of web development with Hangzhou Normal University, and clarified division of labor and development sequence for different websites.


II. Rescue and Support Department


Case Coordination Unit


During June to July 2023,

  • the Case Coordination Unit conducted training for new members, guided volunteers in studying the 2023 Volunteer Guidance Manual, and promoted professionalization of operations


Legal Support Unit


During June to July 2023,

  • the Legal Support Unit improved its internal work system and optimized the breakdown of member responsibilities to help quickly view individual workloads

  • additionally, the Legal Support Unit is currently writing legal education handbooks for 6 new countries, further expanding Avoice's legal support services


Psychological Support Unit


During June to July 2023,

  • the Psychological Support Unit released Course Theme 2, conducted one internal training activity (role-playing), held one internal roundtable forum, further strengthened the professional capacity of the psychological support team, and actively expanded communication channels


Language Support Unit


During June to July 2023,

  • the Language Support Unit completed onboarding for all spring recruits and commenced work

  • the Language Unit completed routine translations in multiple languages, including the English compilation article "Signs of Abuse in Relationships and How to Seek/Provide Help" and the Italian compilation article "Conversation with Author Elena Ferrante: Every Good Storyteller Needs to Write Stories That Feel Truer Than the So-Called 'Truth'", and completed the English translation of the website article "Observation of New Anti-Domestic Violence Policies in Various Countries Since the Pandemic", promoting the dissemination of knowledge on anti-domestic violence and gender equality by breaking language barriers


Empowerment Support Unit


During June to July 2023,

  • the Empowerment Support Unit conducted community management in small groups, hosted one orientation-themed sharing session, held four weekly movie viewings, and launched book club activities, including one online reading session

  • additionally, the Empowerment Support Unit continuously updated the overseas support resource network, completed background checks and data entry for 6 empowerment support database entries, and continuously followed up on client empowerment support work while optimizing workflows


III. Partnership and Expansion Department


Partnership and Expansion Unit


During June to July 2023,

  • the Partnership and Expansion Unit completed spring recruitment review and onboarding training for the Project Collaboration Center, recruiting 8 new members and further strengthening the team

  • the Partnership and Expansion Unit improved the Project Collaboration Center Management Manual and project timeline, clarified workflows, project groupings, and member responsibilities, and established a biweekly working hour tracking system and attendance sheet, further optimizing workflows

  • the Partnership and Expansion Unit executed recruitment channel expansion, completed collection of recruitment information from multiple platforms including WeChat Official Account, Xiaohongshu, Weibo, and Douban, and prepared to launch targeted recruitment campaigns to attract new members to AWDPI

  • the Partnership and Expansion Unit conducted routine due diligence on potential partners, completed basic background checks on 4 organizations covering student associations, social workers, and rescue groups, and communicated to screen past collaboration resources for use as a speaker pool for internally initiated projects

  • additionally, the Partnership and Expansion Unit held two phone meetings with Chiang Mai United Charity Foundation (Thailand), completing initial cooperation discussions


Fundraising Management Unit


During June to July 2023,

  • the Fundraising Management Unit conducted fundraising activities for the Asian Women Development Forum, refined the forum fundraising plan, clarified fundraising targets, and initiated outreach

  • additionally, the Fundraising Management Unit continued to follow up on other fundraising opportunities, such as applications to various foundations and leadership development programs


Rescue Resources Division


During June to July 2023,

  • the Rescue Resources Unit completed the "Rescue Resource Outreach Template," laying the foundation for future resource coordination work


IV. Communications Department


During June to July 2023,

  • the Editorial Unit wrote and published 4 articles: ① "Avoice Her Perspective | Leveraging Women's Power to Tear Down Walls and Open Doors – Reading 'The Moment of Lift'"; ② "Avoice Her Confession Booth | New Letter Column: On PUA in the Name of Love, We're Waiting for Your Voice! (Issue 1)"; ③ "Avoice Healing & Her Voice | Learning to Say 'No' to All Signs of Violent Harm – Lusina's Journey of Self-Rescue"; ④ "Avoice Healing & Her Voice | Conversation with HSTAR Director Ewelina: 'We Provide Assistance to Any Woman Experiencing Psychological Trauma'"

  • the Communications Department head wrote reference documents: "Monthly News Bulletin Work Guide" and "Gender-Neutral Graphic Design," providing optimization guidance for content production


V. Finance Department


During June to July 2023,

  • the Finance Department further advanced financial system development, completed analysis and organization of all past transaction records, and adopted Feishu multidimensional spreadsheets for unified financial management


VI. Human Resources Department


During June to July 2023,

  • the Human Resources Department hosted a new member sharing session and further optimized recruitment efforts, collaborating with the Partnership and Expansion Department to establish a recruitment promotion task force to broaden recruitment channels

  • additionally, the Human Resources Department actively carried out employee relations work, processed onboarding, offboarding, and leave procedures, updated the Employee Relations Team Guidance Manual, and continued daily book sharing


VII. Belgium National Office


During June to July 2023,

  • the Belgium National Office completed leadership transition and director elections, further advancing team building


VIII. UK National Office


During June to July 2023,

  • the UK National Office completed publication of spring recruitment posts, written tests and initial interviews, onboarding and basic training for the General Affairs and Partnership and Expansion Units, and internal transfers or departures of existing Partnership and Expansion Unit members. The UK National Office will continue advancing long-term recruitment and training for the Rescue and Support Unit and Fundraising Unit

  • the UK National Office advanced editorial work, completing articles on the HSTAR Scotland director, the Chinese Women's Alliance event, and an in-person assisted woman, and shared the promotional post for CinaAsia's film screening event "The Missing Her"

  • the UK National Office actively expanded external relations, establishing partnership development with CPSA ED and HSTAR Scotland, and conducted an in-person visit to Shakiti Women's Aid Edinburgh, laying the foundation for future collaboration

  • additionally, the UK National Office continuously advanced internal team building, completing role optimization, document organization, annual work planning, work dashboard optimization, and an orientation event


IX. US National Office


During June to July 2023,

  • the US National Office completed written tests and interviews for spring recruitment, further expanding its office staffing
