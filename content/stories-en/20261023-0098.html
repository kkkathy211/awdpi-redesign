## Talk Information

Who Writes the Story: 
Immigrant Women's Path to Self-Rescue Under the U.S. Justice System


In recognition of Domestic Violence Awareness Month, Vermilion Theater, in partnership with Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI), will host a community talk following the premiere of Juliet & Zhu YingTai.


Time

Friday, October 23, starting at approximately 9:00 PM, immediately after the 7:30 PM performance; duration approximately 30 minutes



## Talk Topics


  • Who is AWDPI

  • Basic framework of U.S. family law and domestic violence cases

  • What is the legal definition of an intimate relationship

  • What behaviors constitute intimate partner violence

  • What is a protection order, how to apply for one, and what protection it provides

  • How cultural differences and language barriers affect immigrant women's ability to express themselves and seek help

  • Drawing from the story in the play and real-life experiences, where can we find support, and how can we participate in community mutual aid


After the premiere, everyone is welcome to join us!

ABOUT VERMILION

Vermilion Theater is an independent professional theater company based in Boston, with production teams and touring activities spanning across the U.S. East Coast. We aim to promote cultural exchange and sharing of bilingual theater works in Chinese and English from Chinese-speaking regions. Founded during a time marked by anti-Chinese discrimination and the pandemic, we are committed to using theater as an axis to collectively imagine and build new communities and public spaces.
