Introduction

On the occasion of the fifth anniversary of AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International), we present this report as a tribute to every ally who has walked alongside us in eliminating gender-based violence and discrimination.


Over the past five years, AWDPI has worked across more than 30 countries and regions in Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, guided by the principles of "Participation, Inclusion, Empowerment," building a global support network covering language assistance, psychological support, legal aid, and community connection, serving over a thousand survivors of gender-based violence and discrimination, publishing 40 cross-national research reports, and partnering with United Nations agencies, governments, and academic organizations to build a global alliance against violence.


This report not only documents how AWDPI has grown from a start-up initiative in Belgium into an international nonprofit force, but also carries the resilient stories of overseas Asian women moving from survival to self-empowerment— their voices will ultimately become a wave of global change.

Acknowledgements



Over five years, AWDPI has proven through action: combating structural violence requires systemic responses. From establishing a support database with "nine-language services and 700 partner organizations" to launching anti-gender-based violence campaigns reaching two million people; from accompanying individual survivors in restoring dignity, to convening global changemakers at the Asian Women Development Forum—we have always believed that true equality begins with a deep response to marginalized struggles. Moving forward, AWDPI will continue to deepen program professionalism, expand international cooperation, and innovate communication models, advancing with firmer steps toward fulfilling our mission—


creating a world of love and equality for women. We extend our sincere gratitude to the 1,106 volunteers, over 100 expert mentors, and all donors who have supported us over the past five years: every act of trust is reshaping the life trajectories of overseas Asian women. On this journey, we are walking together!
