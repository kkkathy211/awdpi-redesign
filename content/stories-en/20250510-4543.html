Empowering the Public Good Sector with Professional Skills


ABC gathers, leads, and develops by conducting Spring, Summer, and Autumn public welfare consulting seasons, digital creation camps, and giving circles each year, professional volunteers guides social resources to provide solutions and funding for public welfare causes, breaking through existing time, capability, and resource bottlenecks within the industry, and promoting the sustained, effective, and healthy development of China's public welfare sector; while leading volunteers to empower and enhance efficiency across sectors, ABC also supports professional volunteers in achieving personal growth, self-fulfillment, and social contribution.


Past working volunteers have primarily come from industries such as management consulting, internet, accounting and auditing, investment, securities, and nonprofit organizations, including professionals from renowned companies such as Accenture, PwC, Deloitte, EY, Nielsen, ByteDance, and Tencent. Student volunteers mainly come from top Chinese universities including Peking University, Tsinghua University, Fudan University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Renmin University of China, Zhejiang University, Sun Yat-sen University, and Southwestern University of Finance and Economics.

A Better Community (ABC) was founded in 2008 and is China's first social enterprise that mobilizes professional volunteers to provide management consulting and research services to social organizations. It currently has six local chapters in Beijing, Shanghai, Chengdu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, and Hangzhou, as well as two online chapters: the Digital Creation Camp and the Social Venture Philanthropy Chapter.


ABC brings together, leads, and develops professional volunteers to provide solutions for China's nonprofit sector in areas including strategic planning, brand communication, project design and evaluation, human resources, fundraising, corporate social responsibility (CSR), research on public welfare topics, impact investment and donation advisory, and digital philanthropy. By doing so, ABC helps overcome the industry's bottlenecks in time, capacity, and resources, promoting sustainable, effective, and healthy development of China's public welfare sector. While empowering volunteers to create cross-sector impact and improve efficiency, ABC also supports their personal growth, self-fulfillment, and social contribution.


As of 2024, ABC has nearly 6,000 professional volunteers who have cumulatively provided consulting, research, digital, impact investment, and donation advisory services for over 300 nonprofit organizations in fields such as education, child protection, elderly care, rural revitalization, environmental protection, and community development, delivering 590 projects with over 720,000 hours of volunteer service time.
