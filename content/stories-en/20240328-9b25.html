AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) is dedicated to providing full and sustained protection and support for overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence, including language support, psychological support, legal support, community support, overseas assistance network support, and support for storytelling and speaking out.


During Women's Month 2024, we completed the compilation of the Six-Country Handbook on Preventing and Responding to Domestic Violence, a handbook based on relevant legal documents from the Netherlands, Belgium, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, and the United States, summarizing and organizing information on how each country defines, penalizes, and provides remedies for domestic violence.
