 Why have there been no great women artists? 


In 1971, art historian Linda Nochlin published an essay titled "Why Have There Been No Great Women Artists?" which sparked widespread discussion in the field of art history.

This question continues to resonate today.

Why have there been no great women artists?

Is it because women lack the desire to express themselves? Or is it because their creative time is consumed by invisible domestic labor?

Is it because women lack talent? Or is their talent repeatedly worn down by self-doubt labeled as "not good enough" or "not important"?

Is it because they do not wish to enter the art history of "greatness," or because they lack the "ticket" to enter? The definition of what constitutes "greatness" has always been controlled by others.

For a long time, the unique perspectives and creative impulses of countless women have been structurally trapped, silently set aside.

Over time, we have even grown accustomed to "silence." Yet in reality, women's authentic experiences and expressions—rooted in real life and emotion—are irreplaceable and inherently valuable and meaningful.

In the past, high barriers to artistic creation prevented many ideas from materializing; today, the widespread availability of AI creation tools is returning the right to create to everyone in an unprecedented way. Art is becoming a more open and inclusive medium of expression. We believe that, with this opportunity, more "great women artists" may emerge.



Project Introduction


VividLifes: A practice community that takes "gender" as a perspective and "art" as a means of expression. We are committed to building a safe, inclusive, and equal space for dialogue, to inspire women's creativity and respond to the challenges that make women hesitate on the path of expression.

  • We believe, women's personal perspectives possess undeniable power. Your experiences, your feelings, your stories—they are unique, and they deserve to be seen and heard.

  •  We believe, expression itself is an act. Every time you name your feelings, give form to your story, it is an affirmation of your own subjectivity.

  • We believe, your voice is also our echo. When you bravely express yourself, you not only represent yourself, but also offer silent women the chance to find resonance in your work.

With this belief, we hope to harness the AI wave to activate more women's courage and ability to express themselves, to speak up, to bravely explore the unknown, and together redefine our standards of "greatness".


"Her Room: AIGC Visual Art Initiative" What kind of project is this?

*Hereinafter referred to as "HerRoom"

HerRoom is a six-week female-led AIGC visual art creation camp. We aim to use AI as a "brush," and through a combination of creation, community-based learning, and mentorship empowerment, accompany you on a complete journey from seeing yourself to learning to create using yourself as method.


What will you gain in this "room"?

  • A new perspective: We will introduce knowledge and case studies from feminist art history, offering you a fresh lens through which to view yourself and the world, connecting your personal expression to broader collective resonance.

  • A new language: We will guide you step by step into AI visual creation, helping you master a powerful and accessible method of expression, turning the ideas in your mind into reality with ease.

  • A group of companions: You will gain a "safe space" formed by like-minded peers, where, in an atmosphere of mutual listening and support among women, you can overcome hesitation in expression and freely share your true thoughts and feelings, sparking creativity.

  • A set of works: At the end of this journey, you will complete a visual art piece truly your own, serving as a precious testament to your self-exploration.

  • An echo: Your final work will be presented in our online/offline exhibition platform, allowing your story to meet the world and gain lasting vitality.


 What kind of journey will we embark on?

This is not an art class; it is a creative practice of self-discovery. You will follow our carefully designed three modules and six sessions, gradually engaging in self-exploration and discovery, transforming these insights into creative momentum and material, and completing self-expression with the support of AI tools.

  • Course format: Online live sessions (via Tencent Meeting) + community-based learning + mentor sharing

  • Course schedule: Starting October 17 (or 18), one session per week, totaling six sessions

  • Course outline:
