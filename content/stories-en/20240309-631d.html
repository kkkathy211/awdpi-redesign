The United Nations' theme for International Women's Day 2024 is "Invest in Women: Accelerate Progress." By 2030, the annual funding gap for gender equality initiatives could reach as high as 360 billion US dollars, making it urgent to advance financing for gender equality.


During the United Nations 2023 International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women "16 Days of Activism," we hosted five online workshops, discussing topics such as "Occupational Illnesses from a Gender Perspective" with over 300 audience members. The theme of International Women's Day 2024 continues the same thread as the 2023 International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women: "Eliminating gender-based violence through adequate funding." Without significant investment to address violence against women and girls, expand prevention programs, and implement effective policies, countries will not be able to eliminate gender-based violence by 2030.


This year, AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International will host four workshops spanning China, Germany, and Ecuador during International Women's Day, joining people across countries who care about gender issues in expressing support for survivors of gender-based violence.

Globally, one in three women has experienced physical or sexual violence. Such violence prevents women from fully participating in social life and brings long-term negative impacts to their families, communities, and countries.


In many countries and regions, violence against women and girls remains widespread. For example, in Albania, more than half of women and girls have experienced gender-based violence at some point in their lives.


Eliminating violence against women and girls is a priority area for UN Women in Albania. In this session, we will guide you through the current state of gender equality in Albania and the efforts made by UN Women and other international organizations to eliminate violence against women.
