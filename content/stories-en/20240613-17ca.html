Who we are

WHO WE ARE... 


In recent years, with rapid social development and change, women have achieved remarkable progress across various fields. However, female (or soon-to-be) college students still face numerous challenges in academics, employment, and life planning. To better address these challenges and enhance personal capabilities and overall competencies, Huita Youth has been running a public-interest growth coaching program for female (or soon-to-be) college students since 2022.

By providing professional growth coaching services, we help female (or soon-to-be) college students clarify life goals, improve self-awareness, build confidence, develop problem-solving skills, and promote personal growth and career development.



Program Background




Golden Decade Plan 3.0


    Why "Golden Decade"? —

     The best time to plant a tree was ten years ago,

     The second-best time is now.


Ages 18 to 25 represent a critical turning point in life. During this period, young women transition from college entrance after high school, to entering the workforce after graduation, experiencing career growth, and even starting families—shifting through different life roles. This stage is profoundly important, yet young women often feel lost and directionless. If supported by life coaches, mentors, and peer networks, and given opportunities for practical creation, these experiences can lay spiritual and material foundations for future growth, even shaping their lives ten years later.



In our two years of service, we have found that young women often face the following challenges:

From an educational perspective, female college students have made significant progress in accessing higher education. With social advancement and rising status of women, more and more women are pursuing higher education and continuously improving their knowledge and skills. However, they struggle to access high-quality information, resources, and opportunities for growth and development. They lack peer support from like-minded individuals and find it difficult to break out of their circles to learn from more outstanding people.

From an employment perspective, female college students face certain challenges. Despite their achievements in higher education, women still encounter unequal opportunities and challenges in the job market. Some employers may practice gender discrimination during recruitment, making it difficult for female graduates to find jobs. Moreover, female students face obstacles in career development. On the path of psychological and professional growth, they can only figure things out on their own. Finding direction and overcoming confusion takes a long time and comes at a high cost in terms of resources.

From a psychological perspective, female college students also face various pressures and challenges. During university, they must not only cope with academic stress but also deal with pressures from society and family. Some may experience anxiety, depression, or other emotional issues, requiring timely attention and support.

These numerous challenges hinder young women's forward progress. Growth coaching is crucial for women's personal and professional development, yet its availability remains extremely low.

According to a report by Catalyst, a global research platform on women's workplace development, two roles are particularly important in women's career growth: mentors and coaches.

Compared to mentors, who are more widely recognized and often supported by corporate resources, the role of professional coaches remains uncommon, and when available, is usually limited to executive coaching for senior corporate leaders.

Coaching helps women take necessary actions to embrace their inner strength and overcome internal fears. However, professional coaching services on the market are expensive, ranging from 200 to 2,000 RMB per hour, which undoubtedly deters many women in need.

We aim to support more women in exploring their personal paths and achieving their dreams. To this end, Sun Binghui, founder of Huita, has launched the["Golden Decade" Public Interest Project.

What is a "coach"?



A coach is a thinking companion who, through prompting reflection, inspires participants to discover their personal and professional potential, supports them in finding and achieving their goals, and helps them align knowledge with action.

A coach's working method differs from that of a mentor or a psychological counselor, even if the coach possesses relevant experience and educational background. Essentially, a coach is a client's thinking partner, focusing on helping the client utilize their own creativity and resources, see pathways to overcome obstacles, and solve their own problems. People need to feel seen, heard, and valued before they desire to grow. A coach will be your partner, listener, and companion, providing you with companionship, communication, consultation, and guidance.

"Everyone on the path to success should have a coach supporting them from behind."

This coaching program will be conducted online, eliminating barriers of time and location. Participants can flexibly schedule coaching sessions with their growth coaches. To ensure optimal session outcomes, we recommend each session last no less than forty minutes.

To prevent loss of coaching resources, we ask participants to observe the following during one-on-one coaching sessions:


  • Within the three-month project period, you are required to have at least six sessions with your coach; there is no time requirement for each session.

  • If you cannot attend a scheduled session, please inform your coach at least 24 hours in advance. If affected by force majeure, please notify your coach as early as possible.

  • During the sessions, you are required to submit written feedback 6 to 12 times, each around 100 words.

  • For sessions without written feedback, we hope you will reflect and review the session in other ways to gain meaningful insights.


Participant Recruitment Requirements




Participant Requirements —

  • Female college students currently enrolled (including soon-to-be college students, with 2024 graduates prioritized)

  • Those currently feeling lost or anxious, seeking direction, with strong motivation for growth, and eager to explore and improve self-awareness

  • Those wishing to enhance workplace skills, leadership abilities, interpersonal communication, and possessing a growth mindset

  • Students whose parents have not received higher education (middle school, high school, or vocational school diplomas are considered as not having received higher education), and those from regions with underdeveloped educational resources, township high schools, or rural households are prioritized

  • Identify with the "Golden Decade" concept, understand gratitude and giving back to society, and have the willingness to pass on the spirit of the "Golden Decade."

  • With support from the project team, able to actively communicate with coaches/mentors, complete feedback forms, and achieve an activity participation rate of over 80%
