Keynote Speaker


Carrie Lam

 Fifth-term Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region

Honorary Permanent President of the Hong Kong General Chamber of Commerce in Shanghai

Carrie Lam joined the Hong Kong government's Administrative Service in August 1980 after graduating from the University of Hong Kong. She was promoted to the highest rank in the civil service in 2006, serving as Permanent Secretary. In July 2007, she was nominated by the then Chief Executive and appointed by the Central People's Government as a principal official, taking office as Secretary for Development. On July 1, 2012, she was appointed as Chief Secretary for Administration.

Carrie Lam's public service career spanned 42 years, including her five-year term as Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. She served citizens in 20 different public roles, covering areas such as finance, security, social welfare, healthcare, district administration, arts and sports, infrastructure, and land planning.

Carrie Lam was elected as the fifth-term Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region on March 26, 2017, and officially appointed by the Central People's Government on March 31, 2017. She assumed office on July 1, 2017, leading Hong Kong through a turbulent period and facing the most serious challenges since its return to China.

Carrie Lam is married and has two sons.


Forum Speakers


Alice Wong

Chief Human Resources Officer, Citibank China

Alice Wong has over 25 years of human resources experience. She joined Citigroup in 2020 as Chief Human Resources Officer for China, leading the China HR team in formulating and executing human capital strategies, supporting China's business plans and group strategic direction, and providing expertise on talent and organizational matters to enterprise leadership and the board.

Alice's first job was as a tax consultant at PwC, and she formally began her HR journey in 1998 as a recruiter. Anita Ching

Vice President of Marketing, Sephora Greater China

Anita Ching currently serves as Vice President of Marketing at Sephora Greater China, responsible for brand management. Sephora, part of the LVMH Group, has been in China for 19 years, operating over 340 stores and multiple online platforms across 100 cities, with over 25 million premium beauty members. Through omnichannel marketing, it serves an average of over one million beauty-loving customers daily.

Anita graduated from The Chinese University of Hong Kong with a Bachelor's and Master's degree in Business Administration. She has over 27 years of marketing and management experience, including brand building, product development, media communication, social media promotion, membership operations, public opinion and PR, advertising design, and visual merchandising, focusing on the beauty, personal care, and nutrition industries. She has held leadership roles in renowned international and local enterprises, overseeing marketing operations across Greater China and the Asia-Pacific region.


Bessie Lee

Chief Executive Officer, Greater China, JLL

Bessie Lee currently serves as Chief Executive Officer for Greater China at JLL, based in Shanghai, leading 16 branch offices and over 18,500 employees across the region. She oversees the company's overall strategy and business operations in Greater China, focusing on better serving Chinese clients through digital transformation and technology empowerment, driving new business growth and increasing market penetration of the company's tech products.

With over three decades of deep experience in media and communications across Greater China, Bessie previously held leadership roles at multiple companies under the WPP Group.
