About the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project is dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and advancing gender equality by providing comprehensive, ongoing protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence. Through building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, and by mobilizing broader youth-driven public welfare forces, the project advocates for public awareness and support regarding the challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. It aims to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.


The project's abbreviated name, "Avoice," is derived from Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program (the English name of the Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project). It signifies using women's voices to convey visions for development, using youth voices to call for gender equality, openly declaring humanitarian values with sincerity, and resolutely singing the enduring song of mission. Avoice is committed to channeling countless public welfare voices into the powerful global movement advancing the Sustainable Development Goals for gender equality!


If you are passionate about eliminating domestic violence and advancing gender equality, and if you hope to gain practical experience and personal growth through an international public welfare project with a global perspective, then welcome to join the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, where you can connect with outstanding volunteers across multiple countries and experienced expert mentors from various fields, allowing youth ideas and public welfare passion to collide and inspire, and using youth power to co-create a home of love and equality for Chinese women.

Department Introduction:

The Program Quality Department consists of four sub-departments: Academic Research, Program Design, Monitoring & Evaluation, and IT Support. The Academic Research sub-department plans and conducts academic research on topics such as gender equality, anti-domestic violence, women's protection, and empowerment, writes research reports and popular science articles, and provides recommendations and strategic adjustments for institutional strategy and program design. The Program Design sub-department is responsible for formulating the organization's development strategy, annual work plans, and leading the planning and execution of large-scale events. The Monitoring & Evaluation sub-department monitors daily operations and program quality, conducts assessments of overall impact, proposes improvement plans based on findings, optimizes workflows, enhances efficiency, and promotes interdepartmental collaboration. The IT Support sub-department handles website design, development, testing, and maintenance, as well as daily IT support in collaboration with other departments.

Department introduction:

The Avoice Chinese Women Global Development Forum, initiated by the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, will invite scholars, experts, and industry leaders from all sectors of society to share and discuss gender-related issues. Based on academic dialogue, the forum aims to integrate with practical philanthropy, offering innovative perspectives on combating gender-based violence through intergenerational, interdisciplinary, and cross-regional collaboration. Through a hybrid model of online guidance and offline support, the forum will build a knowledge-sharing network for global volunteers, creating practical opportunities to engage with gender issues and further expanding the living space and voice of overseas Chinese women.
