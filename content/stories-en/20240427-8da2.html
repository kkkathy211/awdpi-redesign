Introduction




In order to gain an in-depth understanding of the growth paths, management models, and development experiences of U.S. nonprofit organizations, and to provide feasible reference recommendations for the strategic planning and long-term expansion of AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) in the United States, the "U.S. Nonprofit Organization Research Initiative" has been launched, funding relevant research projects and ultimately producing research reports to promote AWDPI's professional development, improve the living and development environment for Asian women overseas, and jointly build a women's community rooted in love and equality.



Research Proposal Review Results


Two applicants have been selected for this funding round,

namely:


Ying Nanqin

Research Topic

Research Report on U.S. Nonprofit Organizations – "Asian Women's Shelter" & YWCA



Xiang Xinquan

Research Topic

A Study of Women's Empowerment Organizations in the St. Louis Region, USA: An Analysis of Development History and Current Status



We thank everyone for their attention and support in this research initiative. We look forward to sharing the research findings and, more importantly, to continuing our journey together with you on women's empowerment and nonprofit development.
