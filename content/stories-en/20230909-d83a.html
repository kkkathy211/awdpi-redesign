Today, many sex education organizations focus on promoting sex education for children in impoverished areas, yet numerous children on the urban margins are still left behind in the gap between resources.

The lack of sex education often makes it difficult for them to access scientific and comprehensive sexual knowledge during their growth, leaving them unprepared with the skills to cope with physical and emotional changes during adolescence, sexual abuse, school bullying, gender discrimination, unintended pregnancy, and sexually transmitted infections. It also makes it harder for them to develop values of equality, inclusivity, and diversity in a society where discussing sex is taboo. Amid the cultural conflicts between urban and rural values, the absence of these competencies makes these children's lives more prone to hardships.

Nanjing, located in central Jiangsu Province at the border of Jiangsu and Anhui, experiences significant cultural conflict and has a large floating population. Many children have migrated to Nanjing with their parents. Meanwhile, Nanjing is rich in university resources. Qinghe Social Worker maintains strong collaborative ties with university-based sexual health student groups and has learned that many university students are eager to conduct sex education for children, but lack suitable teaching materials and venues for activities.

Therefore, Qinghe Social Worker has launched the "Youth in the Wind" project, which aims to train university student volunteers, develop and refine "adolescent sex education courseware," and deliver systematic, scientific, and comprehensive sex education courses to children aged 10–15 in Nanjing and surrounding areas—especially those on the urban margins.

Project Implementation Plan


Sep 2023—Dec 2023: Improve comprehensive sex education courseware

Refine Qinghe Social Worker's comprehensive sex education materials for youth aged 10–15, produce accompanying lecture notes and teaching tools, and conduct course testing to ensure the materials meet the needs of most instructors.

Oct 2023—Dec 2023: Volunteer recruitment and training

Recruit 2–3 members each from 10 university-based sex education student groups in Nanjing's Jiangning, Yuhuatai, Qixia, Gulou, and Jiangbei New Area—districts with high concentrations of universities. After recruitment, conduct 8 training sessions and require participants to pass an assessment.

Jan–Feb 2024 / Mar–Jun 2024: Facilitate sex education activities

During the first half of the project, partner with communities or universities to provide each student group with a teaching venue (in a community or school), and support each group in delivering 12 sessions. Each session will serve 10–15 participants, and the training effectiveness will be evaluated.

Aug 2024: Adolescent (10–15 years) Sex Education Exchange Forum

Invite educators, psychologists, social workers, and parents—professionals involved in sex education—to attend an exchange forum on sex education for 10–15-year-olds, where the project's progress and outcomes will be summarized and reported.

About Qinghe Social Worker


A nonprofit organization dedicated to sexual health and sex education, whose volunteer team has been conducting various sexual health activities since 2012 and officially registered in 2021. It primarily provides sexual health consultation, testing, medical accompaniment, and comprehensive education services for people aged 10–30. We believe communication, understanding, trust, and companionship can create a better world.

The organization's mission is to define health through "life adaptability" and to strive for more equitable living opportunities. Its vision is that no one should be limited by their self-expression and that everyone should be able to access desired medical resources and live a healthy life. Its core values are: equality, inclusivity, freedom, and self-empowerment.

We promote sexual health, reduce sexual crises, advocate for sexual equality, encourage interactions among diverse stakeholders, challenge fixed perceptions about STIs, abortion, and diverse sexualities/genders, advocate for gender-friendly medical environments, and empower marginalized groups to pursue more equitable living opportunities.
