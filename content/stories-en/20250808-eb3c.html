Setting off for Thailand, building a shield together: AWDPI Thailand National Office We sincerely invite you to join!


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) has long been committed to addressing violence and discrimination faced by Asian women overseas, providing practical protection and sustained empowerment support. Through our past services, we have encountered individual cases and cooperation requests from Thailand, and observed service gaps in areas such as legal support, language assistance, and cultural understanding. Based on our existing projects and partnership networks, we are establishing a National Office in Thailand to enhance the responsiveness and coordination efficiency of our local work. To this end, AWDPI is now launching a special recruitment drive for the Thailand National Office, openly recruiting department heads and team members. We welcome partners who are responsible, professionally capable, and share our vision for collective action.


In this recruitment round, we will give priority to applicants who have been living, studying, or working in Thailand long-term to strengthen our talent pipeline. If you are less experienced but full of passion, we will still offer trust and opportunities, and look forward to growing together with you; if you are an experienced expert or scholar in a relevant field, you are also warmly welcome to join the AWDPI family. Let us join hands with the AWDPI Thailand National Office and write a new chapter in the protection and empowerment of Asian women!


About AWDPI

ABOUT


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development. It evolved from Avoice (the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in the UK, Belgium, the USA, Thailand, and Australia. AWDPI has built extensive partnerships with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. AWDPI advocates for attention to the living challenges of Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more development opportunities for Asian women overseas in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them transform from a vulnerable group into a vital force driving social progress.

We are committed to

DEVOTION


providing support and assistance to domestic violence survivors. Through a comprehensive support system and a robust overseas assistance network, we promptly respond to the needs of overseas Chinese women in domestic violence prevention, emergency aid, and follow-up services.


Building community connections. By establishing online community networks and offline mutual support groups, we create an international platform for overseas Asian women to communicate, learn, and support each other, forming peer support networks and enabling self-development.


Enhancing women's influence. Through forums, seminars, workshops, and other activities, we actively promote multi-stakeholder collaboration to analyze experiences, study challenges, explore solutions, and showcase the strength and potential of Asian women.


Launching awareness campaigns and advocacy. We actively conduct public outreach, academic research, and policy advocacy to promote global attention to overseas Asian women and drive improvements in related policies and development environments.

RECRUIT PLAN
