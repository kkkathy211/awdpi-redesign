About AWDPI



AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development.


Evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project) established in 2020, AWDPI now operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams in Belgium, the UK, the US, Australia, and Thailand. It has built a broad global cooperation network with United Nations system organizations, relevant government agencies, international organizations, multinational corporations, and leading academic institutions.


We consistently uphold the core values of "Participation, Inclusion, and Empowerment," providing end-to-end rescue and support for Asian women in crisis, building cross-cultural peer support networks, advancing gender equality through academic research and policy advocacy, and helping every Asian woman access equal opportunities and live with dignity.


As AWDPI's core on-the-ground team in Southeast Asia, the Thailand National Office leverages Thailand's diverse, inclusive social environment and strategic geographic position to deeply engage local communities, international organizations, and social service resources. Focusing on the real needs of Asian women in Thailand, we carry out work in rescue referrals, policy advocacy, academic research, community connection, and cross-border collaboration, continuously advancing regional gender equality and inclusive environments.

AWDPI Leadership System Overview 

At AWDPI, departments are typically led by a department head to ensure operational stability and continuity within the volunteer structure. As core members of the organization, department heads carry primary responsibility for team coordination, task delegation, and project advancement. When a head departs, they must communicate in advance and complete a full handover according to organizational procedures to ensure smooth continuity. In the event of a vacancy, the organization will first consider internal recommendations of outstanding members; if no suitable candidate is available, the management team will temporarily assume the role until a qualified head is found. The AWDPI leadership system aims to ensure long-term organizational stability while offering volunteers opportunities for growth and leadership development.
