01



The World Economic Forum and the Global Shapers Community

The Global Shapers Community was born out of the World Economic Forum (WEF) and founded in 2011 by Professor Klaus Schwab, Founder of the World Economic Forum. As a renowned international youth network, the Global Shapers Community aims to harness individual, community, and global efforts to provide young people with a worldwide platform to co-create the future.


The Global Shapers Community is dedicated to building a diverse collaborative platform that unites and fully unleashes the potential and value of young people. Since its founding in 2011, the Global Shapers Community has launched over 2,000 social impact and development projects, benefiting more than 2 million people. We advocate intergenerational dialogue and co-creation, promoting deep youth engagement in public decision-making and social solution-building, working together toward a more sustainable and inclusive future.


To date, the Global Shapers Community has 11,520 Shapers, spread across 155 countries and regions, in 508 city-based hubs. In each city, Shaper teams self-organize to launch initiatives that meet their community's needs. Projects span a wide range, including disaster response, poverty alleviation, climate change action, and inclusive community building.

World Economic Forum · Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub

The Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub (Global Shaper Community Shenzhen) is composed of young people from diverse professional backgrounds. While pursuing personal growth, we believe youth should be " active drivers of change, not passive observers." As one of the most active hubs in China on issues such as social innovation, technological advancement, mental health, and women's opportunities, the Shenzhen Hub is committed to advancing the Sustainable Development Goals through technological innovation and community co-creation.

Past Project Highlights





DSE Social Enterprise Education Project Team

(Decoding Social Enterprise)



"DSE: Social Enterprise Education" is a strategic initiative by GSC SZ aimed at promoting the value of social enterprises to the public, primarily targeting high-impact groups in China. Through social enterprise education campaigns, workshops, and site visits, the project encourages the establishment of more social enterprises, promotes the practice of social enterprise concepts across various sectors, advocates for supportive policies, and invites more influential individuals and organizations to join the social enterprise ecosystem.





AI Industry Frontier Bootcamp

(AIGC BOOTCAMP)



The "AI Industry Frontier Bootcamp," launched by the Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub, offers foundational AI training to tech enthusiasts with no prior computer science background. As AI advances rapidly, many non-technical individuals with limited resources—"tech novices"—struggle to access quality learning opportunities. The AIGC Bootcamp fills this gap by providing free and accessible AI introductory courses, including online lectures and in-person workshops, enabling more beginners to experience and learn the wonders of AI technology.





Mental and Physical Wellness

(Wellness Wonders)



"Wellness Wonders" is dedicated to creating a multi-format health-enhancing community through the integration of innovation and human-centered care. Through interactive workshops and community events, we help participants relieve stress, build resilience, and cultivate sustainable healthy lifestyles. From mindfulness and self-awareness to healthy eating and outdoor activities, our diverse programs respond to contemporary challenges. Whether you are a working professional, student, parent, or senior, Wellness Wonders believes true health begins with self-awareness and self-care. In the future, we will continue to co-create more community activities centered on mental and physical wellness, exploring the infinite possibilities of holistic well-being and creating health miracles for everyone.





Wild Voice Girls Observers

(Observers of Her)



"Wild Voice Girls Observers" is a cross-sectoral dialogue and mutual support project for women, co-produced by the Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub and Lean In Shenzhen. Since its launch in 2022, the project has released over 20 online podcast episodes and hosted more than 5 offline events, focusing on topics such as workplace dynamics, legal rights, climate change, gender safety, reproductive health, neuroscience, and psychology. It has connected over 20 professionals from diverse industries and life stages, reaching 4,500 podcast listeners and 3,500 readers.





G.R.O.W. Growth Tree Program

(G.R.O.W)



G.R.O.W. stands for Growth, Resilience, Open-mindedness, and Well-being—the first letters spelling the English word "GROW," meaning "growth." The program aims to help Chinese families understand and learn about social-emotional development, promoting children's holistic physical and mental growth.


Launched in 2021, the program has completed four editions, benefiting over 100 families.


Through this initiative, we hope to provide children with fun, interactive learning experiences, build effective and sustainable communication bridges between parents and children, and support children in growing strong, open-minded, and happy.

03

Q&A

Q1: How many rounds are there in the selection process?

A1: There are two rounds in total. The first is the application form; the second is the interview. Those who pass the interview will enter a probationary period (Probation), during which they participate in hub activities. After successfully completing the probationary period, they become full members.


Q2: Is there a fee to apply?

A2: The GSC community does not charge any membership fees. We come together with a spirit of public service and the shared belief in "Shaping the world," creating value for our local communities and society.


Q3: Can I apply if I don't live or work in Shenzhen?

A3: Due to the need for regular project participation, we prioritize applicants who live or work in Shenzhen or nearby cities.


Q4: Is the application bar high? What are the chances of being selected? Are students likely to be accepted?

A4: We welcome working professionals and students who meet the age requirements to apply. Selection will be based on actual circumstances, and criteria will not be fixed to social status. We believe that as long as you take every step of the review process seriously, you will gain valuable experience.


Q5: If I am unable to participate fully due to work or personal reasons after being accepted, can I withdraw?

A5: GSC Community is a global non-profit organization. Every successfully selected member undertakes a commitment and responsibility to serve the community and bring change to their city. We do not require members to participate in all activities from beginning to end, but we do expect each member to actively leverage their influence and contribute alongside peers. If you cannot guarantee your full participation, please consider your application carefully.
