World Political, Economic and Technological Forum .

The World Institute of Political, Economic and Technological Research is an international social think tank focusing on major-power politics, economics, and technological frontiers, aiming to lead the forefront of the era and respond to the questions of our time. The institute brings together hundreds of outstanding scholars and industry experts to accelerate the development of China into a "global talent hub" and "innovation highland," cultivating young talents who understand China, contribute to China, and lead China.





When you open this post,

there are 7 days remaining until December 2, 2023, Beijing time.


The global political, economic, and security landscape is undergoing unprecedented transformation. The tensions between globalization and regionalization, technological advances reshaping modes of production, and the emergence of global challenges such as climate change have made international relations more complex and volatile than ever. Under this backdrop, how can youth respond to these challenges and achieve sustainable development through international cooperation, policy innovation, and technological progress?


On December 2, 2023, the "PASSION: Driving Change in Sync with the Times" Youth Cooperation and Development Summit, co-hosted by Schwarzman College at Tsinghua University, the World Institute of Political, Economic and Technological Research, and YSI China, will be held at the Dali Auditorium of Schwarzman College, Tsinghua University.


We have invited top industry entrepreneurs, experts, investors, and outstanding youth representatives from home and abroad to participate, jointly exploring how youth can leverage their innovative power in the "era of global transformation," "the age of artificial intelligence," "the data era," and "the era of carbon neutrality" to advance global cooperation and development. Together, we will witness the convergence of youth-driven momentum and feel the pulse of our times!
