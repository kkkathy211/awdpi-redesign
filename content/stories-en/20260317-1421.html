In March 2026, the global spotlight on gender equality turns once again to the United Nations Headquarters in New York—the 70th session of the UN Commission on the Status of Women (CSW70). As an international nonprofit organization long dedicated to women's rights and gender justice, AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) proudly presents a series of online parallel events for CSW70, inviting outstanding representatives and frontline activists from diverse fields to exchange insights and engage in dialogue on women's rights protection, gender justice, and global gender issues.


Following two deeply impactful conversations, AWDPI will launch its third major online parallel forum—"Shaping the World's Voice: Women Writers, Global Narratives, and Gender Justice."
