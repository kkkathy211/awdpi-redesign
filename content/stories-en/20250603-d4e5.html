Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub is now recruiting new members!


Shenzhen, like the dawn's first light, pulses with transformative energy across the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area. Between the city's iconic landmarks surges a wave of global youth innovation, where every street corner brims with the potential to change the world—social innovators wielding technology as their pen and public good as their ink, painting visions of the future on the canvas of community.



The Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub precisely uses "sustainable impact" as its compass, building a diverse public good matrix spanning AI literacy, advancing women's power, youth mental development, social enterprise education, and mental and physical health promotion.


Let's embark together on this journey filled with passion and mission!


ABOUT US

Who We Are


The Global Shapers Community (GSC) was founded in 2011 by Professor Klaus Schwab, founder of the World Economic Forum (WEF). Born from the World Economic Forum, the Global Shapers Community is a renowned international youth network organization aiming to harness individual, community, and global forces to provide young people with a global platform to shape the future.


To date, the Global Shapers Community has 11,193 Global Shapers active across 517 city hubs in 154 countries. In each city, GSC teams self-organize to launch initiatives tailored to their community's needs. Projects span a wide range, including disaster response, poverty alleviation, climate change action, and building inclusive communities.





"We are increasingly aware that addressing global challenges requires targeted engagement of youth at local, regional, national, and global levels. This generation possesses the passion, energy, and entrepreneurial spirit to shape the future."

—— Klaus Schwab, Founder of the World Economic Forum


Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub (GSC SZ) brings together a new generation of changemakers from diverse fields such as technology, finance, and the arts. We firmly believe that true youth development should go beyond self-improvement, transforming young people into pioneers riding the waves of change rather than silent observers. We are committed to leveraging technological power and community co-creation to advance the Sustainable Development Goals, actively engaging in social issues such as climate change, technological innovation, mental health, and women's opportunities.

Shenzhen Hub Project Introductions

01

AI Industry Frontier Bootcamp

The "AI Industry Frontier Bootcamp: A Beginner's Guide" is a free training program organized by the Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub, offering cutting-edge AI foundational training to tech enthusiasts with no prior computer science background.


To date, the introductory training course has gathered over 700 participants in the AIGC audience community. The project's free public lecture Bilibili channel has surpassed 1,000 views. The team has successfully hosted over 10 Chinese-language events covering AI applications in education, marketing, and Web3, with cumulative online participation exceeding 300. Additionally, we have partnered with multiple tech communities to organize offline events, attracting nearly 100 participants, providing audiences with opportunities to apply AI from theory to practice—receiving widespread acclaim.


02

Wild Voice Girls Observer

"Wild Voice Girls Observer" is a cross-sectoral dialogue and mutual support initiative for women, co-produced by the Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub and Lean In Shenzhen. Since its launch in 2022, the project has successfully released 21 online podcast episodes and hosted 5 offline events, focusing on topics such as workplace dynamics, law, climate change, gender safety, reproduction, neuroscience, and psychology. It has connected over 20 professionals from diverse industries and life stages, reaching 4,500 listeners and 3,500 readers.


Every exchange and sharing moment embodies Wild Voice's deep appreciation and support for women's voices, putting into practice the belief that "understanding" is not a slogan, but begins with small changes rooted in listening. "Wild Voice Girls Observer" welcomes more people to join, working together to create greater development opportunities for women and a more inclusive and friendly world!

03

DSE Social Enterprise Education

DSE (Decoding Social Enterprise) is a science communication project dedicated to spreading the value of social enterprises. Targeting high-impact groups in China, we use lightweight, engaging activity designs to decode the core concepts and practical models of social enterprises, helping more people understand, care about, and participate in social innovation.


To date, DSE has organized the following activities:

Social Enterprise Weekend Dinner | Sustainable Climate Tech Workshop | Plastic Reduction & Environmental Protection Social Enterprise Visits | China-U.S. Social Innovation Summit | Social Enterprise Education Briefings | Green Energy Social Enterprise Visits | Social Innovation Bootcamp Basecamp 


DSE is not just a project—it is a movement, an awakening, a community activating social innovation vitality, and a bold new attempt to make business and society dance together.


04

Wellness Wonders

Mental and Physical Wellness

Wellness Wonders is dedicated to creating diverse, accessible wellness communities by combining innovation with humanistic care. Through interactive workshops, community events, and recreational activities, we help participants relieve stress, build resilience, and cultivate sustainable healthy lifestyles.


From mindfulness meditation and self-awareness to healthy eating and outdoor activities, our diverse community events respond to contemporary challenges. Whether you are a working professional, student, parent, or elder, Wellness Wonders always believes: true health begins with self-awareness and self-care.


In the future, we will co-create more community activities centered on mental and physical wellness, exploring the infinite possibilities of holistic well-being and creating health miracles unique to each individual.


05

G.R.O.W. Growth Tree Program

G.R.O.W. stands for Growth, Resilience, Open-mindedness, and Well-being—the acronym forming the English word "GROW," meaning "growth." The program aims to help Chinese families understand and learn about social-emotional skills, promoting children's comprehensive physical and mental development.


Launched in 2021, the project has completed four cycles, benefiting over 100 families.


We hope this program provides children with fun, interactive learning experiences, builds effective and sustainable communication bridges between parents and children, and helps children grow strong, open-minded, and happy.

     Q&A More Information

Q1: How many rounds are there in this selection process?

There are two rounds in total. The first is the form application; the second is the interview. Those who pass the interview will enter the probation period and participate in hub activities.

Q2: Is there a fee to apply?

The GSC Hub does not charge any membership fees. Members come together with a spirit of public service and the shared belief in "Shaping the world," creating value for local communities and society.

Q3: Can I apply if I don't live or work in Shenzhen?

Considering the need for regular project participation, we prioritize applicants residing or working in Shenzhen or nearby cities.

Q4: Is the application bar high? What are my chances of being selected? Are students likely to be accepted?

We welcome applications from working professionals and students who meet the age requirement. We will screen based on actual circumstances, and selection criteria are not fixed by social status. We believe that as long as you take each step of the evaluation process seriously, you will gain something valuable.

Q5: If I am accepted but later unable to participate fully due to work or personal reasons, can I withdraw?

The GSC Hub is a global non-profit public service organization. Every successfully selected member commits to serving the community and driving change in their city. We do not require members to attend every single activity, but we expect each member to actively leverage their influence and contribute alongside fellow members. If you cannot guarantee consistent participation, please consider carefully.
