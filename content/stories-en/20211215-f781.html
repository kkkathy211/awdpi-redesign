Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project was invited to attend the Corporate Social Responsibility Conference hosted by the American Chamber of Commerce in Shanghai on December 1 at The Peninsula Shanghai. The event aimed to showcase the achievements of China's public welfare sector to the international community and create opportunities for nonprofit organizations to exchange and learn from one another, exploring collaborative and mutually reinforcing partnership models.



The conference centered on two main themes: "Maximizing the Potential of Corporate Social Responsibility" and "Company-Driven Communication in China."


"Maximizing the Potential of Corporate Social Responsibility" was the theme of greatest interest to all participating nonprofit organizations. Through this theme, participants collectively discussed the importance of corporate social responsibility (CSR) in corporate culture building, empowering stakeholders, and amplifying impact, as well as how companies can strengthen collaboration with nonprofit organizations under current economic conditions to better fulfill their social responsibilities.


Volunteers Zheng Anni and Ji Jialu represented Avoice at the event, promoting and introducing the project's overall status, latest developments, and future implementation plans to other organizations. Several organizations gave positive feedback on the Avoice project and expressed interest in further communication and collaboration.



Through this event, Avoice not only connected with organizations deeply rooted in the public welfare sector but also gained many insightful discoveries through exchanges with them.


"From the Mountains" showcased a wide array of traditional cultural handicrafts at their exhibition booth. This project establishes cultural and artistic clubs in primary and secondary schools in ethnic minority regions, teaching children local songs, dances, musical instruments, crafts, and minority languages. It aims to revive children's ethnic memories, enhance cultural confidence, and cultivate them as inheritors of their ethnic cultures.


"EnPact" introduced the "Resilient Youth" theme of their WEable Youth Innovation Project, sharing how to help youth initiatives find creative ideas and new starting points. From this, we also identified potential opportunities for future collaboration with EnPact on volunteer empowerment.


"Nature for Good" presented a video simulating a nature-inspired art exhibition, with exquisitely designed and highly aesthetic scene renderings that attracted significant attention.


"Junior Achievement" team members already knew Avoice's representatives—beyond marveling at the serendipitous connection, they enthusiastically took photos with us and particularly praised Avoice's project philosophy, expressing strong interest in future collaborative engagement.


Through this event, Avoice connected with numerous like-minded public welfare partners, sharing practical experiences, feedback, suggestions, reflections, and lessons learned in an open and inclusive atmosphere. We believe that on the journey ahead in public welfare, Avoice will continue to advance hand in hand with more nonprofit partners, mutually inspiring and growing together.

The "Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project" initiated by Avoice is dedicated to helping overseas Chinese women who suffer from domestic violence and improving the living conditions of vulnerable women abroad, building a bridge between women in need and various resources, becoming their solid support and warm shelter..

We emphasize self-empowerment of the target female population, while also providing multi-dimensional support, including language, psychological, legal, medical, shelter, channels, education, economic assistance, storytelling and amplifying voices, and community support—expanding the breadth of assistance while fostering a virtuous cycle of mutual support.
