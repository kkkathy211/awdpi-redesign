Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program) provides comprehensive and ongoing protection and support to overseas Chinese women facing domestic violence, including language support, psychological support, legal support, community support, overseas assistance network support, and storytelling and advocacy support, among others. Currently, the project focuses on Europe and North America, while expanding into Southeast Asia, Australia, and other regions, committed to building a bridge between women in need and available resources, serving as a solid backing and warm shelter—Avoice has already provided assistance to nearly 200 women and established a mutual support community of over 500 Chinese women.

Overseas Chinese women facing domestic violence in foreign countries often encounter multiple pressures, including restrictions related to nationality and immigration status, language and cultural barriers, lack of understanding of local laws, prejudice due to their foreign status, and being far from family and friends back home, making the path to escaping domestic violence difficult and arduous.


The Avoice project opens warm and steadfast arms to every overseas Chinese woman who has suffered domestic violence, ready to face hardship and darkness together with her.

If you are overseas and currently experiencing or suffering from domestic violence, please hear us say: It is not your fault—you should not blame yourself; domestic violence is not a private family matter—please bravely seek help from nonprofit organizations!


Avoice longs to meet you, embrace you, understand you, and listen to your story. No matter your identity or situation, we will do our best to provide solutions across all areas and walk beside you as you step out of the shadows into the light. Please believe—we are always here!






To request help or apply to join the Overseas Chinese Women Mutual Support Community, please contact:




Multilingual coverage: English, French, German, Russian, Dutch, Czech, Portuguese, Italian, and more

Translation and communication: Interpretation and translation services, assistance in collecting and submitting application materials

Sign language services: Online sign language services for the hearing-impaired, assisting with communication, information collection, and material submission


Psychological counseling: Customized emotional support and counseling, including psychological guidance for perpetrators

Psychological courses: Development of psychological courses and other forms of psychological learning materials


Legal information: Compilation and translation of domestic violence laws across countries, establishment of a Chinese-language legal database, and production of Chinese-language legal education handbooks

Legal advice: Case analysis for beneficiaries, provision of legal recommendations

Legal outreach: Provision of local legal network information, assistance in contacting legal institutions and aid lawyers, and support in material submission and follow-up communication


Women's mutual support community: Building a mutual support community for Chinese women, expanding social networks, and promoting peer support

Online exchange and learning: Hosting online exchange and learning activities, including domestic violence prevention, self-protection, legal literacy, group psychological counseling, and gender awareness enhancement


Country-specific assistance database: Establishing a Chinese-language assistance database for multiple countries, including detailed information on shelters, medical care, legal aid, psychological support, and financial assistance providers and experts

Information and referral: Providing suitable institutional information, assisting in contacting relevant organizations, submitting materials, and follow-up communication. Collaborating with select institutions and experts in the assistance database to offer beneficiaries fast and effective resource connections


Domestic violence survivors: Listening to survivors' experiences, documenting their true feelings, and sharing their methods and experiences in escaping domestic violence

Anti-domestic violence practitioners: Understanding domestic violence response models and related systems, sharing platforms and information

Other related groups: Interviewing other groups connected to domestic violence survivors (children, family, friends, colleagues, etc.) to present a comprehensive picture of domestic violence incidents and their impacts from multiple perspectives
