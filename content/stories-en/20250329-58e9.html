Recruitment Program

2025 RECRUIT 


AWDPI's current spring recruitment includes 12 departments and 130 positions available, most of which can be conducted online, while a few are open for in-person participation.

Working hours: At least 7 hours per week, flexible and subject to coordination based on departmental needs and personal availability.

Duration of service: Team leaders must commit to serving at AWDPI for at least 1 year, and team members must commit to serving at AWDPI for at least 6 months..

Finance Department

Department Introduction:

The Finance Department is a key back-office unit of AWDPI, primarily responsible for organizational budgeting, fundraising management, and financial reporting. By joining the AWDPI Finance Department, you will have the opportunity to participate in building financial systems for an international NGO and contribute to the organization's strategic success from a financial perspective.

Legal Affairs Department

Department Introduction:

The Legal Affairs Department oversees governance for AWDPI headquarters and its national offices (regional branches), participates in formulating and supervising the implementation of organizational policies, manages legal risks for the organization and its operations, and provides daily legal support including contract drafting and review, legal research, and more. Additionally, the Legal Affairs Department coordinates with external legal counsel.

Communications Department

Department Introduction:

The Communications Department amplifies voices on the survival and development challenges faced by Asian women. Through warm brand design, it expresses women's strength and supports vulnerable women in speaking out via coordinated social media and website platforms. It also increases visibility for expert interviews, academic research reports, psychological and rescue-related public education, and gender-informed perspectives.

         Project Quality Department

        Department Introduction:

        The Project Quality Department comprises five sub-departments: Academic Research Division, Project Design Division, Monitoring and Evaluation Division, IT Information Division, and Curriculum Development Division. The IT Information Division is responsible for website design, development, testing, and maintenance, as well as daily IT support work in collaboration with other internal departments. The Academic Research Division plans and conducts academic research on topics related to gender equality, anti-domestic violence, women's protection and empowerment, writes research reports and popular science articles, and provides recommendations and strategic guidance for institutional strategy and project design. The Curriculum Development Division focuses on developing anti-domestic violence courses, designing art therapy workshops, and establishing anonymous support groups. We hope to combine content and technology to create more practical and accessible forms of education and support, helping more people access assistance. We welcome partners with relevant backgrounds to join us in exploring more effective ways to support trauma survivors.
