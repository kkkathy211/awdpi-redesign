The United Nations' theme for International Women's Day 2024 is "Invest in Women: Accelerate Progress." By 2030, the annual funding gap for gender equality initiatives could reach as high as 360 billion US dollars, making it urgent to advance financing for gender equality.


During the United Nations 2023 International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women "16 Days of Activism," we hosted five online workshops, discussing topics such as "Occupational Diseases from a Gender Perspective" with over 300 audience members. The 2024 International Women's Day theme continues the message of the 2023 International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women: "Eliminate gender-based violence through adequate funding." Without substantial investment to address violence against women and girls, scale up prevention programs, and implement effective policies, countries will not be able to eliminate gender-based violence by 2030.


This year, AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International will present four workshops spanning China, Germany, and Ecuador during International Women's Day, joining people across countries who care about gender issues in expressing support for survivors of gender-based violence.

A key message that comprehensive sexuality education hopes to deliver is gender equality. Beyond literally teaching children "what gender equality is" and "why gender equality matters," it also includes information that helps individuals develop a gender-equality consciousness, acquire knowledge about gender equality, and build the skills to act in gender-equal ways. In this session, we will explore how sexuality education can further support the realization of gender equality beyond standard "gender equality" curricula.
