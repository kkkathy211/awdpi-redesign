AWDPI Survey Invitation | Health Study for Chinese Women in the U.S. (Honorarium Included)

As Chinese women, our experiences and needs are shaped by unique cultural backgrounds, yet our voices are often underrepresented in existing health research.

Your feelings matter, and your health deserves serious attention. Your feelings matter, and your health deserves serious attention.

To address this gap, the School of Nursing at The University of Texas at Austin is conducting a health study specifically focused on Chinese women in the United States. We hope to hear your authentic experiences and use scientific data and resources to provide better support for this community.

We sincerely invite you, if eligible, to join this study. Your participation is not only an act of care for yourself, but also a way to speak up for more Chinese women living in the U.S.
