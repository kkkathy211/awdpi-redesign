Avoice "Her Confidante"

is a column that interacts with readers' letters.

If you want to share moments of female strength,

if you want to voice the pains of women's growth,

if you have questions you'd like to consult about or discuss,

if you have experiences you'd like to offer,

……

we are always looking forward to hearing from you!


Through letter exchanges and sharing,

we hope more women can bravely face and re-examine their past,

we hope to gather women's wisdom through the form of letters,

and bring together a stronger collective female power.


Our email address is

[email protected]


We will select relatively suitable letters

to respond to in the Avoice "Her Confidante" column.

We may also invite professional counselors, lawyers, and other experts,

or volunteer individuals with similar experiences, to respond.


If you do not wish to disclose your real identity,

please be sure to use a pseudonym in your letter.

If you do not wish your letter to be published,

please also indicate this in your letter.


We look forward to meeting you in "Her Confidante"

where healing begins.

🤎
