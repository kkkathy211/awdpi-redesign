Domestic Violence and Women's Self-Protection: A Legal and Psychological Perspective


During Women's History Month this March, Her Perspective (WO) and Avoice the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project co-hosted a "Her Talk" sharing session themed "Love Without Violence—Domestic Violence and Women's Self-Protection." In the first session, we invited project leaders to share the organization's origins, the situation of domestic violence after the pandemic, and a self-help guide for overseas Chinese women facing domestic violence. We also heard from beneficiaries and young volunteers.

Building on this, we have recently prepared a second event in collaboration with Avoice, inviting several guests from the organization who have strong theoretical knowledge and practical experience in law and psychology regarding domestic violence and women's self-protection, to offer more professional and in-depth insights. The specific event time and guest introductions are shown on the poster—sisters interested are warmly welcome to actively participate and join us!


About Avoice


A long-term support project primarily serving overseas Chinese women. It provides comprehensive and continuous assistance to overseas Chinese women suffering from domestic violence, and works to raise public awareness of the living challenges faced by these women by building partnerships with overseas Chinese organizations, women's aid agencies, academic institutions, and government bodies. The project aims to create a safe and equal environment for survival and development for overseas Chinese women at risk of domestic violence.


About Her Perspective (WO)


An online community established for women around the world. Our slogan is "Explore the World, Become a Better Self." In addition to a women-only forum, we are active across multiple social media platforms, including WeChat Official Account, Xiaohongshu, podcast, and Weibo.
