About Us


PeriodPride is a social innovation organization focused on "menstrual health and hygiene," committed to ensuring that all menstrual experiences are safe, comfortable, and dignified through academic research, on-the-ground action, and advocacy communication.


Over the past four years, PeriodPride has:


  • spearheaded the "Call for Tax Exemption or Reduction on Menstrual Products" campaign, which received over 35 million responses.


  • Continuously run public welfare projects such as the "Child-Friendly Toilet Initiative", and emergency menstrual product donations during disasters. and other public welfare initiatives.


  • Been selected as an "Outstanding Case" in the 2021 issue of the Stanford Social Innovation Review, "Outstanding Case";


  • and had its co-founder recognized as a 2024 World Bank "Outstanding Women of East Asia and the Pacific." "

🎖️ We will provide you with opportunities: PeriodPride will connect you to recommended opportunities, including but not limited to speaking at forums and conferences, theoretical and skills training, internal job referrals, recommendation letters for further studies, and resource networking;

—What is worth doing for 48 years?


Some say it takes at least 48 years to achieve a social transformation.


For PeriodPride, gender equality is the change we hope to see and the vision we strive for—even if it takes longer than 48 years.


We hope that by then, our granddaughters and grandsons will find "menstrual poverty" and "period shame" utterly unimaginable—then we will know our mission is complete.


Of course, it's highly unlikely you'll actually volunteer with PeriodPride for 48 years (though if you do, that would be wonderful 😛).


But we firmly believe that each of you who crosses paths with us will move forward at your own pace, cultivating change in different fields. Together, our efforts will connect the dots into lines, drawing a vast star map of gender equality, good health and well-being, quality education, and a world free from poverty.


If you're willing, let today be the first day of our "half-century promise."
