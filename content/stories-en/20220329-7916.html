Event invitation


This event is hosted by Scotland HSTAR and will be held offline in the UK., Scotland HSTAR specially invites Avoice to participate in this event as a partner organization. Everyone is welcome to sign up!

Event introduction


Scotland HSTAR is an organization that supports women and helps them overcome trauma. HSTAR believes everyone has a story to share, and will therefore host a free storytelling gathering for women and related organizations in April—" Storytelling workshop journey of creativity for women ". HST Avoice hopes this storytelling event will inspire your creativity and help you discover the potential to share the story in your heart! This will be a friendly, fun, women-only gathering. You don't need to worry or fear sharing your story—you only need to sincerely share your story with the friends present..


This meeting will be co-organized by the Scottish Storytelling Association and talented, passionate women like you. After the event, participants will have the opportunity to enjoy a delicious meal,, build new friendships,, break down social barriers, and after dinner, we will move into the highly interactive part of the event— the OPEN MIC session will provide everyone with a great opportunity to share your own story and listen to others' anecdotes..  


Through this storytelling gathering, we hope to strengthen collaboration with all outstanding women and spark a wave of gender equality within their communities and around the world.
