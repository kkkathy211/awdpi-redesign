Conference Introduction

In the tide of our times, every moment of change carries infinite possibilities for the future. We have witnessed the revitalization and restructuring of the global economy in the post-pandemic era, while also deeply feeling the many challenges brought by geopolitical tensions. At this intersection of a "once-in-a-century transformation," China plays an indispensable and important role on the international stage, whether in economic, technological innovation, or international relations.

On February 17–18, 2024, we will co-host the "16th LSE China Development Forum" with Taihe Institute.

This year's forum will bring together over 25 renowned scholars and experts from various industries around the globe to jointly launch seven sub-forum topics. At the same time, we will provide participants with opportunities for close interaction and communication with speakers and offer young audiences exclusive career guidance lectures..

We will deeply explore multiple critical issues under a global perspective, including China's cultural discourse power, China's economy, space politics and cooperation, China-Middle East relations, China's global leadership and environmental governance, monetary economics, and technology and AI development, presenting attendees with a grand event combining academic depth and current affairs awareness.

Organizers

Since its founding in 2009, the LSE China Development Forum (hereinafter referred to as "the Forum") has become one of the most influential China-focused forums in Europe. We have always adhered to the original mission of building a bridge for communication between China and the world, and are committed to enhancing mutual understanding and cooperation across cultures. Our mission is to provide a dynamic and in-depth platform for intellectual exchange for global scholars, students, and professionals focused on China, while offering participants valuable learning and interaction opportunities to build connections with global experts and future leaders. system .  


Co-organizer

Taihe Institute, established in Beijing in 2013, is a social think tank with the mission of "promoting mutual learning among civilizations and supporting peaceful development." Upholding the action philosophy of "responding to the needs of the times," it is committed to producing high-quality intellectual outputs, providing policy recommendations for China's development, and offering intellectual support for exchanges between China and the world. Taihe Institute currently has 161 senior researchers and researchers focusing on international relations and China's domestic socio-economic development, producing over 300 research reports annually.
