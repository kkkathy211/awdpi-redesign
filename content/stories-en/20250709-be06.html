Abstract



This lecture is hosted by AWDPI and brings together three female speakers from the fields of tech business, sex education, and venture investment. They will share insights based on their personal experiences on topics including women's soft power development, work-life and parenting education, financing challenges, and multi-identity transitions. These discussions will cover key issues facing women today.


This is a conversation about how women grow, make choices, speak up, and create possibilities in the real world, and also a practical sharing on how contemporary women can break stereotypes and forge their own paths. Whether you are just starting your career, juggling multiple roles, or exploring your way in humanities, education, or entrepreneurship as a "she," you will find resonance and inspiration here.

Lecture Highlights


Contemporary Women's Multiple Challenges

In a rapidly changing society, women face increasingly complex role expectations—they must strive in the workplace, participate in family education, and continuously speak up and break through in fields such as technology and entrepreneurship. Women's empowerment is not only about enhancing individual capabilities, but also a crucial force driving structural social transformation.


From Individual Rhythm to the Direction of the Times

In this ongoing empowerment movement, women's voices are being heard more than ever, yet they still face structural barriers and real-world biases. Amid a shifting social rhythm, how can women define their own pace and direction? How can they proactively break boundaries and confront prejudice in entrepreneurship and self-expression?


Connecting with Her, Speaking Together

This is not only about personal growth, but also about how a generation of women collectively expands possibilities in public spaces. We hope this dialogue will connect more like-minded individuals, empower one another, and jointly reflect on: Today, how can we not only secure space for women, but also ensure their voices become a driving force for societal progress?




Let us carry these questions into our thinking and listen to the insights shared by three female speakers from different fields:

• The practical construction of women's soft power and career transition

• Parenting education and intergenerational communication for working mothers

• Real-world challenges faced by female entrepreneurs in fundraising and resource building


Speaker Introductions and Lecture Topics


Speaker One:

Xie Qiqi (Spencer)

Chief Marketing Officer (CMO), Pixie Travel

Project Consultant, Global Climate Innovation Center (GCIC)

Former Head of Commercial Operations, LinkAI


Lecture Topic:

"Women's development cannot be just talk: The necessity of soft power in women's development"

Lecture Content Overview:

This session explores how to break through gender stereotypes, focusing on soft skills and practical abilities that can change the current workplace landscape, enhance women's influence and voice in reality, and offers concrete suggestions for developing women's soft power.


Speaker Two:

Yang Wenxue

Partner, Dao Capital Advisory

Founder, InFound Venture Club


Lecture Topic:

"Challenges faced by female entrepreneurs in the fundraising environment"

Lecture Content Overview:

This talk addresses the three main doubts women face in investment, the current lack of resources in women's fundraising, and the importance of building social resource networks for women.


Speaker Three:

Deng Fangting

First cohort certified sex education trainer, United Nations CGF;

Senior Empowerment-based Sex Education Instructor;

Intermediate Elder Care Provider;

Co-author of *The Things About Adolescence*,

*Sex Education for Girls in Adolescence*,

and *Sex Education for Boys in Adolescence*


Lecture Topic:

" "How working mothers can provide sex education for their children"

Lecture Content Overview:

Drawing on Ms. Deng's personal experiences with her daughter and several counseling cases, this session will explain how working mothers can effectively carry out sex education for their children.
