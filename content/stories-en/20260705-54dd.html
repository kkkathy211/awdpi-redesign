Cambridge University is a world-renowned academic hub, home to a large number of Chinese international students, scholars, and young professionals from China and around the world.


It offers an inspiring academic atmosphere, abundant knowledge resources, and an open international environment, yet many also experience real pressures in overseas life: intense study and research schedules, loneliness from being far away from family and familiar communities, unfamiliar support systems, and the constant expectation to be "excellent."


Therefore, we are pleased to announce:


The official establishment of the AWDPI Cambridge Chapter.



We hope to bring AWDPI's support systems, resource networks, and community strength to Cambridge, so that beyond achievements and opportunities, there can also be more understanding, connection, and companionship.


Life overseas involves more than just opportunities and freedom.


When we talk about overseas life, people often think of opportunities, freedom, and a broader world.


But for many Asian women living, studying, and working abroad, reality is not always so easy.


They make contributions, pursue passions, and hold ideals in fields such as academia, business, technology, and the arts, while simultaneously navigating cultural differences, identity-based biases, and language barriers in a foreign land.


When discrimination, harassment, violence, or other psychological difficulties occur, seeking help is often much harder than imagined.


Sometimes, it's not knowing who to turn to;

Sometimes, it's fear of being misunderstood;

Sometimes, it's concern that language, identity, or the support system itself might bring new risks.


AWDPI wants to change all of this.


AWDPI's predecessor: Avoice


AWDPI, full name Asian Women Development Plan International, Chinese name Yafu Nüxing Guoji Fazhan Jihua (Asian Women Development Plan International).


AWDPI originated from Avoice, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women, also known as the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project.


From initially providing emergency aid to overseas Chinese women facing domestic violence, to today's mission of advancing the safety, development, and empowerment of Asian women worldwide, AWDPI has consistently focused on the real-life challenges faced by Asian women abroad, striving to offer more choices and possibilities through professional support, resource connections, and community strength.




What is AWDPI?


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, and promoting their capacity building and development.

Our Mission

To eliminate violence and discrimination against Asian women, and to promote the protection and development of Asian women

Our Vision

To create a world of love and equality for women

Our Values

Participation, Inclusion, Empowerment


We hope to build, through these three core values,


Engaging, Embracing, Empowering


a world that is more equal, safer, and more willing to listen to women's voices.


Today, AWDPI has established service networks in multiple regions globally:



North America – registered charity in the United States;


Europe – registered charities in the United Kingdom and Belgium;


China – daily operations center;


Australia and Southeast Asia – local teams in Thailand.

What are we doing?


2026 marks AWDPI's sixth year of operation.


We respond to the real needs of women in overseas life through four major programs that address their lived experiences.



Protection of Overseas Asian Women

Providing legal aid, psychological support, and language assistance in response to discrimination and violence in families, schools, and workplaces.



Violence-Free Spaces

Establishing women's mutual support groups and transnational community networks, so that more women know someone is willing to listen when they feel lonely, helpless, or unsure of how to seek help.



Women's Leadership Program

Through international conferences, leadership development programs, and career advancement support, helping more women discover their potential, build confidence, and gain greater agency in society and the workplace.



Action Against Gender-Based Violence

Partnering with the United Nations, government agencies, academic institutions, and media to advocate and conduct research, striving to bring greater global attention to the safety and development of Asian women.


In recent years, we have:

assisted 310 overseas Chinese women who experienced domestic or gender-based violence;

provided services in 9 languages and sign languages, translated over 40 academic articles;

been covered by 550 international media outlets, reaching over 5 million people;

produced 40 research reports on gender-based violence;

built a volunteer network of 1,106 individuals.


AWDPI UK: What We've Done This Year


The establishment of the Cambridge chapter does not start from scratch.


Over the past year, AWDPI UK has been working to bring women's issues into more real-life community settings, enabling more Chinese and Asian women in the UK to see and connect with one another through events, discussions, and public expression.



How Chinese female international students present themselves on dating apps


We invited a sociology of education PhD candidate from Durham University to share insights on the real-life situations of Chinese female international students regarding intimate relationships, dating apps, and cross-cultural aesthetics overseas.


In this online discussion, many participants began rethinking: after going abroad, have we entered another aesthetic system? Is the preference for "white, young, and slim" merely personal taste, or does it reflect deeper gendered discipline? How do Chinese female international students navigate intimacy, body anxiety, and identity?


It was not just an academic talk, but also a deeply sincere conversation among women.



International Women's Day Art Exhibition


On March 8 this year, International Women's Day, AWDPI collaborated with UK Beijing Elite Club (Jingying Hui) to promote a women's art exhibition and on-site collaborative painting workshop at the Affordable Art Fair Battersea.


We centered activities around women artists from diverse national backgrounds, hoping to make more female creators visible and allow audiences to experience connection and support among women through participation.



UK Chinese Women's Annual Awards Ceremony


AWDPI UK also provided promotional and content support for the annual awards ceremony organized by the UK Chinese Women's Community (UKCWC).


We hope to join hands with more community partners to celebrate Chinese women in the UK who continue to shine.




WeChat Column on Women's Issues


Online, we continue to transform academic research into accessible, discussable, and shareable content through our WeChat column on women's issues.


We hope to enable more people to engage in discussions about gender equality, women's realities, and social structures.



Charity Hiking Events


Offline, we also organize charity hiking events.


From London to surrounding towns, more and more members are joining us, getting to know each other through walking, and finding real friends beyond the screen.



Why establish a chapter in Cambridge?


Cambridge hosts a large number of Chinese international students and scholars from China and around the world.


It offers an enviable academic environment, but also very real pressures: the high-intensity academic setting, loneliness from being far from family, unfamiliar support systems, and the constant expectation to be "excellent"—all of which can be exhausting.


In such circumstances, proactively seeking help often becomes exceptionally difficult.


Therefore, we hope to bring AWDPI's support systems, resource networks, and community strength to Cambridge.


We envision the AWDPI Cambridge Chapter not just as a student club, but as a safe, warm, and action-oriented space.


Here, people can discuss women's growth, life overseas, gender equality, intimate relationships, identity, career development, and mental health support; and when feeling lonely or helpless, know they are not alone.


We hope to bring more understanding, connection, and companionship to Cambridge, alongside academic achievement.


Join Us


If you are looking for:


a Chinese-language community where you can express yourself safely;

an organization that will listen and support you when you are in difficulty;

a group of people who care about women's growth, gender equality, and social issues;


whether you are a student, researcher, or simply want to meet more like-minded friends, you are welcome to join the AWDPI Cambridge Chapter.


Starting today, AWDPI's support network will also be present in Cambridge.


May we meet in Cambridge, and may every overseas Asian woman be seen, understood, and supported when she needs it.
