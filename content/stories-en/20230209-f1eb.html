*Temple Grandin* (2010)

Director: Mick Jackson


Based on the autobiography of Dr. Temple Grandin, an American animal scientist and livestock expert who was diagnosed with autism from an early age (played by Claire Danes), the film tells the story of Temple's unique journey of growth.
