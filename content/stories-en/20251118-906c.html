When violence occurs in an intimate relationship,

silence is not your fault.


When you are overseas, facing language barriers, cultural differences, and even unsure where to begin seeking help—

please remember:

you are brave, you are strong, and you are not alone 🧡

On Saturday, November 29, 2025, AWDPI Australia (Asian Women Development Plan International Australia National Office) will make its first public appearance in Melbourne. The event theme is: "Protecting and Empowering Asian Women Against Violence"


Through this cross-sector public event,

we officially launch AWDPI

in Australia—

bringing international experience into the local context,

connecting local resources with those who truly need them,

and weaving multicultural communities closer together,


to co-create a safer, stronger, more welcoming, and culturally sensitive support network for Asian women in Australia.


This is a milestone event of great significance. On that day, we will bring together voices from diverse fields—representatives from government departments, law enforcement agencies and police officers, family law professionals, mental health and trauma support experts, domestic violence advocates, nonprofit specialists, and multicultural community organizations—who will participate and speak collectively, discussing how to more effectively support Asian women in Australia who are experiencing, at risk of, or seeking proactive protection from violence, from policy, legal, law enforcement, psychological, cultural, and other perspectives.


Event Highlights


⎡AWDPI Australia National Office (AWDPI Australia) Official Launch Ceremony⎦


This event marks the official debut of AWDPI Australia—

our systematic work in Australia begins here.


The event will feature several high-profile guests in attendance and speaking, including:

  • Federal and Victorian Members of Parliament

  • Members of the Victorian Multicultural Commission

  • Law enforcement representatives (police officers)

  • Family lawyers and legal professionals

  • Mental health and trauma support experts

  • Multicultural community leaders

These guests will share their congratulations and expectations for AWDPI Australia, and express their vision for collaboration with us on women's safety, policy coordination, and community support.


Multiple Australian media outlets and community partners will also participate, interview, and report on the event, helping to amplify its impact.


⎡AWDPI Australia 

Official Release of Domestic Violence Prevention Handbook⎦


This event will officially launch the English version of the practical guidebook against domestic violence, "You Are Not Alone: A Support Guide for Asian Women in Australia Experiencing Domestic Violence," compiled by the AWDPI Australia Legal Support Team.


Combining Australia's legal system, cross-cultural characteristics, and real-life help-seeking scenarios, the handbook covers domestic violence identification, state-specific laws, protection order applications, evidence collection, emergency assistance, visas and child protection, and how to safely support someone close to you and other core topics.


This handbook aims to provide clear, practical, and culturally accessible safety guidance for Asian women in Australia.


Printed copies will be available for free to all in-person attendees!



⎡Cross-Sector Domestic Violence Roundtable Forum⎦


The roundtable forum will bring together police officers, family lawyers, multicultural community leaders, and NGO representatives to discuss the critical issue of "cross-cultural domestic violence."


The discussion will be grounded in the real-life experiences of Asian women in Australia and will focus on three core themes:

  • Misconceptions and the legal definition of domestic violence in cross-cultural contexts

  • Survivor protection policies and pathways to help

  • Community support and future directions for collaboration


The forum will examine these issues from legal, law enforcement, cultural difference, and community practice perspectives, jointly exploring how to provide safer, more accessible, and culturally sensitive support for Asian women in Australia.


⎡A relaxed and warm social atmosphere⎦


Let resources truly flow!


At the end of the event, we will provide light refreshments to create a relaxed, open, and opportunity-rich space for all attendees to connect.


On site, you can:

  • engage in face-to-face conversations with speakers and leaders from various fields

  • meet more partners committed to Asian women's safety and empowerment

  • share experiences, exchange resources, and explore collaboration opportunities

This is a rare moment—

when people from different fields and backgrounds, united by the same mission,

truly connect,

to build a stronger, more action-oriented support network

for the safety and future of Asian women.
