#Hear Her, Heal Her#

Call for Online Participants: Mobile Letterbox Campaign


In our past interviews, survivors of domestic violence often shared stories like this:

"When I was trapped in severe depression and attempting to end my life, my sister invited me to travel to Australia. That trip became a turning point in my life."

"After three years of being emotionally manipulated (PUA), I always believed I was worthless. But one day, an old classmate commented on my social media, saying they envied my hair. I realized I had something valuable after all!"


Sometimes, the darkness brought by past experiences is heavy and long-lasting, silently spreading, everywhere. Yet around the shadows, small rays of light always manage to seep through—bringing warmth, giving strength, bit by bit, converging into beams that awaken the long-dormant joy in her heart.



Do you also want to become part of that beam

of light that might illuminate their path?



For this reason, we have launched the#Hear Her, Heal Her# The Mobile Mailbox activity, in the form of "collecting cards and delivering warmth," sends warmth to women who have suffered domestic violence..



At the same time, we are opening an online collection phase. You may handwrite a message of care on a postcard to a female friend who has suffered violence, or share a story from your own life.


(Handwritten example above)


Specific participation methods are as follows:

 

Participation period:

From now until December 15


Participation format:

- Handwrite a message of care or share a personal story, take a photo, and send it via email to[email protected]. The email address will have an auto-reply—please confirm it is correct!

- Or send it directly to our official account by private message.

Note: The language of the handwritten content is not limited, but please indicate the language type in the email or private message!

 

Important notes:

Ø  Email subject format: Hug Card + Personal ID + Language Type

Ø  The card must be handwritten—let warmth be transmitted from the tip of your pen!

Ø  Scans or photos must be clear! Don't let the lens blur your expression.

Ø  While helping others, don't forget to protect yourself! Please do not disclose personal information on the card.

Ø  Please note: Do not include sharp, personally attacking, or inappropriate remarks!

The "Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project" aims to assist overseas Chinese women experiencing domestic violence and improve the living conditions of vulnerable groups of women abroad, building a bridge between women seeking help and various resources, becoming their solid support and warm shelter..

We emphasize self-empowerment for the target group of women, while also providing multi-dimensional support, covering language, psychological, legal, medical, shelter, channels, education, economic assistance, storytelling and amplifying voices, and community support—expanding the breadth of aid while creating a virtuous cycle of mutual support.

If you or someone you know has any of the following needs:

1. Requires relevant consultation and support

2. Wishes to receive Avoice storytelling and amplification support

3. Wants to join the Overseas Chinese Women Protection Community

4. Wants to collaborate or provide resources for the project
