When life is overshadowed by gender-based violence, how can their voices be heard? How can their trauma be healed? Over five years of dedicated work, AWDPI has delivered an answer written in "professional support, continuous empowerment, and warm inclusivity." This is not our monologue, but a chorus of voices from countless survivors and supporters. They once stood in darkness, yet still choose to hold umbrellas for those still caught in the rain.


"AWDPI was the umbrella-bearer who walked with me through that journey."

"AWDPI helped me understand not to blame myself, and to accept myself in any state."

"AWDPI helped me broaden my horizons and further reflect on anti-violence practices."

"Here, I am a whole person, not a victim number."

"Keep trying to reach out for help—never give up, even if your first attempt fails!"



Over the past five years, AWDPI has reached over 30 countries and regions worldwide, guided by the mission of "participation, inclusion, and empowerment," building a global support network that integrates legal assistance, psychological counseling, and community connection into a three-in-one rescue framework.


Over the past five years,

we have cumulatively served over 1,000 survivors of gender-based violence,

facilitated the publication of 40 cross-national research reports,

and partnered with the United Nations, government agencies, and academic organizations

to build a united front against violence.


Let courage become a choice for more people!

If you or someone you know needs help,

please remember: You are not alone..

Follow us, and together let's protect more overseas Asian women.


We lean in to listen, quietly walking alongside,

and these voices and supports

weave into safe harbors that never abandon.

We honor every woman who has stepped out of the shadow of violence,

lighting sparks with courage and resilience.

Let us move forward with firmer steps,

to create a world of love and equality for women.
