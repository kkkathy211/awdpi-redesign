  Organization Introduction

Clear Harbor Healing and Empowerment (CHHE) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to mental health, cultural adaptation, and social integration for Chinese communities in the United States. We believe true support does not only happen in therapy rooms, but also in every moment when someone living abroad—facing cultural differences, identity exploration, career decisions, and struggles with belonging—is genuinely heard and understood. Therefore, we are committed to providing holistic well-being support for Chinese international students and new immigrants in the U.S., helping more individuals not only settle into life abroad, but gradually build a life that truly belongs to them.


In just over a year since our founding, the CHHE team has grown from 0 to nearly 40 members, with our various activities collectively reaching over 10,000 participants.

Why Join Us


   01 International Student and Career Development Support

  • For international students who meet relevant eligibility and institutional requirements, we can provide necessary employment or internship documentation for CPT/OPT applications. Specific qualifications, application procedures, and work authorization are subject to the regulations of your school's international student office and U.S. immigration laws.


  • Clinical Services Department (paid roles): For employees eligible for STEM OPT, if the position and organizational conditions meet relevant requirements, we may assist in completing required employment documentation and procedures. Eligibility for STEM OPT extension is determined by individual degree, job nature, and applicable immigration regulations. For employees who may meet job requirements and legal conditions in the future, the organization may assess the feasibility of long-term employment and H-1B sponsorship based on actual circumstances; this does not constitute a guarantee or commitment of approval.


  • Volunteer roles in other departments: For core members who participate long-term and meet eligibility criteria, the organization may assess the possibility of future legal employment and immigration support based on actual position needs and individual circumstances; this does not constitute any commitment regarding immigration status or visa outcomes.

  Department Overview

Six departments are now launching new recruitment—there's a place for you.

The following departments are recruiting both volunteers and Department Co-Leads.

Clinical Services Department· Part-time Therapist | ((Paid position)

Main Responsibilities:

 ● Provide initial assessments and individual/couples/group counseling to clients

 ● Participate in case discussions; licensed clinicians with appropriate supervision qualifications and meeting organizational requirements may take on clinical supervision duties as needed.


Requirements:

● Hold a valid license to legally provide mental health services in Massachusetts (e.g., LICSW, LMHC, LMFT, or licensed psychologist), or meet qualifications to provide services under qualified supervision (e.g., LSW, LCSW, or postdoctoral candidates).

● Willingness and ability to work with clients from diverse cultural backgrounds; cross-cultural sensitivity is essential.


Compensation and Status Support:

● Part-time, fee-split compensation model; specific percentage determined through discussion based on license level and clinical experience

● Supports CPT, one-year OPT, and three-year STEM OPT extension; strong performers may be considered for H-1B sponsorship


Volunteer / Department Co-Lead

We never want a person's professional background to define what they can contribute to the community.


Many of CHHE's stories began with a simple act of kindness.

Many of our current team members who now independently lead projects, support teams, and drive departmental growth first joined with just a simple wish:


To help those living in unfamiliar environments feel a little less lonely and a little more understood.


We believe many professional skills can be learned through practice.


But care for the community, a serious attitude toward work, and a genuine willingness to understand others' experiences often matter more than a perfect resume.


Therefore, volunteer roles have no professional background requirements.
