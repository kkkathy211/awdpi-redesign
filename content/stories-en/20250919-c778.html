We are pleased to announce:

AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) has officially joined Harvard Innovation Labs (i-lab)!



as a member of i-lab's Social Impact Cohort (social impact track), AWDPI will, under the leadership of its founder, Yimar Yu of fully leverage the diverse resources of Harvard's innovation ecosystem to further advance its international public welfare mission of "anti-violence, anti-discrimination, and women's empowerment." AWDPI will exchange ideas and collaborate with innovative teams from around the world to explore sustainable solutions to social challenges and expand our global impact.


About i-lab


Harvard Innovation Labs (Harvard Innovation Laboratory) is Harvard University's official center for entrepreneurship and innovation, bringing together student and alumni teams from across the university's schools and departments, spanning fields from technology to social innovation. i-lab It provides top-tier resource support, a collaborative ecosystem, and global network connections to the world's most promising social impact initiatives.



Here, members can access:

  • mentorship and expert resources,

  • interdisciplinary innovation exchanges,

  • 24/7 open shared workspaces and facility support,

  • funding and support for entrepreneurial and social impact projects, and

  • a rich array of workshops, forums, and global resource networks.


As a new member of i-lab, AWDPI will participate in a series Orientation of activities, engage in individual discussions with assigned senior advisors, and gradually take part in more resources and exchanges related to the social impact track.



About AWDPI


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas and promoting their empowerment and development. It evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), founded in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in the UK, Belgium, the United States, Thailand, and Australia. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with stakeholders including United Nations system organizations, relevant government agencies, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the struggles faced by Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more development opportunities for Asian women overseas in social, economic, cultural, and educational spheres, helping them transform from marginalized groups into a vital force driving social progress.



Future Outlook


Joining Harvard Innovation Labs is not only recognition of AWDPI's past work but also signifies new growth and opportunities ahead.

We will seize this opportunity to:

  • connect with more international resources,

  • accelerate the implementation of public welfare innovation projects,

  • expand our global impact network,

  • amplify voices for more women, and advance equality and intersectional justice.



AWDPI will continue to stay true to our mission, boldly explore new paths, and ensure more women's voices are seen and heard on the global stage.


Please stay tuned and join us in witnessing the birth of more possibilities!
