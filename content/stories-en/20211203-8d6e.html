Who We Are

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project Committed to eliminating gender-based violence and advancing gender equality, the project provides timely and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence.


The project originated from an international research study conducted during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, when socially engaged Chinese international students discovered that the pandemic had intensified the risk of domestic violence against overseas Chinese women. Taking proactive action, they established a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, mobilizing broader youth-led public welfare forces to raise public awareness and support for Chinese women trapped in domestic violence. The project aims to help these women transform from victims into advocates and practitioners of gender equality.


Since October 2021, the project has been supported by the Shanghai Overseas Chinese Public Welfare Development Foundation which provides fundraising qualifications, funding, project management, and comprehensive public welfare support, working together to build a "home of love and equality for overseas Chinese women."


The project launched its pilot in Belgium, aiming to establish a broad and effective domestic violence response network for Chinese women. To date, the pilot project in Belgium has been successfully completed, and the model has been extended to all European countries and selected countries in North America. Over the next three years, the project will expand primarily into North America, Australia, and Southeast Asia.


The project's short name, "Avoice," is derived from Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program (the English name of the Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project), symbolizing the transmission of development aspirations through women's voices, calling for gender equality through youth voices, openly declaring humanitarian values, and resolutely singing the enduring song of mission. It strives to amplify countless public welfare voices into the powerful global chorus advancing the Sustainable Development Goal of gender equality!

Recruitment Positions

All positions listed below are remote, with no geographical restrictions.

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project

Case Management Volunteers (5 positions)

Case Management Division

Respond to domestic violence survivors' requests for help, coordinate relevant departments, connect with assistance resources, and provide full-cycle case management and follow-up support..


Position Overview

  • Participate in developing case management procedures;

  • Conduct case intake and record beneficiary information;

  • Form case response teams;

  • Coordinate and mobilize relevant resources based on beneficiaries' needs, and connect with internal and external project departments, including but not limited to legal, psychological, medical, language, and emergency shelter services;

  • Provide follow-up support after case closure.


Requirements

1. Priority given to candidates majoring in sociology, psychology, or social work;

2. Priority given to those with work or volunteer experience related to anti-domestic violence, especially in case management;

3. Basic proficiency in English (speaking, listening, reading, and writing); candidates proficient in other minor languages are preferred;

4. Strong team spirit, organizational and coordination skills, communication skills, and time management abilities;

5. Diligent, responsible, and patient;

6. Familiarity with, or willingness to learn about, the basic conditions in Europe, North America, Australia, and Southeast Asia.


Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project

Community Management Division Lead (1–2 positions)

Community Management Division

Establish and operate women's mutual support communities, maintain community engagement, expand community size, and organize online and offline women's empowerment activities.


Position Overview 

  • Develop monthly and annual work plans for the division;

  • Establish and improve divisional work systems and procedures;

  • Develop and refine the Women's Mutual Support Community Management Handbook;

  • Develop community activity plans and supervise implementation;

  • Manage division members and assign tasks;

  • Complete monthly reports and other work reports;

  • Conduct attendance tracking, performance evaluations, and point-based assessments for division members;

  • Organize division meetings, training sessions, and team-building activities.


Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project

Community Operations Volunteers (5 positions)

Community Management Division

Establish and operate women's mutual support communities, maintain community engagement, expand community size, and organize online and offline women's empowerment activities.


Position Overview

  • Operate and maintain women's mutual support communities;

  • Organize activities according to the community activity plan;

  • Post information and engage in daily interactions within the community;

  • Conduct individual communication and respond to inquiries from community members;

  • Record information related to community activities.

Requirements:

1. Priority given to candidates majoring in sociology, social work, communication, or psychology;

2. Priority given to those with work or volunteer experience in anti-domestic violence or community management;

3. Skilled in planning, organizing, and hosting online events;

4. Basic proficiency in English (speaking, listening, reading, and writing);

5. Strong team spirit, communication skills, and time management abilities;

6. Diligent, responsible, and patient.

What You Will Gain

Volunteers who meet performance standards will

  •  receive volunteer/service internship certificates,

  • participate in research projects related to gender equality, women's protection and development, and youth empowerment,

  • be eligible for quarterly and annual outstanding volunteer awards,

  • attend internal and external capacity-building and exchange events,

  • participate in volunteer team-building activities,

  • use volunteer points to redeem project merchandise, learning materials, and tools,

  • take on project management roles and join the International Youth Volunteer Network Alumni Association,

  • receive guidance and recommendation letters from project expert advisors,

  • receive internship referrals from project partners,

  • gain opportunities to collaborate and exchange with public welfare, academic, and foundation institutions domestically and internationally,

  • and learn and grow alongside like-minded peers from around the world.

The "Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project" initiated by Avoice is dedicated to helping overseas Chinese women suffering from domestic violence and improving the living conditions of vulnerable women abroad, building a bridge between women in need and various support resources, becoming their solid support and warm shelter..

We emphasize self-empowerment of the target female population, while also providing multi-dimensional support, including language, psychological, legal, medical, shelter, channels, education, economic assistance, storytelling and advocacy, and community support—expanding the scope of aid while fostering a virtuous cycle of mutual support.
