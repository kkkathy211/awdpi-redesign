Avoice Introduction:

The Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program) was launched in early 2020 at the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic. It is dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and advancing gender equality by providing comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence. Through building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, Avoice advocates for public awareness and support regarding the challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. The project strives to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.
