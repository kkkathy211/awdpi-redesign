On January 7, 2023, the first Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Global Development Forum was successfully held. The event received significant attention and participation from international relief networks, overseas Chinese communities, nonprofit organizations, and academic experts. Over a dozen domestic and international experts and scholars from UN Women, international organizations, academic institutions, renowned universities, and technology innovation companies participated. Drawing on gender data, practical experiences, and research findings across multiple sectors, they examined gender issues through social phenomena and shared insights on topics including technology, philanthropy, workplace dynamics, culture, law, and psychology, offering fresh perspectives and deep analysis on theory and practice in combating gender-based violence.


September 23–24, 2023, the 2023 AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum (also known as the 2nd Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Global Development Forum) will reunite us once again! We will expand the scale of speakers, enhance the innovation of topics, and focus on cross-generational, cross-sectoral, cross-regional, and cross-cultural exchanges and collaborative dialogue. The forum aims to increase the visibility of marginalized groups and promote gender equality in emerging fields. Centered on issues such as combating gender-based violence, women's workplace empowerment, women and intimate relationships, women's health, and overseas Chinese women laborers, the forum will deeply explore the connection between women and sustainable development, build a knowledge-sharing network among organizations and scholars dedicated to Asian women's advancement, create practical opportunities for public engagement in gender issues, and broaden and deepen support for overseas Asian women.

Organizer


AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) Asian Women Development Plan International , initiated at the beginning of the global COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, is an international nonprofit organization centered on women's rights. It currently operates in 30 countries and regions across Europe, North America, Asia, and Australia. By establishing extensive partnerships with United Nations system agencies, government departments in various countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions, AWDPI advocates for attention to the survival challenges faced by overseas Asian women and provides them with sustained protection and support, enabling them to gain greater opportunities for development in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, transforming from vulnerable groups into a vital force driving social progress.
