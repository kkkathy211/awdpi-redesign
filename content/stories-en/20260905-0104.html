Book of the day: The Gender Knot


The book for this session is the classic introductory work by American sociologist Allan G. Johnson:


The Gender Knot


The Gender Knot is a sociological introduction to patriarchy and gender inequality. The book argues that gender inequality is not merely a matter of individual attitudes or conflicts between men and women, but a structural issue.


embedded in social institutions, cultural norms, and daily interactions. Johnson uses the metaphor of a "gender knot" to describe patriarchy: a tangle of social relations and power structures that cannot be untied simply by changing individual beliefs. He particularly emphasizes that people may perpetuate gender inequality even without actively discriminating, simply by participating in existing social systems.



About the author



Allan G. Johnson 


GENDER


Allan G. Johnson is a writer and speaker in the fields of gender studies and sociology in the United States, holding a PhD in sociology from the University of Michigan. After graduation, he taught sociology and women's studies at Hartford College for Women and authored multiple sociology books and novels.


The facilitation of this reading series will also draw from the same author's introductory sociology book, Seeing Sociology: An Introduction (translated title: Seeing the Forest and the Trees), offering double the insight from one reading.


"

This book also emerges from my lifelong concern with the moral nature of human life and the fundamental questions of how it connects us to one another and to the world. What has brought us to our current condition? What binds us to a shared fate, and what drives us to harm one another so deeply? These questions make it impossible to ignore issues of social inequality, injustice, and the denial of human dignity.

"


—Author's Preface



Reading scope



This book club session will focus on the first major part of The Gender Knot:


"What Do We Mean by Patriarchy?"


  • Specifically, the first section:

    "Where Are We?"


  • Corresponding to the latest English edition:

    Part I. What Is This Thing Called Patriarchy?1 Where Are We? pp. 3–25


  • Selected reading:

    Chapter One of Seeing the Forest and the Trees: The Forest, the Trees, and That Thing


We will discuss together:

  • Why do men tend to deny the existence of gender inequality in society?

  • How does the "path of least resistance" operate?

  • When discussing male privilege, why do men often react with defensive feelings of being personally targeted?

  • Do you think women entering top levels of society help break the glass ceiling?

  • In your daily life, studies, or work, what seemingly neutral performance standards are essentially highly "male-identified"?


We hope this book club will be more than just a text-based reading—it will be a space for mutual listening and mutual inspiration.

About Free Yourself Reading Group




Since its first in-person event in 2023, Free Yourself Reading Group has collectively read nearly 20 books on gender studies and sociology.


The group's name comes from the first book read, Escape from Freedom:


"The self becomes as strong as it is active."


May we feel and think spontaneously, for the right to express our thoughts only matters when we truly have thoughts of our own.

—Gezi


AWDPI × Free Yourself Reading Group

AWDPI will co-host an in-person book club in London with Gezi. The event will accommodate around 9 participants, with books read chapter by chapter, and meetings held once a month.


We sincerely welcome women and non-binary individuals to join. While making friends, we can also explore feminist theories closely related to everyday life.


We hope to meet monthly, drawing strength from words and dialogue.
