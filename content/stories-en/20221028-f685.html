Avoice Overseas Volunteer Recruitment Special

Recruitment Summary and Member Introductions


Since the launch of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project's 2022 overseas volunteer recruitment campaign, we have received strong support from hundreds of outstanding volunteers. Our volunteer partners are spread across the globe, including over ten countries and regions such as China, the United States, the United Kingdom, France, Australia, and the Netherlands. Not only do they passionately engage in eliminating domestic violence and advancing gender equality, but they also bring diverse professional backgrounds and life experiences. Here, we extend our heartfelt gratitude to all volunteers for their support of the Avoice project.


After multiple rounds of resume screening, written tests, and interviews, and based on personal qualities, emotional depth, commitment to public good, and practical circumstances, nearly 100 individuals have successfully joined various departments of the Avoice family. We are honored to have met each of you—passionate, thoughtful individuals dedicated to public service.


Our new members come from top universities both in China and abroad, including Peking University, Tsinghua University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Zhejiang University, Harvard University, Yale University, the University of Pennsylvania, UC Berkeley, the University of Southern California, the National University of Singapore, and the London School of Economics and Political Science.



Their academic fields span sociology, psychology, law, history, gender studies, international business, accounting and finance, industrial design, media, computer science, literature, and translation.



Volunteers are located across the world, including Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, the United States, the United Kingdom, Japan, South Korea, Russia, Singapore, Canada, Edinburgh, the Netherlands, Germany, France, Portugal, and Australia.



Their skills include translation in multiple languages (English, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Korean, Japanese, Czech, Russian, Italian), project management, writing and editing, academic research, policy analysis, legal consultation, new media production, software engineering, financial economics, and philosophy.



Previous project experiences of new members include long-term community service volunteers, advocates for women's protection initiatives, TEDx speakers, and self-media creators with established followings.


Although our members reside in different countries, speak different languages, and come from vastly different backgrounds, experiences, and professional skill sets, our shared goal in joining Avoice is the same: to promote gender equality, reduce domestic violence, and rescue, empower, and awaken vulnerable groups affected by domestic violence around the world.



We look forward to our new partners injecting vitality into Avoice's development, and to creating a brighter future together—bringing warmth and momentum to the cause of women's equality!
