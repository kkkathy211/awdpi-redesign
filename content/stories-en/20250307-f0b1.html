Event Introduction

The "SHEnergy·Her Power" Concert and New York Chinese Community Women Excellence Awards Ceremony aims to honor influential and inspiring women within the New York Chinese community, showcasing their achievements and brilliance. Through the form of a concert, it celebrates women's independence, strength, and perseverance, while conveying the spirit of gentleness, kindness, and dedication.


This event will bring together community leaders, entrepreneurs, artists, and elites from various fields to jointly witness this grand occasion in the Chinese community.

Organizer Introduction

Valley Echo Cultural & Creative Company

Valley Echo (Culture) Creative Company is dedicated to promoting cultural and artistic exchanges between China and the United States, building bridges for cross-cultural communication. The company specializes in organizing high-quality economic events, cultural and artistic activities, exhibitions, concerts, and various forums, fostering deep collaboration between Chinese and American enterprises, artists, and institutions. Its services include market promotion for Chinese enterprises entering global markets, cultural event planning, art performance promotion, international artist management, and consulting for international exchange programs. With abundant resources and a professional team, the company strengthens close interactions between the U.S. and China in art, humanities, and business, deepening cultural exchange and promoting the integration of diverse art forms, creating a broader platform for Sino-American cultural cooperation.

Concert Introduction

The "SHEnergy·Her Power" Concert is co-organized with the International Asian Cultural Society (IACS), which has long been committed to the high-end art sector and has established strong collaborative relationships with many outstanding local Chinese young artists. These artists not only possess professional artistic education and extensive stage performance experience but also interpret the strength and charm of women through their talent and creativity. Their outstanding performances will add artistic brilliance to this grand event, vividly embodying the spirit of independence, confidence, and resilience advocated by "SHEnergy·Her Power," and delivering a deeply moving audiovisual feast to the audience.


The musicians will also pay tribute to Chinese American women in New York through diverse musical styles, including Western and traditional Chinese instruments, opera, and popular music.


Artist Introductions

Yin Yuan Cellist, currently studying at Mannes School of Music in New York. She has won numerous international awards, including the Gold Prize at the Singapore International Music Festival, First Prize at the Anselmo International Competition, and First Prize at Italy's AVNI Competition.


Lou Wanru Violinist and violist, studying at Mannes School of Music in New York. With a strong performance background, she has served as concertmaster of symphony orchestras, received top scholarships from world-renowned music conservatories, and won multiple international awards, including the Hong Kong International String Competition and the German Hummingbird Music Award.



Zhou Lingli Lyric coloratura soprano. She completed her undergraduate studies at the Music College of Hangzhou Normal University and earned her master's degree at Mannes School of Music in the U.S. She has won the Silver Prize in the Classical Vocal Category at the Hangzhou International Music Festival and was recognized as an Outstanding Singer at the MRL New York International Music Festival. Her major opera roles include Zerlina in Mozart's *Don Giovanni*, Despina in Mozart's *Così fan tutte*, and Euridice in Gluck's *Orfeo ed Euridice*.


Dong Jin'ou Pipa performer, holding a Master's degree in "Chinese Music and Culture" from Bard College Conservatory. She studied pipa under Professor Zhang Qiang at the Central Conservatory of Music, won the Gold Prize in solo pipa performance at the 2018 Beijing Student Arts Festival, and was awarded the title of "Art Star of Beijing." She holds both a Bachelor of Arts (BA) and a Bachelor of Music (BM) from Bard College.


Chen Xiaowen Suona and guanzi player from Hong Kong, China. He is pursuing a Master of Arts in Chinese Music and Culture at Bard College. A founding member of the New York Echo Chinese Chamber Ensemble and an active member of the Bard East-West Ensemble, he has also served as a guest musician for the Hong Kong Chinese Orchestra and Macao Chinese Orchestra. He won the Silver Prize in the Advanced Professional Category at the 4th Nanyang International Suona Music Competition and holds a Bachelor of Music (Honors) degree from the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts.



Wang Yulin Tenor, who studied at the Opera Department of the Central Academy of Drama and later at the Manhattan School of Music. He has performed leading roles in several renowned operas. In 2024, he won the Metropolitan Opera International Vocal Competition's regional round, has frequently collaborated with Metropolitan Opera artists, and will portray Pinkerton in the upcoming New York production of *Madama Butterfly*.


Vivien L., Singer-songwriter, keyboardist, and music producer, graduated with a degree in Classical Piano Performance from Stony Brook University, and holds a Master's degree in Songwriting from New York University. She is a key member of several popular New York bands and also works as an independent musician and songwriter. Her creative style spans multiple genres, including City-Pop, R&B, and Alternative Pop. Notable works include "The Way Home," "Invisible," and "When We Were Young" (performed by Chen Chusheng and Mianzi).


Zheyu "Pierre" Pi Sound artist, music producer, and drummer. He completed his undergraduate studies in Sound Art and Architecture at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago (SAIC) and is pursuing a graduate degree in Music Technology at New York University. His music and sound works span animation, video games, art exhibitions, and bands. Notable works include *Pause: Intelligent Order Reorganization* (animation), the album *Karma*, and *From Centuries To Centuries* (short film).


Li Qin Soprano, currently pursuing a Master's degree in Vocal Performance at Mannes School of Music, and holds a Bachelor of Music in Vocal and Opera Performance from the China Conservatory of Music. Her previous roles include Susanna in *Le nozze di Figaro*, Zerlina in *Don Giovanni*, Bastienne in *Bastien und Bastienne*, and Gaga in *Red River Valley*. She has won first prizes at the Huanglong Music Festival and the German Wiesbaden International Music Competition.

Zhang Binlin (Lynn) Market Director for Greater New York at SMG Shanghai Media Group & Dragon TV, North America Correspondent for Shanghai Eye, founder of a marketing company, and President of the Tongji Alumni Association of Greater New York. She graduated with a degree in Computer Science from Tongji University and is pursuing a Master's in Market Data Analytics at New York University.
