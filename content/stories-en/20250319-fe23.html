About Veritas China

Veritas China is a non-profit academic organization jointly established by Chinese students from universities at home and abroad, dedicated to promoting liberal arts education in China.

Driven by the vision of advancing liberal arts education, since 2015, we have:


offered over 180 in-person courses and more than 50 online course series, attracting thousands of high school and university students from across China;

operated online content platforms such as the "Veritas Dialogues" podcast, the "Veritas Newsletter," and the "Thematic Seminar Room" since 2018, providing high-quality academic and current affairs information free of charge to tens of thousands of readers and listeners;

cultivated an active academic community and its forum, "Veritas Together," together with past participants, instructors, and organizers of Veritas events.

Group photo of Veritas Academy 2024 participants


Academy Information

Veritas China will host a 9-day in-person summer academy in 2025, aiming to create an immersive liberal arts education experience. The academy will offer approximately 30 small seminar-style courses across various academic disciplines. Participants will select 2–4 courses to attend based on their schedules and academic levels. Additionally, the academy will organize a rich array of extracurricular activities, creating intellectual exchange platforms through academic roundtables, film screenings, and other events.


Starting today, the Veritas Academy 2025 instructor recruitment is officially open. The summer academy is tentatively scheduled to take place in Shanghai from late July to early August 2025. For details, please refer to the instructor application form.

Why Join Us?

An immersive academic exchange platform

Evening event at Veritas Academy 2024


During the 9 days of the summer academy, you will meet a group of instructors, participants, and organizing committee members who share your passion for academics, engaging in intensive exchanges and discussions on your fields of interest. At the academy, you are not only a lecturer but also a facilitator and co-creator of academic dialogue. Participants will not merely receive knowledge but actively construct classroom content through thinking, questioning, and discussion, creating a dynamic and intellectually vibrant space together with you. During the academy, you will also have ample opportunities to participate in or organize diverse evening activities, including but not limited to office hours, panel discussions, guest talks, film screenings, and art workshops. These intellectual collisions and connections often extend beyond the 9 days of the academy, continuing into longer academic journeys and becoming lifelong intellectual companions.



Developing your academic interests through teaching and learning

Veritas Academy 2024 in session


Instructors will have the opportunity to design a short course consisting of eight sessions, introducing their academic interests to motivated high school and university students. Many past instructors have found that participating in Veritas China events improved their teaching abilities, enriched their teaching experience, deepened their understanding of academic issues, and provided support for their future academic development.



Promoting accessible liberal arts education through a non-profit model

Closing ceremony of Veritas Academy 2024


For ten years, Veritas has upheld its model as a non-profit student organization, committed to providing accessible liberal arts education resources and academic exchange platforms for people from diverse backgrounds. The summer academy will charge affordable fees, and any surplus will be used to sustain the organization's future operations. Meanwhile, Veritas China has long offered financial aid to participants in need, supporting their participation in both online and offline programs. This year's summer academy will continue this tradition by providing tuition waivers, free spots, and living subsidies.

Application Steps

Online/In-Person Instructor Application ", select Summer Academy " and complete the application form. " Deadline for Veritas Academy 2025 instructor applications:

23:59 Beijing Time, March 30, 2025 After submitting the form, we will contact you via email.*

Veritas China is committed to providing all members with a safe, friendly, and equitable teaching and working environment. We especially welcome applications from women, applicants primarily educated on mainland China, those planning to offer STEM-related courses, or those from non-academic backgrounds, to enrich the academy's classrooms and dialogues. All instructor applications will be reviewed under the same criteria.

*In addition to the summer academy, Veritas China accepts online course instructor applications year-round. If your personal schedule prevents you from attending the summer academy but you are still interested in teaching for Veritas China, we welcome your application for online courses. If interested, please click "Online/In-Person Instructor Application" and select "Online Courses" to complete the form.
