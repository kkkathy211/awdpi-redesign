In the rush of time, we often move forward hastily, overlooking the energy that could carry us further—our own health and inner growth. On March 10, you are warmly invited to an intimate tea gathering specially designed for young and mature women, where we will uncover the secrets of time and explore the topics of women's health and personal growth.

Event Introduction

Global Shapers Community Shanghai I Hub

During this afternoon, we won't talk about work or daily trivialities—only the things that matter most to us as women: health and growth. This is an open platform, whether you're seeking ways to relieve stress or hoping to discuss inner growth and challenges with like-minded peers. Here, you'll find a safe haven. We will have professional gynecologists and medical practitioners present to answer your questions about female hormones and health.


Event Schedule

Global Shapers Community Shanghai I Hub

Part 1: Let's Talk

We are honored to invite three guests who have unique insights into women's health and personal development. At different stages of life, they have faced their own anxieties, yet each has written her own story. Their stories make up only half of this event—we look forward to you completing the other half with your own.

Part 2: Let's Listen

We have invited the head of gynecology from Xiangya Hospital and the medical manager from a startup specializing in at-home hormone monitoring to provide professional science communication on women's hormonal health. Give yourself some time—you may gain a deeper understanding of your body. Additionally, 2–3 participants will have the opportunity to experience on-site hormone testing.

Global Shapers Community Shanghai I Hub

The Global Shapers Community is a global youth network directly under the World Economic Forum, founded in 2011 by Professor Klaus Schwab, aiming to provide young people with a global platform to shape the future.

The Shanghai I Hub consists of young people from diverse professional backgrounds. While pursuing personal development, we are committed to driving community progress and creating more possibilities for reshaping society. We believe young people should be "active drivers of change, not passive observers." Through our community platform, we support members in achieving faster and better growth, while enhancing the social impact and contributions of both the community and its members through various social initiatives.
