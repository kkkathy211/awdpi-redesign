Forum Overview

Introduction of LACF



In the Year of the Water Rabbit (Gui Mao), the world continues to face mounting challenges. "Instability, uncertainty, and unpredictability" have become defining keywords of our time. As global industrial chains undergo restructuring and domestic economic cycles emerge as a development priority, the international economic landscape is rapidly shifting. Amid high inflation, global economic growth continues to slow. At the same time, the accelerating wave of artificial intelligence is reshaping employment markets. In this era of globalization, how can we, as the new generation, strengthen national confidence and bring traditional Chinese culture to the world? How can China steadily forge a new path forward amid global upheaval?


In 2023, the LSE China Forum will once again bring together experts and scholars from various fields to share their insights and recommendations on China's future development. The forum will focus on current issues in macroeconomics, youth employment, cultural dissemination, and new energy, exploring both the opportunities and challenges China faces during this period of transformation, as well as pathways for innovation and growth.

Sub-forum on Global Industrial Chain Restructuring

· Restructuring of the Global Industrial Chain ·

Breakthrough Opportunities in a New Era



As the world enters the post-pandemic era, multiple shocks have triggered a turning point in the global production order. The previously established global industrial chain has revealed many shortcomings, such as lack of flexibility, and urgently requires deep adjustment. On one hand, global industrial chains are becoming increasingly intelligent, digitalized, and green, with future industries and strategic emerging sectors becoming key areas of accelerated development for nations worldwide. On the other hand, risks and uncertainties in global industrial chains have sharply increased, showing trends toward localization, regionalization, and shorter supply chains, with frequent threats of "decoupling and supply chain disruptions." Amid this once-in-a-century transformation, China faces triple pressures of "shrinking demand, supply shocks, and weakening expectations," while also holding new opportunities to promote industrial transformation and upgrading, strengthen its industrial system, and enhance supply chain resilience. How can China, based on its new stage of development, build a secure and controllable industrial system, advance the sophistication of industrial foundations, and modernize its industrial chains?

Cultural Sub-forum

· Culture in Harmony with the Times ·

Continuity, Development, and Innovation



In recent years, industries rich in "Chinese aesthetics"—such as guochao (national trend) brands, Chinese films, guofeng (national style) animation, and Hanfu fashion—have attracted growing attention. However, some observers worry that young people's enthusiasm for traditional culture is gradually fading, and public appreciation of Chinese culture remains superficial. Internationally, Chinese cultural industries seem to struggle to go global, while Western countries continue to hold Orientalist biases and stereotypical views of Chinese culture centered around "kung fu stars." How can we tell China's story well in the new era and effectively bring traditional Chinese culture to the world?

Youth Employment Sub-forum

· Breaking Through Job-Hunting Challenges ·

The "Poetry and the Distant" of a New Era



The urban youth unemployment rate in the first quarter of 2023 reached as high as 18.3%. According to statistics from the Ministry of Education, the number of university graduates nationwide this year will reach 11.58 million. With unemployment rates remaining high and over 10 million graduates entering the job market annually, "high academic qualifications" are no longer a scarce resource. As the value of degrees as a "ticket to entry" diminishes, we must re-examine China's current education system. The popularity of terms like "the long gown of Kong Yiji" and "mouse literature" forces us to reflect on employment difficulties caused by degree devaluation and the psychological distress young people may experience due to the widening gap between reality and aspirations. Bearing the growing pains of China's industrial transformation and the global pressure of the AI revolution, students caught in the vortex of "degree inflation" are rethinking the personal value of higher education. What adjustments should the education system make? These are the pressing questions of our time.

New Energy Sub-forum

· Green Transition ·

A New Landscape in the Era of Digital Intelligence



By the end of 2020, China clearly proposed its dual-carbon goals. This year, the National Energy Administration has issued multiple guidance documents outlining the opportunities and challenges facing the new energy sector. As a major national strategy, China's green transition will bring new opportunities for future economic development. The new energy market must actively respond to challenges, adapt flexibly, and achieve green transformation efficiently. Today, the interaction and empowerment among technologies have opened up many new frontiers—for example, photovoltaic technology companies are already using artificial intelligence algorithms to increase output and energy efficiency. What development opportunities exist for AI applications in the energy sector? In the context of green transition and the advancement of energy digitalization and intelligence, what new considerations and decisions will investors in the new energy industry make?

LACF Cocktail Party


"Night of Flowing Cup"

Orchid Autumn Banquet: Heart-to-Heart Conversation and Free Expression



On the third day of the third month of the ninth year of Yonghe (353 AD), Wang Xizhi, a Jin dynasty aristocrat and Prefect of Kuaiji, together with 42 prominent political and military officials including Xie An and Sun Chuo, held a "flowing cup" poetry and wine gathering after a purification ritual at the Orchid Pavilion, creating a celebrated moment in Chinese cultural history. This refined Confucian tradition has been passed down to the present day. Drawing inspiration from the Orchid Pavilion gathering, this cocktail reception invites guests to "share heartfelt thoughts and meaningful conversations within one room."
