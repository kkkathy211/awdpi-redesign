In an era surrounded by algorithms, have you ever felt lost?


Have you also noticed:

  • When you open an app, the screen is filled with "You might like" suggestions

  • AI skills are becoming the new core of competitiveness in the workplace


As AI quietly transforms work, life, and even the way we think, how can we maintain our independence and clarity?


We are in the midst of a wave where AI is reshaping the world, and data quietly influences our choices. While many are still anxious about "being replaced," truly aware individuals have already begun to ask: How can we not only avoid being eliminated in this transformation, but instead live freer and more grounded lives?

True stability comes from self-leadership—


When Elon Musk was asked what advice he would give to children growing up in the AI era, he thought deeply and replied: "Follow your heart, and do things that are interesting and fulfilling."


Lu Xi, in her book *Self-Leadership*, proposes: "When algorithms take over thinking and data defines identity, the real threat is not being replaced by AI, but that we voluntarily surrender our cognitive sovereignty." 


On September 21, AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International will host a special sharing session with Professor Lu Xi: "Maintaining Self-Leadership in the Age of AI?"


This is a deep conversation specially designed for women who seek breakthroughs and personal growth.


We will explore:

  • How to build independent thinking that is not carried away by algorithms?

  • How to maintain self-agency and leadership amid technological waves?

  • How can women achieve career advancement and personal transformation through cognitive evolution?
