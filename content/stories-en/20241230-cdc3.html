In an era deeply shaped by globalization and the rule of law, women's rights and the future of enterprises have become a shared mission entrusted to us by our times. As international relations shift dramatically and the global economic landscape undergoes profound transformation, women's status in society, economy, and culture is experiencing unprecedented change. In this historical process, family law and marriage law are not only reflections of legal systems but also solid cornerstones of social justice and gender equality. Amid the wave of economic globalization, while Chinese enterprises pursue international development, they also face complex compliance risks and challenges—especially under the pressure of China-U.S. trade tensions and clashes between transnational regulations. How to ensure compliance while maintaining competitiveness has become a critical issue of our time.




Event Theme


At this seminar, AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International and APEA Asia-Pacific Entrepreneurs Association join hands to explore how, on this rapidly evolving legal ground, we can protect women's rights and help enterprises find a clear, compliant path forward in a complex international legal environment. This seminar will deeply examine two key topics: "Women's International Development and Protection from the Perspective of Family and Marriage Law" and "China-U.S. Trade Friction and Compliance Risk Control for Chinese Enterprises' Internationalization" We will examine women's rights protection within global legal systems through the lens of family and marriage law, while also focusing on corporate compliance challenges under China-U.S. trade friction, revealing the crucial role law plays in global dynamics. Through this seminar, we hope to plant seeds of wisdom and light the lamp of action for every participant, moving together toward a more just and sustainable future.

AWDPI

Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI)



AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development. It evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program) established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in the United Kingdom, Belgium, and the United States, where it is also registered as a charitable organization. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments of different countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the plight of Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more development opportunities for Asian women overseas in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them transform from a vulnerable group into a vital force driving social progress.
