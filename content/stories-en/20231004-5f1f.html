Delphine and Carole: Rebel Muses


This documentary presents the French feminist movement of the 1970s from the perspectives of female actors and film professionals. Although the feminist ideas depicted in the film belong to France in the 1970s, they remain strikingly relevant today, reflecting a certain stagnation in the progress toward gender equality.


Moreover, unlike today, many French actresses in the 1970s were widely regarded as intellectuals—they could sit alongside professors and scholars to discuss social issues, actively participate in social movements, and raise their voices against inequality… Perhaps this is the true meaning of being a public figure.

The Devil Wears Prada


The Devil Wears Prada realistically portrays the difficulty professional women face in balancing love and career. Andy, the protagonist, has always dreamed of becoming a successful career woman like her boss. But in reality, faced with harsh circumstances, she must make difficult choices.


Clearly, Andy's female boss unhesitatingly chooses her career over personal life, while Andy ultimately decides to walk away from the tempting world of fame, money, and power, choosing instead to live the life she truly desires. By presenting the contrasting choices of Andy and her boss, the film conveys a life attitude: no matter what choice you make or what you wish to pursue, staying true to your original aspirations, respecting your inner voice, and making mindful trade-offs will lead you to genuine beauty and happiness in life.

Belle


Set against the backdrop of 18th-century British society, the film tells the story of Dido Belle, a mixed-race girl raised by an aristocratic family, who later influences the abolition of slavery.


The protagonist Dido's noble upbringing grants her certain privileges, yet her skin color hinders her full participation in social rights. This determined and courageous young woman fully exerts her influence, working alongside her uncle Lord Mansfield, the Chief Justice, to advance the process of abolishing slavery in Britain.

The Princess Diaries


The Princess Diaries mainly tells the story of an ordinary American girl who suddenly becomes a princess of a small European country and her journey of personal growth. Although this film is a comedy, it also portrays many difficulties and obstacles the female protagonist faces during her development.


Mia, the protagonist, is an extremely ordinary girl who overnight becomes a royal princess. While this plot may seem unrealistic, it leads to the film's core message—every girl, as long as she has enough courage and self-love, can become her own princess, even without royal bloodline.
