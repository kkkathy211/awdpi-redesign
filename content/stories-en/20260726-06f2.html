Lineup



Signmark Signmark

A Deaf rapper from Finland and pioneer of sign language hip-hop, he is the first Deaf hip-hop artist globally to sign with a major international record label. Facing societal limitations placed on Deaf people from a young age, he has consistently pursued his creative path, developing a unique musical expression rooted in sign language, rhythm, and the body—responding to the long history of marginalization and silencing of the Deaf community. Over the years, he has expanded public understanding of sound, language, and disability through music and activism, becoming one of the most representative figures in international Deaf culture. In 2024, he was named Finland's Speaker of the Year and is currently on his global farewell tour.



Hu Xiaoshu (Alice Hu)

A Deaf visual artist, activist, and sign language educator from Shanghai, holding a Master of Fine Arts from the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna, with 15 years of experience in film and theater performance. She has served as a sign language interpreter at the Eurovision Song Contest and numerous stage performances, hosted multiple international events on accessibility and inclusive arts, and promoted public education on accessibility awareness and diverse creative inclusion services, dedicated to building bridges between Deaf and hearing communities.



Calvin Khan

A Deaf DJ from Germany, whose path into music was unconventional. Though he enjoys electronic music and can hear some of it with hearing aids, as a Deaf person he never considered becoming a DJ. Instead, he pursued an academic career and worked at a university for several years. During pandemic lockdowns, he began experimenting with DJing at home, which led to his debut at an outdoor rave. In 2023, he performed at renowned underground venues in Hamburg such as "Rote Flora" and "Fabrique" (Gängeviertel), and began touring clubs across multiple European countries, Bali (Indonesia), China, and beyond. On the afternoon of August 15, Calvin will also host a Deaf-friendly DJ workshop at A4 Art Museum in Chengdu—stay tuned for the announcement on BassBath's official WeChat account!



Kobby Harding

A performing artist from Ghana, Africa, whose artistic practice spans contemporary dance, pop dance, performance art, painting, and costume-making. His dance works are highly expressive, using movement to build connections across communities. His inspiration comes from the dynamics of living and non-living things, as well as from his encounters with diverse dance groups and cultures around the world.



Chongqing Runbang· Rainbow Home Art Troupe Deaf members

A collective of Deaf and hard-of-hearing artists. Using the body as language, the troupe explores the artistic fusion of dance, visual art, and rhythm through continuous creation and performance, expressing members' unique life experiences and creativity, and advancing disability-inclusion through action, expanding the possibilities of disability arts.



Kyra Han

Chengdu-based visual artist and VJ for this event, aiming to make electronic data feel tangibly present (._.)



Stanley Qianfan

A graduate of the Shanghai Theatre Academy's directing program, provincial Mandarin promotion ambassador of Zhejiang, and top scorer in the national performance exam, he is also a CODA (Child of Deaf Adults). Having learned sign language from his Deaf grandparents since childhood, he will serve as the bilingual host (spoken Chinese and sign language) at this BassBath event.

BassBath is China's first clubbing brand dedicated to creating a Deaf-hearing inclusive environment, co-founded by a Deaf-hearing team (Deaf artist Alice Hu, hearing artists Pudding and vis). Each event emphasizes low-frequency vibrations, aiming to redefine sound beyond the hearing-centric norm—returning sound to its essence as vibration—while integrating multisensory design, workshops, games, and sign language interpretation to create accessible artistic, musical, and party spaces for Deaf people. BassBath centers Deaf agency, featuring Deaf DJs, artists, dancers, hosts, and sign language rappers, while encouraging deep hearing participation, fostering friendships with Deaf people, understanding Deaf culture, and learning sign language. We hope to build connections between Deaf and hearing communities, move beyond abstract labels, and see the diversity in each other.
