Summary




On August 3, 2025, AWDPI successfully hosted an online event titled "Asian Women's Path to Global Leadership." Continuing AWDPI's mission to eliminate discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women and to advance their empowerment and development, this event focused on core challenges and strategies for women in career development and early-stage entrepreneurship. The featured speaker was Ms. Isabel Li (Li Xiaoke), an entrepreneur with extensive experience in the consulting industry, who shared in-depth insights on pathways for graduates entering consulting, precise personal branding, and practical entrepreneurial experiences beyond platform dependency.


Featured Speaker: Isabel Li (Li Xiaoke)

Lecture Theme: The Path to Global Leadership for Asian Women



Key Quotes


"Long-term awareness means that when you start out, you know you're a newcomer trying to build a personal identity—and you must know exactly what that identity is. The clearer it is, the better..." "If you're going on a treasure hunt, at least you need to know what treasure you're looking for." "


"Entrepreneurship begins with the question 'Who am I?'—when you're no longer defined by a title, what can still illuminate others?"


Regarding the common anxiety around career transitions, Ms. Li offered warm and insightful perspectives from her own experience as someone who has walked that path.


★Core Insights ★


  1. The "Golden Pathway" for Graduates Entering Consulting

    Ms. Li noted that for recent graduates or young professionals at the beginning of their careers, joining top-tier consulting firms is not out of reach. She strongly recommended paying attention to the "Graduate Program" (graduate scheme) offered by major consulting firms in the UK and globally. These programs are specifically designed to train newcomers and provide systematic training, making them an excellent entry point into the industry. She emphasized that consulting firms welcome applications from university graduates. Although competition is intense, many Chinese graduates have successfully entered renowned companies through graduate schemes and gradually built their own careers through hard work.


  2. Begin with the End in Mind

    Building a Clear "Personal Identity": Successful career planning starts with clear self-positioning—"Who do you want to become?" Ms. Li referred to this positioning as a "personal identity." Using her own experience of initial discomfort in a foreign country and then bravely taking the first step, she emphasized that the clearer one's personal identity, the more defined one's goals become. Newcomers should reflect: What is my ideal state? What gaps exist between my current state and that ideal? What certifications do I need? What experiences should I accumulate? She advised newcomers to proactively seek honest guidance from experienced mentors within their communities. She also stressed: "The more specific your direction, the more likely you are to achieve it."


    Break Free from Platform Dependency


  3. Building Brand Value from Scratch in Entrepreneurship: When sharing her experience of starting a consulting company from zero, Ms. Li candidly pointed out the challenge of "starting from scratch after leaving a platform."

    The key lies in precisely articulating and marketing the core value of one's personal brand. "When introducing myself as just 'Li Xiaoke, founder of a small company,' I've seen too many puzzled looks. But it was precisely this 'reset to zero' that forced me to clarify my most essential value: helping Chinese enterprises understand the pathways to the UK's systems, culture, talent, compliance, and collaboration in a context-specific way, and crafting truly 'grounded' international strategies."

    Embracing the Power of Uncertainty: The Mutual Healing of Community and AI


  4. "When I'm lost, I'm always held up by kindness," Ms. Li admitted.

    The greatest support during career transitions comes from two sources: the sincere guidance of senior professionals in communities and deep conversations with AI tools. Industry veterans generously share their experiences, breaking down abstract uncertainties into actionable steps. Additionally, she has developed a unique, trusting relationship with GPT: "I confide in it like a friend, asking it to analyze possibilities in the consulting industry from an expert perspective—it has become my 'cloud mentor.'" Conclusion and Outlook



★Ms. Li sincerely shared the core principles and practical experiences behind her journey from platform dependency to independent entrepreneurship, emphasizing the importance of setting clear goals, precisely defining core value, and making good use of resources (communities, mentors). Her insights offer highly inspiring action guidelines for newcomers at career crossroads and for women seeking to break free from platform limitations and realize their personal value.★

Seize the Entry Point: Leverage Graduate Programs to enter the industry


  1. Turn Inward: Extract transferable skills from past experiences (e.g., the core value of "reducing communication costs")


  2. Face the Zero Point: After leaving a platform, rebuild your brand through your ability to solve real-world problems


  3. "You must know: Who am I? What problems can I solve?"—this is the foundation for women to break free from career dependency and achieve self-empowerment.

    True empowerment teaches us to recognize our own lighthouse in the storm. Those late nights spent clarifying self-worth, those helping hands from mentors, those minutes saved for clients—ultimately weave a gentle yet resilient safety net, catching every soul descending through transition.
