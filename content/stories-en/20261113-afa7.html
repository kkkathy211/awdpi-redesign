Hosted by Ruiwen ShePower, and supported by AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International as the exclusive strategic partner, the 2026 Global She Power Forum (China-U.S. She Power Forum) will take place on Saturday, November 14, 2026, at the University of Southern California in Los Angeles, USA.


This forum will bring together female leaders, corporate board members, entrepreneurs, investors, business leaders, scholars, and cultural innovators from China and the United States to engage in in-depth discussions on global women's leadership, capital and business, organizational culture, brand influence, and China-U.S. cross-cultural collaboration.


This is not only a forum on women's leadership, but also a powerful convergence of ideas across industries, cultures, and geographies.


From how Eastern culture can enter the international luxury market to the future trends of China-U.S. trade; from global capital and women's leadership to brand building and the influence of new generations, this forum will explore, through keynote speeches, panel discussions, fireside chats, and high-quality networking, how global business leaders of the new era can break boundaries, build connections, and create lasting impact.


The forum will specially invite Wei Zhang, director of Starbucks and Ralph Lauren and former president of Alibaba Pictures, to deliver a closing fireside chat on women's leadership in global boardrooms, bringing global business perspectives and deep insights to the entire forum.


By gathering diverse voices from the fields of business, investment, academia, culture, and innovation, we look forward to launching, together in Los Angeles, a profound dialogue on leadership, global vision, and future opportunities.
