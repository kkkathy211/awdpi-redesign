About AWDPI



AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development.


It evolved from Avoice (Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in Belgium, the UK, the US, Australia, and Thailand. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with organizations across sectors, including United Nations system agencies, relevant government departments, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions.


AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the survival challenges faced by Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more opportunities for Asian women in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them grow into a vital force for social progress.

AWDPI Heads – Brief Introduction to Key Policies


At AWDPI, departments and divisions are typically led by two heads jointly, ensuring stability, continuity, and mutual support within the volunteer-based system. In some cases, one or more than two heads may be appointed, adjusted flexibly by the organization according to project needs.


As core members of the organization, heads are responsible for team collaboration, task delegation, and project advancement. If a head needs to step down, they must communicate in advance and complete a full handover according to organizational procedures to ensure smooth continuity of work.


When a head position is vacant, the organization will prioritize recommending outstanding team members from within the department to take over. If no suitable candidate is available temporarily, the Founding Team, Secretary General, or Youth Chair Council will temporarily assume the role until a suitable head is found.


The AWDPI head system is designed to ensure long-term organizational stability and provide volunteers with opportunities to grow and develop leadership skills.
