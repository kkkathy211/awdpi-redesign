01 About Us

AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization evolved from the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), established in 2020. AWDPI is dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas, and to promoting their empowerment and development.


AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international non-profit organization. Originating from the Avoice (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), established in 2020, AWDPI is dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas and get them empowered and well-developed.


AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in the United Kingdom, Belgium, and the United States. The organization has built a broad network of partnerships with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions.


AWDPI operates in various countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific with national office and teams in the United Kingdom, Belgium, and the United States. It has established a wide cooperation network, including organizations within the United Nations system, relevant public sector of various countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions.


AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the challenges faced by Asian women living overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. The organization aims to create more opportunities for these women to develop in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them transform from a marginalized group into a vital force driving social progress.


AWDPI calls for attention to the living conditions of Asian women overseas and provides them with ongoing protection and support. We aim to offer more opportunities for Asian women overseas in social, economic, cultural, and educational aspects, helping them grow from vulnerable groups into a vital force pushing for social progress.
