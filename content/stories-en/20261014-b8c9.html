Posting a note, translating a paragraph, or asking AI to polish a few sentences—we are increasingly relying on technology for all forms of creation. Meanwhile, social media platforms' algorithmic recommendations and certain expressive tendencies of AI are, in turn, shaping our perspectives—especially AI's gender biases.



This time, AWDPI's October Academic Roundtable invites Xiaohongshu blogger, researcher, and founder of Genderly, 404, to discuss the relationship between women and technology with us from the perspectives of content creation, academic research, and product development.

We will explore how platform algorithms affect whether women's voices are heard, and how women's experiences and concerns can enter technological design when they participate in algorithmic research and develop products firsthand. Drawing on 404's research and Genderly's practices, we'll talk about the possibilities of recognizing bias and attempting change.
