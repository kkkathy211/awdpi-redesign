OCF Forum .

The Oxford China Forum (OCF) is an annual summit initiated by Oxford students and is one of the largest and most academically rigorous forums on China in the UK. By inviting world-class politicians, entrepreneurs, media figures, and scholars to discussions, it provides diverse perspectives for the public to observe, understand, and reflect on a changing China.

01 Forum Introduction


The 12th Oxford China Forum

Integration and Openness


Founded in 2013, the Oxford China Forum (OCF) has grown into one of the largest and most academically rigorous China forums in Europe. Past forums have been live-streamed globally by platforms such as Sohu, Douyin, and Phoenix News, accumulating over 10 million views. In previous editions, we have had the honor of inviting over 300 distinguished guests, including former UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon, former Australian Prime Minister Kevin Rudd, CEO of Huayi Brothers Wang Zhonglei, ZhenFund founder Xu Xiaoping, Harvard political philosopher Michael Sandel, DJI Chairman Li Zexiang, and renowned dancer Jin Xing . This year's Oxford China Forum will continue the spirit of past editions—we speak for our audience's genuine needs and will respond concretely to these expectations, offering China's solutions from an Oxford perspective.

02 Sub-forum Previews





The forum's keynote speeches will cover core topics such as the AI technological frontier, international competition, regulatory governance, and ethical challenges, helping participants understand how AI is shaping the future world. The panel discussions will focus on AI's impact on the future of work, delving into how we can collaborate to ensure a smooth transition in the labor market and fully unlock the benefits brought by AI.

From algorithmic labor to employment anxiety, from conceptions of time within globalization to how individuals rebuild connections with their "local," we will work with experts from psychology, economics, and sociology to deconstruct the "tyranny of time" and co-create a "survival handbook" for contemporary youth.

From anthropology, philosophy, and psychology to cutting-edge AI technology, we look forward to exploring with you new possibilities for the future coexistence of humans and digital life.

✨OCF Exclusive Event:

OCF X RHODES HOUSE FORMAL 

An Elegant Feast


Oxford, a thousand-year-old academic hall, a place where ideas converge;

Rhodes House, a century-old cultural landmark, bearing witness to the changes of the times.

From Albert Einstein's scientific philosophy, to Nelson Mandela's enduring spirit, to Queen Elizabeth II's royal elegance—this building carries the weight of history and has engraved countless moments that changed the world.



On this grand occasion, we sincerely invite you to attend the 2025 Oxford China Forum Exclusive Formal Dinner, gathering with forum speakers and special guests in this hall of century-old cultural heritage. Savor meticulously selected gourmet dishes, listen to elegant and melodious music, and immerse yourself in an extraordinary event where academia and art intertwine.



Rhodes House's award-winning culinary team will craft seasonal delicacies, blending classic and innovative elements with masterful cooking techniques and elegant presentation, offering a feast for both the eyes and the palate. We have also fully considered diverse dietary needs to ensure every guest enjoys a delightful culinary experience.



At the Oxford China Forum dinner, we have carefully designed two interactive segments that combine intellectual depth with ceremonial elegance, allowing guests to engage in profound exchanges while immersed in exquisite cuisine and graceful melodies, creating unforgettable memories.


"Letters to the Future"

Each guest will write a letter "to their future self" at Rhodes House, expressing hopes for the future, life commitments, or current reflections and insights. Guests may choose to have their letter delivered after one, five, or ten years. These letters will be carefully sealed by the dinner team and returned to you at a special moment in the future. Let the thoughts and aspirations of this night reappear through the passage of time, becoming a unique mark on your life journey.


"Dinner Dialogue"

Guided by global issues, this segment will spark intellectual exchange among guests. Each table will have a mysterious envelope containing a thought-provoking global question. Guests may choose to discuss it during the meal, encouraged to share their insights and explore different perspectives through relaxed yet profound conversation. Finally, each table will select a representative to share the discussion outcomes, summarized by the host, allowing ideas to converge during this elegant dinner.


"Musical Appreciation"

From classical to modern, from East to West, as notes flow, they outline the convergence and resonance of different civilizations, letting music become a bridge beyond language, adding a magnificent melody to this feast of ideas and culture. This night, we will raise our glasses together at Rhodes House—a place where history and future meet—writing an unforgettable, elegant evening through intellectual exchange and flowing melodies.
