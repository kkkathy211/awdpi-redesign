About AWDPI



AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development.


Evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project) established in 2020, AWDPI now operates in multiple countries across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams in Belgium, the UK, the US, Australia, and Thailand. We have built a broad global cooperation network with United Nations system organizations, relevant government agencies, international organizations, multinational corporations, and leading academic institutions.


We consistently uphold the core values of "Participation, Inclusion, and Empowerment," providing end-to-end rescue and support for Asian women in crisis, building cross-cultural peer support networks, advancing gender equality through academic research and policy advocacy, and helping every Asian woman access equal opportunities and a life of dignity.


As a key on-the-ground team in the Asia-Pacific region, the Australia National Office actively collaborates with local communities, legal institutions, and social service providers, focusing on the real needs of Asian women in Australia through referral services, community education, rights advocacy, and community engagement, continuously advancing local gender equality and multicultural inclusion.

AWDPI Leadership Role System Overview 

At AWDPI, departments are typically led by a designated head who ensures operational stability and continuity within the volunteer framework. As core team members, department heads carry primary responsibility for team coordination, task delegation, and project advancement. If a head needs to step down, they must communicate in advance and complete a full handover according to organizational procedures to ensure smooth continuity. When a head position is vacant, the organization will first consider internal recommendations of outstanding members; if no suitable candidate is available, the management team will temporarily assume the role until a qualified head is found. The AWDPI leadership system aims to ensure long-term organizational stability while offering volunteers opportunities for growth and leadership development.
