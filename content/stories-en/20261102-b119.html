About Penn AWDA



Penn Asian Women Development Association (Penn AWDA)


AWDPI | Asian Women Development Plan International


Penn AWDA is a newly established official student organization at the University of Pennsylvania, primarily focused on the development and support of Asian women, while remaining open to students of all ethnicities, genders, and backgrounds. We aim to build an inclusive, open, and mutually supportive student community, enabling more individuals who identify with this mission to participate and collectively advance projects and issues related to women's development, education, career growth, and social impact.


The work of Penn AWDA extends beyond event organization. We will operate across five core functional areas: community support and resource navigation, personal development and events, research and policy, partnerships and external relations, and media and communications. These five functional directions will guide our work.


Specifically, we aim to help students connect with on- and off-campus support resources, provide women with opportunities for personal growth, career advancement, entrepreneurship, and education; promote knowledge sharing and cross-disciplinary exchange through lectures, forums, mentorship programs, and courses; conduct research on issues such as Asian women, gender equality, women's technological advancement, and Women in STEM; establish collaborations with universities, corporations, nonprofit organizations, and international institutions; and expand the organization's influence through content creation and media outreach.


Penn AWDA is currently forming its inaugural team. The five functional departments represent the organization's primary focus areas at this stage but will not limit future development. We welcome members to propose new project ideas based on their interests, expertise, and resources, and to further expand into new functional areas or project teams as organizational needs and practical demands allow.



Organizational Structure


Penn AWDA is led by an Executive Board (core management team), with five functional departments under it:

  1. Community Support and Resources Department | Community Support and Resources Department

  2. Personal Development and Events Department | Personal Development and Events Department

  3. Research and Policy Department | Research and Policy Department

  4. Partnerships and External Relations Department | Partnerships and External Relations Department

  5. Media and Communications Department | Media and Communications Department



Recruitment Information



This recruitment is open to students and scholars at the University of Pennsylvania and other universities in Pennsylvania. Some positions support remote participation, and we welcome applicants from other locations who can commit consistently.


In cases where qualifications and team needs are comparable, we will prioritize applicants who can consistently participate in in-person activities and maintain ongoing communication with the team.


We value members' initiative, responsibility, and tangible contributions, and do not require all applicants to have identical experiences. Whether you excel in research, writing, event planning, education, design, community management, business development, or wish to gain experience in these areas, we encourage you to apply for positions that suit you.



Role Structure | Departmental Position Structure

Each functional department at Penn AWDA will ideally have 1–2 Chairs and recruit several Fellows.

Penn AWDA Organizational Chart


Chair | Department Head

Responsible for overall departmental management, including developing work plans, advancing key projects, coordinating team assignments, monitoring progress, and communicating and collaborating with the Executive Board and other departments. Chairs are primarily accountable for departmental outcomes and project implementation.


Fellows | Department Members

Participate in specific departmental tasks and projects, taking on different responsibilities or project directions based on personal interests, experience, and skills. We encourage Fellows to proactively propose project ideas and engage in the entire process from initial conception and resource coordination to execution, gradually taking on greater responsibilities through hands-on work.



Flexible Structure | Flexible Departmental Setup


The current five departments represent Penn AWDA's main focus areas at this stage. We will make appropriate adjustments to departmental divisions, staffing, and working mechanisms based on actual recruitment outcomes, team capabilities, and project needs.


Members who wish to pursue new projects or topics beyond the current departmental mandates are also welcome to propose them proactively. For ideas that are clearly defined, feasible, and aligned with Penn AWDA's overall mission, we may support the establishment of new project directions or working groups, which could potentially evolve into independent departments in the future.



Term | Duration


The term of office for all Chairs and Fellows is at least one year.


For candidates applying to the Executive Board core management team, all else being equal, we will prioritize those who can commit to longer terms, maintain consistent involvement, and contribute to the organization's sustained development.

Community Support and Resources Department | Community Support and Resources Department

Provide peer support, resource navigation, and relevant information to Penn students, helping those in need understand and access professional support resources both on and off campus, while also conducting resource education related to gender-based violence prevention, wellbeing, student safety, and community mutual aid.


Main Responsibilities:

  • Offer basic peer support and active listening, helping students understand available support options.

  • Compile and regularly update resources related to women's development, gender equality, mental health, and rights protection at the University of Pennsylvania and in the Philadelphia area, including Penn Women's Center (PWC), Penn Violence Prevention (PVP), Center for Civil Rights (CCR), Pan-Asian American Community House (PAACH), The College Office, graduate affairs offices of individual schools, Graduate Student Center (GSC), Office of Student Affairs (OSA), Student Health and Counseling, as well as off-campus support organizations such as WOAR, Women Against Abuse, and RAINN, providing Penn students with clear and reliable resource guidance.

  • Assist students, based on their consent, in contacting appropriate professional resources and provide necessary companion-based referrals.

  • Maintain contact with relevant Penn departments and student support organizations to stay informed about the latest policies, trainings, and support channels.

  • Organize activities related to Gender-Based Violence, Healthy Relationships, Consent, Wellbeing, safety, and resource utilization.

  • Develop and maintain the Penn AWDA Resource Guide, clearly outlining the purpose, applicable scenarios, and contact information for different resources.

  • Organize community support and mutual aid activities, understand member needs, and continuously improve the support system based on feedback.


Specific Requirements:

  • Interest in Student Support, Gender Equality, Wellbeing, or Community Service.

  • Strong listening, communication, and empathy skills.

  • Ability to respect personal boundaries, privacy, and autonomy.

  • Strong sense of responsibility regarding sensitive information and willingness to undergo relevant training.

  • Prior experience in Peer Support, Advocacy, or Community Service is preferred.


Support Boundaries:

Penn AWDA provides Peer Support, Resource Information, and Resource Navigation, but does not offer psychotherapy, medical advice, legal counsel, case investigations, or Title IX determinations, nor should it be considered a Penn-designated Confidential Resource. In cases requiring professional support, we will assist individuals, based on their wishes and specific needs, in understanding appropriate official channels.

Personal Development and Events Department | Personal Development and Events Department

Responsible for Penn AWDA's personal development, career advancement, entrepreneurship, education, courses, and lecture events, providing members with diverse opportunities for learning, exchange, practice, and professional growth. Department members may participate in different project groups or cross-project collaborations based on their interests and expertise; it is not required for each member to handle all work areas simultaneously.


Main Responsibilities:

  • Design and operate the Penn AWDA Mentorship Program, connecting alumni, professionals, founders, researchers, and industry leaders through initiatives such as mentor-mentee matching, one-on-one mentoring, small group mentoring, and office hours.

  • Plan career development events on topics such as resume writing, interviews, networking, personal branding, and career strategy, host Career Conversations across fields including STEM, Business, Policy, Entrepreneurship, Academia, and Nonprofit, and integrate opportunities such as internships, fellowships, research positions, leadership roles, and conferences.

  • Actively explore collaboration opportunities with on-campus entrepreneurship and innovation platforms such as Penn Venture Lab, Penn Wharton Innovation Fund, Startup Challenge, Penn Health-Tech, and Penn Center for Innovation (PCI), to promote women's participation in startup competitions, student venture fund programs, and innovation practices. Invite female founders, investors, product leaders, and technology industry professionals to share their entrepreneurial and career development experiences. Organize thematic lectures, panel discussions, networking events, and innovation initiatives focusing on topics such as Women in Tech, AI, FemTech, Women's Health, Technology for Social Good, and women's entrepreneurship, to foster communication, growth, and leadership development for women in technology, entrepreneurship, and investment fields.

  • Plan and organize seminars, panels, fireside chats, roundtables, forums, and workshops focusing on themes such as Asian Women, Leadership, Technology, Entrepreneurship, Gender Equality, Global Development, and Social Impact.

  • Research and contact professors, corporate executives, entrepreneurs, investors, researchers, and international organization representatives both on and off campus, manage guest invitations, coordination, speaker briefing, agenda setting, discussion questions, and moderator guides, and oversee hosting, guest reception, on-site execution, and post-event reviews.

  • Collaborate with on-campus student organizations, startup incubators, companies, and external partners to co-host lectures, industry forums, career development workshops, and cross-organizational initiatives, expanding Penn AWDA's professional network, resource integration capacity, and project impact.


Specific Requirements:

  • Interest in one or more of the following areas: personal development, career advancement, entrepreneurship, education, event planning, or women's development.

  • Strong communication, information gathering, organizational coordination, or project execution skills.

  • Willingness to proactively propose ideas and participate in the full process from initial planning to implementation.

  • Ability to communicate effectively with members, guests, mentors, and partner organizations from diverse backgrounds.

  • Adequate time management skills, with consistent availability and ability to complete tasks on time.

  • Prior experience in entrepreneurship, career development, mentorship, event planning, teaching, or related fields is preferred.


Position Characteristics:

This department is especially suitable for students seeking to gain experience in event planning, project management, career development, entrepreneurial practice, educational programs, or external guest coordination. Applicants may choose their primary area of involvement based on their expertise and explore other areas through actual projects.

Research and Policy Department | Research and Policy Department

Responsible for Penn AWDA's research and policy analysis on issues concerning Asian women, gender equality, women's technological advancement, and related public issues, providing research, data, and evidence-based support for the organization's projects, events, and advocacy initiatives.


Main Responsibilities:

  • Conduct research on topics such as Asian Women, Gender Equality, Women in STEM, and Women's Development.

  • Organize student research projects and research working groups.

  • Carry out literature reviews, data collection, interviews, qualitative research, and quantitative analysis.

  • Write research briefs, policy briefs, special reports, and other research outputs.

  • Explore collaboration opportunities with Penn professors, labs, research centers, and related research initiatives.

  • Compile and analyze key policies, data, and research related to Asian women and gender issues.

  • Provide research, data, and evidence-based support to other Penn AWDA departments.

  • Help members understand RA opportunities, academic conferences, and other research resources.

  • Establish the Penn AWDA Research Archive and promote the publication of research findings as project circumstances allow.


Specific Requirements:

  • Interest in research, policy, data, gender equality, Asian women, or Women in STEM.

  • Strong skills in information gathering, reading, analysis, and writing.

  • Ability to carefully verify information sources and follow basic research guidelines.

  • Interest in Quantitative Research, Qualitative Research, or Data Analysis.

  • Prior experience in Research, Data Analysis, or Policy Writing is preferred.
