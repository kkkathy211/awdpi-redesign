Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project




The Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project is dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and promoting gender equality, providing comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence.


By building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, and by uniting broader youth-driven nonprofit forces, the project advocates public awareness and support for the living challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. It aims to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.

Toxics Free Future

TOXICS-FREE CORPS



Toxics Free Future is a public welfare initiative launched by Shenzhen Zero Waste Environmental Public Welfare Development Center (shortened to Shenzhen Zero Waste), aiming to promote chemical safety and public health.


Through independent testing, science communication, and corporate advocacy, it works to ensure strict control of harmful chemicals in consumer products, helping the public avoid health risks. It also collaborates with various sectors of society to advance institutional development and industry practices for sound chemical management.

Shanghai Duoyue Public Cultural

Development Center READMORE




Shanghai Duoyue Public Cultural Development Center is a professional nonprofit organization specializing in promoting reading, with three flagship programs: City of Books, Miaomiao Reading, and Duoyue Reading Bridge. Duoyue's mission is to cultivate more lifelong readers in China, using reading to bridge education, illuminate culture, and open minds.


Over the years, it has built a broad reading collaboration ecosystem in Shanghai, connecting government, libraries, publishers, scholars, and grassroots organizations, and has been covered by media outlets including CCTV, China National Radio, Jiefang Daily, Youth Daily, and Shanghai Television.

NatureFields Nature Fields




Founded in 2019, NatureFields is a nonprofit organization focusing on air and soil environmental governance. Its mission is to address neglected environmental issues, with a vision of blue skies, clean air, and naturally sweet fruits and vegetables. Current advocacy campaigns include cleaning port air, reducing carbon and pollution from coal power, and minimizing pesticide and packaging waste.
