When traditional life paths fail to define one's true self, and when societal expectations clash with inner voices, how can women find their own strength within multiple constraints? In this lecture, three speakers will address these questions through their personal "her" practices.


They will share from their own stories: Women's choices never need to be standardized. Whether it is career breakthroughs,, life transitions, or relationship awakenings, we can all become the protagonists of our own lives, defining happiness and shaping possibilities in our own ways.

Speaker Introductions and Presentation Topics

Speaker One: Li Yihan (Kala)



  • Independent anthropologist and curator

  • Founder of Kapila Studio

  • Master's in Anthropology from Fudan University, Master's in Heritage Studies from Leiden University, Netherlands

Lecture Topic

"Hating Every Job You Do? Create Your Own Instead: A Humanist Researcher's Journey to Building a One-Person Company"

Core Content Overview

Exploring real-life cases of women's growth through non-traditional career paths, practical experiences in commercializing academic research, and zero-cost entrepreneurial insights for women building one-person companies


Speaker Two: Qiu Siyu



  • Master's in Mental Health Psychology from the University of Edinburgh, UK

  • Head of the Psychology Division at AWDPI

  • Founder of MosLife, a global Chinese public-interest psychological counseling network

Lecture Topic

"I See You—I Don't Want to See Myself"

Core Content Overview

Revealing the hidden influence of social and family environments on women's choices, common psychological traps women face in intimate relationships, and possibilities for establishing healthy relationship models


Speaker Three: Grace



  • Double degree in Psychology and Economics from the University of Virginia

  • Narrative therapy counselor with over 1,300 hours of counseling experience

  • Founder of Dodo | Du Space, curator of "Flow Path Origin Heart," a mind-body practice community

  • Nearly 10 years of professional experience in management consulting and tech entrepreneurship

Lecture Topic

"Thirty and Beyond—From Corporate Elite to Independent Career: Explorations and Reflections on Transition"

Core Content Overview

Reflecting on how mainstream culture shapes women's career choices through personal experiences, and exploring how to navigate the boundaries between personal paths and mainstream culture


Key Lecture Highlights

Exploring and breaking boundaries

How can humanities and social science researchers break traditional career boundaries and find their place in the market? How can academic research be transformed into public expression and commercial value—finding balance between "the moon and sixpence"? Exploring the boundaries of disciplines and professional norms as a form of practicing gender boundary exploration.

Women's roles in intimate relationships

How can women maintain self-boundaries in intimate relationships, and how can they navigate conflicts between family expectations and personal values? From being trapped in repetitive, maze-like struggles to building healthy relationship models—a profound internal dialogue about self-awareness and growth.

Women in career and identity expansion

At the turning point of turning thirty, how can women explore the self and shape identity through choices and transitions? How can narrative therapy and postmodernist theories help women re-examine mainstream culture and social expectations, expanding both life and psychological space? In gender equality advocacy, how can community support become a vital force for mutual inspiration and collective growth among women?
