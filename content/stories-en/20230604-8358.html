01 About Us


Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project is a growing international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against overseas Chinese women, and promoting their empowerment and development.





We provide rescue and support services for domestic violence survivors. Through a comprehensive assistance system and a robust overseas support network, we have established an effective model for combating gender-based violence, meeting the basic needs of overseas Chinese women in terms of prevention, emergency response, and long-term support.


We build community connections for overseas Chinese women. By creating online community networks and offline mutual support groups, we help prevent isolation and provide an international platform for communication, expression, learning, and mutual aid.


We enhance the influence and potential of Chinese women. We host annual forums to showcase contributions, share experiences, examine challenges, and discuss solutions. We also organize workshops and seminars to strengthen Chinese women's employment skills and leadership capabilities.


We conduct public awareness campaigns, research, and policy advocacy. By building broad partnerships with United Nations agencies, relevant government departments, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions, we promote greater societal attention to overseas Chinese women.

 02 Recruitment Information


Legal Affairs Department (Office of the General Counsel)

Avoice Legal Affairs Department Ongoing recruitment volunteers.

The Legal Affairs Department is responsible for organizational governance at both the headquarters and national office levels, participates in developing and supervising the implementation of organizational policies, manages legal risks across the organization and its operations, and provides day-to-day legal support including contract drafting and review, legal research, and other services. In addition, the Legal Affairs Department coordinates the work of external legal counsel.

The Legal Affairs Department assigns members to the national offices in the United States, the United Kingdom, and Belgium to handle local legal matters. There is currently a shortage of legal affairs volunteers at the national offices.

Legal Affairs Volunteers (Headquarters and National Offices)


Location:

  • Headquarters positions: remote work

  • National offices: currently remote work, but applicants must reside locally (United States, United Kingdom, Belgium)

Responsibilities:

  • Assist in maintaining effective organizational governance, design governance structures, draft and revise governance documents, and support the board and organizational secretariat in researching legal issues related to governance

  • Support the organization's legal registration in multiple countries, ensure national office operations comply with local laws and regulations, prepare and submit annual reports and other required disclosures or filings across multiple jurisdictions, and maintain communication with national nonprofit regulatory authorities

  • Draft and revise internal management systems, including but not limited to data compliance, intellectual property, financial management, volunteer code of conduct, and external cooperation policies, and supervise their effective implementation

  • Conduct periodic internal legal compliance due diligence; perform quality control on the Legal Support Division's work upon request

  • Provide legal advice to the board and senior management

  • Support program departments by participating in their work, providing legal advice, and drafting and reviewing various contracts and other legal documents

  • Assist in dispute resolution matters

  • Liaise with law firms, manage external legal counsel, and coordinate their work

Requirements:

  • Enrolled in or graduated from a reputable domestic or international university; applicants should generally be studying law or hold a law degree; non-law students who intend to pursue or have been admitted to a law degree program may be considered on a case-by-case basis for the internal training program

  • Experience working or interning at well-known domestic or international law firms or corporate legal departments; outstanding law students without internship experience may be considered on a case-by-case basis for the internal training program

  • Strong academic performance, excellent learning and comprehension skills, intellectual curiosity about complex legal issues, and proactive attitude; good teamwork skills

  • Outstanding legal research, analytical, and application abilities; excellent work capacity

  • Ability to use English as a working language; proficiency in other foreign languages (especially French) is preferred

  • Bar admission in New York State, the United Kingdom, or Belgium is preferred

  • Availability of more than seven hours per week and commitment for at least three months is preferred

Note 2: Avoice allows volunteers to serve in multiple departments, but this requires additional time commitments.

Note 3: The Avoice Legal Affairs Department offers an internal training program for law students interested in legal service work, allowing them to experience how real legal work is conducted. Interested applicants should contact[email protected](Zhou Yitong), cc[email protected], stating their interest, academic transcripts, and available working hours.

Legal Support Division, Rescue and Support Department

The Legal Support Division provides legal assistance to individual domestic violence survivors, compiles country-specific legal reports, offers legal consultations, and provides operational management and guidance for legal support work at national offices.

Legal Support Volunteers

Location:

  • Headquarters positions*: remote work

  • National offices: currently remote work, but applicants must reside locally (United States, United Kingdom, Belgium)

Note 4: Applicants for positions at the headquarters or the Belgium office of the Legal Support Division are kindly requested to confirm volunteer availability with the head of the Legal Support Division via the contact information below. If positions are not currently open, applicants may submit their applications in advance to join the Avoice talent pool. Avoice will contact them as soon as recruitment resumes for these roles.

Responsibilities:

  • Connect with individuals seeking help, provide legal aid services, and refer them to appropriate legal aid organizations or law firms according to their specific needs.

  • Develop legal aid handbooks

  • Conduct research and summarize legal aid cases

  • Assist national offices in organizing "anti-domestic violence" legal awareness campaigns locally

  • Improve the database of anti-domestic violence laws in various countries; research and compile anti-domestic violence laws, and produce Chinese-language legal education materials tailored to local Chinese communities

  • Improve the database of legal aid institutions in project countries, establish partnerships with local legal aid organizations and law firms, and build multi-channel referral services for those seeking assistance.

Requirements:

  • Lawyers or law students (senior undergraduates, master's, or doctoral candidates preferred)

  • Passionate about women's rights protection and interested in foreign laws related to women's rights (applicants with overseas study experience or familiarity with foreign legal systems are preferred)

  • Possess basic legal aid capabilities (bar admission preferred; prior legal aid experience preferred)

  • Possess basic legal writing skills (law firm internship experience or participation in moot court competitions such as Jessup, CIETAC, ICC Moot, IHL Moot, or ICC Court Moot is preferred)

  • Possess basic English proficiency (proficiency in other minor languages is preferred)

  • Possess empathy and a sense of responsibility, treat those seeking help with kindness, patience, sincerity, and humility; have good teamwork awareness, communication skills, and time management abilities, and treat other members of the team equally and kindly. 

  • Availability of more than seven hours per week and commitment for at least three months is preferred

Finance Department

Avoice Finance Department Ongoing recruitment volunteers.

The Finance Department is responsible for the organization's financial management, budget planning, fundraising management, financial reporting, and strategic planning. The Finance Department assigns members to national offices in the United States, the United Kingdom, and Belgium to handle local financial matters.

Finance Volunteers (Headquarters and National Offices)

Location:

  • Headquarters positions: remote work

  • National offices: currently remote work, but applicants must reside locally (United States, United Kingdom, Belgium)

Responsibilities:

  • Develop Avoice's strategic plan under the guidance of the Board's Strategy Committee

  • Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial budgets based on strategic development needs of various departments; regularly track financial performance of program departments and adjust financial targets accordingly; closely coordinate with the Fundraising Management Division to establish processes and standards for "funding application-use-accounting"; analyze financial budgets of comparable international nonprofit organizations and summarize best practices in multinational nonprofit financial planning

  • Maintain close communication with finance sub-units at national offices to ensure financial coordination between headquarters and national offices; assist national offices in financial reporting and accounting management

  • Establish standard procedures for budget applications from program departments, define budget ranges for different activities; monitor and analyze budget application progress; manage Avoice's corporate accounts and approve and record funding requests from program departments

  • Prepare monthly project performance analysis reports, and semi-annual and annual financial reports; conduct cost structure analysis of Avoice's operations and identify opportunities for cost optimization; improve the accounting system and enhance the accuracy and efficiency of financial accounting

  • Conduct supervisory audits on Avoice's finances, operations, contracts, and institutional processes; perform project audits for major rescue operations; conduct term-end and departure audits for key project leaders; regularly submit internal audit reports

  • Assigned to the National Office to handle accounting and financial oversight; prepare tax returns and manage all tax filings and tax assessments; develop and implement specific accounting and financial systems, including general ledger preparation, financial statements, budgeting, auditing, and volunteer expense reimbursement.

  • Liaise with accounting firms, manage external consultants, and coordinate their work.

Requirements:

  • Strong command of financial knowledge, with a background in finance or accounting; candidates who have passed CPA, CFA, or ACCA exams are preferred; familiarity with accounting software is preferred.

  • Graduated from a well-known university at home or abroad, with strong learning ability and enthusiasm for learning new things.

  • Prior internship experience in finance departments of well-known enterprises or reputable accounting firms is preferred.

  • Familiarity with nonprofit accounting systems in China, Belgium, the UK, or the US; experience in nonprofit operations is preferred.

  • Work experience in finance in Belgium, the UK, the US, or other regions is preferred.

  • Availability to work more than seven hours per week and commit to at least three months is preferred.
