Whether you are a college student just entering the workforce or a pioneering young entrepreneur, if you struggle with lack of industry resources, practical opportunities, and guidance from top-tier mentors, applications opening March 20 for the 17th Dream Workshop Youth Employment and Entrepreneurship Program will open up a whole new horizon for you.


  

16 years of cultivation, members' footprints spanning five continents

this summer, the 17th edition brings fresh innovations


Dream Workshop Youth Talent Development Center (shortened to Dream Workshop) is a non-profit organization dedicated to youth talent exchange, introduction, and cultivation. Since its launch in 2009, the "Dream Workshop Youth Employment and Entrepreneurship Talent Program" has successfully held 16 editions.


Currently, Dream Workshop members' footprints span five continents, over 30 countries and regions. Dream Workshop has established more than 30 workstations both domestically and internationally, deeply collaborates with numerous prestigious universities, and is highly popular among young students worldwide.


Over 16 years, Dream Workshop has gathered outstanding mentors from fields including technology, finance, media, and the internet, built a nationwide network of university volunteers, and established diverse platforms including entrepreneurship incubation, employment guidance, corporate visits, and workplace internships, supporting youth development.


The 17th Dream Workshop Youth Employment and Entrepreneurship Talent Program, launching in summer 2025, will upgrade its content and launch "Beijing Dream Incubation Camp," "Visits to Leading Enterprises," "CEO Time," "Artificial Intelligence Study Tour," "Dream Seed Program," "Direct Pathway to Internship and Employment," "Mentor Thought Classes," "Microfilm Competition," "Innovation and Creativity Competition" and other diverse projects, offering rich activity formats and practical resource support to build a stage for college students to realize their dreams.




   Mentor Messages for the Global Launch of the 17th Dream Workshop Youth Program



From industry leaders to cross-boundary practical projects

Dream Workshop continues to grow with you through resources


1. Top-tier mentor lineup: zero-distance access to industry leaders

Over 50 invited mentors from fields including technology, media, finance, and artificial intelligence—including entrepreneurs, economists, public welfare leaders, and frontline media professionals—will deliver lectures and guidance, using real-world experience to point you in the right direction.



2. Cross-boundary practice: deep integration of technology × media × finance × AI

· Participate in the Beijing Dream Incubation Camp: Mentors and counselors will guide teams throughout, allowing you to spend ten days with over 100 outstanding young people from around the world, transcending geographical and cultural boundaries to engage in in-depth discussions on diverse themes.


· Visit leading enterprises: Conduct in-depth visits to benchmark companies across technology, finance, and media fields, experiencing corporate culture up close and observing core business operations.


· Experience AI study tours: Focused on practical application, integrating high-quality AI resources, enabling a leap from knowledge accumulation to applied capability during the study tour.


· Participate in the Innovation and Creativity Competition: From public welfare proposals to business concept design, with mentor support for implementation, centered on "practical incubation + top-tier resources", building a complete value chain for youth from idea to industry.


· Deeply engage in the Microfilm Competition: Young people worldwide use images as their pen, focusing on contemporary issues in technology, culture, and more, producing films judged by renowned directors and frontline media professionals.


· Fully lead two major galas: From the Dream-Building Opening Ceremony to the Starlight Night Closing Ceremony, Dream Workshop members take full charge from planning to stage execution.



3. Global youth community: co-create the future with 3,000+ alumni

Members come from top universities worldwide including Harvard, Oxford, Yale, Tsinghua, Peking University, Zhejiang University, and the University of Hong Kong, covering diverse fields such as technology, finance, media, public welfare, and AI. Here, cross-boundary collisions spark inspiration, and networks support long-term development..



4. Full-cycle support: leap from theory to practice

· Direct Pathway to Internship and Employment: Building a high-speed bridge to enterprises, outstanding Dream Workshop members can receive internal referrals for internship positions based on performance.


· Entrepreneurship support: Mentors provide careful guidance and full-process follow-up, helping entrepreneurial projects grow from concept to reality.


· Lifetime resources: Priority access to various cutting-edge summits, industry forums, and corporate open days; connections to internal referrals and entrepreneurial resources; ongoing access to industry updates and mentor guidance.

Open to undergraduate and graduate students from universities worldwide


 Application Requirements


(1) Undergraduate, master's, or doctoral students currently enrolled at universities in China (student ID or enrollment proof required)

(2) Chinese nationals currently enrolled at overseas universities

(3) Currently enrolled university students with interest in technology, media, finance, artificial intelligence, and related fields


 Application Period 


March 20 – July 25
