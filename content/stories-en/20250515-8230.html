Background of the Event

BACKGROUND

       Climate change and disaster risk have become among the most urgent global challenges. Frequent extreme weather events, ecosystem degradation, rising sea levels, and other issues not only threaten the sustainability of the natural environment but also profoundly impact human society's security and development. The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) warns that without immediate action, global warming will exceed the critical threshold of 1.5°C, triggering irreversible and catastrophic consequences. At the same time, the interplay between disaster risk and the climate crisis exacerbates complex problems such as food security, public health, and regional conflicts. Addressing this dual challenge urgently requires systemic innovation and global collaboration.


       Youth are a core force in climate action and disaster risk reduction. As the most creative and action-oriented group, young people not only bear responsibility for the future but also drive breakthroughs in solutions through technology empowerment, interdisciplinary thinking, and global perspectives. From advocating climate justice to developing AI-driven disaster reduction tools, from designing resilient cities to leading sustainable lifestyles, young innovators are redefining what is possible through innovation. Their participation can not only accelerate the implementation of technology but also foster social consensus, drive policy change, and inject vitality and hope into global climate governance.


Event Content

EVENT INFORMATION

1 · Overview of the Event


2025 Youth Climate Action and Disaster Risk Reduction Innovation Challenge Co-initiated by UNESCO Beijing Office, Integrated Research on Disaster Risk (IRDR), Chinese National Committee for Man and the Biosphere Programme (MAB), UNESCO International Centre on Space Technologies for Natural and Cultural Heritage (HIST), School of Social Sciences at Tsinghua University, Zhi-Shan College at Tsinghua University, and the Horizon Global Youth Development Plan. Under the theme of "Activating Youth Power, Building a Healthy Planet," the event focuses on core issues in climate action and disaster risk reduction. It invites experts from universities and research institutions, international organizations, and leading industry enterprises to deliver public lectures, guiding youth to recognize climate change and disaster risks and promoting knowledge on climate action and disaster reduction. The event includes a youth climate action and disaster risk reduction creative video competition, encouraging young people to spread awareness through video content. It also hosts a youth climate action and disaster risk reduction innovation hackathon, guiding youth to apply their professional knowledge and practical experience through team-based project innovation to explore new methods and pathways for addressing climate change and disaster risk. On this basis, it further promotes the establishment of a global youth network for climate and disaster risk action, uniting young talents committed to the field of climate change and contributing youth strength to building a healthy planet.


As a key component of the innovation challenge, the Youth Climate Action and Disaster Risk Reduction Innovation Hackathon aims to build a global collaborative platform for young innovators. It attracts youth teams to respond to the challenges of our time with wisdom and action, using innovative projects to explore cutting-edge approaches to climate adaptation and disaster risk prevention. Participants in the hackathon will form interdisciplinary innovation teams or introduce existing projects at the incubation or startup stage, applying their professional expertise, research findings, and practical experience to project design and concentrated presentation around three key themes. Teams are encouraged to adopt qualitative and quantitative research methods, proposing innovative solutions in areas such as technological innovation, social support, and legal safeguards to contribute to climate change mitigation and disaster risk reduction, with clear emphasis on project feasibility. | Semi-Final Round (June 1 – June 16)


※ Organization Method


The organizing committee will organize expert review meetings by track to score projects. Additionally, promotional videos or related materials will be uploaded to online media platforms, with points awarded based on like counts. Teams advancing to the semi-final must submit an updated project proposal and a 2-minute project video. The project video should vividly present the project's core concept, implementation process, and outcomes.

(1) Project Video Requirements:

◎ Duration: 1–2 minutes, not exceeding 2 minutes.   

◎ Must clearly explain the project's background, design, and expected outcomes.

◎ Must include one high-resolution cover image, in landscape 16:9 format. Content is unrestricted but should reflect project characteristics and be eye-catching.

3 · Evaluation Criteria


This innovation hackathon will follow the principles of openness, fairness, and impartiality to ensure every participating team has an equal opportunity to present their ideas and outcomes. The expert jury will conduct a comprehensive evaluation based on the project's impact, innovation, sustainability, and practicality.


A. Impact

—— The extent of the project's influence on society, environment, and economy, and its ability to promote sustainable development.

B. Innovation

——Whether the project demonstrates novelty, uniqueness, and foresight, and what innovative breakthroughs and transformations it achieves compared to existing solutions.

C. Sustainability

——Whether the project is feasible for implementation, capable of forming a public welfare or business closed loop, and able to establish a sustainable project model.

D. Practicality

—— Whether the project has real-world application value and can solve practical problems or meet market needs.

The "Horizon: Global Youth Development Initiative" brings together high-quality resources from international organizations, world-renowned universities, central and local government agencies, leading enterprises, and financial institutions both in China and abroad. Upholding the mission of "igniting youthful dreams, supporting personal growth, cultivating future leaders, and guiding youth power," the initiative is committed to aligning with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and their specific targets. It aims to raise youth awareness of sustainable development, inspire active participation, support youth-led sustainability practices, cultivate youth leaders in sustainable development, consolidate global youth wisdom and strength in this field, and drive youth worldwide to take concrete actions toward sustainable development.
