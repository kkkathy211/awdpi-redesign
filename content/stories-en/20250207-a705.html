 During your time overseas, are you doing okay?

An unfamiliar environment, fast-paced academics, heavy workloads and tight deadlines might have increased your stress; cultural differences, language barriers, and social adaptation may leave you feeling lonely even amidst crowds. Leaving behind familiar family and friends, facing various life challenges, sometimes you may carry countless words in your heart, yet don't know to whom you can speak…



  Those feelings of anxiety, confusion, injustice, and homesickness may strike in the dead of night, keeping you tossing and turning; those worries you want to express but find hard to put into words may have long been building up, yet you can't find an outlet to confide in. But please remember, you are not alone





A non-intrusive, good listener—a friendly "stranger"—might be exactly what you need!

Lichun (Start of Spring), a new beginning, warmth returns

Lichun, signifies new hope and symbolizes the revival of all things. The Ai Ting Ting Public Welfare Hotline will also officially return on February 3rd, during this vibrant season! We are still here, waiting for your voice.


Whether it's academic anxiety, daily life troubles, or late-night thoughts you can't bring yourself to share with friends—we are willing to be your emotional outlet, a safe, warm, non-judgmental space to listen.


"

Listening hotline, walking alongside you


We are not licensed professionals, but we are volunteers who have completed listening skills training and passed assessments—we understand your struggles and are willing to sincerely listen and accompany you.

During conversations, all content will be strictly confidential—this is the place to help you relieve worries and troubles you can't bring up with friends～



We will remain "strangers" in your daily life,

yet supporters in your emotions.


◀

Who we are?

▶

We are a project initiated by the CPSA Edinburgh Chapter. CPSA, full name Chinese Psychology Student Association Chinese Psychological Students Association, is a nonprofit organization registered in Washington State, USA, an academic and public welfare society composed of psychology students and scholars from both within and outside China. The Ai Ting Ting hotline is a public welfare project established after the organization expanded and systematized, aiming to provide emotional support and companionship to every student in need, helping you find a bit of warmth in overseas academic life.



Important notice


     —About our listeners and confidentiality measures


All listeners are psychology students without professional certification, who have received basic listening skills training and serve as volunteers after completing internships and passing assessments, providing free public welfare services.


During listening sessions, listeners will strictly keep all information confidential, and both parties will sign a confidentiality agreement.


—even internal association members have no right to access the conversation content.


Listeners will not initiate any real-life contact or interference with callers, striving to remain "strangers"


⚠️ The Ai Ting Ting hotline is not a professional psychological counseling or therapy service, nor a crisis or suicide intervention hotline. If you are experiencing such situations, please contact professional institutions directly for help.



Appointment process as follows:

✦

•

✦

Step 1: Find us!


Open WeChat and search: CPSA ED

Follow the Edinburgh Psychology Association "CPSA ED" official account Click on "Ai Ting Ting", "Appointment QR code" in the service menu to enter the appointment page.


  Step 2: Make an appointment


Log in to the appointment page, select a suitable time to book, and wait for our operator to contact you.

  Step 3: Fill in information


Provide your preferred nickname, preferred contact method (WeChat / Tencent Meeting / Zoom), and optionally specify the listener's gender and nickname. We will try our best to accommodate your request, but cannot guarantee matching.

  Step 4: Wait for matching


Staff will contact you around 6 pm on the day of your scheduled slot, match you with a listener, and send two documents: a disclaimer and a confidentiality agreement. Please read them carefully and sign the agreements. Remember to keep an eye on your phone messages.

  Step 5: Begin the session


You will be assigned a listener. After both parties have signed the agreement, the listening session officially begins.

  Step 6: End the session


After the session, you will receive a feedback survey, which you may fill out voluntarily.

We strongly encourage you to leave your experience feedback!

If your experience was positive, you may request to be matched with the same listener next time.
