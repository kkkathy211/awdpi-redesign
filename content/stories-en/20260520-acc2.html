About AWDPI



AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development.


It evolved from Avoice (Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in Belgium, the United Kingdom, the United States, Australia, and Thailand. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with organizations across sectors, including United Nations agencies, government departments in various countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions.


AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the challenges faced by Asian women and provides them with sustained protection and support. We strive to create more opportunities for Asian women to grow and thrive in social, economic, cultural, and educational spheres, helping them become a vital force for social progress.

We are committed to


providing support and assistance to survivors of domestic violence. Through a comprehensive support system and a robust overseas assistance network, we promptly respond to the needs of Asian women overseas in areas such as domestic violence prevention, emergency aid, and follow-up services.


building community connections. By establishing an "online community matrix" and "offline mutual support groups," we create an international platform for Asian women overseas to communicate, learn, and support one another, forming peer support networks that enable self-development.


enhancing women's influence. Through forums, seminars, workshops, and other activities, we actively facilitate multi-stakeholder collaboration to analyze experiences, study challenges, and explore solutions, showcasing the strength and potential of Asian women.


launching public awareness and advocacy initiatives. We actively conduct public outreach, academic research, and policy advocacy to increase global attention to Asian women overseas and promote improvements in relevant policies and development environments.

              AWDPI Leadership Roles – Brief Introduction to Selected Systems


              At AWDPI, departments and offices are typically co-led by two leaders to ensure stability, continuity, and mutual support within the volunteer-based structure. In some cases, one or more than two leaders may be appointed, adjusted flexibly by the organization according to project needs.


              As core members of the organization, leaders are responsible for team collaboration, task delegation, and project advancement. If a leader needs to step down, they must communicate in advance and complete a full handover according to organizational procedures to ensure smooth continuity of work.


              When a leadership position is vacant, the organization will first recommend outstanding members from within the department to take over; if no suitable candidate is available temporarily, the role will be temporarily filled by the co-founding team, Secretary General, or Youth Presidential Council until a suitable leader is found.


              The AWDPI leadership system aims to ensure the organization's long-term stable operation and provide volunteers with opportunities to grow and develop leadership skills.
