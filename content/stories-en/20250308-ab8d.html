March 8, 2025,

we mark the 115th International Women's Day.



In line with the initiative of UN Women, this year's theme is—

"For all women and girls: Rights, Equality, Empowerment."

(For ALL women and girls: Rights, Equality, Empowermen t) .


Social progress that enables every woman to enjoy respect and opportunity;

the fight for rights, so they no longer have to demand their inherent rights;

the pursuit of equality, so they do not constantly have to prove themselves at every step;

empowerment, so they dare to write their lives with dreams as their pen.

2025 also marks the 30th anniversary of the adoption of the Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action.


Since the adoption of the Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action at the Fourth World Conference on Women in 1995, significant progress has been made globally in advancing gender equality.


However, women still face numerous challenges in social, economic, and political fields.. The Global Gender Gap Report 2024, released by the World Economic Forum, shows that the global gender gap has narrowed to 68.5%, but at the current pace of progress, it will still take five generations—until 2158—to fully close the gender gap and achieve full gender equality.


Meanwhile, the Global Gender Gap Report also highlights several important and ongoing realities that deserve attention and continued action:



1. Women's representation in politics has increased, but access to top leadership remains limited.


Currently, women's opportunities to participate in politics are growing, which may help further advance global gender equality. However, despite increasing numbers of women entering politics, the proportion of women in top leadership positions in politics and industries worldwide remains low, resulting in women's underrepresentation in policy-making and decision-making processes.



2. Female labor force participation has rebounded, but imbalances across sectors persist.


With global economic recovery, women's labor force participation has risen from 62.3% post-pandemic to 65.7%, indicating an increased presence of women in the workplace. However, gender gaps remain evident across regions and industries. Data shows that in most economies and sectors, women's employment rates are still lower than men's, especially in high-paying fields with strong decision-making power, where this imbalance still requires further improvement.



3. The proportion of women in the tech industry is rising, but key positions remain unbalanced.


While the gender gap in the technology sector has narrowed somewhat, it remains significant. Since 2016, the proportion of women in artificial intelligence engineering has doubled, indicating growing female talent in this field. However, women remain underrepresented in STEM (science, technology, engineering, and mathematics) roles, with only 29% in entry-level positions and a mere 12.2% in executive roles. Moreover, women's participation in online learning courses in fields such as artificial intelligence, big data, programming, and cybersecurity remains below 31%, indicating that the gender gap in this sector persists. Enhancing women's skills training and career development opportunities remains a key focus for the future.

We will keep taking action!


On the occasion of the 115th International Women's Day, AWDPI hopes to join hands with like-minded friends to further contribute to a world where love and equality coexist.


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development, so that overseas Asian women can gain greater opportunities for advancement in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, and transform from a marginalized group into a vital force driving social progress.


Women's Day is not only a celebration, but also a crucial opportunity to drive change.


During Women's Month, AWDPI will host 10 online and offline events, holding in-depth discussions on issues such as women's rights, gender equality, and self-empowerment, advancing together with everyone to contribute to closing the gender gap and promoting gender equality.


On this important day, let us collectively pay attention to and support women's rights, calling for greater equality and inclusion.

May every woman be free to write her own story, and may the world's sky become broader because of their presence.
