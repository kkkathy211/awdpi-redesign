Hello everyone, welcome to the University Students Leader Club~

To help university students see more diverse paths for growth, overcome confusion and anxiety, clarify their goals, and master more effective methods for personal development,

the University Students Leader Club has partnered with Global Outstanding Youth Xi'an Community, University Student Volunteer Recruitment Network, and Global Outstanding Youth Leader Community to launch the public welfare project "University Growth Experience Book".

After co-creators and students jointly improved the question bank, we have finalized the list of questions.

We now invite 500 people to share your experiences based on this question bank and become co-creators of this book!

The collected stories will later be compiled into the "University Growth Experience Book" and released to the public for free distribution..


  Self-Exploration and Mindset  

●  What methods do you recommend for objectively assessing your strengths and weaknesses?
● If you don't like your major, what alternative paths are available? What preparations should you make?
● When choosing a major, should you prioritize what you love or what offers better job prospects?
● Do you think it's necessary to plan ahead for your four years in university or even your career? If so, how can you effectively plan?
● During your time in university, have you ever experienced real failure? How did you adjust your mindset and regain motivation when facing setbacks, failure, or a collapse of beliefs?
● Did you make any decisions or try anything during university that profoundly impacted your personal growth? What were the associated risks?
● How can one cultivate personal interests and integrate them with personal development?
● What were your greatest gains and regrets during university?
●  What exploration do you regret not trying during university?


  Study & Growth  

● What effective learning methods and techniques can you share to improve efficiency?
●  What "false effort traps" or "growth traps" are university students most likely to fall into? (e.g., excessive slacking, blindly following trends)
● What books, websites, apps, or platforms would you recommend to university students?
● How can the latest technologies (e.g., AI tools) be used to support personal growth?
● Is English (or other foreign languages) important? What are some effective ways to learn them?
● How important are academic grades? What role do they play in personal growth and future development?
● How do you deal with various failures such as exam failure, breakups, or job interview rejections?
●  What do you suggest university students do in their free time or during holidays? How can they use winter and summer breaks to gain an edge?
● When facing many options for further education, how can one choose the path that best suits them?
● What should you pay attention to during and after upgrading from a junior college to a bachelor's program?

  Life & Social  

●  How can students effectively use resources such as senior students, alumni, and mentors? Are there practical tips for building a network? 
● How can you identify "mentors" or "toxic people" around you? 
● If you have social anxiety or are an introvert (i-person), how can you overcome social anxiety? How can you build confidence?
● How to overcome the fear of public speaking? How can you train yourself to speak in public without nervousness?
●  How do you solve the problem of having many ideas and thoughts but being unable to express them to others?
● How should you respond to unfair treatment from professors or classmates to minimize internal stress?
● How can you learn to say no? How should you practice self-love?
● What advice do you have for university students regarding romantic relationships? What should be considered when choosing a partner?
● Should young people focus solely on "making money"? What are your suggestions for student part-time jobs? How should university students plan their finances?
● How should university students build awareness to avoid scams? Do you or someone you know have real-life experiences of being scammed?

  Practice & Internships  

● What benefits do club activities or student union work bring to personal growth? How can you choose the right student organization for yourself?
● Which certificates or competitions are particularly important during university?
● Which skills or habits developed during university have been most helpful for your later career development?
●  How can you bridge information gaps and find useful information among vast amounts of data?
● In what ways can participating in volunteer or public welfare activities significantly support university students' growth?
● How can you land your first internship opportunity? What channels or methods are effective?
● How can you gain real skills during an internship and avoid being stuck with menial tasks? If you do face a "menial task" phase, how should you adjust your mindset? 
● What off-campus growth opportunities do you know of or have experienced during your studies?

  Future Choices & Planning  

● How can you analyze whether you should enter the workforce directly or pursue postgraduate studies, civil service exams, entrepreneurship, studying abroad, or taking a gap year?
● How can you clarify your career direction? How do you determine if a career is right for you? What are some low-cost ways to test and learn from mistakes?
● When applying for positions outside your major, what aspects of your resume do employers value more? What should you pay attention to when job hunting across disciplines?
● What opportunities and challenges exist for positions that require a specific major versus those that don't? (Please describe based on your own experience)
●  What channels do you recommend for obtaining authentic industry information (to avoid being misled by the internet)?
● How can graduates from less popular majors break through in job hunting?
●  Do companies value undergraduate or postgraduate degrees more?
● How can you determine whether you are better suited for jobs within the formal system (e.g., government, public sector) or outside it?
● How can you tell if you are suited for a research career?
● What awareness and conditions are necessary for entrepreneurship? How can one start a business without capital or connections? What is the best timing—during university, after graduation, or after working?
● What are the differences between society and university life? After graduation, what matters most? 
● What industry or profession are you currently in? What hard requirements (e.g., degree, major, certificates, skills) are needed to enter this field? For university students who want to pursue this career, what specific career planning advice do you have? How can students lay a solid foundation for future career development during university?

  Co-Creation Partners  

Responsibilities of Co-Creation Partners
1. Project Preparation Phase: Participate in full-process co-creation, connect various resources
2. Project Collection Phase: The organization internally invites more than 10 individuals to participate in answering questions
3. Project Collection Phase: promote the project externally (e.g., via official accounts, Xiaohongshu, community posts, etc.)
4. After Collection Phase: A message or message of encouragement from the co-creation partner's representative for this project or for university students

Benefits for Co-Creation Partners
1. Deep brand logo exposure across all channels and stages (e.g., articles, posters, the "University Growth Experience Book", etc.)
2. In the "University Growth Experience Book", a dedicated section for the co-creation partner's brand introduction and the representative's message presented separately
3. In-depth collaboration in future events hosted by the organizer (summits, conferences, forums, interviews, sharing sessions, live streams, etc.) deep cooperation (co-hosting, speaker invitations, guest seats, co-mentoring, resource matching, etc.)
4. Access to connect with outstanding university students, outstanding young talents and more
5. Comprehensive support for other projects of the co-creation partner (talent, content, promotion through a new media network with over a million users, etc.)
6. Customized content sections based on the co-creation partner's industry or needs

The University Student Leader Club is a platform under Lingdaodao® for university students' growth and exchange. It offers free access to sharing sessions, events, communities, and more, helping students broaden their horizons, clarify their goals, and become better versions of themselves through listening to senior experiences and interacting with peers.
