Together we create, connecting endless possibilities! The launch ceremony of the Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium and the 2024 "Leaders Speak" (Shanghai) Salon was held in Shanghai, with the simultaneous unveiling of the Shanghai Huangpu HaiChuang Youth Leadership Development Center.
On October 27, under the guidance of the Shanghai Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese and the China Foundation for the Preservation of Chinese Cultural Heritage, the "Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium" was officially launched in Huangpu District, Shanghai. Initiated by the International Youth Leadership Development Special Fund of the China Foundation for the Preservation of Chinese Cultural Heritage and promoted by the Huangpu District Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese, the Consortium marked its launch with the unveiling of the "Shanghai Huangpu HaiChuang Youth Leadership Development Center" and the hosting of the 2024 "Leaders Speak" (Shanghai) Salon.

A highlight of the event was the official establishment of the "Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium." Founded by the International Youth Leadership Development Special Fund of the China Foundation for the Preservation of Chinese Cultural Heritage in Shanghai, and hosted through the HaiChuang Youth Leadership Development Center supported by the Huangpu District Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese, the Consortium serves as a bridge and platform connecting youth communities from elite universities worldwide. Positioned as a non-registered public service platform, it focuses on serving youth communities from top global universities, particularly targeting overseas Chinese youth. Its goal is to leverage the Consortium as a bridge for China-foreign connections, cultural exchange, and services for returnees, fostering close ties and deep collaboration between global elite university youth communities and local governments, mass organizations, and broader social resources.
Geng Jing, Chair of the China Foundation for the Preservation of Chinese Cultural Heritage, sent a congratulatory letter specifically for the event, expressing her hope that the Consortium will become a talent support hub for Chinese-style modernization and contribute to social development. Geng Jing noted that since its establishment, the Special Fund has been committed to identifying and nurturing outstanding youth and promoting exchanges between Chinese and foreign youth leaders. The launch of the Consortium represents a continuation and deepening of the Foundation's "Little Lightbulb" spirit. "We firmly believe that through the efforts of the Consortium, returnee youth and international youth will be provided with an even broader stage, helping them achieve greater success in innovation and entrepreneurship, job seeking, and promoting Chinese culture."

On behalf of Chair Geng Jing, Su Run, Deputy Secretary-General of the China Foundation for the Preservation of Chinese Cultural Heritage, delivered a speech, stating that the establishment of the "Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium" brings together the youth power of elite universities worldwide, building a broad platform for exchange and cooperation. Through this platform, it will help strengthen cultural identity and heritage among global descendants of the Chinese nation, promote exchanges and cooperation between Chinese and foreign youth leaders, and advance the international dissemination and innovation of Chinese culture.

Qi Quansheng, Chair of the Shanghai Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese; Su Run, Deputy Secretary-General of the China Foundation for the Preservation of Chinese Cultural Heritage; Cheng Dong, Vice Chair of the Shanghai Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese; Liang Yan, Chair of the Harvard University Political Economy Association; Yu Xinyi, founder of AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International; and Zhu Honghui, head of the University of Warwick alumni community in the UK, jointly activated the "lighting" device to launch the "Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium," marking its official launch.

It was introduced that the Consortium is dedicated to serving global youth alumni communities, focusing on cross-sector collaboration and resource sharing, aiming to promote in-depth exchanges among elite university youth worldwide. One of its core goals is to help global youth leaders better understand China's development opportunities, especially in areas such as technological innovation, cultural heritage, and policy development. By integrating resources from universities, youth communities, and governments both domestically and internationally, the platform will provide more youth with an open platform for information sharing and support.

Wang Mengdie, an international youth leadership representative and University of Manchester alumnus, moderated the Consortium's launch ceremony.

Qi Quansheng, Chair of the Shanghai Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese, stated that the Consortium's first cohort includes youth communities from a group of top global universities, forming a community alliance. This indicates that overseas Chinese federations will establish connections with more elite university youth communities and overseas Chinese youth, opening up new horizons and prospects for the federation's services to overseas Chinese youth talents and for supporting national and Shanghai development. He encouraged Consortium members to bravely become pioneers in national and social progress, skillfully serve as guardians of mutual understanding between peoples, and willingly act as promoters of youth growth and achievement.
"The establishment of the 'Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium' not only creates a platform for deep exchange and interaction between domestic and overseas youth, but is also an innovative initiative for jointly addressing global challenges and sharing opportunities under globalization," said Shi Haiyan, Vice Chair of the Huangpu Overseas Friendship Association of Shanghai. She expressed hope that the Consortium will gather talent, connect resources, and build bridges widely, play a leading role, and maximize the convergence of community strength from diverse cultural backgrounds to empower the growth and success of youth at home and abroad, contributing positively to mutual learning among world civilizations.

Deputy Secretary-General Su Run and Vice Chair Shi Haiyan jointly unveiled the plaque for the "Shanghai Huangpu HaiChuang Youth Leadership Development Center" and signed a strategic cooperation agreement between the Huangpu District Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese and the International Youth Leadership Development Special Fund of the China Foundation for the Preservation of Chinese Cultural Heritage. Both sides will work together to gather wisdom and strength from different fields, providing young entrepreneurs with a platform to showcase their talents and realize their dreams, jointly promoting the development of creative, innovative, and entrepreneurial initiatives by international youth in Huangpu, Shanghai, and even nationwide.

Deputy Secretary-General Su Run presented the "Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium" flag to Consortium representatives Xu Erjia and Zhu Jiahui, and together with Shen Cong, Director of the Overseas Chinese Affairs Division of the Shanghai Overseas Chinese Affairs Office, and Luan Xuchen, Director of the Grassroots Work Department of the Shanghai Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese, awarded "Coordinating Council" certificates in four batches to the first cohort of Coordinating Council representatives of the "Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium."
"When youth thrive, the nation thrives; when youth are strong, the nation is strong." Liang Yan, Chair of the Harvard University Political Economy Association; Li Zhuorui, Co-Chair of the Stanford China Education Practice and Innovators Association; and Zhu Honghui, head of the University of Warwick alumni community in the UK, jointly read the "Shanghai Declaration of the Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium." They emphasized, "The Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium will always be guided by international youth leadership and hosted by the HaiChuang Leadership Development Center, building an open, inclusive, and progressive organization. We welcome more aspiring youth to join us, to move forward hand in hand, and together create a new era for youth!"
"In this vibrant city of Shanghai, we have collectively witnessed the birth of the Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium. Let us join hands, use our youthful selves to create a youthful world, build a Chinese-style international youth community, and contribute youthful strength to building a community with a shared future for humanity! "

At the launch ceremony, Liang Yan, Chair of the Harvard University Political Economy Association, spoke on behalf of the first cohort of Coordinating Council members, stating that as inaugural Coordinating Council members of the Global Elite Universities Youth Community Consortium, they are ready to take this as a new starting point, gather talent resources from returnees and overseas Chinese youth, leverage the unique advantages of transnational youth communities, and contribute to technological innovation, high-level opening-up, and the construction of a socialist modern international metropolis with global influence.

Hu Xincheng, founder of the Zlead Global Sci-Tech Innovation Summit; Han Qihao, President of the Oxford China Student Union; Wu Dazhong, founder and Honorary Chair of the Columbia University student organization Global Youth Alliance for Sustainable Development (CGELA); Zhou Yi, President of the King's College London China Student Union; Guan Peng, President of the 9th Student Council of Tsinghua University's Schwarzman College and Co-Founder and Chair of the International Asian Cultural Association; and Su Xiaoxiao, a Belarusian social media influencer and Global Communication Envoy from Tsinghua University, among others—representing the first cohort of Coordinating Council members and leaders of elite university youth communities worldwide—participated in the event via video.

The 2024 "Leaders Speak" (Shanghai) Salon, focused on gathering returnee and overseas Chinese youth talent and guiding the ideological, technological, and cultural innovation direction of international youth communities, attracted youth leaders from over 80 renowned universities worldwide. Attendees included elite representatives from top institutions such as Harvard University, Oxford University, Stanford University, the University of Chicago, Columbia University, Tsinghua University, Peking University, Zhejiang University, and the University of Warwick in the UK. Coming from diverse academic backgrounds and covering fields such as political economy, technological innovation, social public welfare, and legal services, they fully demonstrated the diversity and cross-sector collaboration traits of international youth leaders. These youth leaders not only have outstanding achievements in their respective fields but also carry the mission of promoting global cooperation and innovation.
Chinese and foreign youth, along with the on-site audience, delivered speeches and discussions centered on the salon theme: "Understanding China, Integrating into Shanghai, and Playing the Role of Youth Communities as Bridges Connecting China and the World." Liang Yan, Chair of the Harvard University Political Economy Association; Li Zhuorui, Co-Chair of the Stanford China Education Practice and Innovators Association; Zhu Honghui, head of the University of Warwick alumni community in the UK; international youth leadership representatives Yang Zimeng and Huang Piaoying; Chen Zi, algorithm expert at Ant Group's Financial Insurance Division and University of New South Wales alumnus; and Mile, a Zimbabwean student in China, representative of the China-Zimbabwe Exchange Center, and PhD candidate at Zhejiang University, participated in the roundtable forum. They shared their respective communities' experiences and visions for the future. They unanimously agreed that information transparency, technological innovation, and policy support are crucial for youth personal development, and proposed establishing career guidance mechanisms and increasing internship and practical opportunities to help young people quickly integrate into the international community. In particular, youth communities representing scientific and technological innovation demonstrated their active role in advancing technological progress and social development by discussing hot topics such as AI and digital transformation.

Chen Xiaobin, alumnus of Columbia University, USA, and Deputy Executive Committee Member of the World Economic Forum's Global Shapers Community – Shanghai Chapter, co-hosted the "Leaders Speak" session with Zhu Honghui, head of the University of Warwick alumni community in the UK.

Experts from fields such as financial securities, news media, and sustainable development shared their insights on interpreting China's policies, seizing opportunities to integrate into China's modernization, using new media to tell China's stories well, and the ESG responsibilities of international youth in the new era, engaging in in-depth discussions on the opportunities and challenges facing youth development in a globalized context.

"Leaders Speak" is a networked interactive platform and offline salon brand meticulously developed by the International Youth Leadership Development Special Fund of the China Heritage Foundation, showcasing international youth leaders. In 2023, the "Leaders Speak" salon set sail in Huangpu, transitioning from offline to on-site participation, with many international youth community leaders attending the Shanghai salon as firsthand participants, contributors, and witnesses. The "Leaders Speak" salon has traveled from Shanghai to Beijing and then to Shenzhen, attracting hundreds of international youth leaders, including leaders of youth communities from top global universities, to actively participate. This time, the International Youth Leadership Development initiative once again partnered with the Huangpu District Overseas Chinese Affairs Federation to host this salon, aiming to deepen and expand exchanges and cooperation under the "Leaders Speak" platform, making it an important hub for contemporary international youth community dialogue and collaboration.
Statistical data shows that over 40% of the attending guests represent communities deeply engaged in science and technology innovation; 30% are active in public welfare and community building, fully reflecting the new generation of youth leaders' responsibility and commitment in advancing social progress and technological innovation; nearly 20% of the youth representatives are female leaders, demonstrating young women's active participation and leadership in gender equality and social governance.

Invited guests, including French international students—Benoit Sentenac (Sun Bowen) from the University of International Business and Economics (UIBE), Expert Pierre from Tongji University, Clara Corcuff (Gao Lanhua) and Gauthier Avognon (Gu Qi) from the Sino-European Business School at East China Normal University, and Alix Archambault from ESADE Business School in Spain—attended the on-site discussion and gave high praise to the "Leaders Speak" platform. They said the event not only deepened their understanding of Chinese culture and development but also allowed them to meet many like-minded peers.
"Returnee talents and overseas Chinese youth are not only vital bridges connecting China with the world but also a key force in driving the construction of international modern metropolises," said a representative from the Shanghai Overseas Chinese Affairs Federation. "We hope to gather global youth strength through the global top-university youth community, injecting new vitality into urban development with youth innovation." As the event organizers, the International Youth Leadership Development Special Fund of the China Heritage Foundation and the Huangpu District Overseas Chinese Affairs Federation stated that, based on their long-term attention to international youth community development, they aim to use the newly established Huangpu HaiChuang Youth Leadership Development Center in Shanghai as a platform to serve the global top-university youth community, encouraging more innovative and socially responsible youth leaders to participate and become leading forces in the new era.
Su Run, Deputy Secretary-General of the China Heritage Foundation, said that the "Leaders Speak" salon, having launched in Shanghai and in Huangpu, will surely expand from Huangpu to the whole of China and then to the globe. The International Youth Leadership Development Special Fund will continue to leverage the power of international youth civil organizations, enabling more young people to witness China, understand China, and interpret China, to lead their youth and co-write brilliant chapters, becoming recorders, promoters, and accelerators of the new era, and truly deserving international youth leaders.
