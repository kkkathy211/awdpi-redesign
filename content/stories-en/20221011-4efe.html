Introduction to the UK Chinese Women's Coalition


UKCWC, the UK Chinese Women's Coalition, is a UK-registered charity (registration number: 1193654) aiming to unite women within the Chinese community, reduce isolation and loneliness, and promote racial harmony.

Avoice is the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project. It is dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and advancing gender equality, providing comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence. Through building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, and by mobilizing broader youth-driven public welfare forces, the project advocates for public awareness and support regarding the challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. It strives to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from survivors of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners promoting gender equality and protecting vulnerable groups.
