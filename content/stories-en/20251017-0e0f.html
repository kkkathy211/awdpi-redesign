Have you ever felt:

it sometimes feels like playing a game without an instruction manual?

yet remain trapped in narratives of "how things should be"…


This time, we're not just talking about relationships—

we'll use our bodies to feel, art to express, and the power of community to reconstruct our own relationship scripts!



You'll take away these tangible benefits:

  • Gain a «multi-dimensional lens» to understand relationships, see the full spectrum of love and connection, and discover the diverse possibilities of relationships

  • A bodily switch to reboot authentic perception and reclaim the innate power of connection

  • An action-driven advocacy for building support networks, moving from "me" to "we"

  • A group of like-minded peers from psychology, art, and ecological communities to expand your growth-oriented network

What will we discuss? Two roundtables for deep dialogue

Roundtable One

Unlocking Relational Imagination: When Individual Struggles Become Collective Issues 

We'll discuss with psychologists, gender studies scholars, and cartoonists:

  • How can we move beyond "black-and-white" thinking in relationships? Say goodbye to "right-or-wrong" mindsets and embrace complex realities

  • While feminism brings strength, does it also bring new challenges?

  • In the digital age, how can we rebuild warmth in "the nearby" and reclaim embodied connection?



Roundtable Two

The Value and Construction of Diverse Community Networks

Hear from community founders and practitioners:

  • What's the magic formula that makes a community come alive? 🪄

  • From "my" vision to "our" blueprint—how do we face conflict and evolve together?

  • How can we build community networks where everyone is equally seen?



Special Experience Session

  • Opening with a theater experience: Open your senses in a safe and fun environment



 Art screening + Body workshop

  • Starting from "bodily awareness," use movement to weave space and rediscover organic connections with yourself and others



«Relationships begin with bodily presence»


When we put down our phones and return to sensing, when we breathe, interact, and empathize in the same space, real connection truly begins. This is both a mental refresh and a bodily awakening.


Event Highlights Preview

  1. [Brand-new Opening] Rewriting Relationship Scripts – First Experience in Drama Therapy

    Led by David, a registered psychologist from Australia, using drama techniques to guide you in embracing and seeing the possibilities within relationships. Break fixed patterns through safe, interactive experiences.

  2. Diverse guest lineup breaking down cognitive barriers

    We've brought together psychologists, artists, and community founders to explore the multiple possibilities of "modern relationships" from different angles—no standard answers, only inspiration and companionship.

  3. Not just "listening," but truly "experiencing"!

    Special session: Art screening + Body workshop, led by somatic artists, starting from "bodily perception" to re-understand the relationships between self, others, and the world.

  4. Relational Mini-Exercise: From "knowing" to "doing"

    We'll practice small embodied relational exercises together, noticing bodily signals through interaction and rediscovering your own relational rhythm.
