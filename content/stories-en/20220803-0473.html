Vol.1/ Content Overview

Brief introduction

Welcome to "Wild Voice Girls Observer," a series co-produced by Lean In Shenzhen and Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub.


Domestic violence is not mere entertainment gossip, nor is it just a private family matter; it should rightfully become a public issue, with victims' rights protected through judicial and institutional interventions.


Indeed, the term "domestic violence" is not unfamiliar to the general public, but when witnessing or personally caught in it, people are often blinded by the word "marriage."


Love, kinship, ethics, morality... these complex dimensions of judgment place heavy constraints on both victims and potential rescuers..


  • What defines and delineates domestic violence?

  • How can one identify domestic violence and practice self-rescue?

  • As individuals or organizations observing domestic violence from a third-party perspective, how can we contribute?

  • How can victims seek help and rebuild their mental health?


In this episode of "Wild Voice Girls Observer," we are honored to have Shulin, the U.S. lead of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, who has extensive experience in anti-domestic violence work overseas, to deeply discuss this topic, and we hope the insights shared here can support every individual struggling with domestic violence.


Vol.2/ Guest Introduction

Guest introduction

Shulin

U.S. lead of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, currently pursuing a master's degree at Harvard.


She worked for five years at Johns Hopkins University on global women's health (mainly in India and Africa), during which she volunteered part-time in anti-domestic violence efforts, including frontline hotline services and administrative fundraising, and also led a nonprofit organization dedicated to cultivating female leadership.

Vol.4/ Program Introduction

Column introduction

"Wild Voice Girls Observer" is a women's cross-boundary dialogue podcast co-produced by Lean In Shenzhen and Global Shapers Shenzhen Hub.


Each episode invites guests from diverse ages, professions, genders, and backgrounds to share their views on women's issues, aiming to break down communication barriers across different ages, identities, and backgrounds, explore multiple interpretations of the same women's issue through diverse perspectives, amplify voices of women in different fields and life stages, and offer more pathways for thinking about women's social issues.


Lean In Shenzhen: Lean In Shenzhen is a non-profit community dedicated to professional women, committed to building a diverse platform that awakens self-awareness, uncovers women's charm, and unlocks women's potential.


Global Shapers Community Shenzhen Hub: GSC Shenzhen is the only branch of the World Economic Forum's Global Shapers Community established in Guangzhou, targeting future leaders aged 18–28 with entrepreneurial experience and social contributions. The project aims to harness young people's innovation and passion to build a more inclusive and collectively prosperous world.
