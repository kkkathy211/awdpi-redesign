I. AWDPI Annual Forum


AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) was initiated in early 2020 at the onset of the global COVID-19 pandemic. It is a growing international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development. AWDPI has established a broad and effective network to combat domestic violence for Chinese women overseas facing threats of abuse, and promotes greater societal attention to Asian women through extensive collaborations with United Nations agencies, government departments, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. Among its key initiatives are the AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum and the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program). After more than three years of development, these projects have expanded to East Asia, Europe, North America, and Australia.

To summarize our efforts over the past three years and better plan for future development, AWDPI will host its Second Annual Forum (also known as the Second Avoice Chinese Women Global Development Forum), expanding from the first forum into three thematic sub-forums. This announcement is for the Call for Paper and Research Submissions.

For more detailed information about the AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum, please follow our official WeChat account, where we will gradually introduce our exciting forum agenda and highly anticipated distinguished guests!


II. Call for Papers and Research Submissions


1. Purpose of the Paper Review Session

  • To showcase AWDPI's academic standards and capabilities

  • To provide AWDPI members with opportunities to participate in academic activities

  • To build connections with more scholars in related fields

2. Paper Review Process

  • The AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum will allocate 2–2.5 hours specifically for the review and presentation of papers on relevant themes

  • We will select high-quality 4–6 submissions, each of which will be presented by the author in an 8-minute presentation, followed by approximately 10 minutes of feedback and suggestions from expert reviewers

  • If submissions are abundant, we will also select several high-quality papers for online poster presentations, where participants can "move between" different virtual rooms to view presenters explaining their academic posters

3. Eligibility for Paper Submission

 This call for papers is open to

  • current active AWDPI members

  • AWDPI reserve pool and former members

  • friends introduced by AWDPI members who express interest in participating

  • members of the public and students from all walks of life who have strong interest or ideas regarding the forum themes

4. Paper Submission Themes

Any topic related to areas AWDPI has worked on, is currently working on, or plans to engage in, including but not limited to:

  • movements against gender-based violence

  • protection of Asian women overseas

  • violence-free digital spaces initiative

  • women's leadership

  • women and health

  • women and intimate relationships

  • women's political and workplace empowerment

  • overseas Chinese women workers

All academic disciplines are welcome; we especially encourage interdisciplinary research submissions and participation, such as:

  • technology + philanthropy (e.g., women's online forums and their support for and current status in public sphere construction…)

  • policy + women's health (e.g., women's health from medical and social security perspectives…)

  • workplace + education (e.g., cultivating workplace competence among overseas Chinese women…)

  • law + psychology (e.g., psychological trauma in children exposed to domestic violence environments and its correlation with criminal behavior in adulthood…)

  • Paper topics and content should align with AWDPI's vision of "creating a world of love and equality for women" and its values of "participation, inclusion, and empowerment"

5. Paper Reviewers

We will invite AWDPI senior experts and renowned scholars in the field of women's rights to serve as paper reviewers

6. Paper and Research Selection Criteria

  • Submissions in Chinese or English are accepted; for other languages, please contact the Academic Department separately for consultation:[email protected]

  • clear purpose, distinct theme, sufficient evidence, logical structure, and concise language

  • must follow standard academic format, including title, author name, abstract, keywords, main text, and references

  • recommended length: no more than 15,000 Chinese characters or 8,000 English words

  • papers must be original; plagiarism is prohibited, and no infringement of others' legal rights is allowed

  • previously published papers will not be accepted

  • This forum especially encourages authors with strong interest or ideas about the themes—including those who have not yet participated in formal academic forums—to actively submit. Experimental studies, literature reviews, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and other types of submissions are all welcome to promote academic exchange and vitality. We also welcome students without complete research results to submit and share high-quality research proposals, ongoing studies, term papers, or short essays
