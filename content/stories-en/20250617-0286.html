AWDPI has officially joined the Global Summit of Women's international cooperation network, becoming its official "International Partner."

From July 3 to 5, AWDPI will send a delegation to Berlin, Germany, to join outstanding female leaders from around the world in discussions on the theme of "Women: Redefining Value in the Digital Era." "The Women's Davos" invites you to jointly address



new challenges for gender equality. The Global Summit of Women, founded in 1990, is one of the world's most influential transnational women's economic forums, widely known as



"the Women's Davos." The summit rotates annually across different countries and regions, attracting over a thousand female leaders and change-makers from.


government, business, academia, and non-profit organizations. On-site at the 2024 Global Summit of Women

Summit Content:

Topics will include

global trends, AI and leadership, women on corporate boards, entrepreneurial development, social responsibility, and gender equality, with a focus on women's roles, values, and strategies in the digital era. Connecting global resources,




AWDPI will drive change. As an international non-profit organization



dedicated to advancing the well-being and rights of Asian women overseas, AWDPI has always believed that true empowerment for every woman can only be achieved by promoting cross-cultural understanding and inclusion within a global context. In the vibrant global dialogue on gender issues, the voices of Asian women are often marginalized or even ignored. AWDPI's participation in this summit aims precisely to change this reality.


"We come not only with project outcomes, but also with vision and commitment—to tell stories that have not been fully heard, build bridges that have not yet been connected, and claim rights that have not yet been granted on this global stage for women."



At this year's summit, AWDPI will focus on:



Enhancing international influence

By participating in thematic forums and roundtable discussions, AWDPI will comprehensively showcase

its extensive experience and tangible achievements in the well-being, leadership development, and rights advocacy of Asian women. Promoting inclusive diversity In an era of globalization and accelerating technological change, migrant women and women from cross-cultural backgrounds often face dual discrimination based on gender and race. Through this summit,

AWDPI hopes to deepen mainstream policymakers' understanding of the real challenges posed by "double marginalization"

and promote societal inclusivity for women across cultures, languages, and regions. Connecting global resources As a non-profit organization with multi-country collaboration experience, AWDPI will use this opportunity to establish strategic connections with more international platforms and multinational corporations committed to diversity,

exploring diverse forms of cooperation such as joint projects, research collaboration, and funding support.

Advancing substantive empowerment AWDPI's greatest aspiration for this journey is to enable more policymakers, funding institutions, corporate representatives, and the public to truly hear and understand the circumstances and needs of Asian women.

"We are not satisfied with symbolic participation.

We seek real change—helping more women escape domestic violence,



enabling migrant women to access vocational skills training,

and ensuring Asian women's voices are taken seriously in the international agenda.

What we want is not just 'participation,' but genuine 'empowerment.'"
