New Year Greetings

"The departing tiger breaks free from its bonds; the hare chases dragons and snakes."

Time waits for no one; seasons flow like a stream.

Moments are brief, but the path to light is long.

The old tiger carries away the past talismans—what's gone cannot be reclaimed.

Unforgettable are the wise and talented who gathered together, their drops of love merging into a flowing river.

The new hare arrives with fresh peachwood charms—what lies ahead is full of promise.

Now we only wish: across all time and space, hearts united in one direction.

Holding a torch against the wind, shining brilliantly. Bringing warmth nearby, blessing all around.

Speaking up for love—we will never hesitate.

The feast never ends; tomorrow will be as today.


The Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project extends to you

our warmest wishes for a joyful Lunar New Year and a year of great success in the Year of the Rabbit!


May 2023 be a year when we once again join hands—to speak, to shine, to ignite change.!
