01 Who We Are

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project Committed to eliminating gender-based violence and promoting gender equality, the project provides comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence.

By building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, and by mobilizing broader youth-driven public welfare forces, the project advocates for public attention and support for the living challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. It aims to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.


Since October 2021, the Avoice project has received public welfare support from the Shanghai Overseas Chinese Foundation, joining hands to build a "Home of Love and Equality for Chinese Women." Over the next five years, the project will expand its reach primarily across Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, and Australia.


Image source: freepik


The project's abbreviated name, "Avoice," is derived from "Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program" (the English name of the Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project). It signifies using women's voices to convey visions of development, and youth voices to call for gender equality—declaring humanitarian values with sincerity and fulfilling our mission with unwavering determination. It strives to channel countless acts of public engagement into the powerful global movement advancing the Sustainable Development Goal of gender equality.


To strengthen local volunteer teams in each project country and expand the project's overseas influence and operational efficiency, we are now launching a special overseas recruitment drive. If you are overseas and passionate about eliminating domestic violence and advancing gender equality; if you seek practical experience and personal growth within an international public welfare project, then welcome to join the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project. Connect with outstanding volunteers and experienced expert mentors across multiple countries, let youth ideas and public welfare ideals collide, and use youth power to co-create a home of love and equality for Chinese women.
