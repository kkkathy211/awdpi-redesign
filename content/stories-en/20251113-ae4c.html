In the past five years, AWDPI Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, initially focused on anti-domestic violence cases, has evolved into an international nonprofit organization spanning multiple countries and addressing a broader range of women's issues. We have implemented projects in Europe, North America, Australia, and Asia, partnering with allies from all sectors to drive systemic change toward gender equality, ending gender-based violence, and women's empowerment.


As our projects continue to expand and our network of collaborations grows, we have also entered a new phase that requires a more robust governance structure, more efficient cross-border collaboration, and stronger strategic execution capability. This is a


significant but not urgent strategic initiative. We aim to take a long-term perspective to identify partners who truly resonate with AWDPI's mission and possess both vision and action-oriented leadership, to jointly strengthen organizational governance and execution, and to co-lead AWDPI's global growth. Therefore, AWDPI is officially launching a global recruitment drive for the roles of.


Secretary General (Secretary General) and Youth Chair (Youth Chair). They will jointly take on key organizational responsibilities and work alongside all core team members to shape a more professional, collaborative, resilient, and sustainably impactful global network, ensuring more women's voices are heard, respected, and supported.




About AWDPI



AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development.


It evolved from Avoice (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in the United Kingdom, the United States, Australia, and Belgium. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with organizations across sectors, including United Nations system agencies, relevant government departments in various countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions.


AWDPI advocates for attention to the living challenges faced by Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more opportunities for Asian women overseas to develop in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them grow into a vital force for social progress.


Our Mission

To eliminate violence and discrimination against Asian women and promote their protection and development.

Our Vision

To create a world of love and equality for women.

Our Values

Participation, Inclusion, Empowerment




We are committed to



providing support and assistance to survivors of domestic violence. Through a comprehensive support system and a strong overseas assistance network, we promptly respond to the needs of Asian women overseas in areas such as domestic violence prevention, emergency aid, and follow-up services.


Building community connections. By establishing an online community matrix and offline mutual support groups, we create an international platform for Asian women overseas to communicate, learn, and support one another, forming peer support networks that enable self-development.


Enhancing women's influence. Through forums, seminars, workshops, and other activities, we actively facilitate multi-stakeholder collaboration to analyze experiences, study challenges, explore solutions, and showcase the strength and potential of Asian women.


Launching awareness campaigns and advocacy. We actively conduct public outreach, academic research, and policy advocacy to promote global attention to Asian women overseas and to advance improvements in relevant policies and development environments.

Recruitment Information




AWDPI Secretary General ( Global Secretariat - Headquarters)

Chief executive responsible for overall global strategy and multi-country office operations (1 position)


Work Location

Remote work, with possible international travel


Nature of Work

Volunteer


Job Responsibilities

As Secretary General, you will serve as the highest-ranking executive responsible for AWDPI's global operations, working alongside the co-founders and reporting to the Board of Directors, responsible for strategic execution, day-to-day management, and organizational governance across all offices and departments worldwide, including but not limited to:


1. Global Organizational Management

  • Develop and advance the global strategic plan, ensuring coordinated and goal-oriented operations across country offices

  • Oversee operational management, personnel communication, and project implementation across regional and national offices

2. Cross-Departmental Collaboration and Performance Management

  • Manage and coordinate the overall operations of all AWDPI functional departments

  • Promote organizational culture building and performance evaluation mechanisms to enhance team execution and sense of mission

3. Institutional Development and International Governance

  • Strengthen the institutional framework for global multi-site operations (including governance bylaws, financial and HR regulations, project quality assessments, etc.)

  • Coordinate governance structures and internal management among the Board of Directors, Advisory Committee, and country representative offices, promoting transparency, accountability, and compliance

4. Resource Development and International Cooperation

  • Expand strategic partnerships with international organizations, government agencies, universities, foundations, and enterprises

  • Guide global fundraising strategies and donor relationship management, supporting key donor services and project alignment

5. External Representation and Public Engagement (optional)

  • Represent AWDPI at international conferences, deliver public speeches, sign agreements, or participate in multilateral initiatives

  • Maintain the organization's public image and promote global media outreach and issue advocacy


Qualifications

  • Identity  AWDPI mission and values, with awareness of gender equality and diversity and inclusion.

  • Possess a strong sense of responsibility, initiative, and resilience, capable of proactively identifying issues and driving progress without explicit instructions.

  • Able to commit at least 15 hours per week of consistent work time, with strong time management skills.

  • Combine independent thinking with strong execution ability, capable of turning strategic visions into actionable outcomes.

  • Possess systems thinking, project management, and cross-departmental coordination skills, skilled in leading multinational teams and advancing complex tasks.

  • Have cross-cultural management experience,, excellent written and spoken skills in both Chinese and English.

  • Rich in empathy and communication skills, able to coordinate volunteers and experts, resolve conflicts, and foster a team culture of trust and support.

  • Calm, steady, a good listener, respectful of differences, with a servant leadership mindset.

  • Committed to long-term engagement in the fields of nonprofit, women's rights, and international development.

  • Possess over 3 years of senior management experience in nonprofit organizations or multinational projects, familiar with international governance and funding mechanisms preferred.

  • Upon successful evaluation, must commit to serve for at least two years.


Position Highlights

  • Lead an international nonprofit network spanning five continents

  • Directly participate in global gender equality agendas and international cooperation initiatives

  • Enhance leadership within a structured yet open environment

  • Gain international visibility and long-term career development pathways

  • Opportunity to become a co-founder and board member of AWDPI
