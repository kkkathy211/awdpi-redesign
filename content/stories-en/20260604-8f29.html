On April 25, 2026, the Legal Affairs Department of AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International), an international nonprofit organization, successfully conducted the fourth online legal training session, which also marked the second external training session organized by the department for all volunteers. The training was led by volunteer and foreign-trained law master Wu Yutian, and hosted by Xia Mengya, head of the Legal Affairs Department. It aimed to optimize the organization's governance structure, strengthen compliance management capabilities, and support AWDPI's public welfare initiatives to move forward steadily.


At the beginning of the training, Xia Mengya introduced the background and planning of the session. Drawing on her professional expertise, Wu Yutian delivered an in-depth presentation on the governance framework of international nonprofit organizations. The training systematically analyzed established international organizations such as Oxfam and the Red Cross, examining their governance models from dimensions including board functions, decision-making mechanisms, and oversight systems, and distilling standardized governance structures and risk prevention experiences to provide reference models for AWDPI.


In light of AWDPI's current development stage, the training conducted a comprehensive review of its governance model, clarifying core structures such as strategic empowerment by the board, global execution by the secretary-general, and a dual-responsibility system to ensure operational stability. It also identified key directions for optimization, including top-level decision-making and closed-loop supervision. Wu Yutian emphasized that as the organization scales up, institutional development must come first—governance should shift from a "supportive" to a "contributive" model, laying a solid foundation for compliant growth.




The training also focused on upgrading the role of the Legal Affairs Department, clarifying its core functions in governance design, multi-country compliance, and institutional oversight, and exploring pathways for the department to participate in decision-making and strengthen supervision. At the same time, considering the characteristics of nonprofit organizations, the training stressed the need to balance institutional enforcement with volunteer experience, enhancing team cohesion through human-centered management.


As a key component of AWDPI's compliance system development, this training further clarified the organization's governance direction and strengthened volunteers' awareness and understanding of compliance and governance. In the future, AWDPI's Legal Affairs Department will continue improving the governance structure and refining risk prevention mechanisms, advancing the standardization and professionalization of organizational governance, ensuring that every act of public service, guided by rules, conveys warmth and strength to the cause of protecting the rights of Asian women.
