This summer, AWDPI plans to host once again the "AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum.".


This year's forum is expected to be held online over two days, featuring three main components: keynote speeches, panel discussions, and paper reviews. Experts, scholars, and young talents from relevant fields will be invited to engage in in-depth discussions on topics including gender-based violence, overseas female workers' rights, women's career development, women's agency and influence, and women's health.



We are now recruiting 2–8 forum curators for this year's event.


As the primary person in charge of a specific theme or sub-forum, the forum curator will play a crucial role in the preparation process. The ideal candidate should have certain experience and insights in the theory and practice of combating gender-based violence, stay attentive to current affairs and emerging intersectional issues, foster dynamic exchanges of ideas, and be capable of efficiently organizing and managing a series of tasks including theme planning, guest invitations, and event promotion.


We hope you:

  1. are passionate about issues such as eliminating gender-based violence, overseas labor, women's career development, women's agency and influence, and women's health;

  2. have strong team collaboration skills;

  3. possess strong language organization and communication abilities;

  4. demonstrate academic rigor and solid writing skills;

  5. are adaptable (skilled at identifying, raising, and solving problems);

  6. and prior experience in organizing forums is preferred.

 /


Application requirements:

  1. A personal resume (in either Chinese or English);

  2. and a brief forum proposal.


Requirements and reference information for the forum proposal:

- The current proposed themes for the forum are "Orange the World—Building a Future Without Violence" and "A New Era of Shaping Women's Identities." Please select one of these themes and write a corresponding proposal (specific subtopics are open); or propose your own theme and develop the proposal around it.


- Forum schedule: The forum is expected to last two days, with sessions scheduled from 9:00 to 12:00 and 14:00 to 18:00 Beijing time. You may design multiple "keynote speeches" and "panel discussions" according to the time available, with no limit on the number of sessions.


- Forum format: The forum will be held online. Under each main theme, there will be "keynote speeches" and "panel discussions" on various subtopics, which you may define. A list of discussion questions for panel sessions is optional. Sample topics include:


  1. Speech—Seeking Support: A Guide for Domestic Violence Survivors to Access Help;

  2. Speech—The Path of Practice: Learning from Experience, Effective Strategies to Support Domestic Violence Survivors;

  3. Speech—Strength and Light: Challenges and Empowerment of Asian Female Workers;

  4. Speech—Revisiting Barbie: How Culture Shapes Female Identity;

  5. Panel Discussion—Self-Acceptance: Embracing Physical and Mental Health;

  6. Panel Discussion—Gender-Based Violence Among Chinese International Students on Overseas Campuses;

  7. Panel Discussion—She Who Leads the Trend;


- Word count:: Approximately 200–400 Chinese characters.


- Content requirements: Should include, but are not limited to, title, keywords, background of the topic, main content, format of delivery, and profile of potential guests. The proposal should have a clear theme, well-structured framework, rigorous logic, concise expression, and professional terminology.



Volunteer benefits:

  1. Those who participate and complete the forum preparation will receive a volunteer certificate issued by Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI).

  2. Outstanding performers will be recommended for other volunteer positions within AWDPI.


How to apply:

Interested applicants, please send your resume and proposal to [email protected]with the email subject formatted as "AWDPI Curator Application – [Your Name]."


Application deadline:

20:00 Beijing time, May 31
