New Year's Message

This is a message of gratitude and best wishes from the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project.

Over the past year, talented individuals from all walks of life have come together with one heart, striving for good and building love with full strength.

Moving forward against the wind, time has flowed like a river, and our journey has been written in light.


As the New Year begins,

may we, in the coming year,

remain united in purpose and dedication, making the most of our precious time.

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project

Together basking in the spring sunshine, singing the song of great love.

Though this body may travel thousands of miles,

this heart always returns to the same place.
