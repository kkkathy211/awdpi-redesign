AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International UK National Office will pilot a "dual-leadership system" in 2025 and is hereby hosting a special recruitment drive for the UK National Office management team, opening applications for all department head positions.



In this recruitment round, we will give priority to applicants who have studied overseas since their undergraduate studies or have long-term experience living, studying, or working abroad to strengthen our talent pipeline. If you are less experienced but passionate, we will still offer trust and opportunities, and look forward to growing together with you; if you are an established expert or scholar in a relevant field, you are also warmly welcome to join the AWDPI family as an institutional advisor.




Recruitment Information




Head of General Affairs Division

Main Responsibilities

1. Oversee daily administrative management, personnel recruitment, and human resources management;

2. Supervise and manage institutional financial and legal affairs to ensure compliance with relevant laws and regulations;

3. Advance team building and volunteer motivation to ensure active participation of members;

4. Assist in formulating bi-monthly and annual work plans and be responsible for corresponding outcome tracking and feedback.


Position Requirements

1. Outstanding execution, communication, and coordination skills;

2. Prior experience in administration, human resources, or general affairs is preferred;

3. Commit to at least 5 hours per week and be based in the UK for one year.



Head of Partnerships and Outreach Division

Main Responsibilities

1. Develop and maintain local partnerships, establishing collaborations with social organizations, government agencies, and enterprises;

2. Plan and organize various partnership projects and events to enhance the organization's public impact;

3. Support overseas promotion and collaboration for headquarters' major events and projects.


Position Requirements

1. Strong communication and project management skills;

2. Excellent spoken and written English proficiency;

3. Familiarity with UK social and cultural contexts, and skilled in networking;

4. Commit to at least 5 hours per week and be based in the UK for one year.



Head of Support and Assistance Division

Main Responsibilities

1. Coordinate and manage the support team and related assistance services;

2. Plan and organize offline and online awareness campaigns to continuously reach potential beneficiaries;

3. Continuously build and maintain local support networks to ensure professional and comprehensive assistance for beneficiaries.


Position Requirements

1. Deep understanding of domestic violence and gender-based violence, with academic or research experience in related fields;

2. Possess strong communication skills, a sense of responsibility, and empathy, capable of handling assistance cases appropriately;

3. Prior experience in psychological counseling, legal aid, or related support services is preferred;

4. Commit to at least 5 hours per week and be based in the UK for one year.



Head of Fundraising and Resource Management Division

Main Responsibilities

1. Lead institutional fundraising efforts, develop fundraising plans, and execute them effectively;

2. Plan and organize various fundraising events and projects to secure sustainable operational funding;

3. Continuously expand and maintain relationships with funding partners.


Position Requirements

1. Experience in nonprofit fundraising and project management, with knowledge of the UK fundraising environment and relevant regulations;

2. . Strong communication and negotiation skills to engage diverse types of funders;

3. Commit to at least 5 hours per week and be based in the UK for one year.
