✨ Project Organizer ✨

Yangpu District Fuqi Youth Development Center (Fuqi Youth), based in Shanghai and deeply rooted in the Yangtze River Delta region, has youth communities covering the entire country. It was founded by passionate young people from mainland China, Taiwan, and Hong Kong who are committed to educational public welfare. The project was initiated in 2010 and officially registered as a non-profit organization in 2013. "Fu" means "empowerment," and "Qi" means "enlightenment." The organization focuses on providing a series of empowerment and enlightenment training and practical opportunities for youth, delivering high-quality quality education courses to urban migrant children, and cultivating the most professional volunteers in the field of public welfare education.

Fuqi is dedicated to youth capacity development and career planning. We closely collaborate with the education, corporate, and public welfare sectors to research and implement a series of educational programs from secondary school to university. We have launched project brands such as "Voice for Love," "Diggers," "JOBinn," and "Village Show," using in-depth experiential training and diverse public welfare and career practices to help migrant children and outstanding youth become better versions of themselves, positively influence young people, and thereby create positive change in the world.

✨ Project Funder ✨

Alibaba's "Burn, Youth!" Happiness Team (Burn Team) is a vibrant, professional, and reliable youth public welfare organization. Spontaneously founded by a group of sincere and enthusiastic young Alibaba employees, it collaborates with caring and experienced Alibaba mentors from various business units across the company. Drawing from their industry insights, workplace experiences, and life wisdom, they provide practical help and information to college students who care about career development and personal growth, empowering youth to become the fulcrum that moves the world.
