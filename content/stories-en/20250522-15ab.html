Global Youth Leadership Program .

Gather international youth leaders to promote interaction among young elites. Build consensus among international youth in the new era, construct an international youth community, and contribute youthful wisdom and strength to the community with a shared future for mankind. Global consensus, humanistic sentiment, the spirit of China, youth leading the way.




On May 21 this year, the United Nations marked the 6th International Tea Day. The International Youth Leadership Cultural Heritage Fund of the China Heritage Foundation officially launched the "Chinese Youth Leadership Tea Master Inheritance Program" in Beijing today, and is now publicly recruiting co-founders. Centered on tea culture as a bond, the project aims to cultivate young tea masters who possess both traditional craftsmanship and global vision, promoting innovative inheritance and global dissemination of Chinese culture.


Su Run, Deputy Secretary-General of the Foundation and Head of the International Youth Leadership Cultural Inheritance Project Team, stated that the program will build a youth cultural exchange network, help excellent traditional Chinese culture "go global," and contribute Chinese wisdom to mutual learning among world civilizations.


Focusing on Three Dimensions: Cultivating a New Force for Tea Culture Inheritance


The program revolves around three core pillars: talent development, international collaboration, and industrial innovation.


— Talent Foundation: The first phase will recruit 30 to 60 young tea representatives from the core production regions across China for the six major tea categories—green tea, black tea, dark tea, white tea, yellow tea, and oolong tea—covering three groups: tea masters, tea entrepreneurs, and tea enthusiasts.


— Global Connectivity: Collaborating with the Global Elite Universities Youth Community, which includes 100 universities such as Harvard and Oxford, the program will establish Youth Leadership Tea Culture Stations overseas at appropriate times, building channels for international youth tea cultural exchange.


— Technology Empowerment: Building a "Digital Tea Culture Genome Library" and developing a metaverse tea gathering space to achieve digital preservation and innovative expression of intangible cultural heritage techniques. 


Zhang Zixian, Executive Head of the International Youth Leadership Tea Culture Inheritance Project Team, introduced that the program will explore pathways for traditional industry transformation, and simultaneously promote the establishment of an International Youth Leadership Public Welfare Venture Capital Fund to support tea culture inheritance and rural revitalization.


Selecting Outstanding Youth Pioneers to Build a Matrix for Tea Culture Going Global 


Applicants must meet basic criteria: aged between 25 and 40, from production regions certified with national geographical indication, and possess cross-cultural communication skills. Professional requirements are categorized accordingly: Tea masters must master two or more intangible heritage techniques and hold national-level professional certification; tea entrepreneurs must have successful cases of business model innovation; tea enthusiasts must demonstrate significant influence in cultural research or new media communication. 


The program will create an "International Tea Culture Corridor," organizing annual cross-border exhibitions and academic exchanges, and collaborating with the Global Elite Universities Youth Community to host a Global Tea Culture Summit, providing young tea masters with an international stage.


The application channel is now open via the official account "Global Youth Leadership Program." Please send materials to email:[email protected]. The selection process will involve joint interviews by experts from international intangible heritage protection organizations, executives from multinational corporations, and cultural scholars.


"Youth are the vibrant carriers of cultural inheritance. We hope that through the story of a cup of tea, the world can perceive the depth and warmth of Chinese culture," said Zhang Zixian. He added that the project will explore a two-way transformation model between "cultural value and economic value," offering Chinese solutions for global sustainable development.
