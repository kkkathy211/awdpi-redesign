 Introduction:

The Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project has been invited to attend the forum on "International Development Cooperation from a Gender Perspective: Theory and Practice," held on August 23. Hai Chen, Head of the Academic Research Division, will present a paper titled "A Preliminary Analysis of NGO Project Practices in International Domestic Violence Relief and Advancing Gender Equality", sharing Avoice's methodologies and philosophies in delivering domestic violence relief programs overseas, building international project teams, and enhancing the influence of overseas Chinese communities in the field of gender equality with experts and peers from academic institutions, international organizations, and peer organizations.


Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project Since its launch in early 2020, has developed a replicable and scalable project model, and established a sound organizational management structure and operational workflow. From internal governance to project implementation and external collaboration, it enables efficient remote coordination across multiple countries and teams. As an international public welfare project now in a stable implementation phase, Avoice's accumulated experience can offer valuable insights to similar initiatives and peer organizations, helping them seize greater opportunities and broader prospects in international development cooperation, and explore practical pathways and working methods.

About

China Agricultural University International Development

and Global Agriculture (CIDGA)


The College of International Development and Global Agriculture (CIDGA) at China Agricultural University / China South-South Cooperation Academy for Agricultural Development was established in 2017 as a new interdisciplinary platform for research and talent development in international development and global agriculture. Built upon more than three decades of experience in international development cooperation research and talent training—from "bringing in" to "going global"—CIDGA faces the international frontier with a global vision.


About

"Journal of Women's Studies"


"Journal of Women's Studies" is a national academic journal sponsored by the All-China Women's Federation and jointly hosted by the Institute of Women's Studies of the All-China Women's Federation and the China Women's Studies Association. Founded in 1992, it became the official journal of the China Women's Studies Association in 1999. The journal is funded by the National Social Science Foundation of China and is indexed as a source journal in the Chinese Social Sciences Citation Index (CSSCI), a core journal in Chinese Humanities and Social Sciences (HSSCJS), a national core journal (CJC), a core academic journal (RCCSE), a Chinese science and technology core journal (Social Sciences Edition), and a major reprint source journal of Renmin University's "Reprinted Periodicals and Journals." It was also recognized as the most popular journal from 2016 to 2020.
