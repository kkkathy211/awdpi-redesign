May-June Film Screening Event

As May draws to a close, we hope you've fully enjoyed its beauty—whether it's the cicadas singing, the seaside, the shade of trees, or the birds chirping. Avoice has prepared several films with distinct styles, hoping to bring you wonderful visual experiences throughout May and June.


Suffragette

Suffragette


The path for women in the West to fight for their rights indeed came at the cost of bloodshed and sacrifice, defending the rights and freedoms of women with their lives; they are true warriors and fighters. The female protagonist, Maud, was not initially a firm advocate for women's equal rights. She was content with her current job, even though she had worked in factories since childhood and suffered unequal treatment. However, after witnessing her boss humiliate a young female worker, she became enraged. Later, she resolutely listened to speeches advocating for women's rights and defended her own rights in a courtroom full of men. But she saw that men, to protect their own authority, were unwilling to let women step onto the stage of power; they only wanted women to be their appendages. The road ahead was not smooth, but she remained steadfast in her actions, defending rights and dignity. Each of them was gradually awakening: they were first and foremost themselves, independent individuals with the right to pursue freedom and equality, and only then were they someone's wife or mother.

Till We

Meet Again


The feminist film Till We Meet Again offers a philosophical exploration of life. Each time the girl is reborn, she carries no memories from her past lives, yet she repeatedly senses the dangers that led to her previous deaths, overcoming them through intuition—only to face new dangers again and again…

You Are My

Spring


People running for their loved ones, migrant workers fighting to recover their hard-earned wages, frontline workers battling the pandemic who must also learn how to say goodbye, community workers on the home front caring for residents while exposing themselves to risk, and fathers and sons who, having previously lacked communication, are now forced to live together day and night. Unexpected events keep happening, yet a seed of healing continues to take root and grow in people's hearts. This is a film about spring and hope.

Moana

Moana


This film tells the story of Moana, daughter of a chief and future leader, who answers the ocean's call. Despite her father's prohibition, she leaves her island and the life she knows, setting off on a seafaring adventure in a bamboo raft. This film represents Disney's new attempt within its princess series in the wake of feminist movements—not only highlighting non-Western Pacific Islander communities, but also featuring a princess who, for the first time, does not enter into any romantic relationship with a male character. Although the plot is relatively simple and follows Disney's traditional animated storytelling pattern, the animation is exquisitely crafted, the characters are vividly portrayed, and the songs are exceptionally beautiful.
