Five years of walking together, dear friends, let's set off again! AWDPI warmly invites our "old friends" to return and continue empowering Asian women overseas!


Since planting the first seed of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program) in Belgium in March 2020, to gradually expanding operations across 30+ countries in Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific region by 2021, and officially establishing physical National Offices and completing local nonprofit registrations in the United States, the United Kingdom, and Belgium, and with the Australia and Thailand National Office teams officially launching in 2025, Avoice has grown into an international nonprofit organization dedicated to addressing violence and discrimination faced by Asian women overseas, providing real protection and sustained empowerment support—Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) .


Every step of AWDPI's growth bears the deep imprint of each "old friend" who once walked alongside us. On the occasion of AWDPI's fifth anniversary, the special "Old Friends Season" recruitment initiative is now officially launched. This recruitment particularly welcomes former volunteer partners to return, while also opening opportunities for qualified new friends to join. Let us once again write a new chapter together in advancing the well-being of Asian women overseas and supporting their growth as vital forces for social progress.

Department of Program Quality


Department Introduction

The Department of Program Quality consists of Academic Research Division, Program Design Division, Monitoring & Evaluation Division, IT & Information Division, and Curriculum Development Division—five sub-divisions in total. The IT & Information Division is responsible for website design, development, testing, and maintenance, as well as daily IT support in collaboration with other departments. The Academic Research Division plans and conducts academic research on gender equality, domestic violence prevention, women's protection and empowerment, writes research reports and popular science articles, and provides strategic recommendations and direction for institutional strategy and program design. The Curriculum Development Division focuses on developing anti-domestic violence curricula, designing art therapy workshops, and establishing anonymous support groups. We aim to combine content and technology to create more practical and accessible educational and support methods, helping more people receive support. We welcome partners with relevant backgrounds to join us in exploring more effective trauma support approaches.
