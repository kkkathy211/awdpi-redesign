What is the Movers Programme?



The meaning of MOVERS—Youth are participants and advocates of the Sustainable Development Goals, but also actors and leaders driving action.


The Sustainable Development Movers Programme (Movers Programme, abbreviated as "Movers Project") is the official UN Development Programme (UNDP) Asia-Pacific regional youth and children empowerment and sustainable development advocacy initiative..


In 2021, the UNDP China Country Office, in partnership with the China Soong Ching Ling Science and Culture Center for Young People and the United Nations Volunteers (UNV) programme, jointly launched the Movers (China) Sustainable Development Trainer Programme (Movers Programme China, abbreviated as "Movers (China) Initiative") under the framework of the "Building a Community with a Shared Future for Humanity: Promoting Youth Engagement in Sustainable Development" innovation demonstration project, and has since conducted a series of activities.


The Movers (China) Initiative aims, together with partners, to cultivate young people's and children's awareness of sustainable development, entrepreneurial thinking, volunteer spirit, and 21st-century survival skills through designing and delivering workshops and a "Train the Trainer" approach, contributing innovative solutions to societal sustainable development.

This session's theme:

Gender Equality


The annual UN "16 Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence" is here. This year's campaign, themed "Activism! UNiTE to end violence against women and girls," calls on more people to join efforts to eliminate gender-based violence and support women and girls worldwide in securing their rights.


Every year, November 25 marks the UN's "International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women." The "16 Days of Activism" begins on this day and continues until December 10, "Human Rights Day." The campaign aims to mobilize society, the public, governments, and UN agencies to amplify impact and encourage action to end gender-based violence.
