On January 7, 2023, the first Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Global Development Forum was successfully held. The event received significant attention and participation from international relief networks, overseas Chinese communities, nonprofit organizations, and academic experts. Over ten domestic and international experts and scholars from UN Women, international organizations, academic institutions, renowned universities, and technology innovation companies participated, offering fresh perspectives and profound insights into the theory and practice of combating gender-based violence. Drawing on gender data, practical experiences, and research findings across multiple sectors, they examined gender issues through the lens of social phenomena and engaged in discussions on topics including technology, philanthropy, workplace dynamics, culture, law, and psychology.


September 23–24, 2023, the 2023 AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum (also known as the 2nd Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Global Development Forum) will reunite us once again! We will expand the number of speakers, enhance the innovation of topics, and focus on cross-generational, cross-sectoral, cross-regional, and cross-cultural exchanges and collaborative dialogues. The forum aims to increase the visibility of marginalized groups and advance gender equality in emerging fields. Centered on issues such as combating gender-based violence, women's workplace empowerment, women and intimate relationships, women and health, and overseas Chinese women laborers, the forum will deeply explore the relationship between women and sustainable development. It will build a knowledge-sharing network among organizations and scholars dedicated to Asian women's advancement, create practical opportunities for public engagement in gender issues, and expand the depth and breadth of support for overseas Asian women.

Organizer


AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) Asian Women International Development Plan, initiated at the beginning of the global COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, is an international nonprofit organization centered on women's rights. Currently operating in 30 countries and regions across Europe, North America, Asia, and Australia, AWDPI has established a broad network of collaboration with United Nations system agencies, government departments in various countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. Together, they advocate for greater attention to the survival challenges faced by overseas Asian women and provide them with sustained protection and support, enabling these women to gain more opportunities for development in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, transforming them from vulnerable groups into a vital force driving social progress.
