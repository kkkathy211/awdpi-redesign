01 Who We Are


A

Project Overview


Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project Committed to eliminating gender-based violence and advancing gender equality, the project provides comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence. By building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, and by mobilizing broader youth-driven public welfare forces, the project advocates for public awareness and support regarding the challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. It aims to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.


The project is abbreviated as "Avoice," taken from Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program (the English name of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project). It signifies using women's voices to convey the vision for development, using youth's voices to advocate for gender equality, declaring humanistic ideals with a manifest and sincere heart, and singing the long song of mission with unwavering determination. It is dedicated to integrating the myriad voices of public welfare into the powerful, grand chorus of globally advancing the sustainable development goals for gender equality!

02 Position Recruitment


1. Research Division Members (6–8 positions)


(Rolling recruitment; resume submission deadline: 11:59 pm, Beijing Time, December 1)


Job Description:

  • Participate in research on the current situation of survival and development of overseas Chinese women, search for relevant information, and write academic reports. Optional research directions include:

  • Investigation into the prevalence of violence against women among overseas Chinese women: 1–2 people.

  • Investigation into the working conditions of Chinese women in overseas workplaces: 1–2 people.

  • Participate in building a specialized academic database for Chinese women: 3 people.

  • On the basis of participating in the above three projects, members may choose whether or not to additionally participate in:

  • Writing the monthly "Avoice Gender Equality Progress and Overseas Asian Development" news commentary report.

  • Voluntarily take on other tasks or complete additional assignments given by supervisors.


Requirements:

  • Strong learning ability, stress tolerance, and time management skills, with the ability to complete assigned tasks on time and with high quality.

  • A strong sense of responsibility, good at teamwork, and able to maintain timely communication under high-pressure conditions.

  • Excellent English reading skills, adept at reviewing foreign-language literature, and skilled in writing Chinese academic reports and research articles.

  • Proficient in basic research methodologies, with strong capabilities in data collection and analysis.

  • Majors include, but are not limited to, sociology, political science, anthropology, psychology, and history. Candidates should have research interests in domestic violence, women's protection and development, and gender politics.


2. Academic Advisors (ongoing recruitment)


Job Description:

  • Provide professional guidance on topic selection, framework design, and content writing for research reports from the Research Division.

  • Collaborate with the Research Division head to design project research plans.

  • Offer consultation and recommendations for the project's participation in external academic exchange activities.


Requirements:

  • Master's degree or higher, or substantial academic and practical experience in relevant fields.

  • Majors include, but are not limited to, sociology, political science, anthropology, psychology, and history.

  • Rich experience in research related to domestic violence, women's protection and development, and gender politics.

  • Rigorous, serious, and responsible work attitude.

  • Availability of 3–5 hours per month to provide consultation and guidance to the Research Division.


03 Benefits of Participation


  • Volunteer/Internship Certificate: According to Avoice bylaws, members who serve for more than 6 months may apply for a volunteer certificate; those who complete one year of service may receive an internship certificate. Outstanding members are eligible for selection as quarterly or annual outstanding volunteers.

  • Skill Development & Project Attribution: Experience and outcomes from academic report writing, monthly reports, and database development can be included in personal resumes. This experience is beneficial for pursuing careers in academia, consulting, and related fields.

  • Avoice Professional and Expert Network: Avoice volunteers and alumni are from top universities worldwide (including Ivy League, G5, and 985/211 institutions), and advisors are senior professionals from the public welfare, business, and tech sectors, offering a high-quality academic and professional network.

  • Routine Rewards: Departmental internal incentives (including but not limited to milk tea, small prizes, etc.) will be provided to members who perform their duties diligently and achieve excellent evaluation results.

  • Opportunity to receive guidance and recommendation letters from project experts and advisors (available after serving at least 6 months and passing a comprehensive evaluation).

  • Participation in online and offline volunteer team-building activities.

  • Join the International Youth Volunteer Network Alumni Association.

  • Internship referrals and internal recommendations for events hosted by project partners.

  • Participation in research projects related to gender equality, women's protection and development, and youth empowerment.

  • Opportunities for collaboration and exchange with domestic and international public welfare organizations, academic institutions, and foundations.

  • Learning and growing together with like-minded peers from around the world.
