On January 7, 2023, the first Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Global Development Forum was successfully held! The event received significant attention and participation from international relief networks, overseas Chinese communities, nonprofit organizations, and academic experts. Over ten domestic and international experts and scholars from UN Women, international organizations, academic institutions, renowned universities, and technology innovation companies contributed to the forum. Drawing on gender-disaggregated data, practical experiences, and research findings across multiple sectors, they examined gender issues through social phenomena and shared insights on topics including technology, philanthropy, workplace, culture, law, and psychology, offering fresh perspectives and deep analysis on the theory and practice of combating gender-based violence.


In September 2023, the forum returns with even greater impact! This time, we are co-organizing with TOPHER, China's leading business and finance media for women, and Jiemian·Cailian She. "2023 Global Women's Innovation Summit & AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum". The forum will be held both offline and online. The first day (September 22) will take place in person at Orient Shopping Center, Wangfujing, Beijing. Days two and three (September 23–24) will feature online keynote sessions via a virtual conference platform.


Additionally, the virtual woman Reddi will appear as the first-ever female summit ambassador in China, joining participants online and offline to celebrate her power and honor the future!





About the Organizers




AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) Asian Women Development Plan International, launched in early 2020 at the onset of the global COVID-19 pandemic, is an international nonprofit organization centered on women's rights. Currently operating in 30 countries and regions across Europe, North America, Asia, and Australia, AWDPI has built extensive partnerships with United Nations agencies, government departments, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. Together, they advocate for greater awareness of the living challenges faced by Asian women overseas and provide them with sustained protection and support, enabling these women to gain more development opportunities in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, transforming them from vulnerable groups into key drivers of social progress.


TOPHER TOPHER, founded in May 2015, is a Chinese business and finance media platform dedicated to women and a service provider for China's "she economy." It is one of the invested enterprises of Shanghai United Media Group and Jiemian News, holding original content reporting qualifications and a Class I Internet news license. TOPHER focuses on the growth and commercial value of Chinese women, providing industry reports, feature stories, and business insights on sectors related to Chinese female consumers. With an international perspective, it covers women's humanities, consumption trends, behavioral preferences, and industry developments, aiming to become a "unicorn" serving the growth and commercial empowerment of Chinese women.


Jiemian·Cailian She, an influential Class A original financial new media in China, was launched in September 2014 by Shanghai United Media Group. In 2018, Jiemian News merged with Cailian She to form Jiemian·Cailian She, integrating "media + information + data + services + transactions" into one platform, becoming a leading new-type financial information provider in China. Recognized as a Chinese unicorn enterprise by multiple third-party institutions including the Ministry of Science and Technology, IT Juzi, and Sina Finance, Jiemian·Cailian She has a market valuation exceeding 10 billion RMB.




Agenda




September 22 – Offline Summit

The offline summit focuses on "Her Philanthropy," "Her Power," "Her Technology," "Her Workplace," "Her Consumption," and "Her Health"—six core themes that progressively explore women's living spaces.

Details of the offline summit will be released in a follow-up post.


September 23–24 – Online Forum

The online forum will center on "Combating Gender-Based Violence," "Women and Technological Innovation," "Overseas Chinese Women Workers," "Women's Workplace Empowerment," "Women and Intimate Relationships," and "Women and Health", among other diverse topics. It will deeply examine the relationship between women and sustainable development, build a knowledge-sharing network for organizations and academic guests committed to Asian women's development, create practical opportunities for the public to engage with gender issues, and further expand the voice of overseas Asian women based on existing achievements.


In addition, this year's forum will expand the scale of speakers, enhance the innovation of topics, and emphasize cross-generational, cross-sectoral, cross-regional, and cross-cultural dialogue and collaboration, increasing the visibility of marginalized groups and promoting gender equality in emerging fields.


Asian women, including Chinese women, as one of the largest and fastest-growing ethnic minority groups overseas, play a significant role in global sustainable development. Overseas Asian women not only need protection from violence but also require joint efforts from all countries to enable them to fully realize their potential in an equal and supportive environment, thus driving social progress.


We will continue to uphold "Participation, Inclusion, Empowerment" as our core values, build broad cooperative networks, and unite resources and forces from all sectors to advocate for greater attention to the living challenges of overseas Asian women, enabling them to gain more development opportunities in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, and grow from vulnerable groups into key forces advancing social progress.


We look forward to activists from around the world paying attention to and participating in this forum in various forms. We believe that regardless of nationality, ethnicity, age, or gender, every bit of attention will become an important force driving the future!


The online forum is open to the public—welcome all activists to join!
