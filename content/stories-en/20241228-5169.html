APEA X AWDPI partnership launch


The Asia-Pacific Entrepreneurs Association (APEA) and Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) have officially joined forces to jointly establish the APEA International Women's Development Committee, and AWDPI has officially become an important member of the APEA Global Organizational Partnership System. Together, we move forward.




Hand in hand

As a key platform for economic cooperation and development in the Asia-Pacific region, APEA is advised by Antonio Basilio, Global Secretary-General of the APEC Business Advisory Council, who serves as Chair of the Advisory Committee. APEA has long been committed to promoting collaboration and dialogue among young entrepreneurs in the Asia-Pacific region and advancing regional economic synergy.


Through this partnership, AWDPI will work with APEA to actively promote the participation of Asian women in regional exchanges and cooperation, facilitate resource sharing, help Asian women access greater opportunities, and expand their impact in career development, economic growth, and cross-regional collaboration.


Looking ahead

The establishment of the International Women's Development Committee injects new momentum into women's development and cooperation across the Asia-Pacific region. We aim to deliver practical projects and activities that provide more support and resources, helping Asian women carve out space and amplify their influence on the global stage, contributing to regional economic cooperation and social development.



We firmly believe that more collaboration and action

will surely lead to a broader future.



Follow us and join in advancing the journey of Asian women's development!

Organization profiles


APEA

Asia-Pacific Entrepreneurs Association



The Asia-Pacific Entrepreneurs Association (Asia-Pacific Entrepreneurs Association), abbreviated as APEA. " APEA was established under the overall framework of the Asia-Pacific regional economic cooperation and development initiative, serving as a key measure for economic cooperation and development in the region and a vital platform for young entrepreneurs to engage in dialogue, exchange, and mutually beneficial collaboration. Antonio Basilio, Global Secretary-General of the APEC Business Advisory Council, serves as Chair of the APEA Advisory Committee, providing guidance and oversight to APEA's operations. APEA primarily aims to help members enter the field of international trade, explore new business opportunities, and expand overseas operations and build global connections; nominate outstanding entrepreneurs to participate in summits and events hosted by international organizations such as APEC, G20, and the United Nations; promote technological innovation and unleash human creativity, making emerging technologies key drivers of social progress and economic growth; and advance cultural exchange by building an open, inclusive platform for mutual learning and inspiration, continuously fostering cross-cultural cooperation and innovation.



AWDPI

Asian Women Development Plan International



AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas and promoting their empowerment and development. It evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), founded in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established and registered as charitable organizations in the UK, Belgium, and the United States. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with stakeholders including United Nations system organizations, relevant government agencies, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the challenges faced by Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI strives to create more opportunities for Asian women in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them transform from marginalized groups into a vital force driving social progress.
