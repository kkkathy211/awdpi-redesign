01 Who We Are

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project is dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and promoting gender equality by providing comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence.

Through building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, academic institutions, and industry experts, the project widely unites youth-driven public welfare forces, advocates for public attention and support toward the living challenges faced by overseas Chinese women, and strives to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners of eliminating gender-based violence, advancing gender equality, and protecting vulnerable groups.


Since October 2021, the project has been supported by Shanghai Overseas Chinese Public Welfare Development Foundation with fundraising qualifications, funding, project management, and comprehensive public welfare support, working together to build a "home of love and equality for overseas Chinese women."


Image source: rawpixels


To date, the pilot project in Belgium has been successfully completed, and the project model has been extended to all European countries and selected countries in North America. Over the next three years, the project will expand primarily across Europe, the United States, Southeast Asia, and Australia.


The project's short name, "Avoice," is derived from Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program (the English name of the Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project), symbolizing conveying development visions through women's voices, calling for gender equality through youth voices, openly declaring humanitarian values with sincerity, and resolutely singing the enduring song of mission. It is committed to amplifying countless public welfare voices into the powerful global chorus advancing the Sustainable Development Goal of gender equality!
