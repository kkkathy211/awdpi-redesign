On April 19, 2023, Avoice became one of the first organizations to join the Feishu Public Welfare Program. Over the next year, we will gain access to the "Feishu Office Enterprise Professional Edition" product. Our use of Feishu will no longer be subject to user number limits, and we will enjoy expanded file storage and Miaoji storage space. At the same time, our usage permissions in video conferencing, multidimensional tables, email, compliance management, and other areas have also been significantly upgraded, providing crucial support for all aspects of our work.


Here, we sincerely thank Feishu and all its staff for their support of Avoice's public welfare initiatives!


Avoice officially adopted Feishu as our volunteer team collaboration software in May 2022, greatly enhancing our project operations, standardization, and team management. By the end of 2022, the size of Avoice's Feishu team had continuously expanded, approaching 500 members. However, after Feishu updated its product versions this year, the features available in the free version were no longer sufficient to meet Avoice's team collaboration needs. Since then, Feishu staff have maintained contact with us and actively sought solutions. In late March, we applied to the Feishu Public Welfare Program, and within less than three weeks, the relevant Feishu team reviewed our qualifications and informed us of our successful application. The approval of the Feishu Public Welfare Program will play a significant role in advancing Avoice's future development.

To further assist social organizations in socialization and better meet their needs for online collaboration, project management, and knowledge accumulation, the Feishu team launched the Public Welfare Program, creating a series of supportive initiatives tailored for university research groups, student organizations, and nonprofit organizations.


We sincerely appreciate Feishu's assistance and support. We will also respond with the greatest enthusiasm and effort, amplifying and passing on this care and goodwill to more people in need, working toward a world of love and equality for women. We also wish Feishu greater recognition, adoption, and affection from more users in the future!
