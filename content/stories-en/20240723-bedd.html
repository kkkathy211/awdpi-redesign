The U.S. office team of AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) recently attended the high-level forum side event on Global Energy Interconnection at the United Nations Headquarters in New York, themed "Accelerating Global Energy Transition and Interconnection to Promote the Achievement of Sustainable Development Goals."


This side event was part of the United Nations High-Level Political Forum on Sustainable Development and attracted widespread attention from officials, experts, and scholars around the world.




Background of the Event


This annual event was officially launched on July 10, jointly organized by the Global Energy Interconnection Development Cooperation Organization, the Permanent Mission of China to the United Nations, the United Nations Economic and Social Commission for Asia and the Pacific (ESCAP), the United Nations Economic Commission for Europe (UNECE), the United Nations Economic and Social Commission for Western Asia (ESCWA), the United Nations Sustainable Development Solutions Network, and the Latin American Energy Organization. The meeting aimed to explore how to achieve the 2030 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) by accelerating global energy transition and interconnection.


The President of the 78th session of the United Nations General Assembly, Dennis Francis, UN Deputy Secretary-General Amina Mohammed, China's Permanent Representative to the UN Fu Cong, UNDP Administrator Achim Steiner, UN Under-Secretary-General for Economic and Social Affairs Li Junhua, China's Special Envoy on Climate Change Liu Zhenmin, and heads of the UN regional economic and social commissions for Europe, Western Asia, Asia-Pacific, and Latin America and the Caribbean participated in the event in person and online.



Key Remarks and Insights


During the event, President Dennis Francis of the 78th session of the UN General Assembly delivered a speech in person, emphasizing the importance of accelerating the transition to clean and renewable energy systems and calling for global cooperation and bold leadership. UN Deputy Secretary-General Amina Mohammed delivered her remarks via a pre-recorded video, highlighting the critical role of Sustainable Development Goal 7 (ensuring access to affordable, reliable, sustainable, and modern energy for all) in achieving all other SDGs.


Global Energy Interconnection Development Cooperation Organization


In addition, the Global Energy Interconnection Development Cooperation Organization, initiated and established by China, released its first "Global Power Development Index Study" during the event, proposing a multidimensional and systematic comprehensive evaluation framework for power development, scientifically assessing the power development levels of global regions and major countries across four dimensions: supply security, consumption services, green and low-carbon development, and technological innovation.



Conclusion


AWDPI's participation in the UN Global Energy Interconnection side event not only demonstrated Avoice's active engagement on the global stage, but also further strengthened our commitment to global issues and gender equality. By participating in such global forums, we showcase our influence in international affairs, particularly in advancing the role and status of women in energy transition and sustainable development. We believe that through continuous efforts and broad collaboration, we can bring about more positive change for women worldwide and the cause of sustainable development.


Thank you to every friend who supports and follows Avoice. We look forward to working together with all of you to drive more positive change in the future!
