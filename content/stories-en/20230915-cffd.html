World Economic Forum and the Global Shapers Community



The Global Shapers Community is a global youth network directly under the World Economic Forum (WEF), founded in 2011 by Professor Klaus Schwab, founder of the World Economic Forum. As a renowned international youth organization, the Global Shapers Community aims to harness individual, community, and global efforts to provide young people with a global platform to shape the future.


Given the World Economic Forum's unique convening power, the Global Shapers Community is characterized by diversity in demographics, geography, and professional fields. This diverse community unites around a shared aspiration: to channel the boundless energy and passion of its members to build a more peaceful, prosperous, and inclusive world.



GSC Shanghai Hub



The Global Shapers Shanghai I Hub is one of the first hubs established in China, founded in 2012. The hub is primarily composed of outstanding young individuals from various industries. We believe that young people should be "active drivers of change, not passive observers"—through the hub's platform, we aim to foster faster and better development of our members, while enhancing the social contribution and influence of both the hub and its members through the implementation of various social initiatives.

Vision and Mission of the Global Shapers Community


· Vision: We believe that young people will be the core force.


in building solutions, shaping policies, and driving lasting change. This generation of youth, inheriting immense global challenges, can become an essential part of creating solutions by amplifying youth voices and strengthening self-organized impact. With the support of the World Economic Forum, we firmly believe that world leaders will ultimately listen to this generation of young people and take concrete action!


· Mission: We make progress by empowering young leaders, building strong teams, and equipping them to create positive change in their communities and the world.


Specific initiatives include:


1) We invest in community members, especially emerging young leaders around the world, to successfully sustain impact across different levels;


2) We build diverse teams to achieve greater outcomes through collective strength than through individual efforts;


3) We create impact through self-organization of the Shanghai hub to meet the needs of the Global Shapers Community;


4) We join hands and leverage greater influence to impact global affairs and solve global challenges.


The Global Shapers Community in Action



By joining the Global Shapers Shanghai I Hub, you will gain...


· connections with youth hubs in over 400 cities worldwide and support from more than 10,000 outstanding young people;


·  opportunities to co-initiate projects and activities that promote regional development and help build a better local community;


· access to exchange with global leaders from all sectors through the World Economic Forum network, and represent youth voices by proposing initiatives;


· opportunities to represent the global youth community at events such as the World Economic Forum, the Annual Meeting of the New Champions (Summer Davos), and the Global Shapers Summit;


·  access to our digital engagement platform TopLink, where you can connect with 60,000+ Forum partners, learn about the latest Forum initiatives, and interact with other Forum communities.


Application Criteria


1) Age between 18–27 at the time of nomination


2) Entrepreneurial spirit oriented toward global public good


3) Strong commitment to enhancing leadership skills and better serving society


4) Upholding the highest standards of ethical integrity and academic honesty


5) Exceptional personal qualities and strong proficiency in both Chinese and English


6) Strong team collaboration skills


7) Must be a resident of the city where the Shanghai hub is located


8) Willing to actively participate in the work of the Shapers community, uphold its mission and goals, and support the personal growth and professional development of other Shapers


9) Willing to collaborate with the local hub to make positive contributions to local community development


Application Process


Notice: There is no fee for application—please beware of scams 


1) Application 

Application deadline September 30, 2023, Scan the QR code below, visit https://www.wjx.cn/vm/tn7Vvej.aspx, or click "Read More" to complete the application form —the form must include both Chinese and English resumes. Up to two additional supporting documents may be submitted if desired, sent to the email address:[email protected](email subject: "Shanghai Hub Application + Name").

2) Preliminary Screening

The Shanghai Hub Curatorium will conduct an initial review of submitted materials. Shortlisted applicants will proceed to a phone interview.


3) Interview

After passing the preliminary screening, applicants will be interviewed by the Shanghai Hub Curatorium (interview location and time will be notified separately).


4) Observation Period

The Curatorium will send application materials and interview feedback to the China Regional Hub for initial review. Applicants who pass this review will enter a 6-month observation period, during which the Curatorium will assess their performance.


5) Final Review

Applicants who successfully complete the observation period will be officially recognized as Global Shapers and can check the review results on the World Economic Forum website.
