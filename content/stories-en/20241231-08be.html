We approach time with kindness,

turning moments into poetry;

we move forward with sincerity,

making every journey smooth and clear.


Today,

the new year begins anew with fresh hopes,

and the other shore still calls for us to walk together.

We do not wish to be mere observers of life,

but rather a crossing point where helping others brings fulfillment,

awakening more warmth that has long gone unnamed.


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International and

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project

wish you a happy new year—may blessings bloom one after another, and spring mountains come into view!
