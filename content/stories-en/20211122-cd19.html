The "Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project" is dedicated to assisting overseas Chinese women who suffer from domestic violence and improving the living conditions of vulnerable groups of women abroad, building a bridge between women in need and various resources, becoming their solid support and warm shelter..

We emphasize self-empowerment of the target group of women, while also providing multi-dimensional support, including language, psychological, legal, medical, shelter, channels, education, economic assistance, storytelling and amplifying voices, and community support—expanding the breadth of aid while fostering a virtuous cycle of mutual support.

If you or someone you know has any of the following needs:

1. Requires relevant consultation and assistance

2. Wishes to receive Avoice's storytelling and voice amplification support

3. Wants to join the Overseas Chinese Women Protection community

4. Wants to collaborate or provide resources for the project
