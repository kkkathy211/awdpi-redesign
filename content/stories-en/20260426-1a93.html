Mama Cash Resilience Fund 2026

Swipe right to view in Chinese

Project type

Grant / Funding

Host institution

Mama Cash — the oldest international women's fund in the world, dedicated to mobilizing resources globally to support feminist movements led by women, girls, transgender, and intersex people

Overview

The Mama Cash Resilience Fund 2026 is a prestigious global grant programme that strengthens feminist movements by supporting women-led funding organizations around the world. The fund plays a crucial role in advancing human rights, gender equality, and social transformation by investing directly in women's funds. Unlike many traditional grant programmes, the Resilience Fund is rooted in participatory grantmaking and collective decision-making, established by women's funds specifically for women's funds.

Location

Global

Duration and format

1-year grant cycle; funding focuses on strengthening the internal development and sustainability of recipient organizations, not external regranting activities.

Eligibility

  • Only women's fund organizations may apply.

  • Organizations must work from a feminist perspective with a priority focus on advancing the rights of women, girls, transgender, and intersex people.

  • Organizations must be led by the communities they serve, with leadership and decision-making bodies including individuals directly affected by the issues addressed.

  • Organizations must be independent of large institutions or have a clear plan to achieve autonomous operation within 2–3 years.

Benefits

  • €30,000–€40,000 grant

  • fundable areas include leadership development and transformation, organizational learning and evaluation systems, staff well-being and collective care, financial sustainability strategies, digital security improvements, and institutional strengthening and resilience building

Application materials

  • Formal application form (or video application)

  • detailed budget

Cost

Free to apply

Deadline

Refer to official website

Keywords

gender equality, women's empowerment, feminist fund, global solidarity, women's funds, participatory grantmaking

Official link

https://www.mamacash.org

Additional notes

  • Open globally to organizations from all continents

  • political parties, government agencies, or religious institutions are not eligible

Project type

Grant / Funding

Host institution

UNESCO United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization – dedicated to building international cooperation in education, sciences and culture and to promoting gender equality

Overview

Each year, the UNESCO Prize for Girls' and Women's Education recognizes two laureates who are making outstanding efforts in favour of girls' and women's education. It honours successful projects that improve and promote the educational prospects of girls and women and in turn, the quality of their lives. Funded by the Government of the People's Republic of China, the Prize is conferred annually to two laureates and consists of an award of US $50,000 each.

Location

Global

Duration and format

Nominated projects must be established and running for at least two years and show potential to be replicable and scalable

Eligibility

Individuals, institutions or organizations advancing girls' and women's education are eligible for nomination. Nominations are made by governments of UNESCO Member States or non-governmental organizations (NGOs) in official partnership with UNESCO. They can nominate up to three individuals, institutions, or organizations. Self-nominations are not accepted.

Benefits

  • Each laureate receives an award of US$ 50,000 to help further their work in the area of girls' and women's education

  • international recognition

  • attendance at the award ceremony held at UNESCO Headquarters in Paris

Application materials

Nominations must be submitted in English or French via a dedicated online platform

Cost

Free to submit nominations

Deadline

12 May 2026 by midnight, GMT +2

Keywords

gender equality, girls' education, women's education, funding, UNESCO, international award

Official link

Additional notes
