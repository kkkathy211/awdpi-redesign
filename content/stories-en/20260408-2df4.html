About AWDPI



AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development.


It evolved from Avoice (the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in Belgium, the UK, the United States, Australia, and Thailand. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with organizations across sectors, including United Nations system agencies, relevant government departments in various countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions.


AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the lived realities and challenges faced by Asian women, providing them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more opportunities for Asian women to thrive socially, economically, culturally, and educationally, helping them grow into a vital force for social progress.



About the AWDPI UK National Office


The AWDPI UK National Office is a key branch established by the Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) in a core European region, and serves as the central body responsible for implementing AWDPI's mission of protecting and empowering Asian women within the UK.


As the vital hub connecting AWDPI's global network with local services in the UK, the office leverages the headquarters' mature transnational assistance system and international cooperation resources. It has established five core departments: General Affairs, Fundraising, Partnership Development, Support Services, and Academic Research. Fully aligned with the UK's legal framework, social environment, and public welfare ecosystem, the office's core mission is to eliminate gender-based violence and discrimination against Asian women in the UK. It builds a professional, localized support network across the country, delivering comprehensive, tailored public welfare services to Asian women facing hardship.


Since its establishment, the AWDPI UK National Office has consistently upheld the core values of "Participation, Inclusion, and Empowerment," building a comprehensive operational framework that spans emergency case assistance, local resource referrals, cross-cultural community engagement, academic and policy research, and public advocacy for gender equality.


The office actively collaborates with local UK nonprofit organizations, government agencies, legal service providers, academic institutions, and community groups. It offers targeted support to Asian women experiencing domestic violence or rights violations, including one-on-one language assistance, legal referrals, shelter placement, and psychological counseling. At the same time, through localized program implementation, evidence-based research, and policy advocacy, the office raises awareness and understanding of the challenges Asian women face in the UK, continuously improving their living and development conditions, and helping every Asian woman in the UK access equal opportunities and live with dignity.

AWDPI Lead Roles – Brief Introduction


At AWDPI, each department and division is typically led by two co-leads to ensure stability, continuity, and mutual support within the volunteer structure. In some cases, there may be one or more than two leads depending on project needs, with adjustments made flexibly by the organization.


As core members of the organization, leads are responsible for team coordination, task delegation, and project advancement. When a lead plans to step down, they must communicate in advance and complete a full handover according to organizational procedures to ensure smooth continuity of work.


When a lead position is vacant, the organization will first recommend outstanding members from within the department to take over. If no suitable candidate is available, the Founding Team, Secretary General, or Youth Chair Council will temporarily assume the role until a suitable lead is found.


The AWDPI lead system is designed to ensure long-term organizational stability while offering volunteers opportunities to grow and develop leadership skills.
