01

Program Overview


The We Talk project was initiated by Microcosmos Volunteer Camp, an organization committed to youth volunteer empowerment, and is a public welfare growth program targeting university students and working professionals.


Focusing on personal development and career planning, the project encourages mutual empowerment by inviting experienced and socially-minded mentors to answer questions and provide guidance to participants facing challenges in personal or professional growth. We aim to build a platform for friends with practical experience or work backgrounds who are willing to participate in public welfare projects to grow together with diverse youth, and to offer self-improvement opportunities for those seeking relevant guidance.


After a year of refinement and practice, the We Talk project currently includes two main components: "We Talk Exchange Hour" and "We Talk Lab".

Program Content


We Talk Exchange Hour





We Talk Exchange Hour focuses on youth exchange, recruiting mentors to provide one-hour one-on-one consultation services to participants. The nine currently open consultation areas are: academics, civil service exam preparation, competitions, further studies, research, job hunting, entrepreneurship, social practice, and life planning.


If you have outstanding achievements in any of these fields, welcome to apply through the mentor channel to answer questions and guide project participants. If you hope to grow in a particular area, welcome to apply through the participant channel to discuss personal development with mentors. Of course, you may also choose to apply for both roles and gain diverse experiences within the project.

We Talk Lab




We Talk Lab focuses on career empowerment and includes a series of engaging activities such as introductory and advanced Career Awareness Workshops, industry sharing sessions, resume workshops, individual interview workshops, group interview workshops, and career fairs, aiming to provide practical guidance and support participants' growth toward their life goals.


Seven Key Activities

Industry Sharing Session

May 31 (Friday)

The Industry Sharing Session aims to let participants hear authentic voices from within the industry. This session, we have invited two senior HR professionals from the We Talk project to share insider information and personal experiences in human resource management, helping participants gain industry insights, understand development trends, and make informed career choices.


Career Awareness Workshop (Introductory Level)

June 15 (Saturday)

When standing at the crossroads of career choice, how should you balance your inner feelings with external expectations? How can you explore inwardly to find a suitable career? The Career Awareness Workshop (Introductory Level) aims to inspire participants' awareness of inward self-exploration and provide directions and methods to help clarify career paths while identifying personal strengths and capabilities.

Resume Workshop

June 16 (Sunday)

The Resume Workshop will provide practical resume writing tips and advice to help resolve participants' various concerns about resume creation. The workshop includes not only theoretical instruction from mentors but also one-on-one practical guidance, directly addressing and solving resume pain points.

Individual Interview Workshop

June 22 (Saturday)

The Individual Interview Workshop aims to provide more hands-on individual interview practice opportunities to help participants improve their interview skills. Each participant will be paired with a mentor to simulate a real individual interview scenario, with feedback and guidance provided to enhance participants' preparedness and increase their chances of success.

Group Interview Workshop

June 29 (Saturday)

The Group Interview Workshop aims to provide more hands-on group interview practice opportunities to help participants improve their group interview skills. The workshop will simulate real group interview scenarios combined with mentor feedback and guidance, allowing participants to assess their interview abilities while strengthening teamwork and adaptability skills.

Career Awareness Workshop (Advanced Level)

June 30 (Sunday)

Once we have established the awareness and framework for self-exploration, how can we go deeper in understanding ourselves? The Career Awareness Workshop (Advanced Level) aims to help participants, who already have a foundation in self-exploration, access more diverse methods and channels for deeper personal discovery.

Career Fair

July 6 (Saturday)

The Career Fair aims to become a direct communication platform between participants and recruiting organizations, while helping various organizations identify talented candidates. During the fair, organizations will give presentations, and interested participants can interact online with recruiters to engage in deeper job-seeking conversations.

(Please note: if any of the above event times change, participants will be notified separately)

How to Apply




Recruitment Targets and Criteria


Mentors

1. Applicants must have an educational background of senior year undergraduate or above, both current students and working professionals studying or working overseas or in China are eligible to apply.

2. Applicants should possess certain social practice or work experience, and stand out in one or more areas such as job hunting, academic study, civil service exams, further education, competitions, research, entrepreneurship, social practice, or life planning.

3. Applicants interested in peer mentoring, career planning, or vocational guidance will be given priority, especially those with relevant experience.

4. Applicants must have strong self-awareness, analytical skills, empathy, listening skills, and communication abilities, and be capable of providing high-quality advice and guidance to mentees.


Mentees

1. Applicants must be undergraduate or graduate students (no grade restrictions) at universities overseas or in China, or young professionals at the beginning of their careers.

2. Must demonstrate a serious and sincere attitude, with genuine needs.

3. Must have clear objectives and a defined area and direction for exchange.

4. Must be proactive and engaged during the exchange, respect their mentor, and be willing to build a positive relationship.

5. Must comply with project arrangements, follow exchange guidelines, and attend the orientation meeting on time.





Number of Recruits


There is no limit on the number of mentors recruited for We Talk exchanges; the number of mentees is expected to be 50-60 .





Application Notes


1. Applicants are not required to pay any application fee.

2. This project is non-profit, and the project team will not provide any compensation to mentors.

3. Please complete the application form carefully, as the information provided will serve as a key basis for matching mentors and mentees.

4. The focus area during subsequent exchanges must remain consistent with the information provided in the application form and cannot be changed arbitrarily. Applicants are advised to complete this section thoughtfully. A personal resume must be uploaded in the application form, and mentors must upload a personal photo for use by the project team in creating mentor promotional posters.

5. Personal information of participants may be involved during activities in the Lab section. Please follow activity requirements and protect your own and others' privacy.

6. Each workshop session has 20–30 available spots. Spots are limited and selection is competitive—interested applicants are encouraged to apply as soon as possible.

7. If selected, participants are required to meet project team requirements and attend the entire program. By default, both mentors and mentees are expected to attend all activities on time, whether in the exchange sessions or the Lab section. If a participant cannot attend on time due to personal reasons, they will no longer be eligible to participate in other We Talk project activities in the future.

8. All We Talk exchanges and Lab activities will be held online.
