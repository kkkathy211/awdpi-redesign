The second AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International annual conference and fifth anniversary celebration was successfully held from July 25 to 27, 2025, at the Yangcheng Lake scenic area in Suzhou, Jiangsu Province!


Over 30 members participated in the event, including AWDPI co-founders, board members, advisors, department heads, and representatives of outstanding volunteers of the year.



During the event, everyone gathered by the lakeside to jointly celebrate AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International's five-year journey, and through three days and two nights of deep connection, strengthened their collective power, pledging to continue writing warm chapters for AWDPI in the future.


Scenic views of Yangcheng Lake


Overview of the annual meeting activities


Game-based reflection: Immersive experience of women's realities



During the annual meeting, the organizing team carefully designed a series of activities enabling participants to reflect through games and heal their minds through hands-on crafts.


On the morning of July 26, 2025, everyone engaged in a deeply moving "Women's Survival Simulation Experiment" through the board game "Li Zhihui's Survival Game," collectively walking through the life journey of an ordinary woman from childhood to old age. Education, marriage, parenting—each choice in every scenario affected Li Zhihui's self-esteem, social identity, stress levels, and life points. Participants made choices cautiously, profoundly feeling the weight imposed by social systems and the difficulties of women's growth, with sighs and contemplation interwoven in every decision made during the game.





Board game: "Li Zhihui's Survival Game"


At the end of the game, participants filled whiteboards with reflections: "Finding and protecting one's autonomy," "So many social relationships to consider," "Life is too hard"…Li Zhihuis need support from social systems—and this is precisely the meaning of why we gather here together, advancing hand in hand.


Participants' reflections


Craft-based healing: Slow, meticulous, soothing the mind


In the afternoon, everyone participated in DIY craft activities including bead stringing, Suzhou intangible cultural heritage lantern making, and vegetarian mung bean soup, embarking on a journey of emotional healing.


The team members doing DIY



In the "Beaded Time Machine" session, AWDPI's Xiao Qiu from the Psychological Support Division guided everyone to understand the different meanings represented by bead colors and shapes. Team members, through recalling the past and envisioning the future, selected their favorite beads. A variety of beads flowed through their fingers, transforming into unique and beautiful bracelets.

Participants' beaded bracelet creations


During the Suzhou lantern-making session, everyone laid out plain silk and sketched various patterns. As night fell, colorful lanterns lit up along Yangcheng Lake, their glow reflecting with the shimmering lake surface. Later, everyone made Suzhou-style mung bean soup, cooling off in the summer heat with refreshing mint-flavored mung bean soup, collectively experiencing the crispness and delight of this traditional Suzhou drink.

Recalling and envisioning: Advancing hand in hand into a new chapter


Five long years, steps never ceasing.


During the annual meeting, on-site team members connected remotely with online volunteers. Together, they reviewed and envisioned the organization's growth over the past five years. The next day, using fabric collage techniques, they integrated their hopes into fabrics and buttons, creating a collage of tomorrow's vision. Scattered fabric pieces gradually came together, the initial blank canvas filling with diverse colors—just like AWDPI's trajectory, starting from nothing and growing into a vibrant, colorful reality over five years.


Team members' fabric collages


After the meeting, carrying the energy gathered by Yangcheng Lake, everyone will head toward their own mountains and seas. Yet, no matter where we are, we will join hands again. Ahead, the night may be long, but with stars twinkling, we will never walk alone.


Group photo of the annual meeting



What participants said


The annual meeting felt like a microcosm, allowing me to "see" so much.

Long after leaving Suzhou North Station, I still think of Li Zhihui's life, feeling endless guilt, anger, sorrow, and the strength emerging from them. I remember that we gathered here not only because of shared beliefs and goals, but also because of our respective privileges. I remember the emotions, images, and lives carried in the crafts, the night lights, conversations, and moving moments.

It was as if I saw a microcosm of women's conditions—countless possibilities, countless ways of relating to one another. Most importantly, we "see" each other. This seeing is not just an aggregation of experiences here, but also a reflective, mirroring experience. I think I will carry forward the humid summer air of Suzhou nights, holding onto the feeling of walking slowly with a paper lantern, seeing another girl waving at me from a window, and keep moving forward, hoping this world will always have a light shining.

—Qiaoqiao, Head of Curriculum Development



I was very happy to participate in the 2025 AWDPI annual meeting. My biggest feeling is that after five years, I finally saw each of our mutually supporting team members appear before me in person. So whenever I met each team member, I felt deep gratitude—whether they had been with us throughout the five years or were new friends who joined later. I feel that anyone willing to enter this project is a vital presence in advancing women's power and gender equality.

During this annual meeting, unfortunately, I had to return home to work midway and missed the final day with everyone. But what was precious and deeply touching was that every team member made others feel truly valued—not just politely respected, but genuinely honored for your existence and efforts from the bottom of their hearts.

Finally, I was very happy to co-facilitate the beaded bracelet salon with everyone. Actually, as the facilitator, I felt deeply healed, probably because everyone willingly entered that safe, warm space with me in that moment. So once again, thank you all for bringing me this strength, and I wish everyone the energy and mindset needed to move toward your next life goal. May AWDPI continue to contribute to Asian women for five more years, then ten, fifteen, twenty, and even longer.

—Xiao Qiu, Head of Psychological Support Division



My biggest feeling is that the team members are truly beautiful human beings—passionate, pure, warm, and confident. These few days felt like being gently wrapped in a warm current. I think in many difficult moments ahead, I will recall these days and draw strength from everyone's companionship to firmly believe in moving forward.

Li Zhihui's story made me reflect: many times, I've ignored my own feelings and stress in order to maintain social expectations. Making the bracelet helped me face myself more honestly and become more forgiving toward my past, realizing that even a chaotic past, when strung into a bracelet, actually looks quite beautiful~ (laughs). The night talk with uu helped me understand the logic behind voices different from my own. Oh, and I was pleasantly surprised to see male members in the organization. I've always believed gender equality should be advanced by everyone together, and I applaud their contributions.

Finally, let me end with Gou Gou's words, which recently brought me great healing: "This is not your fault. You have the right to hesitate, to be angry, and to choose not to act for now. Most importantly, you are not alone—AWDPI will be by your side whenever you need us."

—Fa Banxia, Head of Monitoring and Evaluation Division



This was my first time attending an AWDPI offline event, and I was thrilled to finally meet many friends I've known online for a long time. Everyone was so genuine, warm, and easy to get along with, making me deeply feel the organization's unique cohesion and support. I'm so glad to be part of AWDPI, and I look forward to continuing to walk side by side with everyone in the future.

The part that impressed me the most was Li Zhihui's survival game, which truly made me reflect deeply. The first time, we only survived until age 35; the second time, although we made it into our 70s, it was spent enduring various injustices and compromises. My written reflection at the time was: "Thank goodness life isn't a multiple-choice test."——Perhaps precisely because of this, we have the chance to keep making mistakes, struggling, breaking free, and moving forward.

Thank you for the inspiration and strength this annual meeting brought. Looking forward to seeing you all again.

—Long Yao, California, USA Representative



It was a great honor to join this family in my fifth year with AWDPI, arriving at a magical moment of connection. When I first arrived at the annual meeting, I was actually very nervous—as an introvert, I hadn't attended such a large social event in a long time. But from the wooden cabin barbecue on the first night, I knew I no longer needed to worry. We chatted aimlessly around the table, rushed together to the balcony to watch the sunset, though we had never met before, we instinctively welcomed each other with smiling eyes and loving hearts.

In just two short days with everyone, I experienced a sense of "hearing" and "being heard" In Li Zhihui's survival game, everyone offered their own advice for different dilemmas; even the same choice could stem from vastly different considerations, allowing me to hear the diverse and rich inner worlds of women. In the bead-stringing workshop, Xiao Qiu guided us to re-examine our past, present, and future using different beads. Though meant to be a quiet activity, I could clearly hear my own inner voice across each stage of life. I believe that whether it's hearing others or hearing oneself, being heard by others or by oneself, these are all precious qualities and sources of strength in today's complex modern society.

Thank you to all the partners who have contributed strength to AWDPI, Wishing AWDPI everlasting growth, and women forever strong and spirited.

——uu, Head of Program Design Division
