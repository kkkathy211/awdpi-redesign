How much do you know about these 16 days?


Every year from November 25 to December 10 marks the global "16 Days of Activism Against Gender-Based Violence." During this period, people around the world unite to speak out and take action to end gender-based violence.


2025 Theme Focus: Eliminating Digital Violence (UNiTE to End Digital Violence against All Women and Girls)


With the development of internet technology, violence and control are no longer confined to real life—they have extended into digital spaces: harassment, exclusion, and bias. Algorithms act like spotlights on a stage, illuminating women's digital footprints.


Yet violence does not cease to be violence just because the setting has changed; silence does not mean consent, but rather that too many people have nowhere to speak; the digital world is not a fictional realm—it equally needs rules, care, and protection.


The "16 Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence" is not an end, but a beginning.


In an era where digital spaces continuously shape public narratives, Asian women's dialogues should not be forced to remain fragmented interactions in front of screens. AWDPI will attempt to launch actions through a "global linkage" approach, bringing together voices originally scattered across different locations into one shared public space, building a connected platform where voices can be seen and responded to.


May the moment we see each other, we realize we are not alone. May these sixteen days become a turning point, enabling more women to no longer retreat, stay silent, or walk alone in the digital world.


We hope this will be more than just a lecture—it will be the beginning of mutual illumination and collectively weaving a support network.


At the intersection of the digital and the real, let us stand closer and walk more steadily.
