At different stages of life, we often find ourselves in moments like these: hesitating on the subway, wondering whether to leave a stable but no longer fulfilling job; pausing before writing a resignation letter, questioning if we truly have the courage to try a new role; torn between family and self, unsure whether to respond to external expectations or follow the voice within. There is no single answer to these questions, but they shape our different life phases and push us to keep growing.


AWDPI wants to create a special afternoon for everyone in New York—a chance to pause from the rush, and a space to reflect on "choices" through art and sharing.


We have invited  artist and curator Xin Gao. Her works feature the circle and order of mandalas, as well as the symbolic power of hearts and fire. They are not only a fusion of visual art and ritual, but also Xin Gao's personal expression of healing and growth. This time, she will share with us how she navigates between different identities and roles, and how these experiences converge in her art into a narrative about women and life.




Perhaps in her story, you will see your own past hesitations and reflections, and also find some courage and inspiration for facing the future.

Guest Introduction


Xin Gao

Female artist | Curator | Spiritual mentor | "A player of life's game"

2025 Artist-in-Residence, School of Visual Arts, New York

2024 Founder of "Heart Gallery" in Chiang Mai, Thailand

2022 Art curation project awarded Gold Prize in EMA Asia-Pacific Region

2021 Selected for G20 YEA Young Entrepreneurs' Alliance

Her works have been exhibited in: New York · Mexico City · Chiang Mai · Shanghai · Guangzhou · Xi'an

She has worked in the PR department of Louis Vuitton China, served as a cultural educator at the British Council and the University of Missouri, USA, and collaborated with brands such as Maserati, Volkswagen, and Alipay. Her work spans art, business, and spirituality, continuously exploring how women can reinvent themselves at different stages of life.


Highlights of the Talk


  • "Five Choices of a 33-Year-Old Woman"

Through five fictional yet real characters, this piece presents women's choices and struggles between migration, family, career, motherhood, and freedom, reflecting the transformation and growth of modern women at different life stages.


  • Continuation and Deepening of Her Work

From the globally mobile series "The World is My Mandala" to the ceramic heart series embodying the imagery of "fire," she continuously uses art to explore "how to find a new self amid transformation."


  • Inspiration from Cross-Disciplinary and Spiritual Practice

From luxury brand PR to independent artist, how did she transcend roles, integrate spiritual practice, and make women's growth stories visible to more people?
