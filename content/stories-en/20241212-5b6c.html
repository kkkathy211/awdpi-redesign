At the recently held 2024 AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum, they—representing diverse fields, regions, and age groups—gathered together, offering a brilliant intellectual feast through their unique perspectives and profound insights.


In the field of intangible cultural heritage (ICH) preservation and dissemination, they have used distinctive wisdom and steadfast dedication to nurture the flourishing of tradition, advancing ICH from local roots to the global stage. In the journey of empowering future female leaders, they have deeply explored women's potential and discussed how to build a sustainable ecosystem for women's empowerment. Their sincere words shared personal experiences and reflections, illuminating the path forward for those navigating confusion or darkness.



Let us listen to their wisdom and explore the greater possibilities for women in the preservation of intangible cultural heritage and the building of women-centered platforms.




It is a firm sense of responsibility toward safeguarding and passing on ICH, and a beautiful vision for constructing women's platforms and empowering female leadership—each word and phrase painting a picture of their wisdom, perseverance, and strength.


May this intellectual feast inspire more women to find cultural strength through ICH preservation and promote social equality and inclusion through the cultivation of female leadership. May more women find their own stages and shine with their unique brilliance.
