A nonprofit organization based in New York, USA Womankind is now calling for submissions Hear Our Voices 2026 for its seventh annual online art exhibition!


This October, coinciding with Domestic Violence Awareness Month in the United States, Womankind will center its theme on "Presence/Pleasure/Power—Existence, Pleasure, and Power" to present, through art, the stories and healing journeys of gender-based violence survivors.


Experiencing gender-based violence may make one feel that their existence, pleasure, and power have been stripped away. Yet in the process of healing, these can be rediscovered, redefined, and reclaimed.


Womankind invites survivors of gender-based violence, allies, and community members to respond to this theme through art and share your vision of "existence, pleasure, and power." Creators from Asian, Asian American and Pacific Islander, and other marginalized communities are especially encouraged to participate.

Hear Our Voices

Hear Our Voices was launched in 2020 by Womankind, a New York-based nonprofit. Through its online art exhibitions, the project shares survivors' stories and healing journeys, creating a shared space for mutual witnessing, connection, and empowerment.

🔗: https://www.hearourvoiceswomankind.com/



Submission Guidelines



All media and styles are welcome, including but not limited to:

  • Painting, illustration, and sculpture

  • Photography, collage, and graphic art

  • Writing and poetry

  • Music and dance

  • Performance art and other forms suitable for online presentation

Submissions can be newly created works responding to this year's theme, or pieces that emerged organically through self-expression or art therapy.
