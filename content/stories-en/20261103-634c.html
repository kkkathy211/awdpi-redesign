Six years of deep commitment, fulfilling promises through action. AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International has consistently stood at the forefront of protecting and empowering Asian women overseas—from emergency case interventions to systemic advocacy, from cross-cultural community building to localized philanthropy. Our work has spanned multiple countries across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific region, lighting hope and safeguarding dignity for Asian women living abroad through concrete, on-the-ground actions.


As a key operational region for AWDPI, the U.S. Office is actively expanding youth volunteer networks across various locations, aiming to engage more young people who care about women's development and public welfare, and to jointly participate in international philanthropic initiatives that contribute to the advancement of Asian women and their communities.



About AWDPI



AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development.


Evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project) established in 2020, AWDPI now operates in multiple countries across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in Belgium, the UK, the United States, Australia, and Thailand. It has built a broad global cooperation network with United Nations system organizations, relevant government agencies, international organizations, multinational corporations, and leading academic institutions.


We consistently uphold the core values of "participation, inclusion, and empowerment," providing end-to-end support for Asian women in crisis, building cross-cultural peer support networks, advancing gender equality through academic research and policy advocacy, and enabling every Asian woman to access equal development opportunities and live with dignity.


As AWDPI's core implementation team in Southeast Asia, the Thailand National Office leverages Thailand's diverse, inclusive social environment and strategic geographic position to deeply connect local communities, international organizations, and social service resources. Focusing on the real needs of Asian women in Thailand, it carries out work in emergency referral, policy advocacy, academic research, community engagement, and cross-border collaboration, continuously advancing regional gender equality and inclusive environments.

We are committed to

providing rescue and support for domestic violence survivors. Through a comprehensive support system and a robust overseas assistance network, we promptly respond to the needs of Asian women overseas in areas such as domestic violence prevention, emergency aid, and follow-up services.


Building community connections. By establishing an "online community matrix" and "offline mutual support groups," we create an international platform for Asian women overseas to communicate, learn, and support one another, forming peer support networks that enable self-development.


Enhancing women's influence. Through forums, seminars, workshops, and other activities, we actively facilitate multi-stakeholder collaboration to analyze experiences, study challenges, explore solutions, and showcase the strength and potential of Asian women.


Launching awareness campaigns and advocacy. We actively conduct public outreach, academic research, and policy advocacy to increase global attention to Asian women overseas and promote improvements in related policies and development environments.

AWDPI Leadership Role System Overview 

At AWDPI, departments are typically led by designated leaders who ensure operational stability and continuity within the volunteer framework. As core team members, leaders bear primary responsibility for team coordination, task assignment, and project advancement. When a leader plans to step down, they must communicate in advance and complete a full handover according to organizational procedures to ensure smooth continuity. In the event of a leadership vacancy, the organization will first consider internal recommendations of high-performing members; if no suitable candidate is immediately available, the management team will temporarily assume the role until a qualified leader is found. The AWDPI leadership system aims to ensure long-term organizational stability while offering volunteers opportunities for growth and leadership development.
