CEGA Environmental Public Welfare "Her" Forum and Project Launch Event


June 5 is World Environment Day (WED). Women are a vital force in advancing environmental development. In the field of environmental public welfare, we see many women taking leadership roles in social organizations. Women possess unique qualities and capabilities to manage various complex issues, while also making distinctive efforts and sacrifices. These outstanding female representatives deserve broader social recognition. On the other hand, as a whole, women's roles in areas such as the environment, climate change, and biodiversity conservation remain underexplored, and the impacts of environmental and climate issues on women cannot be overlooked. The China Environmental Grantmakers Alliance (CEGA) fully recognizes that gender issues are a significant concern within the environmental public welfare sector, and plans to launch the CEGA Environmental Public Welfare "Her" Project, inviting like-minded partners to explore and work together on this issue.


The CEGA Environmental Public Welfare "Her" Project will include three components: CEGA "Her" Forum, to be held annually around June 5 (World Environment Day), focusing on environmental issues from a female perspective, empowering female professionals in the environmental public welfare field and women philanthropists interested in this area, enhancing communication and exchange, and cultivating female leadership in environmental public welfare. CEGA "Her" Voice, supporting outstanding Chinese women in the environmental field to participate in international conferences and events, sharing Chinese experiences, amplifying Chinese voices, and promoting civil society exchanges between China and other countries. CEGA "Her" Stories , compiling stories of "her" contributions in China's environmental public welfare sector, documenting and analyzing the practices and experiences of Chinese women individuals, institutions, and experts on environmental public welfare and related intersecting issues, summarizing the current status, challenges, and obstacles faced by women and related institutions in advancing environmental and climate agendas, and providing references and recommendations for institutions and the sector on how to better support the development of female leadership in environmental public welfare.


On the occasion of World Environment Day 2022, we will host the inaugural CEGA Environmental Public Welfare "Her" Forum and the launch event of the CEGA Environmental Public Welfare "Her" Project (online) on June 10 (morning). Focusing on the environment from a female perspective, we will invite outstanding women from domestic and international environmental public welfare sectors to share their "her" stories, and engage in dialogue with male representatives in environmental public welfare on female leadership and how the "Her" Project can cultivate more outstanding women in the field. We are also honored to have Ms. Yang Ruikan, Partnerships Specialist at UN Women, as the representative speaker from the China office, and Mr. Jia Feng, Chief Expert at the Publicity and Education Center of the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, deliver welcome remarks, witnessing the official launch of the CEGA Environmental Public Welfare "Her" Project.


The CEGA Environmental Public Welfare "Her" Project has received support from all CEGA member partners, as well as annual special support from the Vanke Public Welfare Foundation and the China Foundation for International Studies (CFIS). Together, we are extraordinary!


We welcome friends from all sectors who care about environmental and women's development to attend! Join us in discussing the "her" issues in environmental public welfare and add a "her" touch to World Environment Day!
