At the recent 2024 AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum, individuals from diverse fields, regions, and age groups gathered together, offering a brilliant intellectual feast through their unique perspectives and profound insights.


"Does prioritizing women help advance gender equality?" "What challenges do young female workers in the Philippines face?" "What impacts stem from gender-based inequalities in time use between men and women?" "How does gender discourse hegemony in the public sphere affect women?" "What issues exist regarding the portrayal of women in brand advertisements?" Through debates, they sparked intellectual inspiration; through academic research, they connected reality with theory, deepening analysis and broadening horizons, examining the challenges women face from multiple dimensions and collectively exploring ways to advance women's empowerment and gender equality.



Let us listen to voices from diverse perspectives, seek resonance, and jointly embark on this journey of uncovering women's stories.




Although these topics only represent a snapshot of the countless issues in real life, it is fortunate that there are always people who, with sharp insight and unique viewpoints, identify problems, bravely raise questions, and actively explore solutions.


Words that open new chapters—strong, powerful, and resounding. Inspired by this, let us move forward hand in hand, continuously break through barriers, so that every persistent effort and exploration echoes across the vast horizon, collectively painting a brilliant chapter for women as a community.
