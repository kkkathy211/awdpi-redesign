 Column Introduction:


The Avoice Bi-monthly Report is a work report documenting the main activities and key progress of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project over each two-month period. Within Avoice's daily management, it serves as one of the key mediums to promote internal information exchange and cross-departmental communication and collaboration.

Starting this month, we will officially and continuously release the Avoice Bi-monthly Report to the public. We hope that through this report, we can share with all partners who care about Avoice's development the organization's major work and phased achievements, present a transparent and open Avoice, and help everyone better understand how Avoice turns individual small tasks into significant progress through the collective efforts of departments, volunteers, expert advisors, and partners.



★Avoice Bi-monthly Report – Issue 1, 2023★

Summary of Bi-monthly Work


1

Project Quality Department

·Academic Research Division·

From December 2022 to January 2023, the Academic Research Division completed an outline for a thematic report and produced two issues of the Avoice Watch Monthly Bulletin—selected global highlights on gender equality progress and overseas Asian development. The bulletins covered topics including news commentary, overseas crime reports, gender equality, and Asian voices. For details, please read: December 2022 issue, January 2023 issue.


In addition, the Academic Research Division recruited five new members, successfully completing high-quality recruitment for the academic team and reinforcing the AWDP project group. Welcome to all new members joining the Academic Research Division family!


· Monitoring and Evaluation Division·

From December 2022 to January 2023, the Monitoring and Evaluation Division produced the 2022 Annual Impact Report (in both Chinese and English). For details, please see:


Chinese version


English version


Additionally, the Monitoring and Evaluation Division assisted the Human Resources and Administration Division in building the volunteer training system by drafting the volunteer training handbook; advanced the implementation of team leader training, conducted interviews with six outstanding team leaders, and preliminarily completed the framework of the team leader training system.


· Project Design Division·

Under the framework of UN Women, Avoice successfully held the 16 Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence for the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women. A series of online activities and campaigns were organized, including anti-domestic violence public education classes, film screenings, a traveling mailbox initiative encouraging people to write postcards for survivors of domestic and gender-based violence, academic reviews, youth voices collected from around the world, and legal literacy classes. During the campaign, the activities reached over 3,000 people on platforms such as Xiaohongshu and WeChat, and received 50 postcards in multiple languages.


The division also assisted the Monitoring and Evaluation Division in planning and implementing the volunteer satisfaction improvement project.


2

Support and Assistance Department

· Case Coordination Division·

From December 2022 to January 2023, the Case Coordination Division completed the 2023 new assistance model training for department volunteers.


· Legal Support Division·

From December 2022 to January 2023, the Legal Support Division provided legal aid services to four women seeking help and completed anti-domestic violence legal documents for six countries (Belgium, the UK, France, the Netherlands, the US, and Australia) for future assistance use.


· Psychological Support Division·

From December 2022 to January 2023, the Psychological Support Division provided psychological support services to two women seeking help (including two newly added cases).


The Psychological Support Division launched a professional volunteer recruitment initiative and welcomed six professional counselors into the volunteer network; improved compliance systems by establishing a counselor work guide and client guidelines.


In addition, the first series of psychological courses was launched on the official WeChat account. The content includes characteristics of relationship violence, potential impacts of relational trauma, and how to understand trauma. For details, see: Avoice Psychological Course | Understanding Trauma – It's Not Your Fault. If you feel confused, angry, anxious, guilty, or any other emotions due to past experiences, that is normal—but it is not your fault. Please know, trauma can be healed. From the moment you arrive here, we have already begun a new journey together.


· Language Support Division·

From December 2022 to January 2023, the Language Support Division completed the drafting of the Language Support Volunteer Work Guide; held the first team leader meeting of 2023 to finalize the annual plan; established and began using the Language Support Division work progress dashboard.


· Empowerment Support Division·

From December 2022 to January 2023, the Empowerment Support Division provided empowerment support services to one woman seeking help (including one newly added case), primarily focused on finding an appropriate shelter.


3

Partnerships and Outreach Department


From December 2022 to January 2023, the Partnerships and Outreach Department reconnected with three partners and established formal partnerships with three new organizations, with cooperation planned for large-scale summit and event organization.


Ten new partners were added to the partnership network. The department planned and implemented one forum: the First Avoice Global Development Forum for Chinese Women, and assisted in organizing one major summit: the UNIDO Global Conference on Science and Technology Innovation – 2022 Global C-position Youth Leaders Summit for Sustainable Development.


Review of the First Avoice Global Development Forum for Chinese Women


On the evening of January 7, 2023 (Beijing time), the First Avoice Global Development Forum for Chinese Women was successfully held. The forum invited over ten domestic and international experts and scholars from UN Women, international organizations, academic institutions, renowned universities, and tech innovation companies to participate online. Through keynote speeches, panel discussions, and paper presentations and reviews, the forum facilitated cross-generational, cross-sectoral, and cross-regional knowledge sharing and collaborative dialogue on gender data across fields such as technology, philanthropy, workplace, education, law, psychology, and policy, offering participants profound and innovative perspectives on the theory and practice of combating gender-based violence.


4

Communications Department


From December 2022 to January 2023, the total number of followers across the official WeChat account and other platforms reached 8,420, including 770 new followers on WeChat, 57 on Xiaohongshu, and 73 on the video account. A total of 65 posts were published across platforms, accumulating 50,644 views. The most viewed post reached 3,625 readers: "Avoice Forum | Guest Lineup: Chinese Women Who Ride the Waves! – 5 Days to Go."


The Communications Department produced four original articles, including one interview piece: Avoice Yu Jian • Her Voice | "Sleeping Bag Lady": Don't Dwell in Sadness, Keep Moving Forward; and three column articles: Avoice Her Perspective | Self-Defense: The "Shield of Zeus" for Women Against Domestic Violence, Avoice Her Perspective | Until the Gender Data Gap is Filled, Keep Being a "Difficult" Person – Invisible Women, Avoice Her Perspective | The Path of Women Breaking Constraints – The Neapolitan Novels.


The team produced three short videos, published on Douyin; hosted a New Year movie screening event, screening five films from January 9 to February 4.

The Communications Department finalized the logo design and continues to advance brand identity development.


Two new members have been recruited into the WeChat account operations team, with ongoing recruitment for this and other teams.


5

Fundraising Department


From December 2022 to January 2023, the Fundraising Department submitted one fundraising proposal and received preliminary confirmation from a funding partner.


The Fundraising Department is continuously developing the Fundraising Management Work Guide for training fundraising team members.


The Fundraising Department has partnered with Peking University Consulting Association to conduct research and provide management recommendations on fundraising and partnership development in Belgium.


6

General Affairs Department

· Human Resources and Administration Division·

From December 2022 to January 2023, the Human Resources and Administration Division organized volunteer and advisor recruitment for the communications team, successfully recruiting 14 members.


In addition, the Human Resources and Administration Division revised and implemented the volunteer management system, establishing a volunteer training mechanism and incentive program.


· IT Support Division·

From December 2022 to January 2023, the IT Support Division updated the website through seven versions, published five articles/academic research reports, and received 170 visits from 10 countries/regions; the new website development progress reached 5%.


In addition, the IT Support Division supported two teams in building five Feishu automation tools, used by 126 members, including recruitment forms and volunteer points tracking sheets.
