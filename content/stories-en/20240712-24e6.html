What is the Horizon Plan?


"We aim to illuminate not just ourselves, but the entire world."


The Horizon Global Youth Common Development Program, abbreviated as "Horizon Plan," is an initiative launched by the School of Social Sciences at Tsinghua University. The Horizon Plan is dedicated to cultivating youth with global vision and leadership, promoting global common development, and has established close partnerships with numerous universities, government institutions, and enterprise groups, bringing together high-quality resources from international organizations, renowned universities, government agencies at all levels, and leading domestic and international enterprises and financial institutions.

Our features:

●  Practice-oriented: The Horizon Plan emphasizes the development of practical skills, enhancing participants' global governance capabilities and hands-on competencies through research projects, field visits, and practical mentorship.

● Systematic development: The Horizon Plan is committed to strengthening members' self-identity, leadership, and professional knowledge, encouraging Chinese and global youth to actively engage in sustainable development actions.

● Sustainable Development Goals: The Horizon Plan supports the achievement of the United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, providing global youth with a diverse platform for career development, innovation, and entrepreneurship.


Our achievements:

Since its launch in 2017, the Horizon Plan has initiated programs such as the Global Governance Talent Competency Development Program, Global Governance and International Organizations Workshop, Climate Action and Disaster Risk Reduction Maker Program, and Youth Sustainable Development Camp, establishing a training system primarily focused on university students and young entrepreneurs, covering the youth stage. Nearly 3,000 individuals have participated in various talent development programs and projects. Meanwhile, the Horizon Plan has strongly supported partners in cultivating global competence and cross-cultural communication skills, as well as sustainable development education initiatives, providing curriculum and teaching support for over 10 projects, reaching nearly 1,000 participants.

The Horizon Plan has always regarded building a platform for international youth cooperation and exchange as a key mission, organizing or participating in international youth exchange programs through multiple channels and levels around the SDG agenda. The Horizon Plan also proactively organizes diverse international youth exchange activities. On one hand, it actively invites international youth to study and visit China, promoting dialogue and collaboration between Chinese and foreign youth in sustainable development fields, having organized nearly 20 exchange events with nearly 500 participants. On the other hand, the program actively goes global, having organized six Global Competence Training Camps to countries including the United States, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, conducting in-depth exchanges, visits, and immersive learning at international organizations, multinational corporations, renowned universities, think tanks, and foundations, with nearly 200 participants.

The Horizon Plan has also partnered with large enterprises and local governments to establish Global Youth Sustainable Development Practice Bases, supporting youth in conducting scientific research and innovation in sustainable development fields. The Horizon Plan places strong emphasis on collaboration with governments, enterprises, and professional institutions in sustainable development. It has conducted over 10 research projects with central government agencies on national policy directions; carried out 18 research surveys on regional economic and social development with relevant government departments and professional institutions, covering more than 20 cities and counties across over 10 provinces including Jiangxi, Jilin, Zhejiang, Guizhou, Henan, Hebei, Yunnan, Xinjiang, Qinghai, Sichuan, and Jiangsu; and organized over 20 policy analysis and strategy research forums and conferences with industry associations and related enterprises on healthy industry development, providing intellectual support for advancing sustainable development agendas in relevant fields.


To date, the young leaders supported and nurtured by the Horizon Plan have emerged in various fields: over 40 have interned or worked at major international organizations such as the United Nations Headquarters, UNESCO, UNDP, UNEP, UNFPA, UN Women, IFAD, UNIDO, FAO, UNOOSA, and the International Atomic Energy Agency; over 50 are pursuing advanced studies at world-class universities including Harvard, Yale, Columbia, George Washington, Johns Hopkins, Cambridge, Oxford, and MIT; over 20 have engaged in business and social innovation and entrepreneurship, covering fields such as natural language processing, artificial intelligence, smart parks, carbon neutrality and new energy, new materials, art design and global cultural communication, women's rights protection and development support, and youth innovation and entrepreneurship support; over 20 are active in leading firms such as McKinsey, Bain, Deloitte, PwC, and Morgan Stanley; and nearly 30 have joined central Chinese government departments, provincial governments, and well-known state-owned enterprises and financial institutions.

What will you gain by becoming a Horizon member?

The Horizon Plan runs one cohort annually, each lasting one year. Committed to the five core principles of "awakening, inspiring, supporting, uniting, and taking action," it guides every member to deeply engage in the great cause of sustainable development. During this year, members will:


Receive systematic capacity building:

Personalized development program: The Horizon Plan provides each member with a free, systematic development program, supported by a faculty of top experts from international organizations, public sectors, private institutions, and professional social organizations, offering comprehensive support for members' growth.

Face-to-face exchanges: In addition to the rich course resources accumulated over previous editions, members will have opportunities for in-depth, face-to-face interactions with experts, gaining valuable first-hand experience and knowledge.


Participate in flagship activities to enhance personal capabilities:

Diverse participation: After completing the training, members will have opportunities to participate in multiple flagship activities of the Horizon Plan, enhancing their practical skills from project planning to execution.

Initiative-driven projects: The Horizon Plan encourages members to unleash their creativity by proposing their own initiatives or projects and recruiting like-minded team members. The Horizon Plan's extensive network and resources will provide solid support for members' innovative ideas.


Gain ongoing alumni network support:

Lifelong learning and growth: Even after the program concludes, members automatically become part of the Horizon Plan alumni network, enjoying continuous access to resource sharing and project opportunities..

Continued engagement: Every member of the Horizon Plan alumni network can directly participate in or lead projects, growing together with the Horizon Plan and continuously exploring new possibilities.

How can I get involved with the Horizon Plan?

The Horizon Plan consists of four sub-programs:


Youth Leadership Program: Aims to systematically develop participants' overall capabilities in global governance, responding to risks and changes, and leading youth initiatives, primarily targeting current students at Tsinghua University.


Youth Action Alliance Program: Aims to connect and integrate youth organizations and platforms both on and off campus, domestically and internationally, that are committed to global common development, building a youth network, consolidating shared understanding, and amplifying collective action to jointly advance the achievement of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.


Future Partners Program: Aims to use partners' specific projects as practical and action platforms to enhance participants' hands-on abilities and jointly carry out collaborative initiatives.


Youth Reserve Force Program: Aims to identify and nurture rising talents with strong interest in global governance and sustainable development, supporting them to better understand global issues and relevant fields of knowledge, with a focus on outstanding high school students both in China and abroad.


In the past, the Horizon Initiative has primarily been carried out and carefully organized by members in a volunteer capacity. This year, based on a review of past achievements and management experience, the Horizon Initiative has innovatively introduced a dual-layer management model of "organization" and "project":


At the organizational level of the Horizon Initiative (Programme):


• Executive Director: 1 person, responsible for appointing project personnel and overall organizational management.

•  Executive Committee: 5 to 7 members, assisting in daily operations and the planning and implementation of projects.

• Permanent Executive Leads: 3 members, ensuring the continuous operation and daily support of the Horizon Initiative.



At the project level of the Horizon Initiative (Project):


• Project Lead: Fully responsible for project management and execution, directly accountable for project outcomes.

• Team Lead: Established according to project scale and needs, responsible for executing specific tasks and team coordination.

• Team Members and Volunteers: Enjoy autonomy in their work, receive guidance from the project lead, and share in project outcomes.



Under the above management model, members of each sub-program of the Horizon Initiative will directly participate in project implementation and management, receiving guidance from senior mentors and peer members, and comprehensively enhancing their personal skills and leadership through hands-on experience. Every member involved in an organization or project will receive a bilingual Chinese-English certificate issued by the Horizon Initiative, certifying their contributions and achievements. For core members, we will additionally provide official competency certification from the Horizon Initiative, along with various talent referral opportunities for future career development.

2024 Recruitment Plan?

This cycle will recruit 30–35 like-minded partners. Applicants must be current students or young leaders under the age of 30. Based on the program's existing resources and training framework, all members must possess basic Chinese communication skills.

All individuals meeting the above criteria are welcome to apply, regardless of school, location, gender, academic background, or experience.


Selection Process:

This recruitment includes document review, alumni interviews, and expert evaluation as three stages. Document review will screen candidates for interviews based on application requirements and submitted materials; subsequent alumni interviews and expert evaluations will assess applicants' sense of responsibility, critical thinking, and personal experiences through interviews and discussions. We may also conduct special activities to further evaluate applicants' overall qualities and fit with the program.

Application period: July 9 to July 28

Alumni interviews: August 10–11

Expert evaluation: August 12–20 (tentative)

Results announcement: by August 31 (tentative)



Applicants should highlight the following competencies and qualities:

Social Responsibility: Love for the Party and the country, firm beliefs, inclusiveness and compassion, and a commitment to giving back to society and serving others

Broad Vision: An open-minded attitude and international perspective

Innovation Capability: Solid theoretical research foundation and critical thinking skills

Leadership Potential: Rich experience in teamwork and leadership, outstanding influence

Language Proficiency: Strong foreign language communication skills, both written and spoken

Stress Resilience: Ability to withstand high-intensity, fast-paced work
