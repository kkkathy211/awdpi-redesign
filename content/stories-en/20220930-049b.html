 Introduction:


The 1973 U.S. Supreme Court decision in Roe v. Wade recognized women's constitutional right to abortion and legalized abortion nationwide. Nearly fifty years later, the Supreme Court has overturned this ruling, stating that the U.S. Constitution does not confer a right to abortion and that the decision to permit abortion should be left to individual states—opening the door for states to enact abortion bans. This marks the first time in U.S. history that the Supreme Court has revoked an established constitutional right involving fundamental humanitarian considerations, stripping women of their dignity and autonomy over their own bodies.



To raise public awareness about the real-world implications and potential consequences of this decision, promote understanding of relevant legal knowledge, and provide insights into career development and prospects in the legal profession, 4Grad and Avoice will co-host an online legal education lecture on the U.S. anti-abortion case on October 9.

 

Co-organizers



4Grad (For Grad) is composed of outstanding students and graduates from China and abroad, aiming to build a public-interest information exchange platform for global elites. We maintain close connections with master's and doctoral students, readers, and outstanding graduates from top universities worldwide, jointly creating a nonprofit platform for communication and resource sharing dedicated to serving young global leaders and fostering a warm, multicultural community. To date, 4Grad has organized four offline events, from the Peking University Guanghua Youth Forum to the Columbia China Forum, bringing together elite forces from various fields and enabling East-West ideas and philosophies to interact and collide. 4Grad has also hosted dozens of online events, including a graduate school application sharing session co-organized with the Southwest China Students Association that attracted over 1,000 participants. We have invited more than 30 senior peers currently studying at top 15 U.S. universities or working at globally leading companies, with cumulative participation exceeding 6,000 people.




Avoice is the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project. Committed to eliminating gender-based violence and advancing gender equality, the project provides comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing domestic violence. By building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, and by uniting broader youth-led public-interest forces, Avoice advocates for public attention and support for the challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. We strive to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners promoting gender equality and protecting vulnerable groups.
