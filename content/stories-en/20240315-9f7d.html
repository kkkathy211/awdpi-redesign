2024 Women's Day Workshop Series


From March 8 to 10, AWDPI hosted four diverse gender equality workshops in Ecuador, Germany, and online, reaching approximately 280 participants.



Women's Day Workshop: Session One

The situation of women in the "paradise on earth"—the Galápagos Islands


Guests discussed gender-based violence and the intersecting marginalization faced by women with disabilities in the Galápagos Islands of Ecuador from a local perspective, and engaged with online audiences on how local economic and educational structures impact gender dynamics.




Women's Day Workshop: Session Two

"How Comprehensive Sexuality Education Advances Gender Equality"


Guests explained how comprehensive sexuality education helps build more inclusive and equitable gender norms and relationships; participants discussed the social construction of "masculinity" and the challenges faced in becoming sexuality educators.



Women's Day Workshop: Session Three

"Gender Challenges in Humanitarian Work"


Guests shared gender-related challenges from local projects and workplaces in the humanitarian field; through mini-games and simulated negotiations, participants discussed various everyday inequalities and experienced the real barriers present in humanitarian work.




Women's Day Workshop: Session Four

"How International Organizations Advance the Elimination of Violence Against Women"

——A Case Study of Albania


Guests presented the situation of gender-based violence in Albania, showcased the on-the-ground projects of UN Women Albania, and exchanged insights with the audience about experiences in advancing gender empowerment in European countries.
