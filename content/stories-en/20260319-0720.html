In March 2026, the global gender equality community turns its eyes to the United Nations Headquarters in New York—the 70th session of the UN Commission on the Status of Women (CSW70) is about to convene.


As an international nonprofit organization long dedicated to improving the living conditions of Asian women overseas and committed to eliminating gender-based discrimination and violence, Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) has been invited to organize three online parallel events at CSW70. Among them, the second forum will focus on "Women's Rights Protection, Gender Justice, and Legal Resource Accessibility" as its core theme, featuring insights and practices from four frontline activists. We sincerely invite you, who care about gender equality, to join us!


Forum Two

Legal Resource Accessibility for Vulnerable Women Groups



 Time  March 16, 19:30–21:00 (Beijing Time)

 Format  Online parallel forum

 Registration link 

https://shorturl.at/2Lirp

 Content 

This parallel forum will focus on the real challenges and practical breakthroughs faced by vulnerable women groups in accessing legal resources. Four speakers will share from different perspectives:

  • frontline workers in women's and children's rights protection will draw on decades of local experience to analyze systemic barriers that women face in accessing legal resources and share strategies to overcome them;

  • child protection experts will focus on the plight of girls, revealing how gender shapes their distinct help-seeking pathways;

  • social workers and Women's Federation staff will unpack the multi-agency collaboration mechanism in combating domestic violence, addressing key linkages among the Women's Federation, public security, and the judiciary;

  • marriage law advocates will share how legal resources can be effectively brought from paper to practice through communication and outreach.

Together, these four dimensions will explore how legal justice can become accessible and reach every woman in need.





We are honored to welcome the following guests:



Li Ying

Lawyer for Women's and Children's Rights Protection

A senior lawyer and founder of a highly influential women's organization, she has dedicated 24 years to protecting the rights of women and children, providing pro bono legal services to over ten thousand women and children. She has handled more than 700 cases involving domestic violence, sexual harassment, sexual assault, workplace gender discrimination, and women's marital and family rights, offering legal aid to victims of gender-based violence.


Wang Yanping

Director, Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI)

Senior Social Worker (passed 2022 written exam) and one of the first social work supervisors appointed by the Shaanxi Provincial Department of Civil Affairs. Since the Fourth World Conference on Women in 1995, she has been engaged in women's and children's rights protection and public welfare initiatives in China. She has served as a member of the 13th Executive Committee of the Shaanxi Provincial Women's Federation, an expert on the professional panel for legislative consultation on the "Shaanxi Province Implementation Measures of the PRC Anti-Domestic Violence Law" at the 2020 Shaanxi Provincial Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, an expert in the Shaanxi Provincial Government Office for Women and Children's Affairs' Children's Work Think Tank, and an expert on the Xi'an Municipal Office for Women and Children's Affairs' Committee for Gender Equality and Child Priority in Policy and Legal Review. Her work and action research focus on marriage and family social work, child development and protection, school social work, domestic violence prevention and intervention, women's development, gender education and training, and the cultivation and promotion of social organizations.


Ren Ren Bu Xu Ci Xing

  • Independent media presenter

  • Shenzhen-based marriage lawyer

  • Advocate for gender awareness awakening

Through over a decade of legal practice, she has gained deep insight into the power structures within intimate relationships and their roots in inequality. This experience led her to study philosophy, gender studies, and feminist theory, marking her transition from legal practitioner to gender theory explorer.

Chen Qiang

Beijing Children's Legal Aid and Research Center

Head of the Online Protection Department

He has long been engaged in research on issues related to online protection for minors, contributing to the compilation of publications such as *A Guide to Self-Protection for Youth in the AI Era: Navigating the Online Maze*, *Guidelines for Preventing and Controlling Child Harm and Violence Against Children*, and *100 Questions on Minors' Online Protection*. He is also the lead author of research reports on compliance assessments for platform enterprises' minor online protection, minors' internet addiction, and personal information protection for minors. He was awarded the title of Outstanding Individual in the National "Eighth Five-Year" Legal Education Mid-Term Campaign in 2024 and recognized as an Outstanding Public Interest Lawyer in Fengtai District in 2023.
