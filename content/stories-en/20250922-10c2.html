One

AWDPI Annual Forum


AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) was initiated in early 2020 at the onset of the global COVID-19 pandemic. It is a growing international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development. AWDPI has established a broad and effective domestic violence response and support network for Chinese women overseas facing the threat of domestic violence. By building extensive collaborations with United Nations system organizations, relevant government agencies, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions, AWDPI promotes greater societal attention toward Asian women. Among its flagship initiatives are the AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum and the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program). After more than five years of development, these projects have expanded to East Asia, Europe, North America, and Australia.


To summarize the efforts of the past three forums and better plan for future development, AWDPI is organizing the fourth annual forum—the 2025 AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum. This announcement is for the Call for Papers and Research Submissions.


For more detailed information about the AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum, please follow our official WeChat account. We will continue to introduce our exciting forum agenda and highly anticipated keynote speakers!


Two

Call for Papers and Research Submissions


1

Purpose of the Paper Review Session

  • To demonstrate AWDPI's academic standards and capabilities;

  • To build a bridge for AWDPI members to participate in academic exchange;

  • To promote interdisciplinary dialogue and co-create an academic network with scholars from diverse fields.

2

Paper Review Process

  • Thematic Paper Review and Presentation: The AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum will allocate 2–2.5 hours specifically for the review and presentation of papers on related themes;

  • Selected Paper Presentations and Expert Feedback: We will select high-quality 4–6 submissions, each of which will be presented by the author in an 8-minute presentation, followed by approximately 10 minutes of expert commentary and suggestions;

  • Poster Exhibition and Networking: After the forum, we will launch an online poster exhibition column, turning high-quality submission abstracts into digital posters (posters) to be published on our social media accounts. All submitters will have the opportunity to communicate and discuss with scholars from around the world, jointly exploring new academic insights.

3

Eligibility for Submission

 This call for papers is open to:

  • Current active AWDPI members

  • AWDPI reserve pool and former members

  • Friends introduced by AWDPI members who express interest in participating

  • Members of the public and students from all walks of life who have strong interest or ideas regarding the forum's themes

4

Submission Themes

Any topic related to fields AWDPI is involved in or plans to engage with, including but not limited to:

  • Movements against gender-based violence

  • Protection of Asian women overseas

  • Initiative for violence-free virtual spaces

  • Women's leadership

  • Women and health

  • Women and economy

  • Women and intimate relationships

  • Women and media representation

  • Women's political and workplace empowerment

  • Women and technological innovation

  • Women and environmental protection, cultural heritage preservation

  • Overseas Chinese women workers

All academic disciplines are welcome. We especially encourage interdisciplinary research submissions and participation, such as:

  • Technology + Philanthropy (e.g., women's online forums and their support for and current status in public sphere construction…)

  • Policy + Women's Health (e.g., women's health from medical and social security perspectives…)

  • Workplace + Education (e.g., competence development for Chinese women overseas in the workplace…)

  • Law + Psychology (e.g., psychological trauma in children exposed to domestic violence environments and its correlation with criminal behavior in adulthood…)

Paper topics and content should align with AWDPI's vision of "creating a world of love and equality for women" and its values of "participation, inclusion, and empowerment"

5

Paper Review Panelists

We will invite guest experts from AWDPI's expert database and well-known scholars from society who are committed to women's rights to conduct paper reviews

6

Paper and Research Selection Criteria

  • Submissions in Chinese or English are accepted; for other languages, please contact the Academic Division separately for consultation:[email protected]

  • Clear purpose, distinct theme, sufficient evidence, logical structure, and concise language

  • Must follow standard academic format, including title, author name, abstract, keywords, main text, and references

  • Recommended length: no more than 15,000 Chinese characters or 8,000 English words

  • Submissions must be original; plagiarism is prohibited, and no infringement of others' legal rights is allowed

  • Papers that have already been publicly published will not be accepted

  • This conference encourages authors with strong interest or ideas about the conference themes—especially those who have not previously participated in formal academic forums—to actively join. Experimental studies, literature reviews, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and other types of submissions are all welcome to promote academic exchange and vitality. We also welcome students without complete research results to submit and share high-quality research proposals, ongoing studies, term papers, or short essays
