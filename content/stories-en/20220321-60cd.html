Who we are?


The Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project is dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and promoting gender equality by providing comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence. Through building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, academic institutions, and industry experts, and by mobilizing youth-driven public welfare initiatives, Avoice advocates for greater public awareness and support for the challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. We strive to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who eliminate gender-based violence, advance gender equality, and protect vulnerable groups.

Avoice Spring Recruitment Summary and Member Introductions


Since the launch of Avoice's 2022 spring recruitment, we have received hundreds of outstanding applications from candidates around the world, including China, the United States, the United Kingdom, France, Australia, the Netherlands, and over ten other countries and regions. Though our applicants come from diverse academic backgrounds and have rich and varied experiences, they share a common warmth for public welfare and a passionate, persistent commitment to eliminating domestic violence and advancing gender equality. We extend our heartfelt gratitude to every applicant for their support of the Avoice project.


After multiple rounds of resume screening, written assessments, and interviews, nearly 100 individuals have joined Avoice's teams in the Department of Project Quality, Department of Project Implementation, Department of Communications, and Department of General Affairs. Throughout the selection process, we placed strong emphasis on candidates' fundamental qualities, depth of thinking and expression, and their passion and courage for social public welfare. We are committed to offering opportunities to every enthusiastic and thoughtful individual who aspires to contribute to the public good.


Our new members come from top universities both in China and abroad, including Peking University, Tsinghua University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Zhejiang University, Harvard University, Yale University, the London School of Economics and Political Science, University College London, the University of Toronto, and the National University of Singapore.


Their academic fields span public policy, sociology, psychology, law, history, gender studies, international business, accounting and finance, industrial design, media, literature, and translation.

Their skill sets include multilingual translation (English, French, Italian, Malay, Spanish, Portuguese), project management, writing and editing, academic research and writing, policy analysis, data analysis, social media platform operations, animation, and video production.


Their past project experiences include long-term volunteer service in public communities, advocacy for women's protection initiatives, TEDx speakers, and operators of popular self-media platforms with large followings.


We look forward to our new members providing stronger support and assurance for Avoice's development, injecting greater innovation and vitality. New Avoice members will also participate in their first onboarding training session in the near future, equipping them for their upcoming volunteer roles!
