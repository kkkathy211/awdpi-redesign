How an Asian woman entered the core of public leadership in a local council in Australia? As the first Chinese-Australian Deputy Mayor of the City of Glen Eira, Li Zhang broke through dual barriers of ethnicity and gender with determination and courage, setting an example for Asian women in public life in Australia.



In this dialogue event, we warmly invite her to engage in an in-depth and heartfelt interview with the audience. During the conversation, she will share her personal journey toward public leadership, reflecting on the challenges commonly faced by Asian women in Australian society as well as their unique strengths. Drawing from her extensive practical experience in multicultural programs and organizations, she will also discuss how such platforms can be leveraged to promote the growth, voice, and empowerment of Asian women.


Furthermore, we will delve into the content of her book*In the Town Hall* (*Zài Zhèngshìtīng Lǐ*)—a work that offers both observations on local governance in Australia and a documentary portrayal of voices from multicultural communities. Attendees will have the opportunity to hear her speak about the stories behind the book and her motivations for writing it.


This is more than just an interview—it is a dialogue about identity, women's leadership, and multiculturalism. Through the lived experience of a female leader, we will witness how an immigrant woman finds her voice, stands firm, and breaks through within mainstream political discourse; and we will take this opportunity to collectively reflect on how we understand "Asian women" and the position and potential of this identity within overseas societies.


Event Time

April 30 (Wednesday), 1:30–3:30 PM (Melbourne time)

Format

Online video conference / In-person dialogue and interview + Q&A session


Annisa Li Zhang, Li Zhang

Councillor and former Deputy Mayor of Glen Eira, Australia; author of *In the Town Hall*; community volunteer; advocate for social equity; MBA from the University of Melbourne; BA in Japanese Language and Literature from Nanjing University; business consulting advisor; hiking enthusiast; Australian rules football team captain; nature lover; trilingual reader; bookworm; mother; daughter.
