🌱 Gaia Fellowship Program Introduction


01 Program Overview


Gaia Fellowship Program This is The Gaia Project's first long-term annual initiative, designed to build a platform connecting egalitarian practitioners and socially responsible enterprises: The program offers selected Fellows volunteer or unpaid internship opportunities, while bringing innovative energy to mentor companies*.

This program focuses on enterprises founded by women-led teams or those centering on women's issues. Applicants of any gender or background are welcome to apply.


After mutual matching, participants will spend three months deeply engaging in the daily operations of the company. Throughout the process, the Gaia organizing committee, companies, and volunteers will conduct regular check-ins and mid-term reviews to ensure both the Fellows' learning experience and the company's operational efficiency.

Fellows who successfully complete the volunteer work and evaluation will receive the Gaia Fellow certification, and join The Gaia Project's global community, connecting with like-minded peers and mentors.


*Some positions include internship/volunteer stipends

03 Program Highlights


As a Gaia Fellow, you will gain:

  • Real Work Experience: Participate in real company projects, developing business insight and professional skills;

  • Feminist Business Practice: Learn and apply feminist principles in the business world, advancing gender equality;

  • Social Impact : Contribute to social innovation projects, joining your efforts with a collective force for social progress;

  • Completion Certificate and Recommendation Letters: Upon successful completion of the program and meeting review requirements, you will receive a certificate from The Gaia Project. Outstanding Fellows may also receive recommendation letters from their mentors.

As a Mentor Company, you will gain:

  • Talent Development: Access and nurture promising future talent, building a talent pipeline for long-term growth;

  • Fresh Perspectives and Creativity: Leverage the innovative thinking and perspectives of a new generation to bring fresh inspiration and momentum to your business;

  • Corporate Social Responsibility in Action: Fulfill corporate social responsibility, advance gender equality within your industry, and enhance your brand image;

  • Join The Gaia Project's global entrepreneurial community and become a driving force among women entrepreneurs.

Who We Are

Named after the Earth Mother, The Gaia Project is an organization dedicated to gender equality and advancing sustainable business practices, founded in 2020 by a group of like-minded dreamers and doers. Our goal is to cultivate the next generation of women leaders through education, resource sharing, and practical opportunities.


Our Vision

We believe that by supporting and resourcing women founders and entrepreneurial teams focused on women-centered products and services, we can spark greater innovation and progress, driving society toward a fairer and more sustainable future.
