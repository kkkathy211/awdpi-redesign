The deepest wounds sometimes come from the people closest to us.


Control, belittlement, coldness, isolation—these invisible behaviors have long been ignored in many Asian families, even masked in the name of "love."


AWDPI has partnered with the nonprofit organization PCE Club to launch an online public lecture series:


"Between Love and Safety: Family 'Violence'"


PCE Club (Parents, Children, and Education Club) is a nonprofit educational organization established in 2003, dedicated to emotional intelligence parenting and mental health education, providing a platform for learning and exchange for Chinese families in North America, having served over 3,000 families to date.


This lecture series consists of two sessions. The first session will approach the topic from the perspectives of psychology, psychiatry, and law, unveiling the truths and misconceptions about family "violence," and will respond to and discuss real-life family and emotional issues submitted by community members—without avoidance, without oversimplification.
