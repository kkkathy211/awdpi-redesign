Through direct conversations with these enterprises, you will gain in-depth understanding of different companies' operational models, talent requirements, and career development pathways. This is an exceptional opportunity to connect directly with employers across various industries and find a platform that aligns with your career development goals.

Company Introductions




Technology


Vertilite


Founded in 2015, Vertilite, as an innovative start-up company, develops and markets high power and high speed VCSEL (vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser) and module solutions.

 

Consisting of experienced scientists and engineers from the industry, Vertilite has successfully released products of high performance for diversified applications in 3D sensing, optical communications, industrial automation, biomedical applications, automotive applications, consumer electronics and LiDAR solutions.

TCL


TCL Technology (full name: TCL Technology Group Corporation, formerly known as TCL Group; SSE: 000100) is an electronics and electrical equipment manufacturing company headquartered in Huizhou, Guangdong Province, the People's Republic of China. TCL produces mobile phones, personal computers, home appliances, electrical lighting, digital media, and flat-screen televisions, which are sold both within and outside mainland China. TCL is listed on both the Shenzhen Stock Exchange and the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. The origin of the name "TCL" is no longer traceable, though it is widely believed to derive from the English abbreviation of the company's early name, "TCL Communication Equipment Co., Ltd." (Telephone Company Limited). TCL's brand identity has consistently revolved around the three letters "T," "C," and "L." On June 18, 2007, TCL Group announced a new brand strategy, redefining "TCL" as "The Creative Life." Previously, TCL had interpreted the acronym as "Today China Lion."


Appotronics Corporation Limited (Appotronics)


Founded in 2006, it is a globally leading supplier of laser core components with original technology and core patents. The company was among the first to be listed on the STAR Market, and is also the first stock from Guangdong Province on the STAR Market and the world's first laser display stock. In 2007, Appotronics originally invented the ALPD® semiconductor laser light source technology. This technology created a brand-new semiconductor laser light source, breaking through the application bottlenecks of laser display core components and imaging solutions in the display field, and has become the mainstream technological path in the laser display industry. It is widely used in automotive, cinema, home, engineering, commercial and education sectors, achieving rapid breakthroughs especially in automotive optics and securing designations from multiple internationally renowned automotive companies. Simultaneously, as laser core technology continuously integrates with the latest technologies such as human-computer interaction, intelligent recognition, the Internet of Things, cloud platforms, and big data, the company has already completed forward-looking layouts in new application fields like AR, intelligent robotics, and aviation displays, and has carried out in-depth cooperation with many globally renowned brands and institutions. Furthermore, the company has taken the lead in undertaking national key R&D programs, collaborating with 12 institutions including the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing Institute of Technology, Sun Yat-sen University, and Zhejiang University to advance the development of national laser display technology, jointly promote the laser industry's development, and build a robust laser industry ecosystem. To date, the company has been recognized as a National High-Tech Enterprise and a National Intellectual Property Demonstration Enterprise. It has also been selected for the STAR 50 Index, and has received numerous honors including the China Patent Excellence Award, the National Enterprise Standard "Leader," and the Golden Bull Sci-Tech Innovation Award.


Hisense Group


Founded in 1969, Hisense owns five listed companies—Hisense Visual Technology, Hisense Home Appliances, Sanyo Electric Holdings, Qianzhao Optoelectronics, and Kelin Electric—listed across Shanghai, Shenzhen, Hong Kong, and Tokyo. The group owns multiple brands including Hisense, Toshiba TV, Ronshen, gorenje, and ASKO. In 2023, Hisense maintained steady growth with annual revenue of 202.2 billion yuan and total profit of 13.6 billion yuan, representing an 11% year-on-year increase. Overseas revenue reached 85.8 billion yuan, accounting for 42.5% of total revenue.

 Huaqin Technology


Huaqin Technology Co., Ltd., established in 2005 and headquartered in Shanghai, China, is a global platform-based listed enterprise for smart products. Upholding the mission of improving people's communication and lives, the company provides end-to-end services including product-level and system-level solutions, hardware and software R&D, and operational manufacturing for global technology brands. Its products and services reach over 100 countries and regions worldwide, delivering hundreds of millions of smart devices annually to consumers, including smartphones, tablets, laptops, smart wearables, AIoT devices, data center products, and automotive electronics.

Deqing Optics Technology


Deqing Optics was jointly founded by several overseas-returnee doctors, dedicated to providing automated inspection and control solutions for manufacturing processes such as laser welding, laser surface treatment, and laser additive manufacturing. It is a national high-tech enterprise, a provincial-level specialized, refined, distinctive, and innovative enterprise, a key supported technology unicorn in Haizhu District, and a hidden champion in the intelligent manufacturing niche sector.





GEEKVAPE


Shenzhen Jicona Technology Co., Ltd., established in 2015, is a leading e-vaporization technology enterprise and a national high-tech enterprise integrating the design, R&D, production, and sales of e-vaporization devices. The company has over 8,000 employees worldwide, with products exported to more than 80 countries including the United States, Europe, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East. Jicona Technology boasts strong R&D capabilities, with hundreds of experienced senior R&D engineers, and holds hundreds of product patents, leading the industry in ceramic e-vaporization and chip technology research.

Shanghai X-META Technology Development Co., Ltd.


[X-META · Opportunity Space Full-Sensory VR World] is a technology and entertainment company integrating cutting-edge AI technologies such as LBMAR+, Unity, Unreal Engine (UE), Midjourney, and generative AI. It pioneers a new VR 3.0+ model featuring multi-sensory, hyper-realistic, and highly immersive walk-through and free exploration experiences. The company owns several tech subsidiaries, including software companies and integrated hardware-software companies.


It has received multiple honors, including being named one of the "Top 10 Immersive Industry Quality Enterprises of 2023" by China's Ministry of Culture and Tourism, designated as "Shanghai's Cultural and Tourism Metaverse Demonstration Project No. 001" by the Shanghai Municipal Administration of Culture and Tourism, recognized as a "First Batch National Immersive Demonstration Project" by China's Ministry of Culture and Tourism, and awarded "Top 10 Immersive Industry Quality Enterprises Nationwide." It is also a key project supported by Shanghai's 2022 Cultural and Creative Fund, a research and industry collaboration base for the Shanghai Jiao Tong University-USC Institute of Cultural and Creative Industry, and a teaching case in Fudan University's EMBA program. The company has been repeatedly invited to attend the Shanghai International Film Festival and has been featured and promoted by international media outlets such as CGTN, CNBC, Shanghai Daily, and PR Newswire.

 

The brand has developed or introduced full-sensory VR immersive experiences such as "Le Bal de Paris," "Luoyang Rises: Full-Sensory VR," "Canglan Jue," and co-branded titles with Harbin Ice and Snow World including "The Ice Dragon's Pearl Quest" and "Magic Ice Castle." Since opening over two years ago, it has ranked first on Meituan Dianping's four major lists and is the only brand to win the "Leisure and Entertainment Annual Selection" award on Dianping, with a user satisfaction rate as high as 99.2%. It currently operates in over 20 cities across China. With multiple rounds of financing completed and a valuation exceeding one billion yuan, the company is expanding rapidly and actively recruiting talent—looking forward to your joining!


GoodWe


GoodWe Technologies Co., Ltd. (stock code: 688390), founded in 2010 and headquartered in Suzhou New District, is a Shanghai Stock Exchange STAR Market-listed enterprise integrating independent R&D, production, sales, and service. The company focuses on new energy power supply equipment, with core expertise in power conversion, energy storage transformation, and energy management, aiming to reduce electricity costs, improve energy efficiency, enable multi-energy complementarity, and create energy value. GoodWe has long been dedicated to the R&D, production, and sales of solar and energy storage power supply equipment. It has developed over twenty series of grid-connected and energy storage photovoltaic inverters with power coverage from 0.7 to 350 kW, and is committed to providing comprehensive smart energy management solutions for residential, commercial, industrial, and utility-scale power stations. The company has established subsidiaries or customer service centers in multiple regions worldwide, with products sold in over 100 countries and regions. In 2021, it ranked among the global top 6 inverter brands according to IHS Markit. According to Wood Mackenzie, GoodWe ranked first globally in shipments of residential energy storage inverters in 2019 and among the top three globally in overall energy storage inverters in 2021. In the 2022 energy storage industry chain ranking released by EESA, GoodWe ranked first globally in shipments of low-power energy storage converters (below 30 kW). Amid the evolving trends of the energy internet, as a holistic solutions provider centered on photovoltaic inverters, GoodWe remains committed to power electronics technology, continuously innovating in fields such as new energy conversion, energy storage, photovoltaic building materials, and smart energy management platforms, promoting its products and solutions globally, and becoming a key force in driving global energy transformation—working together with power grids, industrial parks, communities, and customers to usher in a new era of smart energy!

Huawei


Founded in 1987, Huawei is a global leader in information and communications technology (ICT) infrastructure and smart devices. Its 207,000 employees are located across more than 170 countries and regions, serving over 3 billion people worldwide. Huawei is committed to bringing a digital world to every person, home, and organization, building an intelligent world of interconnected things.



Finance


HSBC


HSBC is one of the largest international financial institutions investing in China, with operations spanning banking, insurance, funds, securities, and fintech.

As the flagship entity of the HSBC Group in mainland China, HSBC Bank (China) Company Limited has service outlets in over 50 cities across the mainland, making it the foreign bank with the most extensive network and the widest geographical coverage in the region.

With deep insights into the mainland market and professional expertise in international financial services, HSBC China provides international financial services to individual and corporate clients through its three global businesses: Wealth Management and Personal Banking (including Global Private Banking), Commercial Banking, and Global Banking and Markets.

Guangfa Bank


Founded in 1988 with approval from the State Council and the People's Bank of China, Guangfa Bank is one of the first national joint-stock commercial banks established in China. In 2016, it became a member of China Life Insurance Group. By 2022, state and state-owned legal entity shareholders held over 95% of its shares. In 2023, Guangfa Bank's total assets surpassed the 3.5 trillion yuan mark. The bank currently oversees 49 directly managed branches, 2 specialized institutions, 1 wholly-owned subsidiary, and 968 operating outlets, covering 116 cities at or above the prefecture level across China, as well as Hong Kong and Macao Special Administrative Regions. Guangfa Bank has been selected as a domestically systemically important bank for three consecutive years, has been named one of "China's Best Employers of the Year" for 13 consecutive years, and ranks 60th in The Banker magazine's Global Top 1000 Banks ranking.




Health and Wellness


WuXi Biologics


WuXi Biologics (stock code: 2269.HK) is a global leading Contract Research, Development, and Manufacturing Organization (CRDMO). Through an open-access, integrated biopharmaceutical capability and technology-enabled platform, the company offers comprehensive end-to-end services to help partners discover, develop, and manufacture biologics, enabling the full process from concept to commercialization, accelerating global biologics R&D, reducing development costs, and benefiting patients.

 

WuXi Biologics has over 12,000 employees across China, the United States, Ireland, Germany, and Singapore. With its professional service teams, advanced technologies, and deep industry insights, the company delivers efficient and cost-effective biologics solutions to clients. As of June 30, 2024, WuXi Biologics has supported a total of 742 integrated projects in R&D and manufacturing (excluding COVID-19-related and inactive projects), including 16 commercial manufacturing projects.


Asymchem


Asymchem (Asymchem Pharmaceutical Group, stock codes: 002821.SZ / 6821.HK) is a globally leading, technology-driven CDMO (Contract Development and Manufacturing Organization) offering one-stop comprehensive services. The company has provided full lifecycle CMC services, as well as efficient and high-quality R&D and manufacturing services, to over 1,100 global clients, accelerating clinical research and commercialization of innovative drugs. It ranks among the top global CDMOs for active pharmaceutical ingredients of innovative drugs and is China's largest CDMO company at the commercialization stage for chemical drugs, with dual listings on the A-share and H-share markets. The company operates eight major R&D and manufacturing bases, and over twenty subsidiaries and offices for R&D, production, and sales located in Tianjin, Jilin, Liaoning, Shanghai, Jiangsu, Boston (USA), and Sandwich (UK). With nearly 10,000 employees worldwide, over 48% of whom are R&D personnel, Asymchem has built strong collaborative relationships with global pharmaceutical giants such as Pfizer, Merck, AbbVie, Eli Lilly, Bristol Myers Squibb, and AstraZeneca, gradually establishing an integrated ecosystem for innovative drug services.



WuXi AppTec


WuXi AppTec (stock codes: 300759.SZ / 3759.HK) is an internationally leading life science R&D service company. The company is committed to talent development and infrastructure investment, building a comprehensive R&D and manufacturing service system that spans the entire process from drug discovery to preclinical and clinical development for multiple therapeutic modalities, including small molecules, large molecules, and cell and gene therapies. WuXi AppTec operates in China, the United States, and the United Kingdom, with over 20,000 employees, providing R&D solutions and maintaining strong partnerships with clients in North America, Europe, Japan, and China.


Internet


Tencent


Tencent is a world-leading internet and technology company that enhances people's quality of life worldwide through innovative products and services.

 

Founded in 1998 and headquartered in Shenzhen, China, Tencent has always upheld the principle of technology for good. Its communication and social services connect over one billion people globally, helping them stay in touch with family and friends and enjoy convenient travel, payments, and entertainment.

 

Tencent publishes multiple globally popular video games and other high-quality digital content, delivering rich interactive entertainment experiences to users worldwide.

 

Tencent also provides a series of enterprise services, including cloud computing, advertising, and fintech, supporting partners in achieving digital transformation and business growth.




miHoYo


miHoYo (full name: Shanghai miHoYo Network Technology Co., Ltd., abbreviated as miHoYo or miHoYo; known as HoYoverse outside mainland China) is a video game company founded on February 13, 2012, headquartered in Shanghai, China. Beyond games, miHoYo's cultural offerings include animated series, novels, comics, and music. The company is renowned for developing action game series such as *Genshin Impact*, *Honkai*, and *Zenless Zone Zero*. Among these, *Genshin Impact*, an open-world action role-playing game released in 2020, is one of the highest-grossing mobile games in history. In 2021, miHoYo was listed by China's Ministry of Commerce as a key national enterprise for cultural exports for 2021–2022..

New Consumption


MINISO


MINISO Group was founded in 2013 and is headquartered in Pazhou, Guangzhou. It operates over 7,000 stores across 111 countries and regions worldwide. The company has achieved dual primary listings in the U.S. and Hong Kong and has incubated a sub-brand—TOPTOY, a global lifestyle brand for trendy collectibles.

 

MINISO is committed to becoming the world's leading IP-driven retail group, partnering with over 100 well-known IP rights holders. Guided by its business philosophy of "Happiness Philosophy," MINISO aims to enable consumers to enjoy joyful living through high-quality products that are "good-looking, fun, and functional," offered at highly accessible prices.

 

Over the past 11 years, MINISO has consistently worked to create a diverse, equitable, broad, and supportive development environment for employees of all nationalities and genders. We look forward to your joining us in our mission: "Young and Bold, Making Waves Around the World."

 AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas and promoting their empowerment and development. It evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), a brand initiative established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established and locally registered as publicly fundraising charities in the United Kingdom, Belgium, and the United States. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments of different countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the challenges faced by Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more opportunities for Asian women overseas to develop in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them transform from a vulnerable group into a vital force driving social progress.
