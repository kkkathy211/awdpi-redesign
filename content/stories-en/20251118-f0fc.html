Introduction to "Her Mountain, Myriad Paths"

"Her Mountain, Myriad Paths" is an interview initiative launched by the Curriculum Development Division of AWDPI member interview program . The name originates from our reinterpretation of the word "mountain": "mountain" can symbolize barriers, pressure, and insurmountable obstacles. But today, we say: I am the mountain. There is no need to look up, no need to cling—women themselves are majestic, independent, and courageous beings; "myriad paths" represents each woman's unique journey and life choices.


"Her Mountain, Myriad Paths" aims to showcase the rich diversity of life stories within AWDPI's community of women, making different narratives visible and honoring diverse choices. Through one-on-one in-depth conversations, we document real, vivid, and distinct life trajectories of women. We believe it is precisely these varied paths that form the complete picture of women's strength—no matter where we go, we can find connection and inspiration in each other's experiences, gaining support and insight through sharing. Here, some climb to the peak, some linger halfway up, some plant flowers at the foot of the mountain. Different paths, yet nothing prevents us from seeing each other and uplifting one another.


This is not only a testament to members' growth, but also a dialogue soon to be opened to the public. We look forward to inviting more companions to listen, ask questions, converse, and connect—through each other's stories, illuminating our own paths forward.


On myriad paths we search, yet shall meet at last; among blossoming mountain flowers, we encounter you.



About Jiujie


Qiaoqiao's view of Jiujie:

"When I first met Jiujiu, I was deeply impressed by the composure and confidence she exuded. 'Graduate of a prestigious university', 'National Scholarship recipient', 'Founder of a social innovation organization'... these shining labels and outstanding resume seemed to write a perfect template of 'meritocracy'. However, what intrigued me most was the rare sense of calm beneath her halo. It wasn't until we had in-depth conversations that I gradually got closer to Jiujiu herself, beyond the labels, and saw that the source of energy bursting behind her smile was not a socialized benchmark, but the 'freedom she produced for herself'. That precious self-consistency accompanied her growth, accumulating into the person she is today, who continues to bring energy to more people through coaching. In her view," there is no such thing as a detour—as long as each step is taken with sincerity, every step counts. . "


If you are currently experiencing:

◾ Struggling between societal expectations and your inner desires

◾ Hesitation and anxiety when facing important decisions

◾ Shaped by meritocracy yet still unsure of who you truly are



then Jiujie's sharing may offer you a fresh perspective.


"Jiujie has a deep affection for the image of waves, because waves of thought, waves of awareness, may at the moment feel like nothing more than water rushing toward you, but in the course of life, their meaning will naturally surge into an ocean. And this is exactly how I see her:


nothing is wasted—every moment is a wave."
