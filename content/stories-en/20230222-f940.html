OCF Forum .

The Oxford China Forum (OCF) is an annual summit initiated by Oxford students and is one of the largest and most rigorous academic forums on China in the UK. By inviting world-class politicians, entrepreneurs, media figures, and scholars to discuss and exchange ideas, OCF provides the public with diverse perspectives to observe, understand, and reflect on a changing China.

OXFORD CHINA FORUM

Official visual poster for the 10th Oxford China Forum

Marking the 10th anniversary of OCF, this year's forum will take "Adapting to Change: Global Transformations and Our Exploration" as its theme, integrating past experiences with future aspirations. From the perspective of the profound changes faced by our generation of young people, we will keep pace with the world and the times. This theme also reflects the Oxford China Forum's vision for its next decade. We believe we can continue to innovate and, in the years ahead, offer audiences a unique window through which to discover and understand the world.


We hope that all those at the forefront of this era, who care about social transformation, will find new and different answers at this forum.


On the occasion of our tenth anniversary, embracing change and adapting accordingly, unafraid of global transformations and committed to our exploration, the Oxford China Forum 2023 invites you to meet us in spring!

Forum Schedule

Oxford China Forum (Oxford China Forum) Founded in 2013, it is one of the largest and most rigorous academic forums on China in the UK. OCF is dedicated to bringing together experts across various fields related to China to discuss a changing China with students from both China and abroad.


The 10th Oxford China Forum will consist of online and in-person components.


During the online forum (February 25–26, 2023), we will explore six major themes: AI-generated art, film and art, feminism, education, business, and urbanization. Experts, scholars, and professionals from these fields will show us how social thought, the economy, and art and entertainment are transforming in this era—and what kinds of innovation these changes inspire.


At the in-person forum (March 5, 2023), we have invited medical and political-economic experts and authorities to jointly discuss how profound shifts in the global landscape are reshaping our lives.

Healthcare Panel


Healthcare Systems:

Are We Ready for the Next Pandemic?


As we gradually emerge from the shadow of the pandemic, it is important to look back and examine its impact on individuals, society, and healthcare systems. Healthcare systems are vital because the medical services they provide—including prevention, promotion, treatment, and rehabilitation—are essential for maintaining public health. In this forum, we will attempt to outline the challenges facing healthcare systems.

Political-Economic Panel


Trade and Geopolitics:

China's Economic Structure and Political Position


With economic development, China is increasingly occupying a central role in international trade and supply chains. As the world's manufacturing hub, China is also actively transitioning toward a technology-driven economy. Globalization and world trade are facing unprecedented challenges. Focusing on two international initiatives—CPTPP and the Belt and Road Initiative—we have invited several renowned experts and scholars to discuss and share their insights.

Feminist Sub-forum


The Myth of New Female Identity and Social Security:
How to Create a World Where Women Feel Safe?


In the relationship between subjectivity and social space, the issue of "safety" has long troubled every aspect of women's lives. Even in today's seemingly "gender-neutral" society, many systemic problems that threaten women's sense of security persist. At this turning point in history, how should we create a society where women feel greater "security" and enhance their self-confidence?

AI Art Sub-forum


Artistic Transformation and Emergence:

The Opportunities and Challenges of AI Art


2022 was arguably the inaugural year of AI-generated art. While the rapid development of AI art technology presents opportunities, it also poses challenges to the existing art industry and legal frameworks. Some artists have begun collectively resisting AI-generated art, and the resulting controversy—accompanied by widespread anxiety over job losses—has drawn significant attention. In this sub-forum, we will invite leading figures from relevant industries to guide us through the present and future of AI art.

Film and Cinema Sub-forum


Shifting Lights and Shadows, Love Letters Across Time:
Chinese Film Criticism at a Historical Crossroads


Over the past three years, the Chinese film industry has faced numerous challenges, and filmmakers' expressions seem unable to meet audience expectations. Meanwhile, the rise of streaming economies and small-screen viewing, along with social media platforms, has quietly reshaped the logic of film consumption and dissemination. We are compelled to ask: At this pivotal moment in history, how can film critics continue to write about the relationship between people and images?

Business Sub-forum


"Opportunity in Change":

New Business Transformations in the Digital Economy Era


At the beginning of 2022, the release of the *14th Five-Year Plan for the Development of the Digital Economy* once again emphasized the "digital transformation" of the national economy, marking the beginning of a new era of digital consumption. Meanwhile, with the rise of e-commerce, digital consumption has grown rapidly, and digital business models are gradually expanding globally. Business digitization is increasingly shaping new ways of consumer life for global citizens. This sub-forum will bring together experts from various fields to discuss the development trends of digital business and its broad impact on human society.

Education Sub-forum


China's Education System After the "Double Reduction" Policy
Where Do We Go From Here?


The introduction and subsequent implementation of the "Double Reduction Policy" have fundamentally reshaped the landscape of compulsory education and the broader macroeconomic environment in China. "Change" is not a fixed endpoint, but a constantly evolving starting point. Change has already occurred; yet the post-change educational landscape and future direction in China remain unclear.

Urbanization Sub-forum

Urbanization and Social Risk Management:

Migrant Populations Under Public Health Challenges


At this historical juncture, as drifters between urban and rural areas, and between cities, migrant populations face dual physical and psychological pressures. This sub-forum aims to offer new insights into urban migrant populations from the perspectives of risk sociology and policy analysis, and to discuss with experts how to leverage social resources to promote the physical and mental well-being and future development of migrant populations under current circumstances.
