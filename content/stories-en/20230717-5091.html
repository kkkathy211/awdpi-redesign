Column Introduction:


The Avoice Bi-monthly Report is a work report documenting the main activities and key progress of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project every two months. Within Avoice's daily management, it serves as one of the key media for promoting internal information exchange and cross-departmental communication and collaboration.

Starting from 2023, we will officially and continuously release the Avoice Bi-monthly Report to the public. We hope that through this report, we can share with all partners who care about Avoice's development the organization's major work and phased achievements, present a transparent and open Avoice, and help everyone better understand how Avoice turns individual small efforts—through the work of various departments, volunteers, expert advisors, and partners—into significant, long-term progress.

Bi-monthly Big Data


Monthly Summary of Rescue Work


  • From April to May (incomplete statistics), Avoice received a total of 4 new women seeking help and is currently providing support to 9 clients;

  • In addition, one case was closed, and a total of 5 case feedbacks were received from April to May; among them, 4 out of 5 clients felt very satisfied, and 4 out of 5 clients considered the staff responses to be very timely.


Monthly Summary of Fundraising Work


  • From April to May, Avoice received two donations totaling 1.33 million RMB (including a 1.32 million RMB donation of Feishu product usage rights from the Beijing ByteDance Public Welfare Foundation and a 10,000 RMB cash donation from an alumnus). Thank you to all donors for your support of Avoice's work!


Bi-monthly Key Events


  1. Completed the refinement of country-specific legal education handbooks on combating domestic violence, and collaborated with the IT Information Office to add the handbooks to the self-help guide section of the Avoice website;

  2. Completed the merger of the Legal Support Office and the Legal Academic Office;

  3. Completed cooperation proposals with Haidilao and Green Logistics Technology Group;

  4. Completed the 2023 spring recruitment, receiving 326 applications and hiring 96 new members;


★Avoice Bi-monthly Report – Issue 3, 2023★

Summary of Bi-monthly Work


I. Program Quality Department


Academic Research Office


From April to May 2023,

  • the Academic Research Office concluded its work on violence cases involving Asian women and the status of women in the workplace in Belgium, and has begun data collection and organization for the UK region;

  • In addition, the Academic Research Office produced two issues of the Avoice Watch Monthly Bulletin—selected global highlights on gender equality progress and overseas Asian development. Topics included news commentary, overseas crime news, gender equality, and Asian voices. The April issue has been published; full content can be read in: Avoice Watch Monthly Bulletin, April 2023.


Monitoring and Evaluation Office


From April to May 2023,

  • the Monitoring and Evaluation Office preliminarily established a task management system to improve organizational efficiency;

  • In addition, the Monitoring and Evaluation Office assisted in the functional assessment of the Empowerment Support Office, conducted interviews with responsible staff and volunteers, and formulated the next steps action plan.


Program Design Office


From April to May 2023,

  • the Program Design Office coordinated all work related to the AWDPI Forum, urging various departments to carry out their tasks in an orderly manner.


IT Information Office


From April to May 2023,

  • the IT Information Office continued to advance the development of AWDPI website 2.0 and the maintenance of AWDPI website 1.0, and followed up on the uploading of articles and academic research reports;

  • In addition, the IT Information Office supported the Human Resources Department in completing spring recruitment resume collection and other tasks.


II. Rescue Support Department


Case Coordination Office


From April to May 2023,

  • the Case Coordination Office welcomed a new member in the European region, who has completed recruitment and basic training.


Legal Support Office


From April to May 2023,

  • the The legal support unit newly accepted 2 cases and is currently providing legal support services to 6 women seeking help;

  • In addition, the Legal Support Office expanded its case and consultation intake channels, extending consultation services to three national offices.


Psychological Support Office


From April to May 2023,

  • the The psychological support unit is currently providing psychological support services to 2 women seeking help, and has launched psychological support services for Avoice internal staff;

  • In addition, the Psychological Support Office achieved its spring recruitment goals and improved the volunteer service manual for the psychology team, further advancing the professionalization of psychological support services;


Language Support Office


From April to May 2023,

  • The language support unit is currently providing language support services to 3 women seeking help;

  • In addition, the Language Support Office established a Chinese editing team and an English translation team; language teams collectively completed the translation of four articles and two videos, including "Feminist Activist Axel J. Enjike: 'I believe in feminism that liberates from within outward.'"


Empowerment Support Office


From April to May 2023,

  • The empowerment support unit is currently providing empowerment support services to 1 woman seeking help, while enriching community operations content by hosting the fourth sharing session titled "Breaking the Invisible Ceiling: Speaking Up About Gender Inequality in the Workplace";

  • In addition, the Empowerment Support Office piloted a flexible subgroup system, updated the overseas rescue resource network, and completed background checks and data entry for six empowerment support database entries.


III. Partnership and Expansion Department


Project Cooperation Office


From April to May 2023,

  • the Partnership and Expansion Office completed cooperation proposals with Haidilao and Green Logistics Technology Group;

  • In addition, the Partnership and Expansion Office supported work related to spring recruitment and the annual forum, and completed external project coordination.



Fundraising Management Office


From April to May 2023,

  • the Fundraising Management Office continued to follow up on the "Youth Leadership Development Program (Haina Initiative)" and formed a project team;

  • In addition, the Fundraising Management Office established a Belgium fundraising information database based on the Belgium fundraising strategy plan issued by PCA, and developed and implemented corresponding action plans.


Rescue Resources Office

From April to May 2023,

  • the Rescue Resources Office completed spring recruitment and onboarding training.


IV. Communications Department


From April to May 2023,

  • the total number of followers across official accounts and other platforms reached 9,430, including 465 new followers on WeChat Official Account, 24 on Xiaohongshu, and 22 on LinkedIn; a total of 21 posts were published across platforms, with cumulative views reaching 10,845. The post with the highest readership was "Avoice Recruitment | Waiting for 'You' This Spring!" with 5,450 views;

  • the Writing and Reporting Office published three articles from April to May: ① "Avoice Thank-you Letter | Gratitude to Feishu Public Welfare Program for Supporting Avoice's Public Welfare Cause in the New Year" ② "Avoice International Exchange | Connecting and Empowering Together—UK Delegation Invited to Attend the Chinese Women's Alliance Gala" ③ "Avoice Healing & Her Voice | Yuyuan's Journey Out of Hardship Abroad: 'I Don't Just Want to Reveal the Problem—I Want to Find Solutions'";

  • In addition, the Writing and Reporting Office successfully held a topic meeting, selected several topics, and laid the foundation for producing high-quality articles in the future.


V. Human Resources Department


From April to May 2023,

  • the Human Resources Department organized the spring recruitment, receiving 326 applications and hiring 96 new members. Welcome to the Avoice family, everyone!

  • In addition, the Human Resources Department implemented a new recruitment team structure and held one departmental meeting; organized one onboarding training session for new members, covering 32 participants.


VI. Belgium National Office


From April to May 2023,

  • the Belgium National Office has submitted its registration materials to the Judicial Bureau and organized an offline event in Liège.
