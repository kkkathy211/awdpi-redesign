In the digital age, social media not only records emotions and opinions but also reflects the deep logic of structural inequalities in society. When racial bias spreads in online spaces, does it have a measurable impact on real-world health? This question is especially urgent for women and immigrants.


01

Guest Information



Speaker: Professor Thu T. Xuan Nguyen

Associate Professor, School of Public Health, University of Maryland, College Park


02

Lecture Information


This lecture will focus on "Using social media and mixed research methods to explore the impact of racism and associated hate crimes on the health of women and immigrants." Through three representative studies, it will present evidence ranging from big data analysis to in-depth interviews, revealing how racism infiltrates critical aspects of daily life.
