Horizon Initiative 2025 New Year Message | The Awakening Silent Majority, The Advancing New Generation of Youth



The "Horizon Global Youth Common Development Program" brings together high-level, high-quality resources from international organizations, world-renowned universities, central and local government agencies, leading domestic and foreign enterprises, and financial institutions. Upholding the mission of "Igniting Youth Dreams, Supporting Personal Growth, Cultivating Leadership Talent, and Guiding Youth Power," the program is committed to aligning with the United Nations SDGs and their specific targets. It aims to raise youth awareness of sustainable development agendas, inspire youth engagement in sustainable development endeavors, support youth-led sustainable development practices, cultivate youth leaders in sustainable development, consolidate global youth wisdom and strength in the field of sustainable development, and drive youth worldwide to take concrete actions toward sustainable development.
