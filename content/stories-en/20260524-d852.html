Building Bridges of Collaboration to Advance Public Welfare


Recently, the Head of Strategic Cooperation and Partnerships at the AWDPI Thailand National Office was invited to represent the AWDPI Thailand National Office (shortened as: AWDPI Thailand Office), visiting the Maha Chakri Sirindhorn Nakhon Nayok Special Education Centre (Maha Chakri Sirindhorn Nakhon Nayok Special Education Centre) to participate in a public welfare visit and donation activity, and jointly promote the implementation of public welfare projects with Singapore International School of Bangkok (Singapore International School of Bangkok) and MIXUE Thailand (MIXUE Thailand) management teams.


This public welfare activity was proactively promoted in advance by the Head of Strategic Cooperation and Partnerships at the AWDPI Thailand National Office, who established cooperative relationships, facilitated the linkage between educational institutions and corporate resources, extended the outcomes of the "SISB FEST" project into practical public welfare initiatives, achieving a continuous transition from campus activities to social public welfare.


A leader from the Maha Chakri Sirindhorn Nakhon Nayok Special Education Centre delivered a speech


In-Depth Visits and Exchanges, Focusing on Special Education Needs


On the day of the event, representatives from the AWDPI Thailand Office conducted thorough visits to the school, gaining detailed understanding of the center's practical needs in teaching support, rehabilitation training, and daily student care, and exchanged views on improving the growth environment for children with special needs, supporting social public welfare resources, and future collaboration directions.. At the same time, they actively coordinated on-site execution, donation coordination, and multi-party communication to ensure the smooth progress of the public welfare project.



Staff from the Princess Sirindhorn Special Education Center

introduced the school's background and operations

As a public welfare organization that has long focused on safeguarding the rights of overseas Asian women and supporting vulnerable female groups, AWDPI is consistently committed to advancing women's empowerment and building social support networks. This public welfare visit not only addresses the developmental needs of women and vulnerable groups within the special education community, but also reflects AWDPI's ongoing efforts to extend social care to a broader range of women in need.


Exchanging and taking group photos with teachers and students at the center


Turning Care into Action, Deepening Public Welfare Collaboration


During the donation segment, the donated materials were successfully handed over, and the contributions will support improvements in students' learning environments and their daily developmental needs. Interactive exchange activities between teachers and students were also held on site, ensuring that public welfare extends beyond material support to include emotional care and social attention for children with special needs.




Group photo at the public donation ceremony





This visit was not only a public welfare action, but also a concrete practice by the AWDPI Thailand National Office to deepen strategic partnerships and advance local public welfare development. Going forward, AWDPI will continue to integrate high-quality resources to promote the sustained implementation of public welfare projects, making continuous contributions to women's rights protection and the public welfare sector.
