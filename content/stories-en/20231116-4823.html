Bimonthly Data Overview


Monthly Summary of Support Work


  • Avoice has newly added 3 women seeking help and is currently supporting 12 clients

  • In addition, 5 case feedbacks were received in 2023, with the needs of all clients basically resolved; among them, 4 out of 5 clients expressed high satisfaction, and 4 out of 5 clients considered staff responses to be very timely

  • In 2023, Avoice has cumulatively provided services to 23 clients, covering 10 countries and regions including the UK, New Zealand, Belgium, and the US


Monthly Summary of Fundraising Work


  • Avoice conducted fundraising activities for the AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum, completed fundraising materials, and submitted the forum proposal to potential funders


Monthly Summary of Communications Work


  •  The official account and other platform accounts have a total of 9,920 followers, with 1,131 new followers added, including 1,100 on the official account; 33 posts were published across platforms, accumulating 20,806 views, with the highest-read post being "'2023 AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum' – See You There!" reaching 4,260 readers


Bimonthly Outstanding Volunteers


  • A total of 49 volunteers were recognized as outstanding volunteers; the full list is provided below. Thank you all for your dedication and hard work—let's keep striving in the next bimonthly cycle!



Bimonthly Key Events


  1. On September 19, the Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) was officially launched, marking our growth from the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project into an international nonprofit organization. AWDPI currently holds nonprofit registration and tax-deductible donation status in the UK and the US

  2. Completed application review for 19 candidates and assisted 16 new members in onboarding—welcome to the Avoice family!

  3. Successfully hosted the 2023 AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum, with over 10,000 audience members participating


★Avoice Bimonthly Report – Issue 5, 2023★

Bimonthly Work Overview


I. Program Quality Department


Academic Research Division


From August to September 2023,

  • the Academic Research Division assisted in the preparation of the AWDPI Forum, completed recruitment and screening of paper reviewers, and contributed to the successful hosting of the forum

  • Additionally, the Academic Research Division completed the report on the current situation of Asian women in the workplace in the United States


Monitoring and Evaluation Division


From August to September 2023,

  • the Monitoring and Evaluation Division actively participated in and advanced the implementation of organizational personnel reform


Program Design Division


From August to September 2023,

  • The Project Design Unit completed the planning of a series of events for the 2023 International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women

  • Completed the planning of the AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum


IT Information Division


From August to September 2023,

  • the IT Information Division advanced the development of the AWDPI website 2.0, further promoted the Next.js development and testing of the main website, and strengthened collaboration with Hangzhou Normal University on web development


II. Support and Assistance Department


Case Supervision Division


From August to September 2023,

  • the Case Supervision Division completed maintenance and upgrades of the case registration system, further improving the quality of support services


Legal Support Division


From August to September 2023,

  • The Legal Support Division actively promoted the drafting of legal awareness handbooks for six additional countries, further advancing the expansion of Avoice's legal support services.


Psychological Support Division


From August to September 2023,

  • the Psychological Support Division actively recruited professional volunteers and successfully connected with 3 licensed counselors this period, further enhancing the professionalism of psychological support services


Language Support Division


From August to September 2023,

  • the Language Support Division concluded its current recruitment phase, held a welcome meeting, and completed training for new members

  • The Language Division completed routine translation across multiple languages, helping to break language barriers and promote knowledge on anti-domestic violence and gender equality. For example, articles such as expert explanations on domestic violence in the Russian context


Empowerment Support Division


From August to September 2023,

  • the Empowerment Support Division conducted community management in groups, prepared sharing sessions and book clubs, and held weekly movie nights

  • In addition, the Empowerment Support Division continuously updated the overseas support resource network, completed background checks and data entry for 5 empowerment support resources, and continuously followed up on client empowerment work while optimizing workflows


III. Partnership and Expansion Department


Partnership and Expansion Division


From August to September 2023,

  • the Partnership and Expansion Division conducted routine background checks on potential partners, completing foundational due diligence on 4 organizations covering student associations, social workers, and support groups

  • Additionally, the Partnership and Expansion Division further advanced cooperation plans with the Chiang Mai United Charity Foundation in Thailand


Fundraising Management Division


From August to September 2023,

  • the Fundraising Management Division conducted fundraising activities for the AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum, completed fundraising materials, and submitted the forum proposal to potential funders


IV. Communications Department


From August to September 2023,

  • the Editorial Division wrote and published 4 articles: ① "Avoice Healing Voices · Her Words | Interview with Ewelina, Director of HSTAR: 'We Provide Aid to Any Woman Experiencing Psychological Trauma'"; ② "Avoice Her Confession Booth | Letter Column: 'Unveiling the Truth Behind PUA' (Issue 2)"; ③ "Avoice Her Perspective | Don't Let Them Hold You Back—Reach for the Stars: Reading 'My Own Words: The Remarkable Life of Ruth Bader Ginsburg'"; ④ "Honoring the Future with Her Power | The '2023 Global Women's Innovation Summit & AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum' is Here!"

  • The Promotion Division produced 11 design graphics, 1 short video, and 2 observation bulletins, and continued advancing collaboration with the Dream Transmission Project to support external communications


V. Finance Department


From August to September 2023,

  • the Finance Department further advanced financial system development, completed the preliminary design of the budgeting system, and established the financial dashboard and approval system

  • In addition, the Finance Department supported fundraising efforts by piloting online public fundraising at the Belgium office and conducting research on overseas fundraising markets


VI. Human Resources Department


From August to September 2023,

  • the Human Resources Department completed application reviews for 19 candidates and assisted 16 new members in onboarding—welcome to the Avoice family!

  • The Human Resources Department, together with the Partnership and Expansion Department, published recruitment notices through over 80 partner channels and conducted targeted recruitment for departments including the US National Office

  • The Human Resources Department actively advanced performance management, issued 15 volunteer certificates and 17 internship certificates, and facilitated the bimonthly volunteer recognition process

  • The Human Resources Department actively advanced the construction of the talent pool and reviewed onboarding training progress across departments

  • In addition, the HR recruitment team organized online team-building activities to strengthen internal communication


VII. Belgium National Office


From August to September 2023,

  • the Belgium National Office continued advancing physical office registration


VIII. UK National Office


From August to September 2023,

  • the UK National Office's General Affairs Division organized orientation sessions and general training for UK team members

  • The UK National Office assisted in promoting the 2023 AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum (and the 2nd Avoice Chinese Women Global Development Forum) held on September 23–24, 2023, including reposting promotional posts on partner WeChat accounts

  • Completed the reporting and summary of the UK National Office's work performance and issues in the first half of the year, and finalized the overall work plan for the second half of the year and departmental plans

  • The General Affairs Division guided UK National Office and department heads in learning and implementing OKR and panel work dashboards

  • The Support and Assistance Division refined and optimized the framework for UK support and assistance workflows

  • The Fundraising and Partnership and Expansion Divisions improved their operational guidelines


IX. US National Office


From August to September 2023,

  • the US National Office actively conducted team recruitment and development, having recruited 13 volunteers so far

  • Preliminary planning was conducted for the functions of the Psychological Support Division and the Partnership and Expansion Division
