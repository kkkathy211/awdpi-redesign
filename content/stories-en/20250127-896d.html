The 2025 AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International is about to launch The first Chinese women's visual documentation project. The inaugural exhibition will take Chinese women as its theme, presenting a comprehensive portrayal of the spiritual world and lifestyles of Chinese women living and working in the UK. The visual documentation will be divided into two major themes—light and shadow—and will use diverse Chinese perspectives to illustrate women's inner strength: resilience, determination, purity, integrity, gentleness, decisiveness, openness, and brightness. The documentation will take the form of interviews, engaging women from different fields, industries, and age groups in China, using dialogue to vividly depict how these women, having experienced different stages of life, embark on their unique journeys, move forward resolutely through cold nights without looking back, and ultimately step into the bright sunlight with strong inner strength after continuously challenging themselves and overcoming fear. Someone once said: Life is about using a long, long, and cold journey to earn one shining moment. So how, through what waiting and suffering, did they reach their own shining moment? Stay tuned for the Asian Women Development Plan International's inaugural women's visual documentation project, which will use visual media to vividly tell their inner worlds and the new sparks of life ignited through their encounters with Western workplaces and daily life.


The 2025 AWDPI Asian Women International Exhibition Program launches its inaugural Chinese women's visual documentation project. This forum will center on the two themes of "Strength – Light" and "Dark Night – Shadow", exploring with Asian women across the UK the journeys of different life stages, sharing how they move forward with determination and maintain inner stability when facing pressure and turmoil. In its second phase, Shadow·Documentation, AWDPI will address domestic violence in women's lives, workplace violence and emotional abuse and other pressing issues, showcasing the strength of women who persist through resilience and dark nights. The two photography exhibitions will consolidate discussions on exploration, reflection, response, and protection, helping women face various challenges in life and work with greater confidence and gain renewal through continuous self-evolution.

Recruitment for the second round of visual documentation participants

✦


The Asian Women Development Plan International will use visual documentation to present to Chinese communities in the UK their personal growth and inner development. We actively invite the public to participate as photography subjects. Chinese women who are interested, willing to join the visual documentation project, and ready to share their life stories and inner journeys are warmly welcome to join. Interested individuals may contact the organizer to receive an application form, complete the initial expression of interest as a documentation guest, and provide basic personal information. The form will include questions for reflection and discussion. Candidates will be selected through preliminary and final review stages to become second-round documentation guests. Upon guest consent, interviews will be conducted and documented. The visual documentation will take place in a studio setting, with one to two photo shoots per guest, approximately 10 photos, accompanied by written editing. All guest visual and textual materials will be compiled into a photography booklet and displayed during the exhibition.


Recruitment target: Chinese women

Eligibility: Chinese or ethnic Chinese women living and working in the UK

Number of participants: 15–20 people

Age: No age limit

About the Organizer

✦


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, promoting gender equality, and supporting the holistic development, empowerment, and personal growth of Asian women in multiple aspects, enabling overseas Asian women to achieve development in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, and helping them transform from marginalized groups into a vital force driving social progress.


AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Offices established in the UK, Belgium, and the United States. It has built a broad network of partnerships with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments in various countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions.
