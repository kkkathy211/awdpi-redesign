Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project Team:


" International Youth Volunteers Network Comprised of over 300 outstanding Chinese youth from renowned universities, academic institutions, and enterprises around the world, the network spans more than twenty countries and brings together diverse academic backgrounds, with a particular focus on development areas related to gender equality and youth empowerment. Through implementing the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, participating in academic research on sustainable development, and carrying out cross-cultural exchange and cooperation, they share China's public welfare stories with the international community, demonstrate the responsibility and action of Chinese youth, and amplify the voices of Chinese youth.
