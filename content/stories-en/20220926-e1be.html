 Introduction:

The China Foundation for Poverty Alleviation, in collaboration with the China Environmental Grantmakers Network (CEGA) and the Vanke Public Welfare Foundation, jointly launched the CEGA "Her" Initiative for Environmental Public Welfare in 2022. The initiative consists of three components: Component One, the "Her" Forum; Component Two, the "Her" Voice; and Component Three, the "Her" Story. It aims to empower women in the environmental public welfare sector and promote the development of female leadership through exchange and learning.

Currently, "Her" Voice is publicly recruiting outstanding women in the field of environmental public welfare to support their participation in international conferences and events on topics such as environmental protection, biodiversity conservation, climate change, women's development, and sustainable development. This initiative seeks to share Chinese experiences, amplify Chinese voices, and promote civil society exchanges between China and other countries.


Who is "Her"?


1, Outstanding women in China's environmental public welfare sector;

2, Women who focus on issues such as environmental protection, biodiversity conservation, climate change, women's development, and sustainable development.


What can "Her" gain?


1, CEGA's "Her" Voice will determine the grant amount based on the applicant's different modes of conference participation and provide support in the form of post-event reimbursement. Awardees will receive funding of up to 5,000 RMB to cover conference attendance or speaking engagement costs.

2, By participating in international conferences and events, "Her" can amplify Chinese voices and women's voices on a broader stage, enhance female leadership, gain valuable experience in speaking and attending international events in the environmental public welfare field, and achieve personal empowerment.


What is expected of "Her"?


1, Identify an international conference of interest, prepare personal materials, scan the QR code below, and submit the application. Selected candidates will be notified of the results via phone or email by the project team;

2, Awardees must comply with the project's management and financial requirements, attend the conference as planned, and fulfill all assigned tasks;

3, Awardees must, within 30 days after the conference, submit their outcomes to the project team in any form—such as edited video recordings, recorded reflections, written reports, or curated collections of artwork or photographs (not merely raw photos);

4, Be willing to participate in follow-up sharing salons organized by the project.


What is the application timeline?


1, Application period: September 2022 – May 2023

2, Application requirements: Applicants must submit the application form online at least one month prior to attending the international conference or event. The "Her" project team will review the applications and respond within 10 working days of submission.

Note: Funding is limited. Interested applicants are encouraged to apply as early as possible; applications will close once the funding cap is reached.



Please scan the QR code to fill out and submit your application materials
