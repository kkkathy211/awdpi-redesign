01 Who We Are


A

Project Overview

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project Committed to eliminating gender-based violence and advancing gender equality, the project provides comprehensive and sustained protection and support for overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence. By building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, and by mobilizing broader youth-led public welfare forces, the project advocates for public awareness and support regarding the challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. It aims to create a safe and equitable environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.


The project's abbreviation "Avoice" is derived from "Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program" (the English name of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project). It signifies using women's voices to convey a vision for development, using youth's voices to advocate for gender equality, declaring humanistic ideals with a manifestly sincere heart, and singing the long song of mission with unwavering determination. It is dedicated to integrating the myriad voices of public welfare into the powerful, grand chorus of globally advancing the sustainable development goals for gender equality!
