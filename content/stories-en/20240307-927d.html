The United Nations' 2024 International Women's Day theme is "Invest in Women: Accelerate Progress." By 2030, the annual funding gap for gender equality initiatives could reach as high as 36 billion US dollars, making it urgent to advance financing for gender equality.


During the United Nations 2023 International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women "16 Days of Activism," we hosted five online workshops, discussing topics such as "Occupational Illnesses through a Gender Lens" with over 300 audience members. The 2024 International Women's Day theme continues the message of the 2023 International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women: "Eliminate gender-based violence through adequate funding." Without significant investment to address violence against women and girls, expand prevention programs, and implement effective policies, countries will not be able to eliminate gender-based violence by 2030.


This year, AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International will present four workshops spanning China, Germany, and Ecuador during International Women's Day, joining people worldwide who care about gender issues in expressing support for survivors of gender-based violence.


#ThisWorkshopSeries



The Galápagos Islands in Ecuador are known among Chinese tourists as "paradise on earth." Ecuadorian law protects gender equality and specifically prevents violence against women, guaranteeing equal rights to education and employment for all genders. However, as in other societies, cultural and regional differences hinder the full realization of these rights. Therefore, we will discuss the specific gender challenges faced by women in the Galápagos. We will also focus on the discrimination and isolation experienced by women with disabilities in the Galápagos: when disability rights laws are difficult to implement on these islands, how can we make women with disabilities visible and promote systemic, inclusive support? Additionally, our guests will include local youth and survivors of gender-based violence, to amplify diverse and authentic voices of Latin American women.


Topics Overview

  • Women's Rights in the Galápagos Islands

  • Disability, Discrimination, and Isolation

  • The Situation of Youth in the Galápagos Islands

  • Survivors in Ecuador's Judicial System
