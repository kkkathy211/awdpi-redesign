Forum Introduction




The "Asian Women International Development Forum" is one of the flagship programs of AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International), having been successfully held for two consecutive years. It has received extensive attention and participation from Chinese communities, nonprofit organizations, and media worldwide. Nearly one hundred experts and scholars from UN Women, international organizations, academic institutions, universities, and technology innovation companies have engaged in in-depth discussions on topics including technology, philanthropy, workplace, culture, law, and psychology, based on the current status, practical experiences, and research findings of gender development across multiple industries, offering participants fresh perspectives and profound insights into the theory and practice of combating gender-based violence.

[2024 Asian Women International Development Forum]Coming Soon

"Forum Curator" positions are now open


Are you passionate about gender equality and public welfare?

Want to join us?


This summer, AWDPI plans to host once again the "AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum".


The upcoming forum is expected to be held online over two days, featuring three main segments: keynote speeches, roundtable dialogues, and paper reviews., Experts, scholars, and young talents from relevant fields will be invited to engage in in-depth discussions on issues such as gender-based violence, overseas female workers' rights, women's career development, women's agency and influence, and women's health.


We are now recruiting 2–8 Forum Curators for this year's event.


As the lead organizer of a specific theme or sub-forum, the Forum Curator will play a crucial role in the preparation process. The ideal candidate should have solid experience and insight in the theory and practice of combating gender-based violence, stay informed on current affairs and trending intersectional topics, foster dynamic exchanges of ideas, and be capable of efficiently managing a range of tasks including theme planning, guest invitations, and event promotion. We hope that the Forum Curator can commit approximately 10 hours per week from June to August..


We are looking for outstanding candidates who:

  1. are passionate about issues such as eliminating gender-based violence, overseas labor rights, women's career development, women's agency and influence, and women's health;

  2. have strong team collaboration skills;

  3. possess excellent language organization and communication abilities;

  4. demonstrate strong academic competence and writing skills;

  5. are adaptable (skilled at identifying, raising, and solving problems);

  6. and have prior experience in organizing forums (preferred).

 /


Application Requirements:

  1. A personal resume (in either Chinese or English);

  2. A brief forum proposal.


Proposal Requirements and Reference Information:

- The forum's current themes are "Orange the World—Building a Future Without Violence" and "A New Era of Women's Identity." Please choose one of these themes to develop your proposal, with no restrictions on specific topics; or propose your own theme and design the proposal accordingly.


- Forum Schedule: The forum is expected to last two days, with sessions scheduled from 9:00 to 12:00 and 14:00 to 18:00 Beijing time. You may design multiple "keynote speeches" and "roundtable dialogues" according to the time available, with no limit on the number of sessions.


- Forum Format: The forum will be held online. Under each main theme, you may set up different "keynote speeches" and "roundtable dialogues" on self-determined topics. A list of discussion questions for roundtables is optional. Sample topics include:


  1. Speech—Seeking Support: A Guide for Domestic Violence Survivors to Access Help;

  2. Speech—The Path of Practice: Learning from Experience, Effective Strategies to Support Domestic Violence Survivors;

  3. Speech—Strength and Light: Challenges and Empowerment of Asian Female Workers;

  4. Speech—Revisiting Barbie: How Culture Shapes Female Identity;

  5. Roundtable Dialogue—Self-Acceptance: Embracing Physical and Mental Health;

  6. Roundtable Dialogue—Gender-Based Violence Among Chinese International Students on Overseas Campuses;

  7. Roundtable Dialogue—She Who Leads the Trend;


- Word Count: Approximately 200–400 Chinese characters.


- Content Requirements: Include, but are not limited to, title, keywords, topic background, main content, format, and profile of potential guests; the proposal should have a clear theme, well-structured framework, rigorous logic, concise expression, and professional terminology.



Volunteer Benefits:

  1. Those who participate and complete the forum preparation will receive an official Forum Volunteer Certificate issued by AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International);

  2. Outstanding performers will be recommended for internal referrals to other volunteer positions within AWDPI.


How to Apply:

Interested applicants, please send your resume and proposal to [email protected]with the email subject formatted as "AWDPI Curator Application – Full Name"
