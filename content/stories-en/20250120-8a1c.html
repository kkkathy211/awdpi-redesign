Forum organizer – Introduction to AWDPI


This forum was hosted by AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International), Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI), an organization that advocates for attention to the challenges faced by Asian women overseas and continuously supports Asian women in accessing opportunities for comprehensive development.


AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, promoting their empowerment and development, and enabling Asian women overseas to gain greater opportunities for advancement in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them transform from a marginalized group into a vital force driving social progress.


AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in the UK, Belgium, and the United States, building extensive collaborative networks with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments of different countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions.


Forum Recap


On November 23–24, 2024, just before the 16 Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence, the "2024 AWDPI Asian Women International Development Forum" was successfully held online over two days, with live streaming throughout.


The forum brought together a distinguished gathering of esteemed guests and friends. 54 leaders from various sectors made time to attend and "gathered in one virtual space," joining nearly 1,000 participants and engaging in real-time online interaction with over 10,000 viewers. Through live streaming, the forum facilitated in-depth dialogue on the two major themes "Orange the World—Building a Future Without Violence" and "A New Era of Shaping Female Identity," through four program segments: academic speeches, roundtable discussions, paper reviews, and a debate competition, connecting with government agencies, academic institutions, nonprofit organizations, experts, scholars, opinion leaders, and concerned individuals worldwide who are committed to the development of Asian women, sharing and discussing issues concerning Asian women in fields such as gender-based violence, employment, entrepreneurship, political empowerment, technological advancement, climate change, and film and art. Participants delivered powerful and thought-provoking insights. Though distant as mountains and seas, our hearts unite as one; though far as the horizon, we stand close as neighbors.


2024 Forum Media Impact Overview


Hundreds of Chinese and international media outlets, 726 media reports, nearly 400,000 views, reaching over 2 million potential audiences.


This year's forum reached new heights in scale, format, topics, and academic atmosphere, with discussions that were profound, empathetic, and visionary. Prominent mainstream media outlets at home and abroad reported extensively on the event, adopting positive and constructive perspectives, focusing on in-depth women's issues, recording key insights, and compiling highlights from the four main segments, offering a comprehensive view of the forum. Press releases were distributed to domestic media including NetEase News, FT Chinese, People's Education Network, China Times Network, China Education News, Dazhong Daobao, Meitiejie, Xinhua Hotline, Shenzhen Daobao, and Global News; and to international media including the Associated Press (AP News), PR Newswire, Benzinga, Yahoo! Finance, Times of San Diego, and FOX. Chinese and international media alike focused on this forum, widely disseminating its content and generating enthusiastic responses.

With a potential audience of 112,640,000, daily traffic exceeding ten million, and monthly visits reaching hundreds of millions, the leading media platform NetEase News significantly boosted the forum's promotion. As soon as the news was published, it rapidly triggered a surge in clicks. Over 100 major high-traffic media outlets, including Enterprise Observer Network, Flipboard, FT Chinese, China.com, China Times Network, People's Economy Network, and China Express, reposted and reported on the event. It is reported that media organizations collectively published 235 Chinese media posts, achieving a high 303,314 number of page views and clicks, with this figure continuing to rise.
