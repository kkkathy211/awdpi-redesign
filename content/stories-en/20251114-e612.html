Join NYU Wagner's International Student Association (WISS) and the Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) for an inspiring workshop—gain fresh insights into gender-based violence prevention and career development through myth-busting knowledge sharing and real-world professional experiences.


Event Highlights


Knowledge Sharing

INTRODUCTION

A 20–30 minute session led by Yifei Zhang, School Engagement Coordinator at Womankind, introducing foundational information about gender-based violence.


Career Sharing

CAREER

A 35–45 minute discussion moderated by Tian Tian Wu, Head of AWDPI's U.S. Office, featuring two guest speakers from Womankind who will share their experiences working in the field of gender-based violence prevention and advocacy, exploring how to build a meaningful career, offering practical advice on creating social impact in your profession, and supporting survivors.


Guest Speakers


Yifei Zhang

School Engagement Coordinator

Yifei Zhang serves as the School Engagement Coordinator at Womankind (formerly the New York Asian Women's Center), part of the organization's Community Education and Outreach team. She focuses on serving Asian and international student populations in New York City schools, providing intersectional, trauma-informed, and culturally sensitive support, advocacy, and education services for students and student survivors of gender-based violence. Prior to joining Womankind, she led communications projects for a gender equality-focused nonprofit in China. As an international student during her university years, she began engaging in gender justice advocacy. Through hands-on experience with relevant United Nations agencies and civil society organizations, she has remained committed to social action, particularly in the Global South. As a scholar in "Women's, Gender, and Sexuality Studies" with a focus on sexual and reproductive health justice, she has consistently advocated for comprehensive sexuality education for youth and adults worldwide. Yifei Zhang graduated from New York University with a Master's degree in Global Affairs, concentrating in Global Gender Studies.


Rose Luo

Advocate, Licensed Master Social Worker in New York State

Rose Luo is a Licensed Master Social Worker (LMSW) in New York State and currently serves as an Advocate at Womankind, a New York-based nonprofit dedicated to supporting survivors of gender-based violence. Applying a trauma-informed approach, Rose provides counseling and advocacy services to help survivors reclaim their strength and sense of self. She holds a Master's degree in Social Work from Columbia University and previously worked as a psychotherapist offering mental health counseling for adults on issues including anxiety, depression, and interpersonal relationships.


What You Will Gain


  • Understand common misconceptions about domestic violence and its social context, enhancing empathy and sensitivity

  • Acquire practical experience and methods for supporting survivors in real-world professional settings

  • Hear how women leaders have overcome challenges, achieved success, and transformed passion into career momentum

  • Gain inspiration and advice on career development, social impact, and building a meaningful career path

  • Connect with industry professionals and spark reflections on your future career and social engagement
