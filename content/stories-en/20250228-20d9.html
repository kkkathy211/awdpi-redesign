On January 4, 2025, a seminar themed "Joining Hands to Uphold Women's Rights and Corporate Futures; Advancing Together Toward a Future of Rule of Law and Compliance" was successfully held at Han Sheng Law Firm in Shanghai. Co-organized by Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) and the Asia-Pacific Young Entrepreneurs Council (APEA), this seminar aimed to deeply explore cutting-edge issues concerning women's rights protection and corporate rule of law and compliance development, promoting cross-sectoral exchange and cooperation.



Background and Preparation


Under the profound interplay of globalization and the rule of law, challenges facing women's rights and corporate development have become shared concerns of our time. As international relations and the global economic landscape evolve, women's status in society and the economy is undergoing profound transformation, with family law and marriage law playing a crucial role in this process. At the same time, Chinese enterprises face challenges from Sino-US trade tensions and transnational regulations in their international expansion, urgently requiring solutions to compliance risks to maintain competitiveness. To address these issues, AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) and its APEA Women's International Development Committee initiated this seminar, focusing discussions on "women's rights protection" and "corporate rule of law and compliance development." By conducting in-depth analyses of family law and corporate compliance, the event aimed to provide valuable insights for participants, advance social justice and sustainable corporate development, and collectively move toward a brighter future.

01 Women's International Development and Protection

from the Perspective of Family and Marriage Law

Liu Yanxin, Senior Partner at Han Sheng Law Firm, member of the Shanghai Lawyers Association's Family Law Committee, and Executive Director of the Family and Wealth Inheritance Committee at Han Sheng Law Firm, Attorney Liu Yanxin delivered a keynote speech titled "Women's International Development and Protection from the Perspective of Family and Marriage Law" as a key speaker at the conference.


Attorney Liu provided a detailed interpretation of measures to address domestic violence, especially how judicial mechanisms can safeguard the rights of women and children. As a seasoned lawyer in family and marriage law, Liu Yanxin emphasized that domestic violence is not merely a private family matter, but also concerns social stability and the quality of life for every family member.


She pointed out that the core mission of anti-domestic violence laws is to ensure victims' personal safety. With the revision of the Law on the Protection of Women's Rights and Interests on October 30, 2022, the scope of application of personal safety protection orders has been further expanded, extending their applicability to stages such as dating, courtship, post-breakup, and post-divorce periods; the scope of covered behaviors has broadened from physical harm to include psychological harm, greatly enhancing the practicality of protecting women's personal safety and carrying significant real-world implications.


Attorney Liu illustrated the applicability of personal safety protection orders through two cases. In the first case, involving a couple after a breakup, the woman suffered physical violence and harassment from the man, and the court ultimately issued a protection order prohibiting the man from approaching her. In the second case, a wife had long endured violence from her husband; despite the husband's denial, the court, based on injury photos and police reports, determined a high likelihood of domestic violence and issued a personal safety protection order. These cases demonstrate that courts are flexibly applying legal tools to protect victims' rights in response to domestic violence.



Liu Yanxin emphasized that although the threshold for issuing personal safety protection orders has lowered, the hidden nature of domestic violence cases and victims' inability to resist still leave some cases unresolved. She called for greater legal awareness and education to help victims identify abusive behaviors and protect themselves. Meanwhile, she also noted that child custody and guardianship rights in divorce cases often become contentious issues, and lawyers should provide legal advice based on the best interests of the child.



"May you meet only kind people for the rest of your lives, treated with tenderness. May your children's eyes be filled with starlight, warmed by true love," Attorney Liu Yanxin concluded. With the advancement of legal system construction and the continuous improvement of anti-domestic violence laws, the scope of protection for victims has expanded. She stated she will continue to monitor domestic violence cases and work to enhance the effectiveness of protection orders, ensuring more victims receive legal protection.




02 Export Controls and Trade Sanctions

under Sino-US Trade Frictions

Li Yuling, Director of AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) and Founding and Managing Partner at Han Sheng Law Firm, Attorney Li Yuling delivered a speech titled "Export Controls and Trade Sanctions under Sino-US Trade Frictions." Attorney Li elaborated on U.S. sanctions and export control policies toward Chinese enterprises and discussed the transnational compliance challenges Chinese enterprises face in the context of globalization, as well as China's legal framework and response strategies to counter-sanctions.



Attorney Li pointed out that U.S. sanctions and export control policies have had a profound impact on global enterprises, especially in high-tech sectors. Through "long-arm jurisdiction," the United States can exert influence over enterprises worldwide, particularly in critical industries such as semiconductors and telecommunications technology. Attorney Li used ZTE and Huawei as examples to analyze how export controls affect global supply chains and restrict Chinese enterprises' access to certain markets. These policies have significantly impacted the stability and competitive landscape of the global industrial chain.


When discussing the differences between sanctions and export controls, Attorney Li emphasized that although the two are often intertwined, they differ in legal definitions and enforcement mechanisms. Export controls primarily target specific technologies or products, aiming to protect national security or economic interests, whereas sanctions cover broader areas, including financial, economic, and even cultural dimensions.


Attorney Li further pointed out that Chinese enterprises must operate within a global compliance framework, and especially under the backdrop of external sanctions and export controls, must respond to international market challenges through lawful and compliant means. She also mentioned that Han Sheng Law Firm has partnered with multiple international law firms to provide global legal support for Chinese enterprises, helping them manage transnational legal risks and ensure compliance and competitiveness.



Additionally, Attorney Li discussed China's legal countermeasures in response to external sanctions. As the international economic situation evolves, China has gradually improved its legal framework for counter-sanctions while maintaining openness. These measures not only protect the legitimate rights and interests of domestic enterprises but also enhance China's voice within the international legal system. Attorney Li emphasized that counter-sanctions must be implemented cautiously to avoid international legal conflicts.


Finally, Attorney Li noted that Chinese lawyers are increasingly participating in international legal affairs, effectively enhancing China's influence in the global legal arena. She believes that in the process of globalization, the international influence of Chinese law will continue to grow, providing stronger legal support for the global development of Chinese enterprises. Attorney Li Yuling's speech offered profound insights to participants and provided valuable legal advice for Chinese enterprises navigating the complex international legal environment.
