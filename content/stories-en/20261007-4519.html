Hello, families of the official account!

We are the AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International Thailand Office.


Living in Thailand sometimes feels like watching a movie without subtitles 🎬


A language you can't quite understand, culture you're constantly adapting to, and grievances and pressures you can't put into words...

Have you ever had these moments:

Even though you're trying your best, you still feel lonely

You have questions but don't know who to ask, so you just keep them inside

You want to grow, but lack someone to walk the path with you


If so, you can come here and take a look 👇

✨ This is a "safe space" created for Asian women living in Thailand

You can finally breathe, no need to pretend, no need to explain yourself



Here, you can:

💬 Emotional companionship: All emotions are allowed—you don't need to be perfect

📚 Growth empowerment: From daily life in Thailand to personal growth as a woman, we level up together

👭 Meeting like-minded people: Turns out, someone is walking the same path as you




💛 What matters most to us isn't how outstanding you are

But—whether you've been truly seen and supported


🌙 In a foreign city, there can still be a familiar warmth

✨ Amid the crowd, someone still keeps a light on for you


📣 Follow us—we'll walk alongside you, step by step

💌 Let us know in the comments: What's on your mind lately?
