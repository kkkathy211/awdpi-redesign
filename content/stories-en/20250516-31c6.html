On May 11, AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International, in collaboration with Baoshan Overseas-Educated Scholars Association, successfully hosted a new member welcome event and the themed charity bazaar "Spring Warmth · Love in Action" at Vision 1876 in Dachang, Baoshan. Over 30 members and guests gathered together to welcome new members and share charitable goodwill.

The event began with an indoor meeting, where Huang, Secretary-General of the Baoshan Overseas-Educated Scholars Association, delivered an inspiring speech, followed by a lively and engaging self-introduction session for new members, helping everyone grow closer.


Subsequently, Rainey, AWDPI Shanghai Lead, presented two meaningful charity items and officially launched the charity bazaar:

1

the "Self-Defined Bracelet" designed by Qiu Siyu, Head of AWDPI's Psychological Support Division—each piece is unique, symbolizing "A woman's undefinable life is written by herself,";

2

and the "Imperfect but Deserving of Love Jelly Cat Barcelona Bear" donated by supporter Linsey—this small plush toy carries great love and warmth.

Five categories of charity items—including cultural and creative handmade crafts, lifestyle essentials, clothing, shoes, and bags—drew crowds, while QR code donations and real-time certificate issuance ensured transparent transmission of goodwill. The event ultimately raised 2,285 RMB, which was donated to a partnered public welfare foundation to support women in need.

After the bazaar, a barbecue party on the outdoor lawn unfolded in a relaxed atmosphere, with laughter and music blending together, bringing a warm close to spring.

Thank you to all item donors, participants, volunteers, and partner organizations for your support! In the future, AWDPI will continue joining hands with partners to spread love—more companions are welcome to join this journey of public good!
