About AWDPI



The Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) is an international non-profit organisation dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against overseas Asian women and advancing their empowerment and development.


Evolved from Avoice (the Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project) founded in 2020, AWDPI now operates across multiple countries and regions in Europe, North America and the Asia-Pacific, with national office teams in Belgium, the United Kingdom, the United States, Australia and Thailand. We have built an extensive global partnership network with United Nations agencies, government authorities, international organisations, multinational corporations and leading academic institutions.


Guided by our core values of Participation, Inclusion and Empowerment, we provide end-to-end support for Asian women in crisis, build cross-cultural peer support networks, and drive progress toward gender equality through research and policy advocacy. Our mission is to help every Asian woman access equal opportunities and live a life of dignity.


As a key delivery team in AWDPI's Asia-Pacific region, the Australia National Office works closely with local communities, legal institutions and social service providers. Focused on the real needs of Asian women in Australia, we deliver case referrals, community education, rights advocacy and community connection initiatives, working to advance gender equality and multicultural inclusion locally.

Our Core Work


Survivor Support: We deliver holistic support for survivors of family violence. Through our robust support system and extensive overseas network, we respond promptly to the needs of overseas Asian women across family violence prevention, emergency relief and ongoing care.


Community Connection: We build an international platform for connection, learning and mutual support through online community ecosystems and in-person peer groups, fostering a peer support network that enables women's self-development.


Amplifying Women's Impact: We convene forums, seminars and workshops to bring diverse stakeholders together to share insights, address challenges and explore solutions, showcasing the strength and potential of Asian women.


Advocacy & Awareness: We drive public engagement, academic research and policy advocacy to raise global awareness of the issues facing overseas Asian women, and advance policy reform and a more enabling environment for their development.



AWDPI Australia Organisational Structure

A Note on AWDPI's Leadership Structure 

At AWDPI, each department is led by a Department Head to ensure stability and continuity within our volunteer team. As core members of the organisation, Department Heads oversee team collaboration, task allocation and program delivery.


Should a Department Head step down, a full handover must be completed in line with organisational policies to ensure seamless workflow. When a leadership vacancy arises, priority is given to internal promotion of high-performing team members. If no internal candidate is available, the management team will cover the role on an interim basis until a suitable successor is appointed.


This structure ensures the long-term stability of our organisation while providing volunteers with opportunities to grow and develop leadership skills.
