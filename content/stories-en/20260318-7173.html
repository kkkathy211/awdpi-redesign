AWDPI Event Registration | UN CSW70 Online Parallel Forum Session 3: Her Words, Stories of Justice – How Women's Narratives Can Change the World

In March 2026, the global spotlight on gender equality turns once again to the United Nations Headquarters in New York—the 70th session of the UN Commission on the Status of Women (CSW70). As a long-standing international nonprofit organization dedicated to women's rights and gender justice, Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) has been invited to host a series of online parallel events for CSW70.

Following the profound dialogues of the first two forums, AWDPI will launch its third online parallel forum—
Shaping the Voice of the World: Women Writers, Global Narratives, and Gender Justice

💥[Opening Keynote · Summit Dialogue]
🥇 Deng Yaping
Four-time Olympic gold medalist | Table tennis Grand Slam champion |
PhD in Economics from Cambridge University | Global influencer in sports and culture
From sports queen to advocate for women's development
Every swing of her racket breaks through ceilings

📚 Judith Butler
One of the most influential contemporary philosophers | Founding figure in gender theory |
Author of *Gender Trouble* | Leading intellectual in global gender studies
She defined modern gender studies

[Forum Speakers]
✍️ Zhang Haoyin (Sunny Zhang)
Author of China's first novel on lesbian themes | Senior film journalist |
Chief curator of the International Chinese Original IP Film Festival
From journalism to literature, her pen strikes directly at gender injustice

🎭 Mara Isaacs
Tony and Grammy Award-winning Broadway producer |
Producer of *Hadestown* | Leading figure in global stage arts
Using theater to tell marginalized stories

📖 Jin Ling
Documentary writer | Advocate for special children's education |
Recipient of the United Nations "Outstanding Contribution in International Charity" Award
Using words to illuminate the lives of autistic children, making the silent seen

[Moderator]
🎤 Sun Yi
Vice Chair of the League of Women Voters of Pennsylvania, USA
Former Executive Director of the Pennsylvania Governor's Advisory Commission on Asian and Pacific American Affairs
Championed the implementation of the first trilingual election system in a key swing state in the U.S.
The first immigrant woman to lead Pennsylvania's Asian and Pacific American Affairs Commission

They will take the stage together for the first time, sparking a groundbreaking intellectual collision across sports, philosophy, literature, and theater.
