Two years have passed like turning wheels of time,

and the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project

has now reached its second anniversary.

Through changing seasons and enduring spirit, vibrant and full of promise,

we have come from all corners of the world,

moving toward the same goal.

Though the road has had its challenges,

our original aspiration has never changed.

May we

always retain the passion of youth,

dedicating ourselves to vast mountains and seas of change.

May our bond be enduring, and though miles apart, feel as close as neighbors.

Let us together build

a home for overseas Chinese women rooted in love and equality!



Her voice—


these are the voices of overseas Chinese women,

telling stories of how they once suffered deeply domestic violence, and found themselves trapped in hardship,

until they met Avoice in a foreign land.

With Avoice's companionship and support, they grew together,

stepped out of the shadows, and moved toward the light,

transforming from survivors of domestic violence into advocates for gender equality.




🎂 Happy birthday, Avoice!



Over the past two years since the launch of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, it is the volunteer spirit and dedication of every Avoicer that has built a vibrant and full-of-potential Avoice.

Together, we use women's voices to convey visions of development, youth voices to call for gender equality, declare our humanitarian values with sincerity, and sing the song of our mission with unwavering resolve, channeling countless acts of advocacy into the powerful global movement advancing the Sustainable Development Goals for gender equality!
