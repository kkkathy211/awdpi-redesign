International Women's Day, also known as Women's Day, March 8th Festival, or International Women's Day, has been increasingly observed on March 8 every year since 1921, with public celebrations held in more and more countries.


Depending on the context, the focus of celebration varies. Historically, Women's Day commemorates women worldwide in their struggle for peace, equality, and development; in daily life, we express gratitude, respect, and blessings for women on this day; within the social context, we celebrate women's significant contributions in economic, political, and social spheres. Most importantly, this day holds unique meaning for each individual.


This year, the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project invites you to join the 2023 International Women's Day event series — a lecture titled "Women's Protection and Empowerment in Humanitarian Work," exploring gender perspectives in humanitarian response and peacebuilding.

List of speakers and content


Gabby

Holds a Master's degree in African Studies from the University of Copenhagen and a Master's degree in Gender and Peacebuilding from the United Nations-mandated University for Peace; currently works at the United Nations system office in China.


Overview of presentation content

The presentation will focus on theoretical knowledge, covering gender-based violence (GBV) under humanitarian frameworks — the concept, types, and main manifestations of GBV; it will also include case studies such as the Srebrenica massacre, as well as common challenges faced in responding to gender-based violence.


Xinyuan

Holds dual Master's degrees in Community Organizing and Development and Nonprofit Management from the Jack, Joseph and Morton Mandel School of Applied Social Sciences at Case Western Reserve University; formerly served as a community project consultant at the same school and as an Asian community officer. Currently works on healing-centered collaboration and community building for women and marginalized groups in China.


Overview of presentation content

Based on observations and experiences within Asian communities in non-coastal regions of the United States, as well as interventions and outcomes in collaboration with organizations such as MidTown Cleveland, the speaker will share and discuss issues of racism and misogyny targeting Asian women in the U.S.
