Since 2022, the Asia Innovation Forum for Environmental Sustainability (AEIF) has joined forces with partners to launch the "Sustainable Action Month (SAM)" starting each year from Earth Overshoot Day, as part of the #MoveTheDate initiative to discover and encourage greater participation in sustainable solutions, raising awareness about ecological debt and encouraging more eco-friendly technologies, methods, models, tools, projects, and activities—such as effective resource use, biodiversity conservation, responsible consumption, and circular economy development—to inspire individuals to improve their households and communities through policies, technologies, and strategic management, enabling better use of the planet's finite resources.


Ten years ago on September 25, at the United Nations Sustainable Development Summit held at UN headquarters in New York, 193 member states unanimously adopted 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), calling for comprehensive global action on social, economic, and environmental development challenges from 2015 to 2030, and setting a course toward sustainable development. However, today, only 35% of the 169 SDG targets are on track, nearly half are progressing slowly, and 18% are even regressing.


As one of the main events of "Sustainable Action Month (SAM) 2025", the "Walkers, Walking..." 2025 Sustainable Action Conference will be held online in a low-carbon format on this significant day, commemorating the 10th anniversary of the SDGs. Through inspiring talks by selected sustainable action leaders, we aim to share their insights, innovations, and practical experiences in environmental protection and sustainability, as well as their long-term dedication to advancing eco-innovation and sustainable development, expressing strong hopes and realistic visions for even broader sustainable action.
