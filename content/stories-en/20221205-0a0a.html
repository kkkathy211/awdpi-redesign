"No one knows which comes first—love or violence."


Because of her passion for camping, she calls herself "Ms. Sleeping Bag."

With perseverance and optimism in her heart, she remains joyful despite the many dangers along her camping journey.


Yet life has brought her constant crises and unexpected tragedies: while studying alone in London, she suffered violence from her ex-boyfriend. Facing this unforeseen suffering, "Ms. Sleeping Bag" intends to confront the challenge head-on—reporting and ensuring punishment for her ex-boyfriend as soon as possible—before moving forward into a new life.


Strong and resilient, "Ms. Sleeping Bag" meets every obstacle with calm determination, just as she bravely faces crises during camping trips. But strength is heavy—it is wounds scabbing over, then enduring pain without flinching.



Part.1

The Nightmare Begins: First Experience of Violence


"Ms. Sleeping Bag" quickly got to know and became close to her ex-boyfriend through their shared alumni status. She never expected that violence and control would arrive disguised in the guise of love, revealing their hideous face.


The first act of violence occurred during a dinner. Because she was ten minutes late, her ex-boyfriend became angry and sullen. He ordered only a single meal for himself and canceled their planned live performance. After feeling his behavior was unreasonable, "Ms. Sleeping Bag" decided to go home. On her way to the subway station, he kept calling, pleading for her to return—and she, softened, agreed.



After returning, he claimed to be tired, so instead of attending the show, they went to his apartment building. On the way, "Ms. Sleeping Bag" advised him to stop engaging in illegal trade, sparking an argument between them. Enraged, he threw her onto the garden ground downstairs and dragged her, causing serious injuries to her lips, hands, face, teeth, and other areas.


Fortunately, people nearby noticed his violent behavior, and "Ms. Sleeping Bag" seized the chance to escape to a friend's place for the night.



Part.2

One crisis barely over, another began


To Ms. Sleeping Bag's surprise, his apologies and expressions of guilt were insincere and short-lived.


After she left, he called her over 40 times, threatening to end the relationship if she didn't answer, and unreasonably claimed both parties were equally responsible for the violence.


Yet more unexpected nightmares followed. She gradually discovered that her ex-boyfriend had committed many acts of violence: blocking her contact whenever they argued, stalking her to her apartment and attempting to have sex under the pretense of developing their relationship while later refusing responsibility through emotional withdrawal, smashing items at her home, and publicly defaming her after she received an admission offer...



"His feedback to me never included calm communication—only deception. Everything was deception." Facing both psychological and physical violence, Ms. Sleeping Bag tried to communicate to resolve the issues, but he refused to communicate and blocked all her contact methods.


After learning she intended to report him to the police, her ex-boyfriend threatened suicide. Out of concern, she tried to dissuade and comfort him, but later realized this was merely another performance and tactic of deception—to stop her from exposing him.


Ms. Sleeping Bag felt deeply disappointed, even beginning to sink into a swamp of self-doubt.



Part.3

Insufficient Evidence, Help Out of Reach


Faced with repeated violence and deception from her ex-boyfriend, Ms. Sleeping Bag decided to seek help from the police.


However, because more than a month had passed since the intimate partner violence incident in the garden, surveillance footage was no longer available, and sufficient evidence could not be found—so the police report came to nothing.



Ms. Sleeping Bag then turned to her university for help. But different departments passed the buck, and without surveillance footage, the department responsible for handling crimes could not conduct an in-depth investigation. As a result, the school offered no support or services beyond psychological counseling.


But Ms. Sleeping Bag said, "I feel I am strong enough inside. This experience has also made me a stronger person, a stronger woman. I don't need psychological help—I only need the most direct solution: for him to receive the punishment he deserves."


Part.4

Inner Transformation, Rebirth Through Fire


As Ms. Sleeping Bag repeatedly recounts her experience of being harmed, she is forced to touch her inner wounds and suffers re-injury each time. Yet at the same time, her narrative logic and thinking about the violence have gradually shifted from chaotic to clear and coherent.



She has become increasingly certain that intimate partner violence itself is unjustifiable. She no longer questions whether she was at fault for being abused, but firmly believes the fault lies entirely with the abuser.


At the same time, she opposes the societal trend of victim-blaming, believing attention should focus more on the abuser's problems rather than excusing the abuser's actions.



Part.5

Hoping for Change, Hoping to Be Heard


After seeking help from campus police, consulting numerous women's support organizations, seeking legal aid, and exposing her story through self-media, Ms. Sleeping Bag has grown increasingly convinced that women's organizations should be stronger—acting as solid backbones to support victimized women so that abusers can face real consequences.



She admits she is a relatively proactive and active woman in seeking help and support, willing to contact organizations and invest significant time and energy. But many women may lack the courage or ability to speak up for themselves, and due to lack of platform support and amplification, their voices may be drowned out.


Victims silently endure, while abusers face no moral or legal consequences—this is an extremely undesirable outcome.



Part.6

Gather the Light, Move Forward Without Fear


Ms. Sleeping Bag wants to tell women: "There's no need to drown in sorrow. After experiencing intimate partner violence, start looking forward and begin seeking help to solve the problem. In this process, you must first clearly define your goal and the outcome you want to achieve."


She encourages women to bravely speak up for themselves, connect with resources, and ultimately ensure the abuser is punished. Though she once experienced self-doubt and fear, her strength and courage are enough to illuminate her own path—and inspire others she touches.


May organizations and systems against intimate partner violence grow strong enough to support survivors. May every voice be heard, and every desired outcome realized. And we, too, will do our best to support every person suffering from intimate partner violence—whether through reporting or media advocacy.




May everyone's light be seen,

And if it isn't bright enough, together our collective power can still ignite a prairie fire.
