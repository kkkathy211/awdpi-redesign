About AWDPI



AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women overseas, and advancing their empowerment and development.


Evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project), established in 2020, AWDPI now operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams in Belgium, the UK, the US, Australia, and Thailand. We have built a broad global cooperation network with United Nations system organizations, relevant government agencies, international organizations, multinational corporations, and leading academic institutions.


Guided by the core values of "Participation, Inclusion, and Empowerment," we provide end-to-end support for Asian women in crisis, build cross-cultural peer support networks, advance gender equality through academic research and policy advocacy, and help every Asian woman access equal opportunities and live with dignity.


As AWDPI's global operational hub, the Global Secretariat oversees strategic planning, interdepartmental coordination, and resource allocation, closely collaborating with functional departments and National Offices. We focus on the real needs of Asian women's rights protection, carrying out work in referral support, policy advocacy, academic research, brand communication, and cross-border collaboration, continuously advancing global gender equality and multicultural inclusion.

We are committed to

providing support and assistance to domestic violence survivors. Through a comprehensive support system and a robust overseas assistance network, we promptly respond to the needs of Asian women overseas in domestic violence prevention, emergency aid, and follow-up services.


Building community connections. By establishing an "online community matrix" and "offline mutual support groups," we create an international platform for Asian women overseas to communicate, learn, and support one another, forming peer support networks that enable self-development.


Enhancing women's influence. Through forums, seminars, workshops, and other activities, we actively facilitate multi-stakeholder collaboration to analyze experiences, study challenges, explore solutions, and showcase the strength and potential of Asian women.


Launching awareness campaigns and advocacy. We actively conduct public outreach, academic research, and policy advocacy to increase global attention to Asian women overseas and promote improvements in related policies and development environments.

AWDPI Leadership Role System Overview 

At AWDPI, departments are typically led by designated leaders to ensure stability and continuity within the volunteer-based structure. Leaders serve as core team members, responsible for team coordination, task assignment, and project advancement. When a leader needs to step down, they must communicate in advance and complete a full handover according to organizational procedures to ensure smooth continuity. In the event of a leadership vacancy, the organization will first consider internal recommendations of outstanding members; if no suitable candidate is available, the management team will temporarily assume the role until a qualified leader is found. The AWDPI leadership system aims to ensure long-term organizational stability while offering volunteers opportunities for growth and leadership development.
