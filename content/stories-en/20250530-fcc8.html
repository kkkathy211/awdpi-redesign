The 2nd Asia-Pacific Peace and Development Forum:


"Focusing on Asia-Pacific Peace, Building a Shared Development Blueprint"


In response to today's rapidly changing international landscape, and to promote academic discussion and regional cooperation, the 2nd Asia-Pacific Peace and Development Forum and the Jimmy Carter Presidential Peace Dialogue will be held May 29–30 at venues in Tokyo, including the Waseda University International Conference Center, Itoh Auditorium and the Hotel Royal Park Tokyo Haneda .

The main theme of this year's forum is "How should China, the United States, Japan, and South Korea interact to maintain peace and stability in the Asia-Pacific region," with multiple roundtable discussions and sub-forums on topics such as whether Sino-Japanese relations can sustainably improve over the long term, how youth from China, Japan, South Korea, and other countries can play constructive roles, artificial intelligence and culture in the era of globalization, people-to-people exchanges, women's empowerment, and gender equality.

2025 Jimmy Carter Peace Dialogue & 2nd Asia-Pacific Peace and Development Forum · Women's Sub-Forum Overview


Time: May 30, 2025

Location: Tokyo, Japan 

Theme: "Her Power for Peace: Women's Roles in Asia-Pacific Peace and Sustainable Development"

Theme: The Power of Her – Women's Role in Peace and Sustainable Development in the Asia-Pacific


As a key component of this year's Asia-Pacific Peace and Development Forum, the Women's Sub-Forum focuses on the leadership roles of women in the Asia-Pacific region and globally in conflict mediation, peacebuilding, sustainable development, and public governance. The forum invites political leaders, representatives from international organizations, scholars, and young female leaders from multiple countries to engage in high-level dialogue and experience-sharing on topics such as "Gender and Peace & Security," "Women's Transnational Collaboration," and "Women's Diplomacy in the Age of Artificial Intelligence."


The sub-forum will include keynote speeches, roundtable discussions, and youth initiatives, aiming to promote women's deep engagement and sustained influence in the global peace agenda and to build an inclusive, diverse, and sustainable regional future.
