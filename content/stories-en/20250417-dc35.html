In April 2024, Baguangfen Culture and Sichuan University Press jointly published the original science fiction novel Bian He and the Jade.


Within a year of its release, the book received widespread acclaim, generated explosive word-of-mouth, and ranked 5th on Douban's 2024 Annual List of Science Fiction and Fantasy Books.


Why has this story resonated so deeply with readers?


Perhaps because it is both classical and modern, gentle yet intense—using the ancient parable of Bian He presenting the jade to frame a story of resistance against technological hegemony.


Modern people trapped within algorithms—we are all Xiao Yu, yet we lack her courage. Reading this story evokes both deep resonance and endless regret.


Combined with carefully crafted suspense elements, the entire book reads like walking under moonlight—text glowing with softness, yet every step beneath our feet feels perilous.


Co-published by Baguangfen Culture and Sichuan University Press
