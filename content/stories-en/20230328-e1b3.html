The first Global Women's Summit, co-hosted by the UAE Women's Federation and the World Muslim Communities Council, was successfully held in Abu Dhabi, the capital of the United Arab Emirates. Presided over by Her Highness Sheikha Fatima bint Mubarak, Chair of the UAE Women's Federation, Supreme Chair of the Supreme Council for Mother and Child, Chair of the Family Development Foundation, and Mother of the Nation, the summit celebrated the significant achievements women have made since the early 20th century in their pursuit of political power.


At the summit, over 250 prominent women including politicians, religious leaders, entrepreneurs, social activists, cultural figures, media personalities, and scholars from more than 100 countries discussed the many challenges women face during pivotal historical moments, and explored the crucial roles female leaders play in advancing world peace, social integration, economic development, and other fields.



Avoice, the first project dedicated to protecting overseas Chinese women, was invited to participate in this summit. As one of the few Chinese organizations present, Avoice actively engaged throughout the event, conveying its vision of promoting gender equality and women's empowerment to participants from around the world who also care about and work on gender equality, including organizations and experts from various regions.


During the summit, Avoice had the privilege of communicating and establishing collaborative connections with numerous institutions and experts, including Maja Gojković, Deputy Prime Minister of Serbia and Chair of the Gender Equality Coordination Body; Mariett Westermann, Vice Chancellor of New York University Abu Dhabi; Jackie Armijo, Visiting Professor of Asian Studies at the University of Hawaii at Hilo; Man Chinda, Secretary of State at Cambodia's Ministry of Women's Affairs; and Xiao Wunan, Executive Vice Chair of the Asia-Pacific Exchange and Cooperation Foundation, laying the groundwork for future cooperation between Avoice and governmental bodies, aid organizations, and charitable institutions in more countries and regions.



Over the course of two days, the summit featured 12 thematic sessions. Participants held in-depth discussions on topics including "Sustainable Development, Philanthropy, and Poverty Alleviation," "Lifetime Achievers Among Women," "Women's Leadership in Education and Science," "Women's Leadership in Parliaments and Diplomacy," "Women's Leadership in Media and Creative Industries," "Women in Art and Cultural Heritage," "The Power of Women's Collaboration Networks," "Women's Role in Promoting Circular Economy and Sustainable Development," "Gender Balance During Transition," "Women's Think Tanks and Policy Advisors," "Women's Leadership in Sports and Tourism," and "Women in Global Mega-Trends." They shared their experiences and success stories from diverse perspectives, highlighting the indispensable roles of governments, institutions, academia, media, and the public in addressing challenges faced by women and advancing gender equality across society.



Since its founding in the early days of the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, Avoice's volunteer team has steadily grown, with increasing professionalism and influence. More than 400 volunteers from renowned universities, international organizations, academic institutions, and enterprises around the world have, through remote collaboration and on-site work, provided domestic violence relief and support to over a thousand overseas Chinese women, conducted related surveys and academic research, launched advocacy campaigns against gender-based violence, and laid a foundation of knowledge and experience for replicating and expanding the project in more countries.

Avoice representatives who attended the summit noted, "The various challenges women face in their growth and development are not isolated issues, but rather interconnected causal factors that jointly affect all aspects of their lives. Therefore, in advancing gender equality, the many topics addressed at this summit advocate for active collaboration across all levels of society, calling on the public to pay attention to women's issues, so as to create a safe and equal environment for women's survival and development." Avoice continues to look forward to exchanging and collaborating with more international organizations and platforms, expanding the network for women's protection and empowerment, and building together a home of love and equality for overseas Chinese women.
