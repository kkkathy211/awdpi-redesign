01 Background of the Event

 

Global Entrepreneurship Week (GEW), initiated by the Global Entrepreneurship Network, is the world's largest and most widely-reaching international innovation and technology IP. Every year, it connects over 180 countries and regions to jointly host a series of activities including innovation exchanges, technology transfer, and industry-finance matchmaking, aiming to build a global collaboration network for innovation and entrepreneurship.

GEW China, hosted by the Shanghai Entrepreneurship Foundation for University Students (EFG), has been held in Shanghai for over a decade. It collaborates with government agencies, research institutes, industrial groups, leading investment institutions, and technology enterprises to build a highly influential platform for collaborative innovation and technology ecosystems in China. OHLinkTech has established a long-term strategic partnership with GEW China, continuously organizing core parallel forums and hosting high-level, high-impact, and high-value industry summits focused on cutting-edge deep-tech sectors.

Currently, China's "15th Five-Year Plan" has clearly identified future energy industries as a national priority for forward-looking development, continuously strengthening technological breakthroughs and industrial cultivation in advanced nuclear energy, controlled nuclear fusion, new energy storage, solid-state batteries, hydrogen energy, and other frontier fields, accelerating the construction of a safe, efficient, and sustainable modern energy industry system. Each sub-sector of future energy is now at a critical window period characterized by rapid technological iteration, breakthroughs in key processes, and gradual industrial chain formation. However, the industry generally faces long R&D cycles, high experimental investment, significant engineering challenges, and long industrialization chains, resulting in multiple bottlenecks in technology commercialization, project-capital matching, and large-scale industrial development.

2026 is defined by the industry as the inaugural year of AI Native intelligence, marking the formal transition of artificial intelligence from traditional add-on tools to a foundational force reshaping industries. AI Native technologies are penetrating the future energy sector across all dimensions—scientific simulation, materials research, process optimization, safety management, and system dispatch—dramatically shortening trial-and-error cycles, reducing R&D costs, and accelerating technology iteration and deployment, becoming a new core productive force driving breakthroughs in the future energy industry.

At the same time, future energy is a typical deep-tech sector characterized by long cycles, heavy investment, and high barriers, highly dependent on long-term capital empowerment and sustained support from financial markets. Currently, there remain cognitive gaps and information barriers between scientific research institutions, technology enterprises, and capital markets. There is an urgent need to establish a dedicated high-level dialogue platform to connect frontier energy technologies, AI Native intelligence empowerment, and financial capital, promoting deep integration of technology commercialization, industrial evolution, and capital support, thus advancing the high-quality development of China's future energy industry system.

03 Event Highlights

 

● AI Native + Frontier Industries of Future Energy

Standing at the dawn of the 2026 AI Native industrial transformation era, the conference will focus on five frontier tracks of future energy, deeply analyzing the foundational restructuring value of native AI technologies in energy research simulation, process iteration, production operations, and system optimization, and forecasting technological breakthrough directions and industrial trends during the 15th Five-Year Plan period.

● Deep Industry-Finance Integration for Future Energy Deep-Tech

Precisely connecting deep-tech energy enterprises, industry leaders, research institutions, industrial capital, and frontline investment and financing institutions, the event will host in-depth dialogues on investment logic, value assessment, risk control, and capitalization pathways for long-cycle energy projects, bridging cognitive gaps between industry and capital.

● Major AI Urban Product Strategy Launch

The conference will feature a major strategic launch of a new AI-powered urban product. Focusing on the digital transformation track, it will officially unveil a new generation of digital product strategy systems, share practical pathways for technology implementation, interpret core product capabilities and industrial application values, and showcase innovative explorations of digital technologies empowering the real economy.
