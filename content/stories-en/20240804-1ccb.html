2024

AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum

will soon open in the gilded month of August


This year's AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum will continue to be held online, with live streaming. The forum will host in-depth dialogues on the theme "Orange the World—Building a Future Without Violence"

and "Women's Identity Shaping in a New Era" with government agencies, academic institutions, nonprofit organizations, experts, scholars, opinion leaders, and concerned individuals worldwide who care about the development of Asian women, presenting an intellectual feast that unites wisdom, exploration, and passion.




★ Academic Speeches ★

In the academic speeches session, experts and scholars from around the world will focus on how survivors of gender-based violence can access multi-dimensional support, the challenges and opportunities faced by Asian women workers in the workplace, gender role representation in cultural products, and the shaping of women's identities in the new era, sharing the latest global practices, observations, and insights.


★ Roundtable Discussions ★

In the roundtable discussions, representatives from nonprofit organizations, legal experts, gender studies scholars, and outstanding women leaders from different countries will engage in deep discussions on how to support overseas students in preventing campus gender-based violence, how women can break through constraints and embrace their authentic selves, and how women can lead trends and drive global development in education, culture, science and technology, sparking intellectual exchange through diverse perspectives and creative thinking.


★ Paper Review Session ★

In the paper review session, academic papers related to gender issues will be collected and presented, and reviewed on-site by academic experts , narrowing the gap between "academic research" and "public dissemination," and encouraging greater youth participation in thinking about and discussing gender issues.


★ Debate Competition ★

In the demonstration debate session, debaters will explore "In today's context, does 'women first' help advance gender equality?" focusing on the potential benefits and challenges of such attitudes and actions in promoting gender equality. Through debate, diverse perspectives and viewpoints will be presented, aiming to deepen understanding, enable comprehensive exploration, and foster broad discussion on gender equality.

About the AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum


As one of the flagship projects of the Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI), the AWDPI Asian Women Development Forum is dedicated to:

building dialogue mechanisms and networks with governments, organizations, enterprises, and individuals worldwide to promote the comprehensive development of Asian women overseas, jointly foster safe, equal, and inclusive environments, and increase their opportunities to participate in political and economic affairs;

inviting disadvantaged overseas Asian women to participate in research and discussions on employment and protection, enhancing visibility for vulnerable groups and thereby increasing external attention and support for their living conditions;

focusing on emerging fields and innovative issues to explore the problems, opportunities, and challenges faced by overseas Asian women in emerging areas of development, promoting their equality and advancement.
