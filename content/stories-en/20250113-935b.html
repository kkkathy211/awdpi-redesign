AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is dedicated to advancing gender equality and women's development, eliminating discrimination and violence against Asian women, and building a more inclusive and equitable social environment.


As a key action platform of AWDPI, the Advocacy and Impact Division is seeking passionate individuals who care about public welfare and gender issues—directors, members, and advisors. The Advocacy and Impact Division not only plans and implements impactful thematic activities, but also promotes gender equality and women's development into broader public discourse and action platforms through policy advocacy, public education, and cross-sector collaboration, expanding AWDPI's social influence globally.


Whether you are passionate about event planning and organization or skilled in resource development and integration, no matter where you are located or whether you participate online or offline, we warmly welcome your involvement.


If you are a senior expert or scholar in a related field, you are especially welcome to join the AWDPI family as our institutional advisor and help advance the global progress of gender equality and women's development.


Let us create real change together through every action!




Department Name for Recruitment



AWDPI Advocacy and Impact Division

Department Introduction

The Advocacy and Impact Division is a key action platform of AWDPI, committed to planning, organizing, and promoting thematic activities centered on women's development and gender equality. It also advances sustained attention and support for gender equality issues across broader social, policy, and cultural spheres through policy advocacy, media engagement, and public participation.


The division aims to build cross-cultural and cross-regional communication bridges through creative and effective online and offline activities, enhancing AWDPI's global visibility and influence. By employing diverse and sustainable formats, the division seeks to spread the concept of gender equality, inspire continuous public attention to women's development, and drive practical implementation of related issues.





Toward this goal, the division organizes a rich calendar of annual activities, including regular community engagement, online advocacy, and empowerment workshops, as well as International Women's Day on March 8, the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women on November 25, and the 16 Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence. These activities complement each other, deepening sustained attention to gender equality while leveraging key dates to spark broader public engagement, promoting wider understanding and support for gender equality and women's development..



Department Goals


 1. Brand Building

Establish AWDPI's brand identity in the field of gender equality and women's development by designing recognizable and widely disseminated thematic activities, enhancing international visibility and influence.


2. Influence and Outreach

Utilize diverse online and offline platforms to convey AWDPI's mission to various audiences through thematic activities, policy advocacy, and public campaigns, advancing gender equality onto public agendas and expanding influence among international communities, governments, NGOs, and academia, thereby encouraging broader attention, support, and action.


3. Cross-Sector Collaboration

Build strategic partnerships with governments, non-profits, academic institutions, and corporations to promote joint initiatives, expand program reach, and enhance practical outcomes.


4. Resource Integration

Use activities as platforms to connect human, financial, and partnership resources, expand networks, and build a sustainable support system, accumulating valuable social capital for AWDPI's long-term development.


5. Public Engagement

Design open and diverse activity formats to attract active participation from people of all backgrounds, inspire broad societal attention and support for gender equality and women's development, and build a strong foundation for widespread public involvement.

About the Organization



AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development. It evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Program, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in the United Kingdom, Belgium, and the United States, and has completed formal registration as a charitable organization in these locations. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments in different countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the survival challenges faced by Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more opportunities for Asian women overseas to develop in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them transform from a marginalized group into a vital force driving social progress.



Our Mission

Eliminate violence and discrimination against Asian women, and promote the protection and development of Asian women

Our Vision

To create a world of love and equality for women

Our Values

Participation, Inclusion, Empowerment

We are committed to

  • Providing rescue and support for domestic violence survivors. Through a comprehensive support system and a robust overseas assistance network, we promptly respond to the needs of overseas Chinese women in domestic violence prevention, emergency rescue, and follow-up services.

  • Building community connections. By establishing online community networks and offline mutual support groups, we create an international platform for Asian women overseas to communicate, learn, and support one another, forming peer support networks and enabling self-development.

  • Enhancing women's influence. Through forums, seminars, workshops, and other events, we actively facilitate multi-stakeholder collaboration to analyze experiences, study challenges, explore solutions, and showcase the strength and potential of Asian women.

  • Launching awareness campaigns and advocacy. We actively conduct public outreach, academic research, and policy advocacy to increase global attention to Asian women overseas and promote improvements in relevant policies and development environments.
