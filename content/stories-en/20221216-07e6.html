Welcome Party



The 2022 Avoice Fall Welcome Party was attended primarily by new members joining Avoice, department heads, and Avoice's founders. The purpose of this event was to help new members gain a comprehensive understanding of Avoice's development history and future plans, and to plant a sun-facing "seed" in the hearts of newly joined members.

New Members' Voice


The new members also presented themselves in video format, radiating positive energy,

During the welcome event, two new friends gave online self-introductions: one is Wu Junmei, and the other is someone working in scientific research, Silas.



Ms. Wu Junmei has over 15 years of experience in human resources recruitment and has engaged in public welfare projects and volunteer work this year. She looks forward to meaningful collaboration and exchanges with everyone in the future.

Slias, although a foreigner, is studying Chinese seriously. He is very happy to meet everyone and hopes to build a great relationship with all of you in the future.


04

Team Voice


Tan Jie from the Coordination Management Office shared why she decided to join our organization and work on such projects. She gave a detailed introduction to the specific work content, processes, and some project outcomes of the Coordination Management Office. She also shared her insights from working in this department—both regarding organizational development and personal growth—and advised members not to overexert themselves while helping others. Volunteers should also pay attention to self-care.


Doirs, head of the UK branch of the Cooperation and Expansion Center, shared her motivation for joining the organization and discussed characteristics, challenges, and difficulties related to domestic violence. She encouraged volunteers to actively learn and participate in training to improve their volunteer service capabilities. This also helps better protect themselves, strengthen legal awareness, and always remember their original intentions.


Annie from the General Affairs Department gave a detailed introduction to the specific responsibilities of the General Affairs Department, shared her understanding of feminism and gender equality, and explained the OKR system and points system in detail. She also answered questions regarding volunteer hours and certification.


05

Q&A Session


Questions submitted by members in the survey were answered one by one. We also welcome everyone to communicate and exchange freely whenever questions arise in future work!


06

Free Exchange


Our welcome party has ended, but the warmth of the organization will always remain. We hope to work together with our newly joined members to create a better future for Avoice, and to meet a better Avoice—and better versions of ourselves—together in the future!
