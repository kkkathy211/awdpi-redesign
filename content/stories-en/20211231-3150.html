This is a New Year's message of gratitude and blessings from the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project.


Over the past year, with the gathering of dedicated individuals and shared efforts, we have moved forward together, overcoming challenges without losing sight of our original aspirations;

as the New Year arrives, may we always keep the passion of youth and work for the well-being of countless mountains and seas;


though distances may be vast, we remain close at heart,

let us jointly build a home for overseas Chinese women rooted in love and equality.
