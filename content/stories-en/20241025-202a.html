AWDPI partners with the first-ever HYPSM CSN | Philanthropy-themed: The Infinite Possibilities of Career, Volunteering, and Public Welfare



At the 2024 inaugural HYPSM Career Speed Networking (CSN) event, we have carefully curated keynote speeches and panel discussions focused on the philanthropy and nonprofit sector, offering you in-depth insights into the industry.

 

In an era of globalization and rapid technological advancement, the public welfare sector is also encountering new opportunities and challenges. If you aspire to contribute social value through your career and leave your mark in the field of philanthropy, the philanthropy-themed session at the first HYPSM 2024 Career Speed Networking event will provide you with inspiration and opportunities!

 

As a key component of the CSN event, we are honored to invite several distinguished guests from the philanthropy sector, who have not only achieved remarkable success in this field but have also explored new possibilities at the intersection of public service and career development. Harvard alumni will share valuable career insights and personal experiences during keynote speeches and panel discussions, reflecting on their own professional journeys to discuss how to create social impact through work, how to balance personal growth with social responsibility, and how to address global challenges through innovation and collaboration in a rapidly changing world. Through these authentic career stories, you will gain a deeper understanding of the current landscape, development trends, and future career opportunities in this sector.

 

Whether you are aspiring to commit yourself full-time to public welfare, or contribute your strength through volunteer service, this event will provide you with valuable advice on job-seeking strategies, skill development, and career advancement in the nonprofit sector, helping you better understand how to integrate career development with social responsibility.


Keynote Speaker

01

Wang Zhenyao

✦

Wang Zhenyao, professor and a pioneering figure in China's philanthropy sector, is Chairman and Founding Dean of the China Academy of Philanthropy at Beijing Normal University, and former Director of the Department of Social Welfare and Promotion of Philanthropy at the Ministry of Civil Affairs. He holds a Master of Public Administration from Harvard Kennedy School and a Ph.D. in Law from the School of Government at Peking University. In 2008, he was awarded the title of "National Model in Earthquake Relief" by the Central Committee of the Communist Party, the State Council, and the Central Military Commission; the same year, he was recognized as one of the "30 Influential Social Figures of China's 30 Years of Reform and Opening-up."


In June 2010, Beijing Normal University appointed Professor Wang Zhenyao as Dean of the China Academy of Philanthropy. Professor Wang has dedicated his work to research in philanthropy, child welfare, and elderly care services, leading numerous significant projects on charity legislation, public policy reform, and the development of child and elderly welfare systems, establishing the China Academy of Philanthropy as a highly influential social policy think tank.


Professor Wang served for 22 years at the Ministry of Civil Affairs, where he participated in establishing China's rural direct election system, the urban and rural minimum living standard security system, and the four-tier national emergency response system for natural disasters. During the Wenchuan earthquake relief efforts, he pioneered the "one province assisting one severely affected county" paired support model, and also contributed to the establishment of national basic social welfare systems such as the orphan allowance and the elderly high-age allowance.


Founded in 2010, the China Academy of Philanthropy at Beijing Normal University is China's first professional think tank dedicated to philanthropy and social policy. Upholding the mission of "using charity to advance social progress," it leverages strengths in industry trend analysis, policy research, and social value leadership. By providing systematic knowledge products and consulting services, it supports policy-making and promotes socially responsible institutional development, aiming to advance the establishment and improvement of China's modern charitable and social service systems, and striving to become "the most influential social policy think tank in the era of good economics."

Panel Discussion Guests

01

Liu Xiaogang

✦

Ms. Liu Xiaogang is widely regarded as China's first true professional philanthropist. She graduated from the Foreign Languages Department at Sun Yat-sen University, then worked for two years at the Research Division of Guangdong Provincial Commission of Foreign Economic Relations and Trade. She later resigned from her government post to launch a career in electronic technology products, founding Orient Technology in Hong Kong and creating "Orient," China's first generation of branded computers—nearly 80% of mainland electronic factories were at the time her clients. In 1994, she returned to Guangzhou, where she became a pioneer in China's real estate industry amid the economic boom following new housing reforms. In 2003, she gave up her business dreams and committed herself to philanthropy as a lifelong career.


Starting in 2004, she pursued nonprofit management at Harvard Kennedy School, earning an MPA degree two years later. Since then, she has fully dedicated herself to philanthropy, becoming the first Chinese entrepreneur to professionally operate charity as a career. In 2007, she was elected President of Guangdong Lions Club; in 2009, she founded Qianhe Community Foundation, China's first foundation named after a community. In May 2011, Liu Xiaogang accepted an invitation from Wang Shi to join the Beijing Alashan SEE Ecological Association as Secretary General. She also serves as Vice President of China Lions Association and Deputy Director of the Center for Philanthropy and Nonprofit Studies at Sun Yat-sen University. In 2017, she became the first international director from mainland China elected to Lions Clubs International.


Qianhe Community Foundation, established in 2009, was initiated by entrepreneurs and scholars with strong public welfare commitment and social responsibility. It is the first public welfare foundation in China named after a "community" and one of the first charitable organizations officially recognized in Guangdong Province. It ranks first nationwide in the FTI (China Foundation Transparency Index), is a recipient of the inaugural "Golden Tangerine Award" for domestic foundations, and was listed in the TOP30 of the 2018 China Charity Credit List. Positioned as a hub for community philanthropy in the Pearl River Delta, the foundation supports emerging community-based public welfare organizations and individuals in addressing real community needs; provides donors with effective giving channels and professional services to jointly realize community care; and promotes participatory philanthropy, enabling more residents to easily and joyfully engage in local community initiatives, fostering co-construction, co-governance, and shared benefits to advance community justice, care, and sustainable development.

02

Liu Qian

✦

Liu Qian is Founder and Chair of Hi China International Youth Development Alliance, holds a Ph.D. from Tsinghua University and a master's degree from Harvard University, the first foreigner to receive the Presidential Medal of Uzbekistan, and recipient of both the Jiangsu Province Youth "May 4th" Medal and the Nanjing Youth "May 4th" Medal.

Liu Qian has long been committed to supporting youth development in a global context and promoting exchanges and cooperation between future world leaders and China. As founder of Hi China International Youth Development Alliance, she has launched initiatives such as "Harvard China Journey" and the "China Youth Leadership Program" to help global youth leaders better understand and collaborate with China. Responding to the Belt and Road Initiative, Liu Qian has led Hi China in deep collaborations with high-level governments, enterprises, universities, and think tanks in Belt and Road countries, organizing the "President X" program to encourage Chinese youth to conduct case studies and internships in these countries, thus promoting China's cooperation with Belt and Road nations.

Liu Qian serves as Chair of the Harvard Kennedy School China Alumni Association, Director of the Harvard Alumni Entrepreneurs Association (China), Director of the Harvard Beijing Alumni Association, member of the World Economic Forum's Global Shapers Community, member of the Jiangsu Provincial Youth Federation, Standing Committee member of the Nanjing Youth Federation, and Director of the Nanjing Overseas Returnees Association. She has previously worked and interned at the Institute of International Finance, the United Nations Economic and Social Council, and China International Capital Corporation Limited. She earned a Master in Public Policy from Harvard Kennedy School in 2015, where she served as President of the Harvard China Student and Scholars Association. She holds a Bachelor of Economics in Finance from Zhejiang University and a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Economics from the University of Michigan.


Hi China International Youth Development Alliance is a platform initiated by Harvard alumni from China and guided by Harvard professors, connecting outstanding youth from around the world across various fields. It is the only Chinese youth non-governmental organization with special consultative status with the United Nations. Hi China is dedicated to supporting youth development in a global context and promoting exchanges and cooperation between future world leaders and China.

To better help outstanding youth from various countries and fields understand China, settle in China, and realize their dreams in China—deeply integrating the future of global youth with China's development—Hi China International Youth Development Alliance has established three alliances: the University Cooperation Alliance, the Youth Practice Alliance, and the Youth Entrepreneurs Alliance. These three alliances serve as bonds to closely connect global youth, using China as a platform for practice, and empowering youth through opportunities in coursework, internships, exchanges, capacity building, and business collaboration.

03

Liu Hong

✦

A.B. 2009 - Social Studies

A.M. 2010 - Regional Studies: East Asia


Liu Hong, co-founder of PEER China (PEER Yiheng Zhiyou), was born in 1987 in Wuhan, Hubei Province. She graduated from Harvard University in 2010 with a combined bachelor's and master's degree, and subsequently worked for four years at the Institute for Advanced Humanistic Studies at Peking University, serving as Assistant to the Director, Director of the Cultural China Talent Program, and Deputy Secretary-General of the first and second Songshan Forums. During her undergraduate studies in 2007, she co-founded "PEER Yiheng Zhiyou" with friends and began focusing on youth development and rural community education through educational practice at a rural high school in Shaodong, Hunan. In 2014, she committed full-time to educational philanthropy, and in 2015, she registered the Beijing Yiheng Zhiyou University Student Volunteer Service Center. For the past fifteen years, she has firmly believed in the importance of humanistic and community-based education for youth growth and regional development in remote areas, growing alongside students, teachers, and volunteers in county towns and rural communities. She currently serves as Council Member and Secretary-General of the Beijing Yiheng Zhiyou University Student Volunteer Service Center. In 2017, she was selected as a Xinyin Fellow by the Xinyin Foundation. From 2021 to 2023, she served as a council member of the SEED Social Innovation Seed Community. From 2023 to 2024, she was a visiting scholar at the Stanford Center for Chinese Economic Research at Stanford University.


PEER Yiheng Zhiyou (Beijing Yiheng Zhiyou University Student Volunteer Service Promotion Center) is a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting educational equity between urban and rural China, improving educational resources in underdeveloped regions, and advancing liberal arts, humanistic, and holistic education. As of October 2023, PEER has conducted over 164 service-learning winter and summer programs and special projects in more than 30 secondary schools in county towns and rural areas across Hunan, Guizhou, Shaanxi, Gansu, Guangxi, Hubei, and Jilin, directly serving approximately 11,000 students through short-term programs and engaging over 1,890 volunteers. Since 2015, PEER has established independently designed "PEER Spaces" in 11 secondary schools in Hunan, Guangxi, and Guizhou, providing long-term support centered on secondary school students' growth. Each space hosts one to two long-term volunteers who co-create learning, living, and public spaces with the students.

Host

01

Yu Xinyi (Yimar)

✦

Overall Director of the first Harvard-Yale-Princeton-Stanford-MIT Career Speed Networking event. Founder of Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) and the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, co-founder of the Beijing Yixingzhe Public Welfare Foundation SRP (Social Responsibility Pioneers) Program, Master's candidate at Harvard Kennedy School of Government, and Seminar Director of the 2024–2025 Harvard Chinese Students Association. She previously served as a 2023 Antarctic Ambassador, conducting scientific research in Antarctica alongside Sylvia Earle, the "Mother of the Oceans," and 120 other ambassadors. She has worked as an EU intern on global issues including economic technology, environmental energy, and pandemic response, advocated for vulnerable children in crisis at the United Nations, and frequently participated in international and domestic impact conferences as a youth representative. She has received numerous honors, including World Economic Forum Global Shaper, Global Youth Leadership Award, G20 YEA Young Entrepreneur Alliance Young Leader, AACYF Top U25 Chinese Americans Under 25, member of Tsinghua University Horizon Global Youth Development Program, and Honorary Fellow of Zhejiang University Qiangying Global Program.


Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) is an international non-profit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas and promoting their empowerment and development. It evolved from Avoice (Overseas Chinese Women Protection Program, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), a brand project established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in the UK, Belgium, and the US, where it is also registered as a publicly fundraising charitable organization. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments in different countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the survival challenges faced by Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more opportunities for Asian women overseas to develop in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them transform from marginalized groups into a vital force driving social progress.
