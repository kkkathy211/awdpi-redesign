May 17–19, 2024 The first annual AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International conference was successfully held in Ningbo, Zhejiang! AWDPI co-founders, board members, advisors, department heads, and outstanding volunteer representatives attended the conference.



This gathering was not only a review of the hard work from the past year, but also a vision for jointly building a future home of love and equality for women. Amidst the mountains and rivers of Ningbo, participants deepened their connections, collectively witnessed AWDPI's remarkable achievements over the past year, and jointly envisioned an ambitious blueprint for creating safe, equitable environments for survival and development for more women threatened by domestic violence.


Conference Activity Overview






Cultural Exchange, Healing the Heart




During the conference, the AWDPI team participated in a series of rich and diverse activities. From making traditional Ningbo cuisine to experiencing Jiangyong Nüshu (a unique script created and used exclusively by women in Jiangyong, China), attendees deeply felt the profoundness of Chinese culture and the power conveyed through women's writing; from the natural beauty of Fenghua scenic area to sound bowl music healing, magazine collage, and OH Cards subconscious projection salons, everyone embarked on a journey of integration between mind and nature. These activities provided relaxation and healing for participants amidst their busy lives of study and work, and also deepened emotional bonds and mutual recognition among them.

Moving Forward Together,

 Building a Home of Love and Equality for Women 




At the closing of the conference, the team collectively reviewed AWDPI's achievements and experiences from the past year, and looked ahead to future development directions and goals.


In the future, the AWDPI team will continue to move forward hand in hand, providing comprehensive, sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing domestic violence worldwide, further strengthening exchanges and cooperation with the international community, and jointly striving to build a safe, equal, and loving home for women—lighting a beacon of hope for those in need, so they may regain new life under the light of love and equality!






Tribute and Gratitude:

 Every Volunteer Is a Shining Star 




The successful hosting of the first AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International annual conference would not have been possible without the hard work and selfless dedication of every volunteer. It is precisely due to their quiet support and steadfast belief that the team has been able to unite and provide solid protection for women in distress.


Finally, we extend our highest respect and sincerest gratitude to all volunteers! You are the most valuable asset and source of strength for this organization. At the same time, we also look forward to building connections with more partners, jointly contributing to the creation of a home of love and equality for women!
