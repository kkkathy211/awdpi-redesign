One

Preface: Who We Are


Project Introduction

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project Dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and promoting gender equality, the project provides comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence. By establishing a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, and by mobilizing broader youth-driven public welfare forces, the project advocates for public attention and support for the living challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. It aims to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.


The project's abbreviated name, "Avoice," is derived from "Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program" (the English name of the Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project). It signifies using women's voices to convey visions for development, and youth voices to call for gender equality—proclaiming humanitarian values with unwavering sincerity and singing the enduring song of mission with steadfast commitment. It strives to channel countless public welfare voices into the powerful global movement advancing the Sustainable Development Goal of gender equality.

Special Recruitment for Legal Positions


As the Avoice project expands its activities overseas, legal academic research,, legal outreach and collaboration,, legal aid support,, and legal compliance affairs, the demand for legal professional volunteers in these specialized fields continues to grow.

To further enhance Avoice's legal expertise and support the project's continued expansion, we have decided to launch a special recruitment drive for legal positions starting mid-September 2022.

This special recruitment for legal positions is open on a long-term basis and operates under a rolling recruitment system:, early applicants will be given priority consideration until all positions are filled..




Online and Offline Recruitment Sessions


This special recruitment includes two tracks: the "Online Legal Positions Recruitment Session" and the "Offline Legal Positions Recruitment Session."

The online legal positions are open to applicants from all countries and allow remote work (a minimum of 7 hours per week, with leave permitted under special circumstances);

the offline legal positions are open to applicants who will reside in Belgium for the next year and require in-person work in Belgium upon joining (a minimum of 7 hours per week, with leave permitted under special circumstances).

Below is an overview of the positions available in both sessions:
