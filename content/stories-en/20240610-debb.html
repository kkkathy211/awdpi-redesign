The Global Shapers Community is a network of centers initiated by the World Economic Forum ("Davos") and founded and led by young people with exceptional potential and outstanding achievements who are committed to contributing significantly to their communities. Through the Global Shapers Community network, members collectively drive community development.







The World Economic Forum and the Global Shapers Community



The Global Shapers Community is a global youth network directly under the World Economic Forum (WEF), established in 2011 by Professor Klaus Schwab, founder of the World Economic Forum. As a renowned international youth organization, the Global Shapers Community aims to harness individual, community, and global efforts to provide young people with a global platform to shape the future.

Given the World Economic Forum's unique convening power, the Global Shapers Community is diverse in terms of demographics, geographic regions, and professional fields. This diverse community unites around a shared aspiration: to channel the boundless energy and passion of its members to build a more peaceful, prosperous, and inclusive world.


Global Shapers Community Beijing Hub

BEIJING

The Davos Global Shapers Community Beijing Hub is one of the first youth hubs established by the World Economic Forum in China. It currently brings together dozens of outstanding young talents from various industries who are highly capable, deeply mission-driven, and have achieved remarkable success in their respective fields, actively contributing to regional progress and development.


Joining the Global Shapers Community Beijing Hub

JOIN US

 - Connect with youth hubs in over 400 cities worldwide and receive support from more than 10,000 outstanding young people;

 - Co-initiate projects and activities with community members that promote regional development and help build a better local environment;

 - Engage through the World Economic Forum network with global leaders across sectors and represent youth voices by proposing initiatives;

Application Process for the Global Shapers Community Beijing Hub

APPLY

Notice: No fees are charged for the application—please beware of fraud

Individuals who have not completed the official application, review process, and formal identity verification on the World Economic Forum website are not recognized as Global Shapers of the World Economic Forum.

Application

Application period begins upon publication of this announcement and ends at 24:00 on July 14, 2024, Click "Read More" to download and complete the application form (access code: 3rvi) . Applicants must also attach Chinese and English resumes (each no longer than one page) and may submit up to two supplementary materials (if including video, no longer than 60 seconds). Send all files to the email address[email protected](email subject: "Beijing Hub Application + Full Name")

Preliminary Screening

The Beijing Hub Steering Committee will conduct an initial screening based on submitted materials. Shortlisted applicants will proceed to the interview stage.

Interview

After passing the preliminary screening, applicants will be interviewed by the Beijing Hub Steering Committee (interview location and time will be notified separately).

Observation Period 

The Steering Committee will send the application materials and interview feedback to the China Regional Hub for preliminary review. Applicants who pass this review will enter an observation period (In principle, the observation period is six months), during which the Steering Committee will evaluate the applicant's performance.

Applicants are required to initiate or participate in social impact projects led by the community during the observation period, contributing to local development.

Final Review

Applicants who successfully complete the observation period will receive official recognition as Global Shapers and can check the review results on the World Economic Forum website.
