Her Village X Avoice: Love Without Violence

March marks International Women's Day and Women's History Month. In response, Her Village will host a series of events centered around the theme: "What can we do for women in crisis?" The first event, at the beginning of March, will be a storytelling session titled "Love Without Violence—Domestic Violence and Women's Self-Protection," co-organized by the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project and Her Village. Sisters who are interested are warmly welcome to participate and join us!


Event Theme

Violence looms like a persistent dark cloud over intimate relationships. Women are the primary victims of domestic violence, and the suffering and brutality they face occur widely—both in developing and developed countries:

In India, a woman was doused with sulfuric acid by her husband in the middle of the night, burning half her face and causing her to lose one eye—simply because her dowry at marriage was insufficient. In the Middle East, women must not only escape war zones but also constantly guard against the threats of sexual assault and assassination. Even in seemingly highly civilized regions such as Europe and North America, underground human trafficking networks remain rampant.

Silence makes everything possible—violence grows in silence. Laws teach women to speak up and resist, yet not all cries for help are heard or seen. In some countries and regions around the world, even protective legal frameworks for women remain incomplete.

On the eve of International Women's Day, we have invited the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project to share their work. They are dedicated to providing comprehensive and sustained protection for Chinese women experiencing domestic violence, and to building a home for Chinese women rooted in love and equality.

In this first Her Village X Avoice event, they will discuss the project's origins, the state of domestic violence after the pandemic, and a self-help guide for overseas Chinese women facing domestic abuse. We will also hear from beneficiaries and young volunteers.


About Her Village

An online community built for women around the world. Our motto is: Explore a broader world, become a better self. In addition to our women-only forum, we are active across multiple social media platforms, including WeChat Official Account, Xiaohongshu, podcasts, and Weibo.

  • Avoice Project Beneficiary Zhezhe: The harms of domestic violence and awareness of self-protection

  • Case Management Lead Zhajikuai (Fried Chicken): Sharing experiences in connecting with beneficiaries

The Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project is dedicated to helping overseas Chinese women who suffer from domestic violence and improving the living conditions of vulnerable groups of overseas Chinese women, building a bridge between women in need and various resources, becoming their solid support and warm shelter..

We emphasize self-empowerment for our target group of women, while also providing multi-dimensional support, including language, psychological, legal, medical, shelter, referral channels, education, economic aid, storytelling and amplifying voices, and community support—expanding the reach of assistance while fostering a virtuous cycle of mutual support.
