 Introduction:


Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project is dedicated to eliminating gender-based violence and advancing gender equality by providing comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence. By building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, and by mobilizing broader youth-driven public welfare forces, the project advocates for public awareness and support regarding the challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. It aims to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.


On the occasion of the "International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women" on November 25 and the "16 Days of Activism," Avoice has released its first impact report, sharing the goals set since the project's launch in 2020, the intervention models designed, the scope of work covered, and the key achievements attained..


We hope this impact report will help Chinese women living overseas, fellow professionals working in the public welfare sector, and all who care about gender equality better understand the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project and the struggles and aspirations of overseas Chinese women on their journey toward gender equality. More importantly, we hope this report inspires confidence and encouragement, reflecting precisely what the project's English name "Avoice" signifies—an increasing number of voices and forces are joining the movement to advance gender equality.. Let us use women's voices to convey the vision for development, use the voices of youth to call for gender equality, declare humanistic ideals with a manifestly sincere heart, and sing the long song of mission with unwavering determination, gathering the myriad voices of public welfare into the powerful, resounding chorus of the global effort to advance the sustainable development goals for gender equality!
