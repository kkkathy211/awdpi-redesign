AWDPI X Peking University: Announcing the Speakers for "Voices Unsilenced: Echoes of Light"

From a young age, we are taught the standard answer to "hearing and being heard," defaulting to the presence or absence of sound to divide "normal" from "different." But the ruler that defines "normal" should never come in just one measure. Within lives that are "unheard," there is also deep professional dedication, creative romance, and inner harmony—vibrant lives that do not rely on an "inspirational narrative."

🤟 This time, Peking University Love Association Sign Language Branch × AWDPI jointly present the themed public welfare event "Voices Unsilenced · Echoes of Light."

👤 We have invited 6 guests from different fields—each bringing their own life experiences and professional insights. They will not peddle sorrow or glorify suffering, but instead join us in asking: If the world had never had sound to begin with, what would "normal" be? And who gets to define it?
