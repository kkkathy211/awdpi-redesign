The Listening Hotline, walking alongside you

Who we are?

▶

We are a project initiated by the CPSA Edinburgh Branch. CPSA, full name Chinese Psychology Student Association Chinese Psychological Students Association, is a nonprofit organization registered in Washington State, USA, and is a public welfare, academic social group composed of psychology students and scholars from both within and outside China. The Avoice Listening Hotline is a public welfare project established after the organization expanded and improved its systems, aiming to provide emotional support and companionship to every student in need, helping you find a bit of warmth during your overseas study life.



Important Notice


    —Regarding our listeners and confidentiality measures


All listeners are students with psychology backgrounds, not certified professionals, who have received basic listening skills training and serve as volunteers after completing internships and passing assessments, providing free public service .


During listening sessions, listeners will strictly keep all information confidential, and both parties will sign a confidentiality agreement.


. Even internal association personnel have no right to access the content of conversations.


Listeners will not initiate any real-life contact or interference with callers, striving to remain "strangers"


⚠️ The Avoice Listening Hotline is not a professional psychological counseling or therapy service, nor is it a crisis or suicide intervention hotline. If you are experiencing such situations, please contact professional institutions for help immediately.



The appointment process is as follows:

✦

•

✦

Step 1: Find us!


Open WeChat and search: CPSA ED

Follow the Edinburgh Psychology Association "CPSA ED" official account Click on "Avoice Listening", "Appointment QR code" in the service menu below to enter the appointment page.


  Step 2: Make an appointment


Log in to the appointment page, select a suitable time to book, and wait for our operator to contact you.

  Step 3: Fill in information


Provide the nickname you'd like to be called, preferred contact method (WeChat / Tencent Meeting / Zoom), and optionally specify the gender and nickname of the listener. We will try our best to accommodate your request, but cannot guarantee a match.

  Step 4: Wait for matching


Our operator will add you and send two documents: a disclaimer and a confidentiality agreement. Please read them carefully and sign the agreements.

  Step 5: Official start


You will be assigned a listener. Once both parties have signed the agreement, the listening session officially begins.

  Step 6: End the session


After the session, you will receive a feedback survey, which you may fill out voluntarily.

We strongly encourage you to leave your experience feedback!

If your experience was positive, you may request to be matched with the same listener next time.
