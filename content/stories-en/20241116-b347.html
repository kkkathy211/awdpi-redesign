On October 26, AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) was invited to the U.S. Embassy in China to participate in the first Ivy+ Philanthropy Festival. As one of over twenty nonprofit organizations exhibiting outdoors, AWDPI sent three volunteers and prepared abundant promotional materials.



The on-site volunteers arrived early, thoughtfully set up AWDPI's Booth No. 24, and placed pull-up banners and brochures in accessible locations for visitors to view and collect. The materials provided detailed information about AWDPI's mission, objectives, and contact channels.



At 2 p.m., attendees began arriving steadily. Many showed curiosity about AWDPI, approaching the booth to learn more about its philosophy and specific programs.


"What does AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) do? Could you please introduce it in detail?"

"What kind of support do you mainly provide for women?"

"How can I join your volunteer team?"

"What types of activities do you organize regularly?"


The audience's enthusiasm for women's protection and AWDPI was fully reflected in their questions—so many that a long queue formed at the booth, with people eager to collect materials and learn more. The atmosphere was vibrant and energetic.


Moreover, while promoting their own causes, representatives from other nonprofit booths also exchanged brief introductions. Volunteers and staff gathered together, sharing their organizations' missions and exploring opportunities for resource linkage, broadening their understanding of volunteering and sparking new ideas for social impact.


Around 3 p.m., distinguished guests from the U.S. Embassy in China, the United Nations, and organizations under the World Economic Forum attended the festival and delivered on-site speeches, igniting waves of applause and enthusiasm throughout the venue.


This further inspired both attendees and staff with passion and aspiration for charitable work.



The first Ivy+ Philanthropy Festival concluded amid cheers and applause. During the event, many expressed support and encouragement for AWDPI's mission, and some attendees sincerely told the volunteers, "You are truly doing something extraordinary!"


Finally, let's hear from AWDPI's volunteers who participated in the festival!


Volunteer A

Participating in this Ivy League Philanthropy Festival as a member of AWDPI, I deeply experienced the truth that "philanthropy knows no borders, and love is shared by all."


Each booth represented a unique story—from supporting children's development and advancing the rights of the blind, to environmental protection and promoting cultural diversity. Though each organization had its own focus, they shared a common goal: spreading love and hope to every corner of the world. Through exchanges with fellow nonprofits, we not only gained valuable experience and inspiration but also built deep friendships and collaborative networks. This festival motivates us to keep moving forward. I believe every small effort can accumulate into a force that changes the world. I look forward to rallying more like-minded individuals and partnering with more organizations to promote the comprehensive development of Asian women overseas, and to contribute to building a more harmonious and inclusive world!

Volunteer B

I was especially delighted to have in-depth conversations with many fellow philanthropy enthusiasts during this event! I learned about many fascinating nonprofit organizations, working across diverse fields, yet all radiating passion for their causes and for philanthropy itself! Many thanks to the organizers—I hope there will be more opportunities like this in the future. May our world keep getting better!

Volunteer C

Through this Ivy League Philanthropy Festival, I discovered many nonprofit organizations working from different angles and in various fields, and had meaningful exchanges with each—some in-depth collaborations, others simple introductions. This festival provided a rare and valuable platform where the voices of philanthropy could come together and be heard by the public. Philanthropy is actually a long and simple journey; if along the way we can find like-minded companions and receive affirmation and encouragement from passersby, I believe we will be able to walk further and longer.


In the future, AWDPI will continue striving to uphold our vision and mission, constantly improving the support system for overseas Chinese women, engaging in in-depth discussions on social and academic issues, and growing with the support and blessings of everyone toward a better future!
