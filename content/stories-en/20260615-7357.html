AWDPI Australia National Office is honoured to announce that we will partner with Table Tennis Australia to launch the Rally Her Collective (Her Perspective: Rallying Together Through Table Tennis) women's table tennis community project,.


a program designed for women and girls, offering a free four-week table tennis experience and community engagement initiative, aiming to create a safe, friendly, and culturally inclusive space for women through accessible, fun, and low-barrier physical activities.


This project is delivered by Table Tennis Australia with support from the National Foundation for Australia-China Relations, in collaboration with AWDPI Australia. Through this partnership, we aim to integrate women's empowerment, sports participation, cultural inclusion, and community connection, creating more opportunities for women of Asian and diverse cultural backgrounds to access public life and community spaces.




About Table Tennis Australia

Table Tennis Australia (TTA), established in 1933, is the national governing body for table tennis in Australia and oversees the sport at the Olympic, Paralympic, and Commonwealth Games.


As the central organisation for the development of table tennis in Australia, Table Tennis Australia connects the national, state, and territory table tennis systems, and maintains close ties with the International Table Tennis Federation and the Oceania Table Tennis Federation, continuously promoting the sport's popularity, participation, and high-performance development across Australia.



Program Schedule

The program will be held at the Museum of Chinese Australian History in Melbourne and officially launch on Friday, 26 June 2026, from 11:00am to 3:00pm.


Activities for the following three weeks will continue every Friday from 12:00pm to 2:00pm on 3 July, 10 July, and 17 July 2026.


The launch on 26 June will be divided into two parts:



11:00am to 12:00pm

Program Welcome and Introduction Session

This session is primarily for women who are genuinely interested in learning more and continuing to participate in the subsequent three-week women's table tennis community program. Participants will have the opportunity to learn about the content, participation methods, and follow-up arrangements of the Rally Her Collective project, and can also get to know the project team and other participants in advance.


AWDPI Australia will specifically invite around 30 women interested in ongoing participation to join this session and continue to the official launch event from 12:00pm to 3:00pm.


12:00pm to 3:00pm

Open Experience Session

This session will be open to a broader community audience. On-site, professional coaches will lead fun and relaxed table tennis activities, allowing more women to engage with the sport in a friendly, low-barrier environment, build connections, and experience the support and strength that come from community participation.


Under the guidance of professional coaches, participants will have the chance to enjoy a relaxed table tennis experience, meet more women from diverse cultural backgrounds, build connections, boost confidence, and collectively explore the relationships between sport, mental and physical well-being, community engagement, and women's empowerment.



Why table tennis? Why a women's community project?

For AWDPI, women's empowerment does not only happen in meeting rooms, policy discussions, or service systems—it can also take place in a relaxed sports space; in a genuine conversation, a friendly activity, or an experience of being seen, understood, and respected. AWDPI Australia National Office has long focused on the intersecting issues of gender, culture, language, social participation, and safety support faced by Asian women in Australia. We believe that true support involves not only responding to challenges, but also creating more opportunities for women to enter public spaces, build social connections, enhance confidence, and become active participants in community life.


The Rally Her Collective project reflects AWDPI's ongoing commitment to women's participation, mental and physical well-being, and community connection. Through our collaboration with Table Tennis Australia, the project not only offers women access to table tennis, but also aims to further advance gender equality, cultural inclusion, and community participation within sports spaces.


For many women of Asian and diverse cultural backgrounds, having a space that truly welcomes, understands, and respects them is crucial. Rally Her Collective hopes to use sport—a relaxed and open format—to encourage more women to step into the community, build connections, and gain confidence, a sense of belonging, and support through participation.
