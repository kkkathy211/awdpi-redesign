Dear participants and all friends following this forum:


The "2024 AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International Forum," originally scheduled for August 2024, has received overwhelming responses and support. Inspired by this enthusiasm, we feel even more compelled to elevate the quality and impact of this year's forum.


To further enrich the program, invite more distinguished speakers, and enhance our technical platform to offer a smoother and more interactive experience, after extensive consultation and careful consideration by the organizing committee, we have decided to reschedule the forum to: November 23–24, 2024.


We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this change may cause. Please rest assured, however, that this decision was made after thorough evaluation and is intended to best protect the interests of all participants and ensure the highest quality of the forum.


Whether attending academic presentations, roundtable discussions, paper reviews, or debate performances, we believe every individual, organization, institution, and corporate representative will share our eagerness to "come together" to share, discuss, and explore how modern women can break through barriers and achieve bolder, brighter self-expression and sustainable development.


We look forward to meeting you in November 2024 for an even more inspiring intellectual feast.
