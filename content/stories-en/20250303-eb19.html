Following "Reunion Dinner," we present a new immersive psychological theater production "BOND"


In January 2025, SpArkling's play "Reunion Dinner" premiered at Gene Frankel Theatre, a renowned Off-Off-Broadway theater in downtown New York, with five sold-out performances, offering audiences a triple sensory feast of sight, sound, and taste.


This March, director and playwright Annora Dong and Sophia Zhu push boundaries once again with their new physical interactive psychological drama, *BOND*. The script is adapted from *Harvard Neural Bonds* by Dr. Ann Cai, a psychology education doctorate who previously taught at Harvard University and MIT, and is now a professor in the Department of Social Sciences and Humanities at a prestigious private university on the U.S. East Coast.


This production will be specially presented as part of the 2025 New York United Nations Commission on the Status of Women (CSW) side event, staged at an Off-Off-Broadway theater. Deeply exploring themes of mental health, social expectations, and self-discovery, the play will guide audiences on a journey to explore psychological struggles and paths to self-redemption.


Photo Credit: Zhenyu Meng



Story Synopsis


Separated by a curtain, yet bound by fate. Bai Yunxiao (April) and Han Xiaodi (June), two women, explore emotion, trauma, and selfhood amid shifting light and shadow.


  • Bai Yunxiao (April) A gentle and rational assistant professor of psychology, she tries to heal others to mend her own past wounds, yet beneath the surface, turbulent emotions surge, forcing her to finally confront her own vulnerability and obsessions.

  • Han Xiaodi (June) A sensitive and detached graduate research assistant, she oscillates between self-doubt and self-pressure, yearning for care yet resisting dependence, constantly searching for the meaning of her existence through escape and confrontation.



When two destined souls collide, what ripples will be set in motion?





Immersive Experience


Your choice determines your perspective!


We have divided the audience seating into two halves. Your choice will determine whose perspective you embody.

  • Left side: Dive into the inner world of Bai Yunxiao (April), experiencing her rationality and vulnerability.

  • Right side: Enter the psychological space of Han Xiaodi (June), feeling her resistance and longing.


Photo Credit: Zhenyu Meng


Inspired by narrative psychology, audiences on each side will experience different characters' psychological depth and narrative arcs. Through the integration of physical expression and interactive art,*BOND* will externalize the characters' inner conflicts, immersing you in the intertwining and collision of two souls.

SpArkling Theater Collective

SpArkling Theater Collective was founded in January 2024 by two young female artists, Annora Dong and Sophia Zhu. The co-founders met at the Experimental Theatre Wing of New York University's Tisch School of the Arts, and united by both their distinct artistic sensibilities and deep resonance, established the theater collective. Drawing artistic inspiration and energy from cities such as New York and Shanghai, SpArkling is dedicated to illuminating people and the world through creativity and passion, using avant-garde experimental theater and physical theater. The studio's core mission is to use art to transcend cultural boundaries, build deep human connections, and continuously promote cross-cultural artistic exchange on the international stage.
