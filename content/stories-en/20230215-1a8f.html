The Hong Kong General Chamber of Commerce (Shanghai) hosted the [SHE IMPACT Forum – Restructure & Innovate] on March 8, inviting numerous outstanding women from various industries to discuss how female influence can break through limitations in the post-pandemic era, create new opportunities, and exercise leadership and creativity to generate sustainable value for industries, society, and the world. The [SHE IMPACT Forum – Restructure & Innovate] is the first event of the Chamber's "Inspirational Women" initiative in 2023, aiming to connect more elite women in Shanghai and jointly create greater value.


 Areas of focus for the Hong Kong General Chamber of Commerce (Shanghai) Inspirational Women initiative 

- Women dedicated to technology and entrepreneurship

- Women in art, design, and cultural fields

- Women advancing sustainable development and environmental protection

- Women in Fortune 500 companies and management

- Women committed to promoting charitable causes

Speaker profiles 

(In no particular order)

Lan Zhenzhen

Chief Corporate Affairs & Public Engagement Officer, L'Oréal North Asia & China

Lan Zhenzhen joined L'Oréal in Hong Kong in 1993 and later became a founding team member in mainland China. She currently leads corporate communications and government affairs for the China, Japan, and South Korea markets. She has been honored as an "Outstanding Entrepreneurial Woman" by the All-China Women's Federation, received the SABRE Award (the "Oscars" of public relations), and was awarded the Knight of the National Order of Merit by France.

Li Jielin (Karen Li)

Chief Executive Officer, Vinda International Holdings Limited

Ms. Li Jielin joined Vinda in 2012 and serves as CEO of Vinda International Holdings Limited, overseeing the company's overall development and strategic planning. With years of experience in the hygiene products industry, she possesses deep insights into the Chinese market and extensive experience managing overseas markets. She has unique expertise in corporate governance, category management, operational efficiency, and risk control, and holds a forward-looking vision for sustainable business development.

Huang Weihua (Vera Wang)

General Manager, Millennium Harbourview Hotel Shanghai

A Guangdong native born in Shanghai in the 1960s and who emigrated to Hong Kong in the early 1980s, Vera's hotel career has spanned Hong Kong, the United States, Hangzhou, and Shanghai. Returning to work in Shanghai's hotel industry is her most heartfelt homecoming.

Ku Suiling (Eleanor Ku)

Sales Vice President, BRITA Water Purification Systems (Shanghai) Co., Ltd.

Eleanor Ku has over 20 years of marketing experience with multinational FMCG companies in Hong Kong and mainland China, having worked with Mead Johnson, Danone, and Unilever. She joined the German water purification brand Brita in 2017 as Vice President of Marketing and currently serves as Sales Vice President. Eleanor is also a certified coach and actively promotes coaching-based leadership culture within organizations.

Au Yee Lam (April Au)

Founder, Animal Communication

- From a Hong Kong musical family, ran a performing arts group for over 20 years, promoting animal rescue, adoption, and sterilization education.

- Studied animal communication in 2009 under Rosina Maria Arquati, a leading authority in the field.

- After Rosina's passing in 2018, began carrying forward the practice of animal communication.

- Through her unique teaching method, has enabled 99% of participants from the United States, Canada, Norway, Hungary, Malaysia, Japan, mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan to successfully connect with animals, helping thousands of families understand animal-related issues.


Fung Lijuan (Jessic Fung)

Co-founder, ISS-Vision Events

Founder, Xin Su Yoga Lifestyle Studio

Co-founder, Soul Well

Jessic Fung is a co-founder of the event company ISS-Vision Events and formerly served as Business Director for the Consumer Market at GSMA Mobile World Congress – Shanghai. With over 20 years of experience in event planning and execution both domestically and internationally, she has participated in the planning and execution of numerous concerts by international superstars, major sports events, and international conferences. Jessic is also dedicated to advancing the wellness industry, having founded Xin Su Yoga Lifestyle Studio and the wellness community Soul Well, promoting public awareness of holistic mind-body-spirit health management.

Lam Hiu Ying (Juliana Lam)

Founder & Chairperson, Julius Group

Founder & CEO, INNOTIER

Juliana Lam is the founder and chairperson of Julius Group, a private-label glove manufacturer. Julius Group is a forward-thinking company committed to ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) as its core values, striving to transform traditional glove manufacturing through technological research and innovative solutions, relentlessly reshaping and building a sustainable business. In 2014, she became the only female recipient of the Hong Kong Young Industrialist Award and the first female president of the Hong Kong Glove Industry Association. Ms. Lam is also the founder and CEO of the UK brand INNOTIER, a mission-driven company that partners with global high-tech leaders to develop a series of innovative products using cutting-edge global technology to support sustainable living.

Cheung Yim (Flora Cheung)

Assistant General Manager & Head of East China Region, Bank of East Asia (China) Limited

Ms. Flora Cheung joined the Bank of East Asia in 2000. From February 2007 to January 2010, she served as Deputy Branch Manager of the Shanghai Branch; from February 2010 to March 2013, as Director of Network Management at BEA China; from April 2013 to April 2018, as Branch Manager of the Shanghai Branch; and since May 2018, as Assistant General Manager and Head of the East China Region at BEA China. With extensive experience in the banking industry, she is also passionate about promoting economic and cultural exchanges between Shanghai and Hong Kong. Additionally, Flora currently serves as Vice President of the Hong Kong General Chamber of Commerce (Shanghai).

Chan Kam Po (Beatrice Chan) 

Vice President, Hong Kong General Chamber of Commerce (Shanghai)

CEO, Junbao (Shanghai) Pet Food Co., Ltd. 

Beatrice is the current Vice President of the Hong Kong General Chamber of Commerce (Shanghai), overseeing the Inspirational Women initiative and the Food & Beverage & Hotel Services Group. With over 20 years of experience in management and brand strategy at multinational food companies, she has worked with German pet food brand Junbao, JDE Coffee, US-based Heinz Longfeng, and French Danone. She has successfully launched and developed multiple health food lifestyle products. Beatrice is also President of the Hong Kong University Shanghai Alumni Association and a council member of the Shanghai Women Entrepreneurs Association, committed to advancing female influence and sustainable development.

Cheung Dak Yan (Emily Cheung)

Founder, Appreciator Cultural-Tourism Tech Platform & Appreciator.io Web3 Art Platform

Ms. Emily Cheung is the founder and CEO of Appreciator, a cultural-tech platform. Appreciator is the first Asia-Pacific shareholder of the Van Gogh Heritage Foundation in the Netherlands and also a market partner of the Picasso Foundation in China. Its vision is to use technology to connect young Chinese people with world culture, thereby promoting the preservation and promotion of cultural heritage and intangible cultural heritage. Prior to this, Emily co-founded CREATEC Digital, an independent advertising agency consistently ranked as China Advertising Awards' Agency of the Year, specializing in brand strategy and digital communications. In her spare time, Emily is dedicated to growing SHMADNESS, a community for independent artists and creatives. SHMADNESS is a non-profit community composed of top young creative professionals in Shanghai, which has successfully organized or co-organized over 100 offline events, fostering greater artistic collaboration between brands and independent creatives.

Wah Yuen Tong (Jenny Wah)

Co-founder, Lian Ge Cultural Communication
