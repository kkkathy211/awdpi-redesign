Spring is deepening, the breeze is gentle and pleasant, and AWDPI's charity hikes have successfully completed two spring gatherings in April. From the forested hidden gem of Sandhurst to the Thames riverside in Henley-on-Thames, we used hiking as a medium and charity as our mission. A group of partners who love life and carry kindness stepped through grassy trails and along river embankments, experiencing the beauty of nature through walking and passing on the warmth of public good through companionship, leaving behind many unforgettable memories.

April 4 Sandhurst Hike


ABOUT SPRING


This time we went to Sandhurst. The path was lined with fresh greenery and the air was especially refreshing—walking felt so comfortable that it instantly lifted the fatigue of daily life. Along the way, we encountered some particularly gentle horses, so tame that we could pet them directly, which was deeply healing; we also met two wolfhounds, taller than all of us, who looked imposing at first but were actually very sweet and adorable, adding much joy to our hike.


Continuing forward, we reached a lake with stunning scenery—crystal-clear water surrounded by lush trees. We rested there for a while before moving on. The trail wasn't difficult, and everyone looked out for each other, making the walk easy and relaxed. When tired, we found a spot for a picnic, sitting together in a circle sharing homemade food, chatting, and basking in the sun—an especially cozy moment.

April 18 Henley-on-Thames Hike


ABOUT SPRING


For the second hike, we headed to Henley-on-Thames, long known as one of the happiest towns near London. After visiting, we can confirm it truly lives up to its reputation. We walked along the River Thames, and every step felt deeply soothing.


Upon arriving at the town, we immediately felt at ease—fresh air, historic buildings, green trees everywhere, and the winding River Thames in view. It was love at first sight.


We strolled slowly along the riverside, the path gently sloping, warm wind blowing, making the walk especially pleasant. The scenery along the way was beautiful—old buildings, manor estates, and country lanes, all full of charm. The locals were also exceptionally warm, approaching us to share stories about the town's history. Hearing these tales deepened our appreciation of its happiness—the sense of ease and relaxation warmed our hearts.


This hike didn't follow a fixed route. Since everyone had different energy levels, we adjusted according to each person's pace. Those who didn't want to walk fast could wander slowly; those who wanted to enjoy the views could stop to take photos, and some even visited small shops in the town. The entire experience was deeply relaxing.


Midway, we found a scenic spot to rest, unpacking our own lunches and water to replenish energy. As we ate and chatted, partners who had barely known each other began to grow closer, engaging in lively and heartfelt conversations.


Carrying warmth with every step, moving toward beauty through companionship


The two charity hikes in April—one a leisurely forest walk, the other a healing riverside journey—featured different routes and distinct scenery, yet both carried AWDPI's original charitable purpose and the passionate companionship of our partners. We stepped outdoors, drew close to nature, conveyed the power of public good through walking, and gained friendship and warmth through companionship.


Every step forward was a practice of charity; every moment of companionship was a transmission of warmth. Partners helping each other, kindness from strangers, and every surprise and joy along the hike became the most precious memories of April.


Spring may have passed, but warmth remains, The April charity hikes have concluded successfully, but AWDPI's journey in public good never stops. In the future, we will continue to hold more meaningful public welfare activities, invite more partners to join us, spread warmth with our steps, practice public welfare with our actions, and encounter more beauty and touching moments.
