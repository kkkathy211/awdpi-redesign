From around the world

write a postcard for survivors of domestic violence


November 25 marks the United Nations' International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women. The "16 Days of Activism" against gender-based violence begins on this day each year and continues until December 10. The campaign aims to encourage people worldwide to take action and help eliminate gender-based violence.


Today, violence against women and girls remains one of the most widespread human rights violations in the world. According to United Nations statistics, approximately 736 million women globally—nearly one in three—have experienced gender-based violence at least once in their lives, including violence in workplaces and online spaces.


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International will relaunch the "16 Days of Activism" campaign during the "Avoice Mailbox" initiative. You can show your support for survivors of gender-based violence in one of two ways:


# Hear Her, Heal Her #

Handwrite a card to send encouragement, care, and support to women who have experienced domestic violence and other forms of gender-based violence, allowing love and courage to be conveyed through your pen. Please take a photo of your card and post it on social media platforms such as Xiaohongshu, Weibo, Instagram with hashtags like #stopgenderviolence, #hearherhealher, #LoveWithoutViolence and tag our account. Our account names are as follows:


Xiaohongshu, Weibo

@Overseas Chinese Women Protection

Instagram

@avoice_womenaid


Alternatively, you may send the photo of your card directly via private message to our official account, or email it to our mailbox with the subject line [Avoice Mailbox]:


[email protected]


If your message is written in a language other than Chinese or English, please indicate the language used when submitting.


Notes:

1. While supporting others, please protect yourself—do not disclose personal information in your messages;

2. Please avoid expressions containing personal attacks or non-inclusive language;

3. By submitting your content, you agree to grant AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International the right to use your work for public welfare promotion and outreach. The original author retains the right to be credited. If no name is provided upon submission, we will credit the work as "Anonymous."


# Hear You, Heal You #

Sometimes, when you experience gender-based violence in daily life and want to talk about it, do you hesitate to speak up?

Sometimes, when you feel confused about issues related to gender-based violence, do you find no one to discuss them with?

Perhaps you can step into this warm and safe anonymous tree hole, share your thoughts, and we will be here to listen, understand, and support you.


How to participate:

Participation period: November 25 to December 10, 2023

Participation method: Send an email to

[email protected]

Email subject format: Tree Hole Letter + Your Name


We will listen to your story like a friend and offer help within our capacity. Please note that this activity is not a professional psychological counseling service or a long-term pen-pal program. After receiving your letter, we will within 14 days select and reply to letters as appropriate.


Your concerns may also be shared by other women, so we may publish selected letters and responses on our official account to inspire and support more people. If you do not wish for your letter and the response to be made public, or if you want certain sensitive content to be redacted when published, please clearly state your request at the beginning of your letter. If no such request is indicated, we will assume that all content except basic personal information may be published.
