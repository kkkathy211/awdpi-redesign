Avoice Themed Sharing Session

Participant Recruitment


Protecting Each Other, Love Without Violence




# Wherever there is the Harbor #

Where Is There No Safe Harbor





Written and typeset | Avoice Team



To the friend reading this letter right now:

    Hello.

    We are the Avoice Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project,, a youth volunteer organization dedicated to providing assistance and support to women facing the threat of domestic violence, safeguarding women's basic rights and interests, volunteer group, and also a good friend who hopes to accompany and protect you.


    Perhaps you were once hurt by someone you trusted most, losing hope in love; or perhaps you are now in a difficult situation, torn between emotions and responsibilities; or maybe you have already left past pain behind and decided to write a new life with a smile—no matter what, we are willing to face with you the coldness and darkness that invade the vibrant life, and do our utmost to provide support, whether psychological, financial, or legal.

    If you are willing to trust us, we will be waiting for you at the "Protecting Each Other, Love Without Violence" themed sharing session. This will be a small tea gathering, tentatively scheduled for November 20, lasting about 1–2 hours, at a relatively quiet teahouse or café, aiming to create a comfortable environment for open conversation. All photos taken during the event will be anonymized with faces obscured, fully protecting your privacy. Participants will all be female friends with experiences similar to yours, so we encourage everyone to get to know one another and share freely, healing yourselves and gaining courage in an atmosphere of trust and encouragement.

    On November 25, 1993, the United Nations issued the *Declaration on the Elimination of Violence against Women*, expressing the hope that no woman would be excluded from legal protection.

    Twenty-eight years later, today, we sincerely invite you to share your story, hoping that we may in some way help ease your pain. At the same time, your experience might help those about to fall into the abyss or already deeply trapped, turning past sorrows into weapons against violence, gradually smoothing their sharp edges through repeated confrontations and embraces, ultimately transforming into warm, rounded kindness extended to fellow survivors.



    If you are interested in attending this event, please fill out the information collection questionnaire below:

https://www.wjx.cn/vm/Qk2ILAa.aspx

(Click the link at the bottom left to access it directly)


Leave us your personal information and contact details, and select the city you wish to participate in. The questionnaire offers four cities: Wuhan, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Hangzhou. To maximize comfort and create a safe atmosphere, as well as to ensure safety and health during the pandemic,, each city's sharing session will accommodate around ten participants, selected on a first-come, first-served basis. We will notify you via text message whether your registration is successful, and we appreciate your understanding. Friends unable to attend need not feel too regretful— we will continue to host city-based themed sharing sessions, and we believe that one day, we will meet.

    Please believe, no matter where you are, we are here.
