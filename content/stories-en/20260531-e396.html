On April 29, 2026, Li Danyang, Head of the Partnership Development Division at AWDPI Thailand Office, together with Charisma Gualberto, Head of the Academic Research Department, visited the Thailand office of the Association for the Promotion of the Status of Women (APSW), for an exchange and dialogue. The two parties held in-depth discussions on gender-related issues and potential directions for academic collaboration, and engaged in productive conversations with APSW's Gender and Development Research Institute (GDRI), taking a substantive step forward in their cooperation.




As a well-known non-profit organization sponsored by Her Royal Highness Princess Soamsawali of Thailand, APSW has long been committed to the protection of women's and children's rights, with extensive experience and broad influence in social assistance, capacity building, policy advocacy, and academic research. During the exchange, the GDRI director systematically outlined the multiple challenges currently facing Thai women in social development, including increasing pressure from family caregiving responsibilities, economic vulnerability under climate change, and an ongoing need to strengthen support systems for domestic violence survivors, further highlighting the importance and urgency of relevant research and action.



Through in-depth dialogue and exchange of perspectives, both sides reached strong consensus: against the backdrop of accelerating regional mobility, the group of "new-generation Chinese women in Thailand" is gradually emerging as a significant subject worthy of attention, yet this field currently suffers from a clear research gap, calling for systematic academic review and data support.



Based on shared values and research interests, the two parties formally established an academic collaboration intent, planning to jointly launch a special study titled "Living Conditions of the New-Generation Chinese Women in Thailand." This project will integrate AWDPI's transnational perspective with GDRI's local research strengths, using in-depth field research and data analysis to comprehensively map this group's realities in career development, social integration, and rights protection.



This visit further deepened mutual understanding and laid a solid foundation for future exchanges and cooperation in related research fields.
