One | AWDPI Annual Forum

The 2024 AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) Forum is about to launch. This year's forum will center on "Orange the World — Building a Future Without Violence" and "A New Era in Shaping Women's Identities," engaging in in-depth dialogue with government agencies, academic institutions, nonprofit organizations, experts, scholars, opinion leaders, and concerned individuals worldwide who are committed to the development of Asian women, offering an intellectual feast that unites wisdom, exploration, and passion.


[Paper Review]As a core and continuing component of the AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International Forum, the paper review segment will be expanded and innovated upon the foundation of the past two editions, and will once again open a public call for research paper submissions, with selected works to be publicly presented at the forum..

 

For more detailed information about the 2024 AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International Forum, please follow our official WeChat account, where we will gradually introduce the exciting forum agenda and the much-anticipated distinguished guests!


Two | Call for Papers and Research Submissions

1. Purpose of the Paper Review

• To showcase AWDPI's academic standards and capabilities;

• To build a bridge for AWDPI members and scholars engaged in gender-related research to participate in academic exchange;

• To promote interdisciplinary dialogue and co-create an academic network with experts and scholars from diverse fields.


2. Paper Review Process

• Thematic Paper Review and Presentation: The AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International Forum will dedicate 2–2.5 hours specifically to the review and presentation of papers on related themes.

• Selected Paper Presentations and Expert Commentary: From the submissions, we will select 4–6 high-quality 4–6 papers, each given an 8-minute presentation slot, followed by approximately 10 minutes of expert commentary and suggestions.

• Online Poster Exhibition and Exchange : If the number of submissions is sufficient, we will add an online poster exhibition session. High-quality submissions will be turned into digital posters (posters) for attendees to freely "navigate" and view across different virtual meeting spaces. Presenters will have the opportunity to personally explain their academic posters and engage in in-depth discussions with scholars from around the world, jointly exploring new academic insights.


3. Call for Submissions – Target Contributors

 This call for papers is open to:

• Current AWDPI members

• AWDPI Talent Pool members and former members

• Partners referred by AWDPI members

• Individuals from all walks of society and current students who have strong interest in the forum themes and are engaged in related research


4. Call for Paper Themes

Any topic related to fields AWDPI is involved in or planning to enter, including but not limited to:

• Anti-gender-based violence movements

• Protection of Asian women overseas

• Violence-free digital space initiatives

• Women's leadership

• Women and health

• Women and intimate relationships

• Women and media representation

• Women's political and workplace empowerment

• Women and technological innovation

• Women and environmental protection, cultural heritage preservation

• Women and artistic expression

• Women and education

• Women and social change

• Women and family roles

• Women and migration

• Women and digital security

• Women and religious beliefs

• Women and literature

• Women and international relations

• Women and sports

• Women and aging

• Overseas Chinese women workers

All academic disciplines are welcome. We especially encourage interdisciplinary research submissions, such as:

• Technology + Philanthropy (e.g., how women's online forums support and shape the public sphere)

• Policy + Women's Health (e.g., women's health from medical and social security perspectives)

• Workplace + Education (e.g., cultivating workplace competence among overseas Chinese women)

• Law + Psychology (e.g., psychological trauma in children exposed to domestic violence and its correlation with adult criminal behavior)


Paper topics and content must align with AWDPI's vision of "Creating a World of Love and Equality for Women" and its values of "Participation, Inclusion, and Empowerment."


5. Paper Review Panel

Experts and scholars from fields such as gender equality, international communication, women's empowerment, and philanthropy will be invited to review the papers.


6. Paper and Research Selection Criteria

• Submissions in Chinese or English are accepted; for other languages, please contact the Academic Department separately for consultation:[email protected]

• Clear purpose, distinct theme, solid evidence, logical structure, and concise language

• Must follow standard academic format, including title, author name, abstract, keywords, main text, and references

• Recommended length: no more than 15,000 Chinese characters or 8,000 English words

• Papers must be original; plagiarism is prohibited, and no infringement of others' legal rights is allowed

• Previously published papers will not be accepted

• This forum encourages authors with strong interest or ideas about the themes—especially those who have not yet participated in formal academic forums—to actively submit. Experimental studies, literature reviews, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and other types of submissions are all welcome to promote academic exchange and vitality. We also welcome students without complete research results to submit and share high-quality research proposals, ongoing studies, term papers, or short essays
