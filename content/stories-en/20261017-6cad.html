AWDPI event registration | Join us for a hearing-friendly gathering

✨ Voices Unsilenced · Echoes of Light:
What if the world had never had sound?

Sound has never been the only bridge connecting us.
The understanding in a shared glance, the warmth conveyed through gestures,
the sincerity carried by words,
all enable us to cross differences and reach one another.

October 25,
AWDPI, together with the Sign Language Club of Peking University's Love Society,
invites you to explore sign language culture,
and through authentic experiences and exchanges, hear expressions that have long been overlooked.
Let the world hear the voices that cannot be heard. 🫶

📅 October 25 (Sunday) 13:00–17:00
📍 Underground Space, Building 2, Peking University Innovation and Entrepreneurship Center

Exciting on-site raffle prizes await you!
