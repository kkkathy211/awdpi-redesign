The COVID-19 pandemic has disrupted the existing order. Whether it's social isolation during the pandemic, increased living burdens, or the growing uncertainty in the post-pandemic era, all of these have made mental health challenges more severe.


In those exhausted and powerless moments, perhaps an inner healing journey can offer stronger internal strength.


The Women's Power column of the Stanford Social Innovation Review will host an online "Healing and Self-Empowerment Workshop for Women in the Pandemic" on April 24 (Sunday) from 8:00–10:00 pm Beijing time. We have invited guest partners from diverse fields including art therapy, positive psychology, and life coaching to co-empower participants.


By attending this public welfare workshop, you will gain:

  • a warm, supportive space for women to help one another

  • the ability to practice self-awareness, self-care, and self-empowerment when facing negative challenges

  • inspiring stories, exercises, and insights from art therapy, positive psychology, and life coaching




Zi Jun (Jun)



Jun has experienced teaching Chinese in Cambodia and studied literature writing and mind-body healing in the UK and US. She co-founded Splash Institute (Happiness Action Institute HA), a healing-focused nonprofit, and now practices expressive art therapy, dynamic and static meditation, and other healing methods. She is dedicated to online and offline workshops and course programs, while also writing literary and spiritual content.



During the panel session, Jun will begin by introducing her area of expertise and discussing the essence of healing, then transition into practical mind-body regulation techniques for daily use—especially during pandemic-related home confinement. She will share what to do and what not to do when facing overwhelming negative information and emotional overload.


In the workshop session, Jun will focus on women's healing through "Heart-Abdomen Energy – Inner Love." Starting with a meditative mindfulness practice targeting the heart center and lower abdomen to initiate inner experience, the session will then move from stillness into gentle movement and free-form dance, and finally return to a balanced, centered state through chanting and poetry reading. This process will help participants explore feelings of "emptiness" or coldness in the abdomen and heaviness or stagnation in the heart center, supporting them to strengthen inner power and awaken the wisdom of body and mind.


Shi Lin



Shi Lin is currently Senior Director of International Admissions and Partnerships at the University of Chicago Harris School of Public Policy. After completing her undergraduate studies, she joined a Fortune Global 100 company as a management trainee and became a human resources manager within three years. She then pursued further studies in the US, during which she participated in global management of a youth employment program with an international organization. She has long focused on personal growth and leadership development, providing counseling and support to ambitious young people aged 20–40 across China and the US.


In 2020, Shi Lin founded Shifei Hall, aiming to bring together interesting souls, break free from labels, and unlock everyone's potential through curiosity and action, helping them realize self-worth. To date, Shifei Hall has provided community resources and one-on-one support to over 500 students, helping them deepen personal growth during life transitions and create their own fulfilling lives.



Shi Lin will introduce some classic and easy-to-apply practical exercises from the fields of positive psychology and co-active coaching, helping participants accumulate awareness of themselves and their lives in daily routines, enhance their ability to notice and expand positive events and emotions, and form a positive feedback loop that is under their own control.


Yan Xiao Jing (Athena Yan)



Athena is a life coach who previously worked as a consultant at McKinsey. After discovering her mission at age 24—to heal and empower more people's personal growth—she transitioned careers and became McKinsey China's first female manager for leadership, diversity, and inclusion culture, and built WAG Women's Alliance, China's first cross-company women's empowerment platform, from the ground up. She is also a high-quality content creator in the personal growth space and host of the podcast "Self Evolution Theory," which has accumulated over 3 million plays across platforms.



Athena will share, from her own life experiences, how she practiced self-healing during low points in life, and how healing and integrating feminine energy helped her become a more radiant and whole version of herself.



Banzema (Valencia Xu)

Social entrepreneur and design thinking mentor.

Chief curator of the Women's Power column in the Chinese edition of Stanford Social Innovation Review. Founder of Yue Shi Pride, a social innovation organization focused on "menstrual health and hygiene." Holds an MPA from Columbia University. Also a multi-hyphenate writer and theater director; her fiction has been published in Guangming Daily, Xinhua Wen Zhai, and others.

"Women's Power" is a column of the Chinese edition of Stanford Social Innovation Review.
