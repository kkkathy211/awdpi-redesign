   The "[Mo's People] Initiative" is a global mental health public welfare project launched by Mo's Psychology Hut in 2025, collaborating with NGOs and nonprofit organizations at home and abroad,, aiming to connect global public-interest psychotherapists to help individuals with mental health concerns access more online and offline pro bono counseling resources, and also to enable a large number of professional psychotherapists to be seen.


Why launch

the [Mo's People] Initiative

    Since 2016, national Level 2 and Level 3 psychological counselor certificates have been abolished, resulting in a mix of industry certifications and qualifications, making it difficult for clients to verify counselors' credentials and vulnerable to "secondary trauma." The [Mo's People] Initiative aims to uncover more professional volunteer psychological counselors, focusing on counselors' personal supervision, clinical practice, and comprehensive professional training background.


[Mo's People Initiative]

Requirements for joining

    As a public welfare project, the [Mo's People] Initiative aims to help clients access more public welfare psychological support resources, so the primary requirement is that psychotherapists to provide a certain number of pro bono counseling sessions Secondly, professional training background and personal supervision hours & frequency must meet certain standards, and possess sufficient clinical competence to join the network.


How to define pro bono psychotherapy

    The public welfare psychological counseling in the "Morshengren" project includes both free public welfare psychological counseling and counseling charged at public welfare rates. The number of sessions and the price are determined by the individual counselor, and the platform does not enforce any mandatory intervention.


Benefits of joining the [Mo's People] Initiative

   The hut focuses on empowering both seekers and helpers, and to encourage more professional psychological counselors to join, the hut offers corresponding benefits to all counselors in the network, including personal promotion and group empowerment workshops, etc.; for referrals made to clients, there will be no commission taken, hoping to help clients find suitable counselors while also helping them understand each counselor's unique characteristics.


Goals of the [Mo's People] Initiative

  1. For clients: individuals can find local Chinese-speaking pro bono psychotherapists within their area through the [Moshengren] network, as well as gain access to more online professional pro bono counseling resources.


  2. For therapists: they can join the global Chinese-speaking pro bono psychotherapist community, promoting professional growth and personal empowerment among counselors.
