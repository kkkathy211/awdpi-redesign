About AWDPI


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas, and promoting their empowerment and development. It evolved from Avoice (Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), established in 2020. AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in the United Kingdom, Belgium, and the United States. AWDPI has built a broad network of partnerships with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments of different countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. AWDPI advocates for greater attention to the living challenges faced by Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI aims to create more opportunities for Asian women overseas to develop in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, helping them transform from a vulnerable group into a vital force driving social progress.



Our Mission

To eliminate violence and discrimination against Asian women and promote the protection and development of Asian women

Our Vision

To create a world of love and equality for women

Our Values

Participation, Inclusion, Empowerment

We are committed to

  • providing rescue and support for domestic violence survivors. Through a comprehensive assistance system and a robust overseas support network, we promptly respond to the needs of overseas Chinese women in domestic violence prevention, emergency rescue, and follow-up services.

  • building community connections. By establishing an online community matrix and offline mutual support groups, we create an international platform for Asian women overseas to communicate, learn, and support one another, forming peer support networks that enable self-development.

  • enhancing women's influence. Through forums, seminars, workshops, and other activities, we actively facilitate multi-stakeholder collaboration to analyze experiences, study challenges, explore solutions, and showcase the strength and potential of Asian women.

  • launching awareness campaigns and advocacy. We actively conduct public outreach, academic research, and policy advocacy to promote global attention to Asian women overseas and push for improved policies and development environments.

08 Program Quality Department


Department Introduction:

The Program Quality Department consists of four sub-departments: Academic Research Division, Program Design Division, Monitoring and Evaluation Division, and IT Information Division. The Academic Research Division is responsible for planning and conducting academic research on topics related to gender equality, anti-domestic violence, women's protection and empowerment, writing research reports and popular science articles, and providing recommendations and strategic guidance for institutional strategy and program design. The Program Design Division is responsible for designing the organization's development strategy, planning, and annual work plans, as well as leading the planning and implementation of large-scale activities. The Monitoring and Evaluation Division monitors the daily progress and quality of organizational and project operations, organizes assessments of overall effectiveness and impact, and proposes improvement plans based on evaluation results to optimize workflows, enhance efficiency, and promote interdepartmental collaboration. The IT Information Division is responsible for website design, development, testing, and maintenance, as well as organizing internal IT support in collaboration with other departments.
