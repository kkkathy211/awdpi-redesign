 01 Who We Are 


 Project Overview 

Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project Committed to eliminating gender-based violence and promoting gender equality, the project provides comprehensive and sustained protection and assistance to overseas Chinese women facing the threat of domestic violence. By building a collaborative network with government agencies, overseas Chinese organizations, civil society groups, and industry experts, and by mobilizing broader youth-driven public welfare forces, the project advocates for public attention and support for the living challenges faced by overseas Chinese women. It aims to create a safe and equal environment for their survival and development, helping them transform from victims of domestic violence into advocates and practitioners who promote gender equality and protect vulnerable groups.


The project is abbreviated as "Avoice," derived from Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program (the English name of the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project). It signifies using women's voices to convey a vision for development, using youth voices to advocate for gender equality, declaring humanistic ideals with a manifest and sincere heart, and singing the long song of mission with unwavering determination. It is dedicated to integrating the myriad voices of public welfare into the powerful chorus of global efforts to advance gender equality and sustainable development goals!

 02 Position Recruitment 

Program Design Division

The main responsibilities of the Program Design Division are divided into the following areas:


Designing project strategies, implementation plans, organizational operation strategies, optimizing workflows, and internal and external collaboration mechanisms, while providing technical support for project execution across departments.


Program Design Division Lead/Member (1–2 positions)


Job Description:

1. Project Top-Level Design (Strategy Consulting):

Responsible for the top-level design of new Avoice projects (e.g., Violence-Free Spaces, Anti-Violence Campaigns, Women's Employment Promotion, and Leadership Development).


2. Organizational Operation Strategy (Operations Consulting) :

  • Utilize Feishu / management systems to enhance efficient collaboration within and between departments.

    (e.g., knowledge base setup, department user manuals, individual user guides, department training, workflow optimization design, etc.)

  • Internal training and external collaboration mechanisms: department wiki to consolidate important documents and task assignments; department user manuals (primarily for external collaboration); internal information management (document management).

  • Workflow optimization and design: has already helped the Assistance Coordination Division design a case management system; future collaboration with other departments is expected.

3. Short-Term Project Design and Coordination (Event Planning):

Coordinate with multiple departments to design and execute short-term projects (including media campaigns, events, roundtables, etc.).


Requirements:

1. Prior experience in strategic work is preferred.

2. Prior experience in public welfare project design is preferred (experience with non-profit consulting firms such as ABC is acceptable).

3. Prior experience in event planning/execution within campus activities is preferred; relevant experience in consulting, external relations, or event planning and execution is applicable.



IT Support Division

The main responsibilities of the IT Support Division are divided into the following areas:


Developing the Avoice website, conducting web design and data analysis, maintaining daily website operations, and providing IT support for project activities across departments.


  • Website Development and Maintenance

  • Web Design

  • and Data Analysis


Full-Stack Web Development Member (2–3 positions)


Job Description:

1. Participate in Avoice's front-end (or client-side) and back-end (or server-side) development.

2. Design the architecture for new websites and web-based applications, and develop Application Programming Interfaces (APIs).

3. Develop updates to enhance the usability and functionality of the website's back-end, and maintain the front-end to ensure proper operation.

4. Optimize websites to ensure mobile compatibility.

5. Collaborate with graphic designers to develop attractive and functional websites.

6. Create servers and databases for the website.

7. Supervise projects at every stage of the System Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

8. Collaborate with other professionals, including DevOps engineers, cybersecurity experts, graphic designers, and other web developers.


Requirements:

1. Prior experience in full-stack web development; familiarity with js/html/css; experience with front-end and back-end frameworks such as React, Django, or Express is preferred.

2. No prior development experience but with the background and willingness to learn (e.g., CS, Data, Engineering, or related fields).

3. Familiarity with HTML, JavaScript, and CSS for front-end development, and programming languages such as Java, Python, and Ruby for back-end development.



Web Design Member (2–3 positions)


Job Description:

1. Participate in Avoice's web design.

2. Develop front-end code based on visual design mockups, and be responsible for implementing, editing, and enhancing web interface interactions.

3. Responsible for implementing, editing, and enhancing interactive designs for the organization's website, products, and project pages to improve user experience.

4. Responsible for front-end user interface development, ensuring browser compatibility, and improving, designing, and implementing user experience.

5. Collaborate with back-end developers to implement program functions and workflows.

6. Participate in front-end development for various organizational projects, converting design mockups into static pages.

7. Use HTML+CSS+JavaScript to develop and maintain projects, meet page redesign requirements, and follow up on bug fixes during development.

8. Communicate with back-end developers to complete front-end and back-end integration.


Requirements:

1. Prior experience in UI/UX design, or strong interest and ideas about web design, with willingness to learn and coordinate with content-producing departments.

2. Familiarity with, or ability to quickly learn, web design tools such as Photoshop, CorelDraw, Frontpage, and Dreamweaver.

3. Ability to use web design tools; prior web design experience is preferred.



Data Analyst (2–3 positions)


Job Description:

1. Collaborate with the Program Design Division and Monitoring & Evaluation Division to conduct organizational impact analysis (Impact Analysis).

2. Work with the Academic Research Division to conduct baseline research on sociology, international relations, women's issues, and development topics (similar to industry research).

3. Conduct daily data collection, cleaning, and analysis for the organization, industry, and peer organizations.


Requirements:

1. Strong sensitivity to data logic and foundational knowledge in statistics.

2. Strong communication skills.

3. Proficiency in data analysis using Excel, Tableau, or Python; experience with tools such as Pandas and Matplotlib is preferred.

4. Familiarity with at least one of SQL, Python, R, or SPSS; familiarity with mainstream databases such as MySQL and SQLServer; experience in data visualization is preferred.

5. Prior knowledge or experience in impact analysis, SROI, or impact frameworks is preferred; background or interest in social science research is also acceptable.

 03 Work Benefits 

  • Volunteer/Internship Certificate: According to Avoice bylaws, members who work for more than 6 months may apply for a volunteer certificate; those who complete one year of service may receive an internship certificate. Outstanding performers may be nominated for quarterly and annual outstanding volunteer awards.


  • Skill Development & Project Attribution: Website development experience and outputs can be showcased on GitHub; web design work can be included in personal portfolios; data analysis experience can be listed as project experience on resumes. High-performing contributors may become Project Managers. This experience supports job applications in consulting, data analysis, and business analytics.


  • Avoice Professional and Expert Network: Avoice volunteers and alumni are from major universities worldwide (including Ivy League, G5, and 985/211 institutions), and advisors are senior professionals from the public welfare, business, and tech sectors, offering a high-quality professional network. 


  • Daily Rewards: Members who perform diligently and achieve excellent evaluation results will receive internal incentives from their departments (including but not limited to milk tea, small gifts, etc.).


  • Receive guidance and recommendation letters from project experts and advisors.


  • Participate in online and offline volunteer team-building activities.


  • Join the International Youth Volunteer Network Alumni Association.


  • Receive internship referrals and internal recommendations from project partners.


  • Participate in research on topics related to gender equality, women's protection and development, and youth empowerment.


  • Opportunities for collaboration and exchange with domestic and international public welfare organizations, academic institutions, and foundations.


  • Learn and grow together with like-minded peers from around the world.
