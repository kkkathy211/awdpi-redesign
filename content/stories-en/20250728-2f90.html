In the tide of globalization, more and more Asian women are bravely stepping onto the world stage.
Yet in unfamiliar cultural and professional environments, how can we build confidence and influence?

And how can we connect with a broader world?


This time, we have invited a female role model with deep experience in both Eastern and Western workplaces and cultures,

to share her insights and experiences from two decades of working across borders.



Event Time

August 3, 2025, 13:00–14:00 (Beijing Time)


Featured Speaker

Li Xiaoke (Isabel Li)


Live Streaming Link

Tencent Meeting 851-351-827



Speaker Profile | Li Xiaoke (Isabel Li)



  • A London-based strategic advisor and cross-border business expert, specializing in cultural communication, stakeholder trust-building, and helping enterprises achieve long-term impact in overseas markets.


  • Head of Greater China at International Market Access (IMA), a consultancy firm.


  • Formerly served for 10 years at London & Partners headquarters as an inward investment manager.


  • Founder of the non-profit platform "TIMA," connecting education, business, and government to support international cooperation in emerging industries.


  • With two decades of experience in China and the UK, she provides "bridge-style" insights for Asian enterprises expanding into Europe.




"Twenty years ago, I came to the UK with a heart full of curiosity about the world. But what I didn't expect was that what truly shaped me wasn't the opportunities the world gave me, but the moment I chose to break through my own limits."

—— Isabel Li


In this event, Ms. Li Xiaoke will draw from her 20 years of overseas life, sharing personal stories, professional insights, and practical advice to guide us through her growth and breakthroughs in cross-cultural environments.



What You Will Gain

  • Real Stories: Challenges and transformation on the journey of studying and working abroad


  • Professional Insights: How to break through cultural and gender barriers


  • Actionable Advice: Building your own cross-cultural influence


  • Interactive Dialogue: Direct exchange of ideas with the speaker


  • International Community: Connect with other Asian women on the global path


Whether you are already overseas or about to embark;

Whether you are a student, a professional, or an entrepreneur;
this sharing session will open new perspectives and possibilities for you.
