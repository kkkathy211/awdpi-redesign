Introduction


In recent years, the trend of youth involvement in nonprofit organizations has become increasingly evident, and the rise of youth-led nonprofit groups has drawn attention to their development. Unlike in the past, public welfare communication—an essential component of the nonprofit sector—has taken on new significance thanks to the internet and new media technologies. Therefore, research on youth-led public welfare communication in the context of new media is both necessary and important. Through this nationwide survey, we aim to gain an in-depth understanding of the overall development of youth nonprofit organizations and explore the challenges they face in public welfare communication, thereby supporting the growth of youth-led initiatives and public welfare communication.



2

About the Research Project


This is the first study in China focusing on national youth nonprofit organizations whose implementation teams have members aged 28 or younger; it is also the first large-scale public welfare communication research project in the country. At the same time, this research will enrich the theoretical framework of public welfare communication in China and help fill the current gap in research on youth nonprofit organizations' communication practices.



3

We Invite You to Participate


To help everyone access the research findings as soon as possible, we sincerely hope that you, having read this far, will take part in the survey. If the average age of your nonprofit team is 28 or younger, we warmly invite you to join the collaborative research project titled "Analysis and Research on New Media Network Communication of Youth-Led Nonprofit Organizations in China" by completing the questionnaire below. If you are not working in the nonprofit sector, we still encourage you to share and like this post so that more people can learn about and join this youth public welfare communication survey. This will allow us to gain deeper insights into the current state of youth nonprofit organizations and public welfare communication, and promote healthy development in this field.


The data collected from this study will be used solely for academic research purposes. Completing the questionnaire will take approximately 15–20 minutes. To ensure the validity and authenticity of the responses, we kindly ask you to complete the questionnaire patiently. We will later distribute thank-you red packets based on questionnaire submissions. Thank you again for your participation and careful responses!
