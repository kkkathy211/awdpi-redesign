Women's Month Special Event

FemPower Tech X Ida Tin

In 2025, marking the 30th anniversary of the Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action, global calls for gender equality and women's empowerment are growing stronger than ever. At this pivotal historical moment, FemPower Tech joins hands with Ida Tin, the founder of the term "FemTech", to launch a special International Women's Day event. Focusing on women's health technology, we explore how technology can empower women and advance the full realization of gender equality and female autonomy.

Event Highlights


Online Conversation with Ida Tin: As the creator of the term "FemTech" and co-founder and CEO of Clue (a menstrual tracking app), Ida Tin will share her personal journey and entrepreneurial story, offering deep insights into global trends and future directions in women's health technology. The event will also include an interactive Q&A session, allowing you to exchange ideas, raise questions, and co-explore the future of FemTech with Ida Tin.


Cutting-Edge Insights into Women's Health Technology: From period tracking to disease screening, from reproductive health to menopause care, FemTech is reshaping women's health autonomy through technological innovation. We will examine the current state and future of the global FemTech market, revealing how technology empowers women's health.


Simultaneous Online and Offline Livestream: This event aims to enable everyone passionate about women's issues to join this in-depth dialogue on women's rights, equality, and empowerment. The online livestream breaks geographical barriers—wherever you are, you can join the discussion anytime. We have also arranged an offline venue, providing a space for face-to-face interaction, enabling communities passionate about women's health to fully engage. Attend in person, immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere, exchange ideas with like-minded individuals, and co-envision the future of women's health.

About Ida Tin


Ida Tin is a Danish entrepreneur and co-founder of Clue. She coined the term "FemTech" and is committed to advancing women's health technology. In 2013, she founded Clue, a menstrual tracking app that provides menstrual cycle management and related services to over 12 million users across more than 190 countries. Her vision and practice have brought transformative change to the field of women's health. Ida Tin was awarded the "Internet Woman Entrepreneur of the Year" under the European Commission's "European Pioneers" awards, and is a leading figure in the women's health technology industry.
