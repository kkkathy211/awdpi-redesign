In 2024, after five years of research and development by the iWanna team, five years of curriculum iteration, the upgraded "iWanna2024 Psychological Career and Graduate/PhD Application Support Program" will officially launch on April 15 The "2024 Support Program" is an online course and community service!


composed of five core components: expert lectures, mentor Q&A sessions, career workshops, a psychological professionals community, and exclusive information five core components providing course content and community support, with a service duration of approximately 4.5 months..

The "2024 Support Program" will take " "Exploring New Career Pathways, Empowering Future Psychologists" " as its theme, and throughout the program will be oriented toward psychological career development, serving as a psychological study-abroad accelerator for participants.

Participants in the early stage will immerse themselves in cutting-edge information about psychology as a discipline and the psychological industry, quickly expanding their personal horizons, bridging industry information gaps, and eliminating information asymmetry; in the mid-stage under the guidance of mental health professionals, they will connect graduate studies with career development, conduct deep self-exploration, and find their ideal positioning as "psychological professionals"; in the later stage they will move from theory to practice, eliminating application confusion and preparing thoroughly for the 2025 Fall application season.


Through guest lectures by university admissions officers and seasoned practitioners, iWanna will invite more authoritative admissions officers and mentors involved in overseas admissions to provide participants with frontline, up-to-date program insights and admission intelligence.

In the mentor Q&A component, the "2024 Support Program" will increase the rotation frequency of on-site mentors to cover as many subfields of psychology as possible, including emerging interdisciplinary fields.

Mentor Q&A coverage by specialization: counseling psychology, school counseling, and other counseling subfields, social work, art therapy, clinical psychology, developmental and educational psychology, cognitive psychology, social psychology, psychological assessment, industrial-organizational psychology, public health, UI/UX, and more.


Mentor Q&A coverage by country/region: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Hong Kong SAR, Macau SAR, Australia, multiple European countries (e.g., Netherlands, Germany, Switzerland), New Zealand, and more.


The "2024 Support Camp" mentor Q&A session will be divided into master's and doctoral special sessions, inviting iWanna star mentors respectively: Isis, a fully funded PhD student in Clinical Psychology at Florida State University, and Christy, a PhD candidate in Social and Educational Psychology at the National University of Singapore..


This powerful, real-life mentor team will allow participants to truly stand "on the shoulders of predecessors," explore their relationship with psychology, and have ample opportunities to reflect on their intrinsic motivations for entering the psychological profession.

In the psychological career workshop component, participants will, under mentor guidance, experience psychological career planning from theory to practice, avoid detours, and find development directions suited to themselves.


In addition, in line with the concept of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, we look forward to working with camp participants to embrace diversity and mutual support within the psychological professionals community, creating an inclusive and supportive environment.

The "2024 Support Program" will also cover multiple countries and regions worldwide,, offering participants an immersive "global experience" and helping them see more life possibilities through iWanna.

More importantly, the philosophy of the "2024 Support Program" does not stop at individual concerns, but rather aims to inspire participants' sense of mission as future psychological professionals from a broader, human community perspective, encouraging them to embrace a richer, deeper spiritual world.
