The following article is sourced from the Peking University Lingang Innovation and Technology Center , authored by Yanan Innovation

Peking University Lingang Innovation and Technology Center .

The Peking University International Innovation Center in Lingang, Shanghai, jointly supported by Peking University and the China (Shanghai) Pilot Free Trade Zone Lingang New Area, is a social service institution dedicated to technological innovation. The center focuses on key industrial sectors such as biomedicine, artificial intelligence, integrated circuits, new materials, and low-altitude economy, striving to become a world-class innovation platform with international influence.




On March 8, the 2025 Young Women's Innovation and Development Forum grandly opened at the Peking University Zhengda Conference Center. Hosted by the Peking University Lingang Innovation and Technology Center, Yan Yuan Incubator, and Yan Yuan Venture Capital, and organized by Yanan Innovation, this year's forum was themed "Youth Innovation Without Boundaries, She Towards the Future." Focusing on cutting-edge fields such as the AI technological revolution, green economy, and life sciences, the event brought together over ten outstanding female entrepreneurs and industry leaders from technology, business, and academia, along with more than 260 audience members, to discuss women's unique value and mission in the wave of innovation and to witness women's shining moments on the stage of the era.

Scene from the 2025 Young Women's Innovation and Development Forum


At the opening of the forum, Wang Yu, Executive Director of the Peking University Lingang Innovation and Technology Center, Founding Partner of Yan Yuan Venture Capital, Deputy Secretary-General of the Peking University Alumni Association, and Secretary-General of the Peking University Finance Alumni Association, delivered a speech on behalf of the organizers. He noted that 2025 marks Peking University's "Year of Scientific and Technological Innovation," and expressed the hope that by building the Yan Yuan Science and Technology Innovation Ecosystem, integrating key resources for innovation, a stronger, co-built, and shared resource foundation could be provided for Peking University alumni and ecosystem partners. He encouraged female innovators and entrepreneurs to actively participate in the development of the innovation ecosystem, achieving full-chain breakthroughs from innovation's "0 to 1," "1 to 10," and "10 to 100."

Wang Yu, Executive Director of the Peking University Lingang Innovation and Technology Center, delivering his speech


During the keynote speech session, Wu Lingwei, Chairperson of Yilai Technology, Founder and Chief Advisor of AA Accelerator Group, Investment Partner at Hongtai Fund, and Founder of the China Accelerator Alliance, delivered a talk titled "In the AI Era, Women Must Step Forward!" She shared insights on technological democratization brought by the AI era. She pointed out that from ChatGPT to DeepSeek, AI is reshaping the logic of life and work at a disruptive pace. Women should start from "using" AI, skillfully leveraging open-source tools and scenario-based applications, and transform empathy, wisdom, resilience, and humanistic care into innovative advantages. Through concrete cases, Wu Lingwei demonstrated how women can use technology as a lever to unlock business value, emphasizing that in the era of "technological democratization," women need to break down mental barriers and integrate AI into their lives and work.

Wu Lingwei, Founder of AA Accelerator Group, sharing her insights


In the first themed roundtable, "Her Power in Innovation Breakthroughs," Xu Tingting, Co-founder and Vice President of Business and Marketing at MechaMind (Beijing) Robotics Technology Co., Ltd., delivered a talk titled "Three Keys to Opening a New World," reflecting on her journey in hard-tech entrepreneurship. She shared three reflections with the audience: "focus on what truly matters," "life's margin for error is much higher than you think," and "leverage the unique advantage of empathy." She called on female entrepreneurs to "focus on people, focus on life," balancing career and the warmth of life through perseverance, and to break industry barriers with wisdom and resilience.

Xu Tingting, Peking University alumna and Co-founder of MechaMind Robotics, sharing her insights


In the first roundtable on the theme "Her Power in Innovation Breakthroughs," guests including Lin Lijun, Chairperson of Zhimei E-Commerce Group; Wang Jingbo, Co-founder of Gongsi Bao; Yang Ting, Vice President of Suoyun Technology; Tian Mei, Executive Assistant to the Chairperson of DHgate Group and China Lead of Tashan International Women's Community; and Long Long, Co-founder of Southern Health, discussed topics such as "moments of breaking boundaries," "the power of resilience," and "ecosystem co-creation." They shared their courage, wisdom, and boundless possibilities in female-led innovation breakthroughs, recounting the most transformative moments in their careers and exploring how women can turn their unique traits into greater advantages and build more supportive innovation ecosystems.

Roundtable Forum — Her Power in Innovation Breakthroughs


In the second roundtable on the theme "Her Energy in Entrepreneurial Advancement," guests including Xie Linghuihui, journalist at China Media Group; Lin Huijun, Vice Chair of Qingchuang Huizhi Research Institute; Wang Tiantian, Chairperson of Shanghui International Education Group; Wang Nan, Founder of GLOW&FLOW, a rising pearl brand, and Lead of 1% Lady Club women's community; and Wu Ting, Co-founder of Jiang's Ancient Tea, shared unforgettable moments from their personal growth and entrepreneurial journeys around topics such as "entrepreneurial trends," "growth stories," and "female consumption." They expressed hopes for a brighter future for women entrepreneurs and shared perspectives from their respective fields on the most promising "she economy" sectors over the next three years, encouraging women to bravely be themselves and not be defined by others.

Roundtable Forum — Her Energy in Entrepreneurial Advancement


During the flash talk session, Zhao Can, best-selling translator, CITIC Press's Translator of the Year, and translator of The World I See—The Autobiography of Fei-Fei Li, recounted Li Fei-Fei's legendary journey from working in a laundry shop to becoming a leader in AI, illustrating how women can break technological frontiers through resilience and wisdom. Liu Chao, Chief Planner of "Waste-Free Village Hugexiang" in Xiong'an New Area, Hebei, shared her work on public welfare libraries and environmental practices, channeling women's innate sensitivity to sustainable development into rural revitalization and creating a new model of circular economy. Sun Yuling, CEO of Saicun (Beijing) Biotechnology Co., Ltd., used Saicun as a case study to explain the breakthrough strategy in life sciences—"clearly identifying weaknesses, deeply exploring pain points, and daring to tackle tough challenges"—and called on women to maintain long-termism and an altruistic mindset in technical fields.

Thus concluded the "Youth Innovation Without Boundaries, She Towards the Future"—2025 Young Women's Innovation and Development Forum. This forum was not only a feast of ideas but also a profound meeting of minds. Every shared perspective and every intellectual exchange revealed the determined strides and boundless potential of young women on the path of innovation. "Youth Innovation Without Boundaries, She Towards the Future" is more than just a slogan—it is our shared belief and aspiration. We hope all women will continue to forge ahead on the path of innovation, daring to innovate and break through, shining with the brightest light in their respective fields!

"Yan Yuan Science and Technology Innovation Ecosystem" is a systematic, professional, branded, and international science and technology innovation service platform under the Peking University umbrella, jointly initiated by the Peking University Office of Technology Development and the Peking University Alumni Association, and primarily constructed by the Peking University Lingang Innovation and Technology Center. It consists of eight components: Yan Yuan Lab, Yan Yuan Incubator, Yan Yuan Venture Capital, Yan Yuan International Science and Technology Innovation Competition, Yan Yuan HUB, Yan Yuan Science and Technology Innovation Review, Yan Yuan Salon, and Yan Yuan Public Welfare.


"Yanyuan Sci-Tech Innovation Ecosystem" is based at Peking University, oriented toward industries, and serves the globe. It aims to support scientific and technological innovation from "0" to "+∞" across all stages and fields, building a global platform for high-quality sci-tech resources to exchange and converge, and deeply serving innovation centered on the transformation of scientific and technological achievements.

Shanghai Lingang Peking University International Science and Technology Innovation Center


Leveraging Peking University's innovative strengths and talent resources, the center focuses on five key industrial directions—biomedicine, artificial intelligence, integrated circuits, new materials, and quantum technology—deploying innovation, capital, talent, service, and policy chains along the industrial chain to generate innovative outcomes. Through deep incubation and financial support, it promotes the commercialization of scientific and technological achievements, incubates high-tech enterprises, and strives to become a world-class innovation platform with international influence, supporting the industrial development of the Lingang New Area and contributing to Shanghai's goal of becoming a global science and technology innovation hub.
