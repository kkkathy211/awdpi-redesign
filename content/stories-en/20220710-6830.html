Cedric Behrel, Co-Founder and Managing Director of Trinity CineAsia, said: "You Are My Spring is a delicate portrayal of society at the onset of the pandemic in China, featuring outstanding actors and crew members. Inspired by real-life events, the film easily resonates with audiences. We are deeply grateful to Wanda Pictures and 1905 Pictures for trusting us to bring this landmark film to the UK and Ireland."

China-UK Film Industry Forum and Post-Screening Meet-and-Greet

To allow UK audiences to gain deeper insights into the film, CineAsia in collaboration with 1905 Pictures and Goldsmiths University of London Chinese Student Association, the China-UK Film Industry Online Forum and Post-Screening Meet-and-Greet for You Are My Spring will be held on July 17 at Goldsmiths, with live streaming on Zoom. Everyone is welcome to attend either in person or online.

This forum will:

  • Share behind-the-scenes stories of the making of You Are My Spring
  • Extend into discussions on the current state of the Chinese film industry (in the post-pandemic era)
  • and share how young filmmakers can gradually become involved in the film industry

Spring Flower Gift

The film was originally scheduled for early April, but its release was delayed due to the pandemic. Though spring arrived late, reunion is inevitable. To help everyone experience the beauty of spring alongside the film, CineAsia in partnership with UK-based Chatime ChaTime, we are giving away "Spring Flower Gifts". Present your ticket stub or booking confirmation for You Are My Spring at the Chatime Piccadilly Circus store to receive a bouquet of flowers and enjoy an 80% discount on beverages. The floral wall in-store is waiting for you to take photos!


We believe life will always reward those who cherish it with fragrance. So for those wishes yet to come true, why not start now—make a promise with the person who walked through spring with you —look at the clear sky, look at the green fields, and on July 8, bring flowers and watch a grand "spring" together in the cinema!
