Vol.3/ Key Quotes Collection

Golden sentence

1. The essence of gender-based violence, especially domestic violence, is actually the inequality of power within the family;


2. First, as a bystander, cherish the trust the survivor places in you—do not judge them. Non-judgmental listening is difficult, but also essential;


3, everyone's personality, circumstances, and resources are different. At this moment, if you know of any resources, you can share them with the survivor as an advisor, not as their superior telling them what to do next;


4, bystanders are actually the first line of protection for survivors—they may first turn to trusted family members or friends to speak up, before taking further steps. Bystanders can absorb more information early on and then convey it to the survivor in a way that builds trust;


5. The cycle model of domestic violence: domestic violence generally consists of three stages:

The first stage is the tension-building phase, accompanied by constant arguments and minor violence;

The second stage is the acute battering incident phase, involving severe violence, physical assaults, etc.;

The third stage is the honeymoon phase, when the abuser apologizes, pleads for forgiveness, and hopes the survivor will reconcile.
