About Yale AWDA



Yale Asian Women Development Association (Yale AWDA)


AWDPI | Asian Women Development Plan International


Yale AWDA (Asian Women Development Association) is a newly established official student organization at Yale University, primarily focused on the development and support of Asian women, while being open to students of all ethnicities, genders, and backgrounds. We aim to build an inclusive, open, and mutually supportive student community, enabling more individuals who identify with this mission to participate and collectively advance projects and issues related to women's development, education, career growth, and social impact.


The work of Yale AWDA extends beyond event organization. We will operate across community support and resource navigation, personal development and events, research and policy, partnerships and external relations, media and communications as five core functional areas.


Specifically, we aim to help students connect with on- and off-campus support resources, provide women with opportunities for personal growth, career development, innovation, entrepreneurship, and education; promote knowledge sharing and cross-disciplinary exchange through lectures, forums, mentorship programs, and courses; conduct research on issues such as Asian women, gender equality, women in technology, and Women in STEM; establish collaborations with universities, corporations, non-profits, and international institutions; and expand the organization's impact through content creation and media outreach.


Yale AWDA is currently forming its inaugural team. The five functional departments at this stage represent the organization's primary focus areas but will not limit future development. We welcome members to propose new project ideas based on their interests, expertise, and resources, and to further expand into new focus areas or project teams as organizational needs and practical demands allow.



Organizational Structure


Yale AWDA is led by an Executive Board (core leadership team), with five functional departments under it:

  1. Community Support and Resources Department | Community Support and Resources Department

  2. Personal Development and Events Department | Personal Development and Events Department

  3. Research and Policy Department | Research and Policy Department

  4. Partnerships and External Relations Department | Partnerships and External Relations Department

  5. Media and Communications Department | Media and Communications Department



Recruitment Information



This recruitment is open to students and scholars at Yale University and other universities in Connecticut. Some positions support remote participation, and we welcome applicants from other locations who can commit consistently.


In cases where qualifications and team needs are comparable, we will prioritize applicants who can consistently participate in in-person activities and maintain ongoing communication with the team.



Research shows that women often refrain from applying when they do not meet all listed criteria. We value initiative, responsibility, and tangible contributions, and do not require all applicants to have identical experiences. As long as you are passionate about the role, we warmly welcome your application!




Role Structure | Departmental Positions


Each functional department at Yale AWDA will ideally have 1–2 Chairs and will recruit several Fellows.


Yale AWDA Organizational Chart


Chair | Department Head

Responsible for overall department management, including developing work plans, advancing key projects, coordinating team assignments, monitoring progress, and communicating and collaborating with the Executive Board and other departments. Chairs are primarily accountable for departmental outcomes and project implementation.


Fellows | Department Members

Participate in specific departmental tasks and projects, and may take on different responsibilities or project directions based on personal interests, experience, and skills. We encourage Fellows to proactively propose project ideas and engage in the full process from initial conception and resource coordination to execution, gradually taking on greater responsibility through hands-on experience.



Flexible Structure | Flexible Departmental Setup


The current five departments represent Yale AWDA's main focus areas at this stage. We will make appropriate adjustments to departmental divisions, staffing, and working mechanisms based on actual recruitment outcomes, team capacity, and project needs.


Members who wish to pursue new projects or topics beyond the current departmental mandates are also welcome to propose them proactively. For ideas that are well-defined, actionable, and aligned with Yale AWDA's overall mission, we may support the establishment of new project directions or working groups, which could potentially evolve into independent departments in the future.



Term | Duration of Commitment


The term for all Chairs and Fellows is at least one year.


For candidates applying to the Executive Board, all else being equal, we will prioritize those who can commit to a longer term, maintain consistent involvement, and contribute to the organization's sustained development.

Community Support and Resources Department | Community Support and Resources Department

Provides peer support, resource navigation, and relevant information to Yale students, helping those in need understand and access professional support resources both on and off campus, while also conducting resource education on gender-based violence prevention, wellbeing, student safety, and community mutual aid.


Main Responsibilities:

  • Offer basic peer support and active listening, helping students understand available support options.

  • Compile and update information on resources such as the SHARE Center, OIEA (Office of Institutional Equity and Accessibility), Yale College Dean's Office, GSAS Dean's Office, McDougal Graduate Student Center, Office of Student Affairs, Yale Health, and relevant off-campus services.

  • Upon a student's request, assist in connecting them with appropriate professional resources and provide supportive referral accompaniment.

  • Maintain contact with relevant Yale offices and student support organizations to stay informed about the latest policies, trainings, and support channels.

  • Organize activities related to Gender-Based Violence, Healthy Relationships, Consent, Wellbeing, safety, and resource utilization.

  • Develop and maintain the Yale AWDA Resource Guide, clearly outlining the purpose, applicable scenarios, and contact information for each resource.

  • Organize community support and mutual aid events, gather member feedback, and continuously improve the support system based on input.


Specific Requirements:

  • Interest in Student Support, Gender Equality, Wellbeing, or Community Service.

  • Strong listening, communication, and empathy skills.

  • Ability to respect personal boundaries, privacy, and individual autonomy.

  • Strong sense of responsibility regarding sensitive information and willingness to undergo relevant training.

  • Prior experience in Peer Support, Advocacy, or Community Service is preferred.


Support Boundaries:

Yale AWDA provides Peer Support, Resource Information, and Resource Navigation. It does not offer psychotherapy, medical advice, legal counsel, case investigations, or Title IX determinations, and should not be considered a Yale-designated Confidential Resource. In cases requiring professional support, we will assist individuals in understanding appropriate official channels based on their wishes and specific needs.

Personal Development and Events Department | Personal Development and Events Department

Responsible for Yale AWDA's personal development, career advancement, innovation and entrepreneurship, educational courses, and lecture events, providing members with diverse opportunities for learning, exchange, practice, and professional growth. Members can participate in different project teams or cross-project collaborations based on their interests and strengths; no member is expected to handle all work areas simultaneously.


Main Responsibilities:

  • Design and operate the Yale AWDA Mentorship Program, connecting Alumni, Professionals, Founders, Researchers, and Industry Leaders through initiatives such as Mentor–Mentee Matching, One-on-One Mentoring, Small Group Mentoring, and Office Hours.

  • Plan career development events on topics such as Resume, Interview, Networking, Personal Branding, and Career Strategy; host Career Conversations across fields including STEM, Business, Policy, Entrepreneurship, Academia, and Nonprofit; and curate opportunities in Internships, Fellowships, Research, Leadership, and Conferences.

  • Collaborate with Yale Ventures, Tsai CITY, Startup Yale competitions, and related student funds to invite female Founders, Investors, Product Leaders, and tech professionals to share innovation and entrepreneurship experiences, with thematic projects on Women in Tech, AI, FemTech, Women's Health, Technology for Social Good, and female entrepreneurship.

  • Plan and organize events such as Seminars, Panels, Fireside Chats, Roundtables, Forums, and Workshops, focusing on themes including Asian Women, Leadership, Technology, Entrepreneurship, Gender Equality, Global Development, and Social Impact.

  • Research and contact professors, corporate executives, entrepreneurs, investors, researchers, and international organization representatives from on- and off-campus; manage speaker invitations, coordination, speaker briefings, agendas, discussion questions, and moderator guides; and oversee hosting, guest reception, on-site execution, and post-event reviews.

  • Collaborate with on-campus student groups, startup incubators, companies, and external partners to co-host lectures, industry forums, career development workshops, and cross-organizational initiatives, expanding Yale AWDA's professional network, resource integration capacity, and project impact.


Specific Requirements:

  • Interest in one or more of the following: personal development, career advancement, innovation and entrepreneurship, education, event planning, or women's development.

  • Strong communication, information gathering, organizational coordination, or project execution skills.

  • Willing to proactively share ideas and participate in the entire process from initial planning to on-the-ground implementation.

  • Able to communicate effectively with members, guests, mentors, and partner organizations from diverse backgrounds.

  • Possesses basic time management skills, able to commit consistently and complete tasks on time.

  • Prior experience in Entrepreneurship, Career Development, Mentorship, Event Planning, Teaching, or related fields is preferred.


Position Features:

This department is especially suitable for students who wish to gain experience in event planning, project management, career development, entrepreneurial practice, education programs, or external guest coordination. Applicants can choose their primary area of involvement based on their strengths and explore other fields through hands-on projects.

Research and Policy Department | Research and Policy Department

Responsible for Yale AWDA's research and policy analysis on issues concerning Asian women, gender equality, women in STEM, and related public affairs, providing research, data, and evidence support for the organization's programs, events, and initiatives.


Main Responsibilities:

  • Conduct research on topics including Asian Women, Gender Equality, Women in STEM, and Women's Development.

  • Organize Student Research Projects and Research Working Groups.

  • Carry out Literature Reviews, Data Collection, Interviews, Qualitative Research, and Quantitative Analysis.

  • Write Research Briefs, Policy Briefs, special reports, and other research outputs.

  • Explore opportunities for collaboration with Yale Professors, Labs, Research Centers, and related research initiatives.

  • Compile and analyze key policies, data, and research related to Asian women and gender issues.

  • Provide research, data, and evidence support to other departments within Yale AWDA.

  • Help members access RA Opportunities, Academic Conferences, and other research resources.

  • Build the Yale AWDA Research Archive and promote the publication of research findings as projects develop.


Specific Requirements:

  • Interested in Research, Policy, Data, Gender Equality, Asian Women, or Women in STEM.

  • Strong skills in information gathering, reading, analysis, and writing.

  • Able to carefully verify sources and follow basic research ethics.

  • Interest in Quantitative Research, Qualitative Research, or Data Analysis.

  • Prior experience in Research, Data Analysis, or Policy Writing is preferred.

Who are we looking for?



We are not just looking for members who are willing to attend events, but for partners who are eager to actively contribute to organizational development, drive project implementation, and grow together with the team.


If you are passionate about the development, education, technology, career advancement, public issues, or social impact for Asian women; if you want to turn your professional skills, campus resources, creativity, and passion into real-world projects; if you are ready to take responsibility, explore new directions, and build long-term collaborations within a growing organization—we welcome you to join Yale AWDA.


Whether you excel in research and analysis, event planning, curriculum design, media operations, resource coordination, or external collaboration, there is a place for you at Yale AWDA.


Empower Asian Women · Build Community · Create Lasting Impact


We look forward to building, together with you, a more open, supportive, and action-oriented community, creating more opportunities for growth, connection, and development for women.
