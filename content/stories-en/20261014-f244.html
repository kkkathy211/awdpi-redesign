AWDPI Event Registration | Online – Gender Expression in the Age of AI

🕑 Date: October 18, 2026 (Sunday)
UK Time 14:00 | China Time 21:00

Posting a note, translating a paragraph, or asking AI to polish a few sentences—we are increasingly relying on technology for all forms of creation. At the same time, platform algorithms and certain expressive tendencies of AI are shaping our views in return—especially AI's gender biases.

This time, AWDPI's October Academic Roundtable invites Xiaohongshu blogger, researcher, and Genderly founder 404 to discuss, from the perspectives of content creation, academic research, and product development, the relationship between women and technology.

We will explore how platform algorithms influence whether women's voices are heard, and how women's experiences and concerns can enter technological design when they participate in algorithmic research and develop products themselves. Drawing on 404's research and Genderly's practical work, we'll talk about the possibilities of recognizing bias and driving change.
Join us for this discussion on October 18!
