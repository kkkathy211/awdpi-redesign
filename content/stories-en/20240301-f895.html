The United Nations' 2024 International Women's Day theme is "Invest in Women: Accelerate Progress." By 2030, the annual funding gap for gender equality initiatives could reach as high as 360 billion US dollars, making it urgent to advance financing for gender equality.


During the United Nations 2023 International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women "16 Days of Activism," we hosted five online workshops, discussing topics such as "Occupational Illnesses through a Gender Lens" with over 300 audience members. The 2024 International Women's Day theme continues the 2023 International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women theme: "Eliminate gender-based violence by providing adequate funding." Without significant investment to address violence against women and girls, scale up prevention programs, and implement effective policies, countries will not be able to eliminate gender-based violence by 2030.


This year, AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International will host four workshops spanning China, Germany, and Ecuador during International Women's Day, expressing solidarity with survivors of gender-based violence alongside individuals across countries who care about gender issues.

About AWDPI


AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International was founded in 2020 as an international nonprofit organization centered on women's rights. Currently operating in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, AWDPI advocates for attention to the living challenges faced by Asian women overseas through a broad network of collaborations with United Nations agencies, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. It provides sustained protection and support to empower these women with greater opportunities for development in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, transforming them from vulnerable groups into key forces driving social progress.
