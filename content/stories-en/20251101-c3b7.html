See the invisible violence · Protect children's safety · Listen to women's courage

See the Unseen Violence · Protect Every Child · Hear Every Woman's Voice


Domestic violence is never just about visible wounds.
It may hide in emotional blackmail, in a dismissive word, in financial control, or in a threat disguised as "for your own good."


At this AWDPI Australia film event, we will come together to watch the film— *After My Storm*—a powerful film revealing invisible harms within families and intimate relationships.


The film tells the story of a woman's experience of violence and control within her family and intimate relationship. It exposes the complex layers of physical, emotional, psychological, and coercive control, and reveals how deeply such violence affects the lives and inner worlds of survivors and their children.


1

Key Themes


Through film clips, we will focus on the following three themes for discussion:


1

Violence and Its Invisible Forms

Violence and Its Invisible Side: Physical Violence, Manipulation, Intimidation and Psychological Abuse

Recognizing subtle forms of control, manipulation, and psychological harm, and understanding how "violence" exists invisibly in everyday life.


2

Children Should Not Be Witnesses or Tools of Violence

Children Should Never Be Witnesses or Instruments of Violence

The shadow of violence falls not only on adults but also deeply impacts children's development. How can legal and social support help children return to a safe world?


3

From Fear to Speaking Out: The Power of Law and Women's Courage

From Fear to Voice: The Power of Law and the Courage to Speak Out

When fear is no longer hidden, courage begins to grow. With the support of the law and the community, every time a survivor speaks out, it is a rejection of violence—and a step toward self-liberation.


2

Special Guest


We are honored to invite Carroll Lin Liyun, Managing Lawyer at AnTu Law Firm, as our special guest.



She will offer professional insights from the perspective of Australian family law, guiding us through:


  • Legal responsibilities and boundaries within family and intimate relationships

  • How to identify and respond to domestic violence

  • Legal protection and support pathways for survivors and children


3

What You Will Gain by Participating


  • Real-life inspiration: Understand reality through film and learn to identify hidden forms of violence.

  • Legal insights: Access the most practical information on seeking help and protection under Australian family law.

  • Interaction and connection: Engage directly with the guest and share reflections in a safe, supportive space.

  • Gift surprise: In-person participants can join the Q&A session and receive a special gift!
