International Artificial Intelligence Association (IAIA)

The International Artificial Intelligence Association (IAIA) was born in San Francisco, a global hub for AI innovation, and is a non-governmental, non-profit international organization spanning 22 countries with 30 branches worldwide. IAIA actively participates in high-level United Nations meetings and the core circle of global AI governance, having provided strategic advisory services to presidents and prime ministers of multiple countries. It serves as a key international nexus connecting top-level governance with industrial implementation.

United Nations Association of Nigeria (UNAN)

The United Nations Association of Nigeria (UNAN) is an officially recognized civil society organization by the United Nations in Nigeria. Founded in 1959 by renowned educator Professor Babs Fafuwa, UNAN has been deeply engaged in Nigeria's civil society for over six decades. UNAN has long taken responsibility for advancing Nigeria's alignment with core United Nations agendas and is currently mobilizing, inspiring, and educating all sectors of Nigerian civil society to collectively achieve the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI)

AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International) is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating discrimination and gender-based violence against Asian women living overseas, and promoting their capacity building and development. It evolved from Avoice (Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project, Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program), established in 2020.

AWDPI advocates for attention to the survival challenges faced by Asian women overseas and provides them with sustained protection and support. AWDPI is committed to helping Asian women overseas gain greater development opportunities in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, empowering them to become vital forces driving social progress.

Project Background

)

02

In March 2026, the 70th session of the United Nations Commission on the Status of Women (CSW70) convened at the United Nations Headquarters in New York. Representatives from countries around the world held in-depth consultations on the central theme "Accelerating Gender Equality — Ending Poverty, Strengthening Institutions, and Advancing Gender-Responsive Public Finance," and issued a strong call to action on the intersection of artificial intelligence and gender equality: The gender gap in digital technology is becoming a root cause of a new wave of systemic inequality.


As an international non-governmental organization participating in CSW70 side events, the International Artificial Intelligence Association (IAIA) understands that action must follow commitment. To this end, IAIA has partnered with the United Nations Association of Nigeria (UNAN) to launch this project, taking concrete action to implement the spirit of CSW70—providing foundational AI education to girls and women in Nigeria, breaking down digital barriers, and redefining their relationship with the future.


We sincerely invite university students passionate about international public service to join this United Nations-affiliated, globally impactful project as online volunteer teachers.
