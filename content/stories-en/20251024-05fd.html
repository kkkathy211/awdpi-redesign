In 2025, AWDPI welcomed a new group of volunteers. In August, we held a warm and empowering welcome event to convey the organization's mission and vision to our new members, and to help them feel the support and care from their peers through interactive activities.


From Research to Action: The Birth of a Global Support Network

In 2020, a research project on the rights violations of overseas Chinese people unexpectedly received a large number of domestic violence appeals from overseas Chinese women. This finding revealed that among women living across borders, violence issues are more hidden, and they face greater difficulties in accessing support. Seizing this opportunity, the Avoice project was born.


At this welcome event, founder Yimar, introduced that Avoice is an international humanitarian aid project that provides multidimensional assistance and resource linkage support for Asian women experiencing domestic violence. Initially operating only in Belgium, the project gradually expanded to countries and regions including the UK, the US, Australia, and Thailand, forming a transnational cooperation mechanism. Five years on, Avoice has grown into a women's protection organization spanning multiple countries and regions—


AWDPI (Asian Women Development Plan International)—which has established, through frontline aid, external collaboration, internal support, and coordination among national offices, an assistance system covering language, legal, psychological, and social integration services. , Today, it has become the strongest voice and support for countless overseas Asian women. Another


founder, Liu Feng, stated that "Every person is an owner of this organization." This philosophy encourages every member to participate in building AWDPI with the mindset of "my organization," making public good truly belong to every member and to every person in society. United by Belief: Making Equality Our Common Language


At the welcome event, long-standing AWDPI members systematically introduced the organization's vision, structure, and culture to the new volunteers, and designed interactive sessions to help new members get to know one another, express themselves, and build connections.

During the event, volunteers used


the scenario-based board game *Her Awkward Moments*as a medium to discuss, in small groups, common situations of gender inequality in daily life. Some chose silence, some attempted gentle responses, and others bravely spoke up directly. During the discussion, veteran gender equality practitioner Wang Yanping provided interpretations based on real-life cases, sparking deep cross-perspective reflections and heartfelt resonance among participants. In

the outstanding volunteer sharing session, legal support team volunteer Chang Jingzi, said: "The success of every case relies on the full collaboration of the entire department. My colleagues at AWDPI are pure-hearted and resolute, and they make me feel the power of authenticity." Jingzi joined out of interest, but stayed for the cause of gender equality. Human Resources team volunteer Zhou Wenxin


said: "Every name on the HR list corresponds to a person who is taking action. I hope that anyone who believes in equality and is willing to work for it can proudly call themselves a feminist." When sharing her hopes for AWDPI's future, she quoted a former AWDPI beneficiary who later became a volunteer: "I came for myself, for us, and for more women." This transformation from beneficiary to activist is the most touching embodiment of AWDPI's philosophy—women who are seen are also using their own strength to illuminate others. Going forward, AWDPI will continue to bring issues affecting overseas Asian women into the public eye, host diverse thematic workshops, strengthen collaboration among national offices, and explore more cross-sector initiatives to create a safer and more dignified living environment for women.



After five years of growth and the dedicated efforts of members across different countries, AWDPI has proven, through countless cases and stories, that public good is a co-created endeavor—it is the power of every individual.



Join us, and help make the world a little less violent and a little more equal.
