Global Citizen Year




[Organization Introduction]

Founded in 2008, Global Citizen Year is an international educational nonprofit focused on youth leadership empowerment. It currently runs two programs: the Take Action Lab and the Academy, aiming to connect innovative and transformative global leaders through top-tier academic and practical programs, and to cultivate a new generation of young global leaders to jointly address global challenges such as climate change, disease prevention, education improvement, and poverty elimination. The Take Action Lab program lasts four months and involves field internships and practical work with local organizations and communities in various developing countries, systematically learning how to solve real-world global challenges. The Academy program, launched in 2020 during the global pandemic, is a 12-week online leadership course combining academic and practical components, with youth leaders from over 100 countries participating. The program has invited dozens of world-renowned innovators and change-makers such as Melinda Gates, Sheryl Sandberg, and Sundar Pichai to teach and share, and provides one-on-one mentoring with top professionals across various industries. Global Citizen Year maintains close partnerships with hundreds of charitable foundations, organizations, and individuals, offering scholarships and financial aid to all eligible applicants in need.

YSI China



[Organization Introduction]

Young Sustainable Impact, abbreviated as YSI, originated in Norway and is dedicated to supporting outstanding youth in addressing sustainable development challenges through business innovation. YSI launched in China in 2018, with a team composed of young innovators and entrepreneurs committed to changing the world through business innovation. It builds platforms for Chinese youth, organizes sustainability and business summits, and focuses on advancing sustainable development and business innovation in Greater China. YSI China has successfully hosted five Changemakers Summits, incubated multiple startups in fields including education, circular economy, healthcare, and rural empowerment, and received coverage from over thirty media outlets including People's Daily, China Daily, and Global Times.

Pine Nut Youth Study Society



[Organization Introduction]

Pine Nut Youth Study Society was founded in September 2015, dedicated to helping young people aged 16–28 solve career growth and development challenges through effective career innovation courses and diverse social innovation projects. Over the past eight years, it has reached over 1,000 universities across more than 65 cities, directly helping 100,000 young people with career development. In August 2020, it won the United Nations Development Programme's "Most Watched Project" award, was shortlisted for the Xiangguang Award's Top 10 Social Enterprises of the Year (the highest honor for social enterprises), was named one of the "Top 20 Most Promising Social Enterprises" in April 2021, was included in the University of Hong Kong Faculty of Social Sciences' Social Enterprise Case Collection in June 2021, was listed on the Forbes China U30 Social Enterprise List in September 2021, and won the National Gold Award at the 8th China Youth Entrepreneurship Competition organized by the Communist Youth League Central Committee in December 2021.

AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International



[Organization Introduction]

AWDPI Asian Women Development Plan International is an international nonprofit organization centered on women's rights, evolved from the Avoice Overseas Chinese Women Protection Project (Against Violence to Overseas Chinese Women Program) established in 2020. It was initiated at the beginning of the global outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, dedicated to eliminating violence and discrimination against Asian women and promoting their protection and development.

AWDPI operates in multiple countries and regions across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific, with National Office teams established in the UK, Belgium, and the United States. It has built extensive partnerships with various sectors, including United Nations system organizations, relevant government departments in different countries, international organizations, multinational corporations, and academic institutions. Together, they advocate for attention to the survival challenges faced by overseas Asian women and provide them with sustained protection and support, enabling greater development opportunities in social, economic, cultural, and educational fields, transforming them from vulnerable groups into a vital force driving social progress.
