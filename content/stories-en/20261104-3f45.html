{ My Health }



Dear friends:


After arriving in the United States, we are all striving to adapt to a new life.


Busy with studies, work, and family, constantly shifting between different roles, we often habitually place our own feelings last.


In intimate relationships, are we receiving enough understanding and support? Amid life's pressures and cultural differences, how are our physical and mental well-being?


As Chinese American women, our experiences and needs are shaped by a unique cultural context, yet our voices are often rarely heard in existing health research.


Your feelings matter. Your health deserves serious attention.




To fill this gap, the School of Nursing at The University of Texas at Austin is currently conducting a health study specifically focused on Chinese American women. We hope to listen to your authentic experiences and use scientific data and resources to provide better support for this community.


We sincerely invite you, if eligible, to join this study. Your participation is not only an act of care for yourself, but also a way to speak up for more Chinese American women living in the U.S.


This study is conducted in collaboration with Asian Women Development Plan International (AWDPI) We are committed to using scientific research methods to provide stronger data support and resource connections for the health of Chinese American women.



You may qualify to participate if:

✅ You are 18 years old or above
✅ You identify as a woman
✅ You identify as Chinese
✅ You currently reside in the United States
✅ You have been in an intimate partner relationship within the past year (e.g., marriage, cohabitation, or dating)


If you meet the study criteria and choose to participate, you will: receive:

  • free access to health-related information and support resources;

  • complete 5 online surveys over one year (easy to use, flexible timing);

  • and receive up to $175 in total compensation!
