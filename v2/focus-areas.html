Program 1

Protection of overseas Asian women

GoalBuild a full, fast and effective aid network for Asian women who suffer gender-based or domestic violence. This began as Avoice and now serves Asian women more widely.

What we do 7

Language support

Translation in many languages, and sign language.

Legal aid

Case intervention, pro bono help and a basic legal handbook.

Psychological counselling

Counselling tailored to each woman, including help with PTSD.

Shelter

Links to domestic violence shelters.

€

Emergency relief fund

Emergency money for women facing extreme poverty, illness or unemployment.

Global aid network

Aid resources and partner organizations around the world.

Survivor stories

Recording survivors' stories and sharing how aid agencies work.

Program 2

Violence-free spaces

GoalA violence-free community where Asian women talk, study, share their experience and support each other, with tools for a better life.

What we do 3

International communities

Online communities by country, with workshops on gender equality, self-defence and legal basics, and group counselling.

Women's support groups

Local groups with book clubs, film screenings and fireside chats, and peer-to-peer support.

Virtual violence-free spaces

Working with researchers on non-violence standards for online and virtual communities.

Program 3

Women's leadership

GoalGive Asian women leadership skills, supportive communities and job opportunities, so they can reach their full potential.

What we do 3

Conference of Asian Women Development International

Held every year. The third gathered 54 leaders, nearly 1,000 people in person and 10,000+ online.

Female leadership programs

Workshops, expert panels and roundtables, with companies that back women's careers.

Jobs and advancement

Mentors from many industries, professional training and a network of women-friendly employers.

Program 4

Campaigns against gender-based violence

GoalWork with governments, research institutes, social organizations and media to raise awareness of what Asian women overseas face, and to change policy.

What we do 4

Case studies

Focused studies of gender-based violence and the challenges around it.

Country and regional research

The state of gender-based violence in specific countries. 40 reports so far.

Public awareness

Campaigns every International Women's Month and during the 16 Days of Activism.

Policy and law

Evidence-based research that asks governments to better protect and support women.
