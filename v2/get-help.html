What we can help with

I need Legal support

If you face the police, a court case, a protection order, visa or immigration questions, divorce or custody.

Message us on WeChat Or find your local hotline

I need Psychological support

If you feel tense, anxious or afraid, or simply need someone to talk to and be with you.

Message us on WeChat Or find your local hotline

I need Language support

If you need an interpreter at appointments, or translation of legal or council documents.

Message us on WeChat Or find your local hotline

I need Empowerment support

If you need emergency shelter, help finding work, or social benefits.

Message us on WeChat Or find your local hotline

Violence includes

Any gender-based act that causes, or could cause, physical, sexual or mental harm, including threats and taking away someone's freedom.

  • Physical harm
  • Sexual harm
  • Psychological abuse
  • Control of money or freedom
  • Threats
