Our research team

The research team is one of AWDPI's core departments. It supports the design of our programs, so help for survivors rests on evidence.

Members come from many fields. With guidance from experts and advisors, they publish reports, popular-science articles and a monthly review on domestic abuse, women's protection and gender equality.
