Before you give

Who receives your gift

Donations on this page go to Asian Women Development Plan International US, Inc. (EIN 93-2511417), a nonprofit corporation recognised by the IRS as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3). Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by U.S. law.

What your gift is used for

Your gift supports the work of AWDPI United States with Asian women affected by domestic violence, gender-based violence or discrimination. AWDPI United States may also fund AWDPI programs in other countries; it decides on and oversees each grant itself. A gift to a specific campaign is used for that campaign.

Receipts

After each donation on this page you receive an email receipt from Asian Women Development Plan International US, Inc., with its EIN, the date and amount of your gift, and whether you received any goods or services in return. Keep it for your tax records.

Gifts made on GoFundMe are processed by GoFundMe. For certified nonprofit fundraisers, PayPal Giving Fund may receive the gift, send your tax receipt and then pass the funds to AWDPI United States.

Monthly gifts

A monthly gift is charged once a month, starting on the day you give, until you cancel it. To change or cancel a monthly gift, or to ask about a refund, write to us@awdpi.org.
